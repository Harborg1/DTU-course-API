        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>De studerende vil opnå praktisk erfaring i metoderne og viden bag følgende AM teknologier
1. Mask-projection vatphotopolymerization af plast 
2. Mask-projection vatphotopolymerization af metal og keramik kompositter
3. Metal Laser Powder Bed Fusion
4. Plast Laser Powder Bed Fusion
5. Binder Jetting af metaller og keramer
6. Ekstruderingsbaseret AM

De studerende vil gennemgå den samlede proceskæde fra design, simulering, slicing, forberedelse af eksperiment, selve printprocessen, rengøring af emnet, efterfølgende varme- og overfladebehandling og til endelig materialekarakterisering. Hver gruppe vil fokusere på et eller flere aspekter ved en specifik AM teknologi ved at gennemføre relevante eksperimenter i overensstemmelse med de opstillede projektmål.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Eksperimentelt projektarbejde (i grupper af 2-4 studerende), rapportskrivning, præsentation af projektarbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset giver den studerende dybdegående viden om og praktisk erfaring med industrielt førende 3D print/Additive Manufacturing (AM) teknologier ved at arbejde med avancerede projekter i grupper. Projektarbejde omfatter alle faser af et projekt, herunder planlægning og udførelse af relevante laboratorieundersøgelser samt tolkning af eksperimentelle forsøgsresultater.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The students will gain hands-on experience using one of the several possible AM technologies. The different types of AM technologies available are
1.	Mask-projection vatphotopolymerization using polymers
2.	Mask-projection vatphotopolymerization using slurries infused with metals and/or ceramics
3.	Metal Laser Powder Bed Fusion
4.	Polymer Laser Powder Bed Fusion
5.	Binder Jetting using metals and ceramics
6.     Extrusion-based Additive Manufacturing

The students will go through the entire manufacturing process chain including design, simulation, slicing, build preparation, AM, post-process cleaning and finishing, post-process heat and surface treatments followed by materials characterization. Each group will focus on one or several aspects of a specific AM process to perform experiment-based research to further the stated goals of the project.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Experimental project work (teams consist of 2-4 students), preparation of report, presentation of project work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives the student in-depth knowledge and practical experience with industry leading Additive Manufacturing (AM) technologies by working on cutting-edge projects in small groups. Project work comprises all stages of a project, including planning and performing relevant laboratory investigations as well as interpretation of experimental results.</Course_Objectives>
