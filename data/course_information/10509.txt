        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset vil bestå af præsentationer af Ph.D.-studerende af videnskabelige artikler fra anerkendte tidsskrifter. Emnet for de valgte artikler skal ligge inden for området avanceret elektronmikroskopi samt dets anvendelse i studiet af atomare strukturer og dynamik. Artiklernes formål, resultater og konklusioner skal præsenteres, og en kritisk stillingtagen til metoder og resultater skal foretages. Den enkelte studerende skal redegøre for hvad det nye i artiklerne er og for hvorledes dette kan bidrage til vedkommendes forskningsprojekt. Hver præsentation skal resultere i et kort resume med de vigtigste resultater og konklusioner. Disse sider samles for kurset, således at en database af interessante artikler løbende opbygges.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Seminarer bestående af præsentationer givet af deltagerne i aktuelle emner i atomart opløst elektronmikroskopi.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende en kritisk tilgang til litteraturen samt viden og overblik over nyeste forskning inden for avanceret elektronmikroskopi og dets anvendelse i studiet af atomare strukturer og dynamik. Endvidere trænes videnskabelig formidling og diskussion samt evnen til at vurdere etiske aspekter.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Registreres af den ph.d.-studerende som et specialkursus (ph.d.) i PhD Planner.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will consist of presentations by PhD students of scientific articles from recognized journals. The topic of the selected articles should be in the field of advanced electron microscopy as well as its application in the study of atomic structures and dynamics. The purpose, results and conclusions of the articles must be presented, and a critical assesment of methods and results must be made. The individual student must explain what is new in the articles and how this can contribute to the person's research project. Each presentation should result in a brief summary with the main findings and conclusions. These pages are compiled for the course, so that a database of interesting articles is continuously built up.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Seminars consisting of presentations given by the participants on current topics in atomically resolved electron microscopy.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The students will develop a critical approach to the literature as well as knowledge and overview of recent research in advanced electron microscopy and its application in the study of atomic structures and dynamics. Furthermore, scientific communication and discussion as well as the ability to assess ethical aspects are trained.</Course_Objectives>
