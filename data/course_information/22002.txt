        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Hvert år vil der blive foreslået flere forskningsprojekter inden for hørelse eller andre relaterede sanseområder. Først vil de studerende blive introduceret til projekterne gennem præsentationer og demonstrationer. Derefter bliver de inddelt i grupper og tildelt et af projekterne. Hver gruppe — under vejledning af en ph.d.-studerende, postdoc eller et fakultetsmedlem — vil designe et eksperiment, indsamle data og analysere resultaterne for at teste en specifik hypotese relateret til projektet.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Each year, several research projects in the field of hearing, or in other related sensory systems, will be proposed. First, the students will be familiarized with the projects through presentations and demonstrations. Then the students will be divided into groups and assigned to one of the projects. Each group—under the supervision of a PhD student, postdoc or faculty member—will design an experiment, collect data and analyze the results to test a specific hypothesis related to the project. Finally, they will present their findings during a poster session.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and laboratory work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide experience in measurement and analysis methods that are used in hearing research. The aim is furthermore to train the participants in oral presentations of scientific material and to produce, present, and discuss a poster.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The contents of the course will be adapted from year to year.</Remark>
