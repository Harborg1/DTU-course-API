          <Course_Objectives>Målet med kurset er at introducere de studerende til en international tilgang til bæredygtigt byggeri, set i lyset af nedenstående:
• en analystisk metode i relation til bæredygtighed
• de grundlæggende parametre for design af bæredygtigt byggeri
• særligt fokus på de parametre, der ikke normalt indgår i sædvanlige beregninger af bæredygtighed
• særligt fokus på betydningen af fejl og skader i forhold til bæredygtighed
• særligt fokus på betydningen af materialespild i forhold til bæredygtighed
• særligt fokus på funktionalitet og arealudnyttelse i forhold til bæredygtighed</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Based on a study tour, the study objectives are analyzed and processed from the perspective of sustainability in a broad sense and from a general holistic view.
The students must also expand their international network on sustainable construction through the study tour.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures with discussion
Project work in smaller groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to
introduce the students to an international approach to sustainable construction, seen in the light of the following:
• an analytical method in relation to sustainability
• the basic parameters for the design of sustainable construction
• special focus on the parameters that are not normally included in usual calculations of sustainability
• special focus on the importance of errors and damages in relation to sustainability
• special focus on the importance of material waste in relation to sustainability
• special focus on functionality and area utilization in relation to sustainability</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Part of the continuing education programme Master of Sustainable Construction.
The course includes a study trip.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>The course starts in December, and blends into other courses on the Master in Sustainable Construction throughout the spring semester. This is taken into account in the course planning.
A supervisor is assigned to each group. The groups themselves choose their travel destinations and study subjects after further agreement with the supervisor.
4-6 online lessons are held (half-day, corresponding to the other courses on the programme). Here, the students will, among other things, meet engineers and architects who work with sustainable construction around the world.</Remark>
