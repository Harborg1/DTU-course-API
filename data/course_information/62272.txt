          <Contents>Indholdet af kurset "projektbaseret produktion" omfatter den praktiske del af planlægning og styring af bygge og anlægsprojekter. Det tager udgangspunkt i den indledende design og projektering, men med hovedvægt i den fysisk produktion på byggepladsen.

Optimering: Lean construction (flow, value, variability, and waste), value stream mapping.

Styring: Staderegisterering, Earned value management, 

Områderne understøttes gennem relevante it-værktøjer som excel, BIM, Tactplan, m.fl.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The content of the project-based production course is the practical part of planning and controlling construction projects. It starts from the initial design and planning but with the main emphasis on the physical production on the construction site.

Production and contracting forms: Turnkey, DB, DbD contracts, on/offsite production, prefabrication

Quality planning: Qualification, quality levels, quality plans, control plans, supervision plans

Time planning: Kanban, SCRUM, PERT, Gant, location-based

Financial planning: Reference class forecasting, successive calculation, cost databases,

Uncertainty management: estimation and sensitivity analysis 

Optimisation: Lean construction (flow, value, variability, and waste), value stream mapping.

Control: registration of completion, Earned value management,

The areas are supported through relevant IT tools such as Excel, BIM, Tactplan, etc.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course introduces management of time, cost, and quality in construction from planning to handover. Weekly assignments prepare students for the oral exam. Guest lecturers present key industry roles, helping students identify suitable internships. An optional study trip offers insight into international building practices.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Anlægsteknik 2: Styring af byggeprocessen
Relevant guidelines form værdibyg</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to develop competencies for the student to plan and manage project-based production of construction and infrastructure by integrating management areas such as time, cost and quality throughout the construction life cycle from the initial conceptualisation to the final handover. The course links general skills within project management (62074) and production (62079) with construction's central stakeholders, contract forms, and special regulatory conditions.</Course_Objectives>
