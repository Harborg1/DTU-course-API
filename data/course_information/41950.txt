          <Contents>Kurset dækker mekaniske egenskaber ved træ og træbaserede produkter, statiske beregningsmodeller for lige træelementer (bjælker og søjler) i brud- (ULS) og anvendelsesgrænsetilstand (SLS), varighedsafhængige belastninger på trækonstruktioner, fugtighedens indflydelse på design af trækonstruktioner, konstruktion af træbaserede elementer (konstruktionstræ, limtræ og andre Engineered Wood Products (EWP)), fastgørelsesmidler og design af samlinger ved hjælp af dyvler, bolte og søm samt lokale ustabilitetsproblemer, dvs. søjlevirkning og kipning. Kurset består af forelæsninger og løsning af opgaver i løbet af kurset.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course cover mechanical properties of wood and wood-based products, statical calculation models of straight timber members /beams and columns) in the Ultimate (ULS) and Serviceability Limit States (SLS), duration dependent loads on wood structures, influence of moisture on design of timber structures, construction wood-based elements (structural timber, glued laminated timber, and other Engineered Wood Products (EWP)), fasteners and joint design using dowels, bolts and nails, as well as local instability problems, i.e., flexural and lateral torsional buckling. The course consists of lectures and solving assignments throughout the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>1. Blaß and Sandhaas: Timber Engineering - Principles for Design, ISBN 978-3-7315-0673-7.
2. Design of Timber Structures - Structural Aspects of Timber Construction. Volume 1. Edition 3: 2022. Swedish Wood.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course provides a fundamental understanding of the mechanical properties of wood such as strength, stiffness, load duration and moisture, and how the material wood is used as basic beam and column elements for structural design. The course also covers basic connection design using dowel type fasteners such as nails, dowels and bolts.</Course_Objectives>
