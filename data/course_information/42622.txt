          <Contents>Bæredygtighedsudfordringen samt teknologiens og ingeniørkunstens rolle.
Livscyklusanalyse og verdensmål.
Metoder til kortlægning af offentlige kontroverser om teknologi.
Analyse af teknologiske projekters sociale og demokratiske bæredygtighed</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Undervisningen er baseret på forelæsninger, gruppearbejde, individuelle læseopgaver og skriftlige gruppeopgaver. Grupper opfordres til at mødes på campus, når det er muligt.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Sismondo, S. (2009). An Introduction to Science and Technology Studies. 2. ed. West Sussex, UX: Wiley Blackwell.

Gianni, R., Pearson J, et al. (2020). Responsible Research and Innovation: From Concepts to Practices. 1. ed. New York: Routledge.

Hauschild MZ, Kara S and Røpke I (2020). Absolute sustainability: Challenges to life cycle engineering. CIRP Annals 69(2): 533–553.

Hauschild MZ (2018) Introduction to LCA Methodology. In: Hauschild MZ, Rosenbaum RK and Olsen SI (eds) Life Cycle Assessment: Cham: Springer International Publishing, pp. 59–66.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med dette kursus er at give de studerende perspektiver og værktøjer til at kunne forstå og kritisk analysere, hvordan teknologisk innovation og teknologitunge projekter påvirker samfund og miljø. De vil kunne identificere og adressere trade-offs og dilemmaer i kontekst af miljø- og social bæredygtighed samt anbefale ansvarlige strategier for at opnå bæredygtighed.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The sustainability challenge and the role of technology and engineering.
Lifecycle assessment and sustainable development goals. 
Methods for mapping public controversies about technology.

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The teaching is based on lectures, group work, individual reading assignments and written group assignments. Students will also engage in minor periods of data collection and a larger case analysis. Presence is strongly encouraged due to the importance of engaging in group work. Groups are advised to meet on campus whenever possible.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Sismondo, S. (2009). An Introduction to Science and Technology Studies. 2. ed. West Sussex, UX: Wiley Blackwell.

Gianni, R., Pearson J, et al. (2020). Responsible Research and Innovation: From Concepts to Practices. 1. ed. New York: Routledge.

Hauschild MZ, Kara S and Røpke I (2020). Absolute sustainability: Challenges to life cycle engineering. CIRP Annals 69(2): 533–553.

Hauschild MZ (2018) Introduction to LCA Methodology. In: Hauschild MZ, Rosenbaum RK and Olsen SI (eds) Life Cycle Assessment: Cham: Springer International Publishing, pp. 59–66.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The purpose of this course is to equip students with perspectives and tools to be able to understand and critically analyse how technological innovation and technology intensive projects impact society and the environment. They will be able to identify and address trade-offs and dilemmas with respect to environmental and social sustainability and recommend responsible strategies for the attainment of sustainability.</Course_Objectives>
