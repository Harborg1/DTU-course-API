          <Contents>Kurset indeholder gennemgang af entrepriseformer og ansvarsfordeling i byggeriet. Muligheder for at reducere fejl i planlægning og udførelse samt betydningen heraf undersøges. Endvidere arbejdes der med prissætning og projektledelse inkl. værktøjer hertil.

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course includes a review of types of contracts and the distribution of responsibilities in construction. Opportunities to reduce errors in planning and execution, as well as their significance, are examined. Furthermore, work on pricing and project management, including tools for this, is carried out.
The course includes a week-long study trip (typically to Nuuk), where there will be an opportunity to see different building sites and talk to different actors to shed light on some of the issues that have been addressed more theoretically.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project assignments with accompanying lectures, exercises, information search, group work and presentations. The course includes a study trip</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide methods and competencies to be part of engineering project management and understand the professional dimensions of this work. Including what roles are typically in a building project, often determined by the form of contract.
The students are made aware of which agreements the construction is typically based on and thus the division of tasks between the participants in the building project.</Course_Objectives>
