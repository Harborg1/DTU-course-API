Optimal power flow (OPF) problem:

- Optimal varmeflowformulering og øsningstilgange


Udbygning af energinetværk
- Kapacitet og afsendelsesoptimering i et netværk
- Vedvarende balancering gennem regional integration
- Trængselsindtægter

Integration af multi-carrier energisystemer:
- Interface og synergier mellem energibærere;
- Strategier for drift og koordinering af multi-carrier energisystemer;

          <Course_Objectives>Multi-carrier energisystemer er kendetegnet ved stærk drifts- og planlægningskoordinering på tværs af flere energisystemer, herunder elektricitet, brint, naturgas og fjernvarme, for at udnytte synergierne mellem energibærerne, forbedre pålideligheden og omkostningseffektiviteten af ​​energileverancen med minimal påvirkning på miljøet. Formålet med kurset er at give de studerende den nødvendige baggrundsviden om multi-carrier energisystemer.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction

Optimal power flow (OPF) problem:
- AC power flow variables and equations;
- Optimal power flow formulation;
- Solution approaches for transmission and radial distribution grids, including DC-OPF linearization and convex relaxation.

Optimal gas flow problem:
- Gas flow variables and equations in steady state and dynamic models, including line-pack;
- Optimal gas flow formulation and solution approaches.

Optimal heat flow problem:
- Heat flow variables and equations in steady state and dynamic models, with storage in pipelines;
- Optimal heat flow formulation and solution approaches.

Expansion of energy networks:
- Join capacity and dispatch optimization in a network
- Renewable balancing throughout regional integration
- Congestion rent

Multi-carrier energy systems integration:
- Interface and synergies between energy carriers;
- Strategies for multi-carrier energy systems operation and coordination;
- Multi-carrier energy flows and impact on renewables penetration.
- Impacts of CO2 limit constraints</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, computer exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Multi-carrier energy systems are characterized by strong operational and planning coordination across multiple energy systems, including electricity, hydrogen, natural gas, and district heating, to exploit the synergies between the energy carriers, and improve the reliability and cost-effectiveness of energy delivery with minimal impact on the environment. The aim of the course is to give the students the necessary background knowledge of multi-carrier energy systems. The students will learn to model and solve the basic operational problems in individual energy systems and analyze their integration.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is mandatory for students at Sustainable Energy Systems and they have first priority. Any available seats are allocated by lottery</DTU_Remark>
