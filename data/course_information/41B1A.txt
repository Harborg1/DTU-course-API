          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to
• introduce the students to analytical methods in relation to sustainability
• introduce the students to the basic parameters for the design of sustainable construction
• special focus on the parameters that are not normally included in usual calculations of sustainability
• special focus on the importance of errors and construction damages in relation to sustainability
• special focus on the importance of waste of materials in relation to sustainability
• special focus on functionality and the use of space in relation to sustainability

Based on the students' cases, sustainability is analyzed and treated in a broad sense and from a general holistic view</Course_Objectives>
