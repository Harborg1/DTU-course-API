        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The students will by themselves formulate the master's thesis; the academic focal point of which must be one or more of the subjects included in the master's programme.
The project is to be based on a concrete project or a challenge, primarily taken from one of the students' companies. It can be a concrete construction project, a method or a process (e.g. management or change process).
The students receive supervision, primarily from one of the lecturers associated with the programme. The supervisor is agreed with the person concerned and with the head of studies.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online teaching / supervision.
The students will often work together on the thesis in groups of 2-3 students.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The master thesis completes the Master of Sustainable Construction.
The thesis must be based on one or more of the topics that have been dealt with in the previous courses in the master programme, and must demonstrate the student's ability to work independently with sustainability in construction.</Course_Objectives>
