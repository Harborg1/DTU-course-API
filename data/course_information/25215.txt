          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to enable the students to use a combination of classical and modern statistical methods to draw correct inference from collected observations. All methods will be motivated by examples in marine science. Focus will be on simple and practical methods, selection of the right method for the task, and on validating the results. Observations in marine science are often spatially explicit, and hence spatial methods will be covered. By the end of the course the students should be able to formulate (on paper and in code) simple models, estimate model parameters from these, quantify uncertainties, and test hypothesis.</Course_Objectives>
