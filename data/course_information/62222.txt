        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer til følgende fagområder:

- Informationsteknologi samt IT anvendelser i en forretningsmæssig kontekst, herunder finansielle, organisatoriske og juridiske konsekvenser ved anvendelse af IT løsninger (fx databeskyttelse, ansvar og håndtering af brugerdata) samt IT hardware og systemers energiforbrug i digitale løsninger

- IT software design og softwareudviklingsprocesser samt opbygning af digitale systemer på anvenderniveau, herunder sammenhængen mellem krav, design og implementering samt samspillet mellem frontend, backend og database

- Brugerforståelse og design af digitale løsninger, herunder visuelt design af brugergrænseflader, usability, user experience og accessibility (fx personas, behovsanalyse og designprincipper)

- Website design og implementation, herunder anvendelse af digitale værktøjer og platforme til opbygning af webbaserede systemer samt grundlæggende forståelse af underliggende teknologier (fx HTML, CSS og JavaScript), herunder scripting af både klient og server på anvenderniveau i webbaserede systemer samt databaser og datalagring på anvenderniveau i webbaserede systemer

- Datanetværk på anvenderniveau

- Modellering af systemer ved hjælp af UML (fx use case og activity diagrams)

- Projektplanlægning, herunder opgaveopdeling, estimering og risikohåndtering (fx opdeling af funktionalitet i udviklingsopgaver) samt test og evaluering af digitale løsninger</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, klassediskussioner, øvelser og projektarbejde. Kurset omfatter en struktureret række øvelser, der understøtter projektudviklingen, herunder forberedende øvelser (fx programmering). Projektet kan gennemføres i grupper, men hver studerende skal være ansvarlig for en klart afgrænset del (inkl. implementering). Projektet dokumenteres gennem et website og en projektportefølje.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>- Eventuelle bøger, der skal anskaffes, fastlægges ved kursusstart.
- Relevante artikler, værktøjer samt andre materialer fra internettet.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give den studerende en forståelse af, hvordan digitale systemer designes, udvikles, vælges og anvendes i en forretningsmæssig kontekst på anvenderniveau.

Endvidere skal kurset sætte den studerende i stand til at arbejde med udvikling af digitale løsninger fra problemforståelse og brugerbehov over idé og konceptualisering til design, implementering og evaluering af et færdigt produkt på anvenderniveau, herunder at kunne træffe og begrunde valg af digitale løsninger i praksis.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the following subject areas:

- Information technology and IT applications in a business context, including financial, organisational, and legal implications of IT solutions (e.g. data protection, responsibility, and handling of user data), as well as energy consumption of IT hardware and systems in digital solutions

- IT software design and software development processes, as well as the structure of digital systems at an applied level, including the relationship between requirements, design, and implementation, and the interaction between frontend, backend, and database

- User understanding and design of digital solutions, including visual design of user interfaces, usability, user experience, and accessibility (e.g. personas, needs analysis, and design principles)

- Website design and implementation, including the use of digital tools and platforms for building web-based systems, as well as a basic understanding of underlying technologies (e.g. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript), including scripting of both client and server at an applied level in web-based systems, as well as databases and data storage at an applied level in web-based systems

- Data networks at an applied level

- Modelling of systems using UML (e.g. use case and activity diagrams)

- Project planning, including task decomposition, estimation, and risk management (e.g. breakdown of functionality into development tasks), as well as testing and evaluation of digital solutions</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, classroom discussions, exercises, and project work. The course includes a structured sequence of exercises supporting project development, including preparatory exercises (e.g. programming). The project may be carried out in groups, but each student must be responsible for a clearly defined part (incl. implementation). The project is documented through a website and a project portfolio.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>- Any required textbooks will be determined at the start of the course.
- Relevant articles, tools, and other materials from the internet.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide the student with an understanding of how digital systems are designed, developed, selected, and used in a business context at an applied level.

Furthermore, the course enables the student to work with the development of digital solutions from problem understanding and user needs through ideation and conceptualisation to design, implementation, and evaluation of a finished product at an applied level, including the ability to make and justify choices of digital solutions in practice.</Course_Objectives>
