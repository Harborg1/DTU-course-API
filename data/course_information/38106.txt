        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Den ugentlige undervisning består af et onlinemodul omhandlende et emne indenfor entreprenørskab, hvori der introduceres en række forskellige tilgange (f.eks. teoretiske, praktiske, abstrakte, konkrete). De studerende vil afprøve forskellige strategier og metoder indenfor entreprenørskab, både gennem onlinemoduler og en række onlinespil/simuleringer. Onlinespil/simuleringer har en central rolle i kursets læring, da disse giver mulighed for at efterprøve de forskellige strategier, og eftersom spillene foregår i konkurrence mellem de studerende, sammenligne effekten af forskellige strategier. Afhængigt af situationen kan det vise sig at nogle gange kan det være bedst at være den første til at handle, mens det andre gange kan være bedre at vente og reagere på andres handlinger. Kurset giver de studerende en bred vifte af værktøjer, teknikker og metoder der danner grundlaget for et entreprenørmindset.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Onlinekursus med individuelle opgaver og gruppearbejde, understøttet af onlineaktiviteter og  seriøse spil, som både vil foregå individuelt og i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Kursusmateriale bliver gjort tilgængeligt på Learn.</Course_Literature>

Kurset støtter de studerende i at navigere i det dynamiske og komplekse miljø mellem teknologi og forretningsudvikling, både i en iværksættervirksomhed, et universitet, eller en etableret virksomhed.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Each session consists of a online module on a specific topic related to the entrepreneurial mindset. The session will introduce a range of approaches (e.g. concrete, abstract, theoretical, practical) on how to address the topic at hand. In online games and modules, students will explore how these different approaches can be applied. Students try out the approach, and learn quickly by trying out different strategies in the modules and games. Because of the competitive nature of the games, students will also learn from other students; sometimes it might be best to be the first one to take action, sometime is might be better to first see what others are doing. At the end of the course, students are equipped with a wide variety of tools, techniques, methods and experiences that will help them to apply an entrepreneurial mindset in the context they are working in.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online Modules and Online Activities/Serious Games, which students will carry out both individually and in teams</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to prepare students to use an entrepreneurial mindset in a variety of professional contexts. The course will help students develop skills to cope with the dynamic and complex environment where technology meets business, which can be either in a startup, but also in a university or company setting.</Course_Objectives>
