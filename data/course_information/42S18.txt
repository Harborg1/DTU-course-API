        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>The goal of this course is to give a solid understanding of sustainable financing, covering regulations, debt mechanisms, and investments that support green projects across industries. The course will explore key financial tools related to impact finance like green bonds, EU taxonomy, impact investing, and venture capital while analyzing global trends and regulatory changes that influence sustainable finance. The course addresses the social, ecological, and economic impacts of regulations, as well as the risks and returns of sustainability investments. At the same time the course considers economic rebound mechanisms and behavioral rebound mechanisms. By course end, participants will be ready to integrate sustainability into corporate financial strategies and drive organizational change considering human behavior.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is lecture-based combined with group exercises, cases and practical theory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The objective of this course is to understand the foundations of sustainable financing and how to decarbonate society through finance. We look at transition risks and climate risks and which financial instruments are used to encourage the development of green projects on macro and micro level in organizations and in society.</Course_Objectives>
