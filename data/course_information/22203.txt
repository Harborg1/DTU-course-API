        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Studerende vil modtage teoretisk og teknisk undervisning fra tre fagspecialister, der dækker:
• Liposomsyntese
• Nanopartikelkarakteriseringsteknikker og nanopartikel-celle-interaktioner
• Elektrospinning og fremstilling af nanofiber-scaffolds
Med udgangspunkt i dette fundament arbejder de studerende i tværfaglige grupper for at udtænke et klinisk relevant problem inden for lokal lægemiddelafgivelse. De designer en løsning, der integrerer liposomale lægemiddelafgivelsessystemer, elektrospundne materialer og in vitro-celletestning. Hver gruppe udvikler en struktureret projektplan og indsender et projektforslag, der beskriver baggrund, hypotese, metode, risikovurdering, tidslinje og forventede resultater. Forslaget præsenteres mundtligt til feedback inden laboratorieimplementering.
I løbet af den efterfølgende 3-ugers laboratorieperiode udfører de studerende deres planlagte projekt, herunder materialefremstilling, fysisk-kemisk karakterisering og biologisk evaluering. Kurset afsluttes med en afsluttende gruppepræsentation og aflevering af en struktureret laboratoriejournal, der dokumenterer dagligt eksperimentelt arbejde, refleksioner, fejlfinding og dataanalyse.

Kurset er bevidst struktureret som en forskningsorienteret innovationserfaring. De studerende vil ikke modtage trinvise instruktioner; I stedet forventes det, at de tager initiativ, træffer informerede beslutninger og tilpasser deres strategier, efterhånden som udfordringer opstår.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Undervisningen er baseret på Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate-tilgangen. Under 13-ugers kurset er undervisningen en blanding af forelæsninger, opgaver og gruppearbejde, der støtter den kreative proces med at skabe idéer til og designe sundhedsteknologiske løsninger. I løbet af 3-ugersperioden går vi i laboratoriet og implementerer ideerne. De fire studielinjer har individuelle spor i kurset, men det optimale resultat kan kun opnås ved samarbejde mellem de forskellige spor.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

Kurset integrerer avancerede lægemiddelafgivelseskoncepter med praktisk laboratoriearbejde og projektbaseret læring. Det lægger vægt på translationel tænkning, eksperimentelt design, teamwork, dokumentation og evaluering af sundhedsteknologier.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Students will receive theoretical and technical instruction from three subject specialists covering:
•	Liposome synthesis
•	Nanoparticle characterization techniques and nanoparticle-cell interactions
•	Electrospinning and fabrication of nanofibrous scaffolds
Building on this foundation, students work in interdisciplinary groups to conceive a clinically relevant problem within local drug delivery. They design a solution integrating liposomal drug delivery systems, electrospun materials, and in vitro cell testing. Each group develops a structured project plan and submits a project proposal outlining background, hypothesis, methodology, risk assessment, timeline, and expected outcomes. The proposal is presented orally for feedback prior to laboratory implementation.
During the subsequent 3-week laboratory period, students execute their planned project, including material fabrication, physicochemical characterization, and biological evaluation. The course concludes with a final group presentation and submission of a structured laboratory journal documenting daily experimental work, reflections, troubleshooting, and data analysis.
The course is intentionally structured as a research-oriented innovation experience. Students will not be provided with step-by-step instructions; instead, they are expected to take initiative, make informed decisions, and adapt their strategies as challenges emerge.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The teaching is based on the Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate approach. During the 13 weeks course, the teaching is a mixture of lectures, assignments and group work which facilitate the creative process of conceiving and designing healthcare solutions. During the 3-weeks period, we go into the lab to implement the ideas. The four specializations have individual tracks in the course but excellence can only be achieved by collaborations between the different tracks.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Lecture slides, notes and articles.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>This course is designed to provide students with experience in the full development cycle of a healthcare product, following the Conceive–Design–Implement–Operate (CDIO) framework. The course integrates advanced drug delivery concepts with practical laboratory work and project-based learning. It emphasizes translational thinking, experimental design, teamwork, documentation, and evaluation of healthcare technologies.</Course_Objectives>
