        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Physical modelling of distributed energy resources across different energy domains.
Electric vehicles, storages, photovoltaic systems, virtual power plants. 
Voltage control and compliance with grid codes in distribution grids.  
Static and dynamic simulation studies with Matlab-Simulink-Simscape Electrical</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>a) Lectures and problem solving
b) Physical modeling of distributed resources (such as electric vehicles, storage and photovoltaic systems) and interaction within the electrical distribution grid as well as other energy domains</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>G. M. Masters, Kevin F. Hsu, Renewable and efficient electric power systems, 3rd edition, 2023
and supplementary material</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course focuses on a selection of distributed energy technologies and assesses their influence across different energy domains.
Electric vehicles, storage systems, photovoltaic systems are modelled from a component and grid perspective in order to assess their role in active distribution networks.
Renewable energy communities and virtual power plants are explored as a way to integrate those technologies into the power system.
Load flows in radial distribution grids and dynamic simulations in Matlab-Simulink-Simscape Electrical are used to assess selected control strategies for exploiting the potential of the resources.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>E-learning is used in the form of on-line quiz (home assignments), interactive quiz and electronic correction system.
YouTube videos on Mattia's channel support the learning process.
Basic knowledge of Matlab-Simulink is highly recommended - students with no knowledge of the software are strongly advised to take online tutorials before the course begins.</Remark>
