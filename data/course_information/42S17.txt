        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>In the 1. Part of the course, we explore how sustainability is perceived, and sustainable leadership is practiced across cultures and sectors, and how these different approaches have implications for global sustainability transformations. We analyze organizational strategies as well as community-level implementations to sustainable change. We reflect on how to increase our sphere of influence to lead to more globally relevant and impactful sustainable transformations. 2. part of the course integrates theory, reflection, and practice to develop personal leadership. Participants work within a structured leadership framework supported by a 360-degree feedback process. Personal development goals are defined and tested through leadership experiments, supported by peer coaching circles that ensure reflection and accountability.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is lecture-based combined with group exercises, cases and practical theory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the 1. part of the change management course is to learn how to best empower yourself and others to realize and execute sustainable change in an international context. Learning objectives 1-5.
The 2. part of the course develops participants’ personal leadership practice in general by integrating leadership theory with professional experience. Leadership is addressed as a values-based, ethically grounded, and behaviorally enacted discipline rooted in self-awareness. Through structured reflection, 360-degree feedback, and small-scale leadership experiments, participants strengthen individual leadership capabilities and formulate a focused development trajectory. The course introduces a coherent leadership development framework that supports continued growth across the programme. Learning objectives 6-10</Course_Objectives>
