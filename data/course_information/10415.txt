        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Almindelige design og karakteristika ved kommercielle atomkraftreaktorer, konversion af termisk energi til elektricitet, reaktorkontrol og -drift, kobling af de nukleare og konventionelle anlæg i et atomkraftværk, analyse af reaktor transienter, sikkerhedssystemer, tekniske og politiske aspekter ved brændselscyklus inkl. uran ressourcer, brændselsfremstilling, oparbejdning og behandling af radioaktivt affald.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, regneøvelser, journal club</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>S. Marguet, “The Technology of Pressurized Water Reactors”, Springer, 2022, I. Crossland, Nuclear fuel cycle science and engineering, Woodhead Publishing, 2012 and lecture notes.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset sigter mod at bibringe den studerende et overblik over reaktordesigns og deres karakteristika. I kurset vil vi behandle såvel eksisterende som den næste generation atomkraftsystemer, men med et fokus på design og drift af letvandsreaktorer, der er den mest udbredte reaktortype i dag. Kurset behandler varmeproduktion, nuklear termohydraulik og termodynamisk konversion til elektricitet, og vi diskuterer principperne for reaktorsikkerhed. I kurset diskuterer vi de forskellige trin i brændselscyklus.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Survey of commercial reactor types and common design characteristics, conversion of thermal energy to electricity, control and operation of reactor, coupling of the nuclear and the conventional islands of a power plant, reactor transient analysis, nuclear safety design, technical and policy aspects of the fuel cycle covering uranium supply, fuel fabrication, reprocessing and waste disposal.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, journal club</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>S. Marguet, “The Technology of Pressurized Water Reactors”, Springer, 2022, I. Crossland, Nuclear fuel cycle science and engineering, Woodhead Publishing, 2012 and lecture notes.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide the student with an overview of different nuclear reactor designs and their characteristics. The course discusses the current and the next generations of nuclear energy systems but focuses on the design and operation of Light Water Reactors, the most common type of commercial nuclear reactors operated today. We cover nuclear heat production, nuclear thermal-hydraulics, and thermodynamic conversion to electricity. The course introduces the principles of nuclear reactor safety and covers the various steps of the nuclear fuel cycle.</Course_Objectives>
