          <Contents>Pakkekoblet og kredsløbskoblet overførsel af information. Protokoller i datakommunikationsnetværk. Ethernet og WiFi lokalnet. Datakommunikation i mobilnet: Netværksarkitekturer, principper for deling af kapacitet, dækningsforhold,

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser samt opgaveregning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give en generel introduktion til netværk til datakommunikation med fokus på udvekslingen af information, herunder hvordan dette kan ske pålideligt og/eller sikkert. At give deltagerne kendskab til principperne bag nuværende typer af datanet, som f.eks. lokalnet, internettet og mobilnet.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Packet-switched and circuit-switched transfer of information. Protocols in data communication networks. Ethernet and WiFi local area networks. The Internet: Fundamental principles and important protocols (IP, TCP, UDP, DNS, HTTP, etc.). Data communication in mobile communication networks: Network architectures, principles of capacity sharing, coverage, procedures (attachment, pageing, handover). Fundamental concepts of network security: Encryption, authentication, certificates and cryptographic hash functions. Simulation of data communication networks and protocols.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, and problem solving.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To give a general introduction to data communication networks with emphasis on the exchange of information, including how this exchange can be made reliable and/or secure. To give the participants an understanding of the principles of contemporary types of data communication networks, such as local area networks, the internet and networks for mobile communication.</Course_Objectives>
