          <Contents>Kurset fokuserer på den tværfaglige anvendelse af ingeniørkompetencer i løsningen af virkelighedsnære problemstillinger. De studerende vil, igennem ugentlige opgaver og en afsluttende øvelse, lære at håndtere projekter med en stigende kompleksitet, sådan at emner som (digitalt) gruppearbejde, anvendelse af naturvidenskabelige og ingeniørmæssige grundfag samt problemforståelse i en virkelighedsnær kontekst berøres:

-	Anvendelse af matematik og omsætning af litteraturviden til praktisk brug ved en automatiseret løsning af ingeniøropgaver, sådan at manuelle beregningsoperationer ikke gentages på en uhensigtsmæssig måde.

Efter gennemførsel af kurset vil den studerende have fået en indføring i repræsentative opgaver, en maskiningeniør kan arbejde med i dagligdagen.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online undervisning, tegneøvelser, gruppearbejde, projektarbejde og værkstedsøvelser</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Projektarbejde er en af de mest udbredte arbejdsformer inden for ingeniørdisciplinen. Det er derfor forventeligt, at maskiningeniøren har førstehåndskendskab til at arbejde individuelt og i grupper, med opstilling og konkretisering af projekter, samt anvendelse af en bred palet af ingeniørkompetencer i en tværfaglig kontekst. Dette arbejde kan foregå traditionelt med fysisk tilstedeværelse, men kan også benytte diverse digitale kommunikationsværktøjer.

Kursets formål er at introducere til ingeniørfagligt projektarbejde, ved at inkludere aspekter som gruppedynamik og digital dannelse såvel som anvendelsen af traditionelle ingeniørkompetencer. De studerende vil i kurset få opbygget den grundlæggende forståelse for en problemstilling i en ingeniørmæssig sammenhæng. Herunder opbygningen af et fagligt, professionelt ordforråd og anvendelse af f.eks. matematik, materialekendskab (bl.a. om materialers CO2 footprint) og teknisk tegning/anvendelse af et avanceret CAD-program i løsningen af konkrete ingeniørproblemstillinger.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course focuses on the cross-disciplinary application of engineering competences in the solution of realistic problems. During weekly assignments and a concluding exercise, the students will learn to handle projects with an increasing complexity, such that topics such as (digital) group work, application of basic subjects within natural and engineering science as well as problem understanding in a realistic context are treated:

-	Digital cooperation, application of mathematics, mechanics, material choice and technical drawing to analyze individual components.
-	Application of mathematics, strength of materials, material choice and technical drawing to analyze mechanisms or component assemblies.
-	Application of mathematics and conversion of literature knowledge into practical use by an automated solution of engineering problems, such that manual computational operations are not going to be repeated in an undesired way.

After completion of the course, the student will have received an introduction into representative tasks a mechanical engineer can face on a daily basis</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online lectures, drawing exercises, group work, project work and workshop exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Project work is one of the most applied working methods within engineering. It is thus to be expected that the mechanical engineer has a first-hand knowledge of working individually as well as in groups, with establishing and clarification of projects, and also application of a wide range of engineering competences in a cross-disciplinary context. This work can be carried out in a traditional fashion with physical attendance, but also through various digital communication tools.

The purpose of the course is to introduce project work within the field of engineering, by including aspects such a group dynamics and digital manners as well as the application of traditional engineering competences. During the course, the students will form the basic understanding of a problem in an engineering context. Among these the formation of a professional and scientific vocabulary and application of e.g. mathematics, material knowledge (including the CO2 footprint) and technical drawing/use of an advanced CAD program during the solution of specific engineering problems.</Course_Objectives>
