        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>De studerende arbejder i små grupper gennem hele forløbet med problemer relateret til antibiotikaresistens. 

Baggrunden for emnet vil blive behandlet i et review projekt, som vil blive opsummeret i skriftlig form. Viden vil herefter blive omsat til en projektplan, der fører til design af et mikrobiologisk vækstforsøg til bestemmelse af antibiotikaresistens. 

Antibiotikaresistensen måles ved hjælp af et prototypesystem, som er designet og bygget af de studerende. Systemet er baseret på et 3D printet fundament med en mikrocontroller, som henter online data ved hjælp af basale scripts. Skylabs innovative laboratorier vil understøtte de forskellige dele af prototypefremstillingen. 

De studerende vil selv lede projektet med vejledning fra instruktører. I en afsluttende mundtlig præsentation vil "Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate" tilgangen blive evalueret sammen med de opnåede resultater.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The students will work in small groups throughout the whole course on problems related to antimicrobial resistance.  

The background of the topic will be addressed in a review project which will be summarized in written form. The knowledge will hereafter be translated into a project plan which leads to the design of a microbiological growth experiment for determination of antimicrobial resistance. 

Antimicrobial resistance will be measured using a device which is designed and built by the students. The device is based on a 3D printed frame with a microcontroller which acquires online data using basic scripting. The innovative laboratories of Skylab will support the different aspects of prototyping the device. 

The students will lead the project themselves with guidance from instructors. In a final oral presentation, the “Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate” approach will be evaluated together the obtained results.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is project based and uses a mixture of lectures, exercises and lab work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This course introduces the students to the “Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate” approach to obtain an understanding of problem-based engineering.  In a cross disciplinary project, the students will learn to conceive a microbiological study plan and prepare samples accordingly. Hereafter a prototype instrument for measuring planned data will be designed and build. The course will partly take place at DTU Skylab which will give the foundation of pursuing innovation and instrument prototyping on own initiative after the course.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is only for students enrolled in the BSc programmes Biotechnology and Technical Biomedicine.</DTU_Remark>
