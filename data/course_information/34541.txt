          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus giver en introduktion til entreprenørskab med særligt fokus på teknologiske produkter. Målet er at bringe studerende med forskellig baggrund sammen, med det formål at udvikle kommercialiseringsstrategier for teknologier skabt på DTU.

Kurset vil introducere den studerende til metoder, modeller og værktøjer som kan bruges til at: identificere og evaluere et markedspotentiale for en DTU teknologi; udarbejde en forretningsplan; at kommunikere denne effektivt for potentielle investorer (pitch). Kurset baseres på forskellige dele af entreprenørskab, strategi, ledelse og marketing. Kurset vil også have en ”grøn” vinkel for at fremme interessen for grønne startups.

Kurset er ikke kun relevant for studerende som gerne vil være entreprenører, men også for ingeniører som gerne vil fungere som innovatorer i etablerede firmaer.</Course_Objectives>

          <Course_Objectives>This course is an introduction to technology entrepreneurship. The aim of the course is to bring together students from different backgrounds, in order to develop commercialization strategies for technologies developed at DTU. 

The course will introduce students to concepts, models and tools that can be used in order to: identify and evaluate market opportunities for a university technology; to develop a business case; and to effectively communicate (pitch) the business case to create interest and attract resources. The course will draw upon the fields of entrepreneurship, strategy, management and marketing. It will also provide a sustainability angle to spark the students’ interest towards the creation of SDG-oriented technology startups. 

This course is relevant not only to the students who want to become entrepreneurs, but also to the ones who aspire to become the “engineers of the future” and work as innovators in established companies.</Course_Objectives>
