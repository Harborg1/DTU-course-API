        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kromatografiske principper og basal teoretisk baggrund for ionbytnings-, hydrofobisk interaktions- og affinitets-kromatografi samt gelfiltrering og expanded bed adsorption. Kromatografiudstyrs opbygning, herunder pumper og detektorsystemer (HPLC, high performance liquid chromatography). Oprensningsstrategier og deres optimering. Udvælgelse og kombinationer af oprensningsmetoder. Kolonne bærematerialers kemiske og fysiske egenskaber. Pakning af kolonner. En kort introduktion til myndigheders godkendelse af procedurer til oprensning af protein baseret lægemidler. Håndtering af proteiner samt en introduktion til andre metoder, der anvendes i forbindelse med protein oprensning i laboratorieskala f. eks. SDS-PAGE, ultrafiltrering og frysetørring. Ovenstående illustreres gennem eksperimentelle øvelser/projekter med anvendelse af HPLC udstyr til oprensning af udvalgte hovedsagelig rekombinante proteiner.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Selvstudium med vejledning, eksperimentelle øvelser og enkelte forelæsninger.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Teoretisk og praktisk indføring i kromatografiske metoder til oprensning af rekombinante proteiner i milligram til gram-skala.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The chromatographic principles and basic theoretical background of ion-exchange, hydrophobic interaction and affinity chromatography, gel filtration and the expanded bed adsorption. Chromatographic systems design, including pumps and detector systems (HPLC, high performance liquid chromatography). Purification strategies and their optimization. Selection and combination of purification methods. Column materials chemical and physical properties. Packing of columns. A brief introduction to the authorities' approval of procedures for purification of protein-based drugs. Handling of proteins, as well as an introduction to other methods used in protein purification on a laboratory scale, for example, SDS-PAGE, ultrafiltration and freeze drying. The above is illustrated through experimental exercises / projects using HPLC equipment for purification of selected mainly recombinant proteins.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Self studies with consultations, experimental exercises and a few lectures.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Theoretical and practical introduction to chromatographic techniques for purification of proteins in milligram to gram quantities.</Course_Objectives>
