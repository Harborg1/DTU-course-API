          <Contents>Dette onlinekursus består af syv moduler, som hver dækker forskellige aspekter af faste oxid-celleteknologi (SOC) – fra grundlæggende virkemåde og cellekomponenter til karakterisering, test og dataanalyse af SOC’er.           <Course_Objectives>Faste oxid-celler (SOC’er) er elektrokemiske enheder, der kan fungere både som brændselsceller og elektrolyseceller – nøglekomponenter i fremtidens energisystemer. Er du nysgerrig på materialerne bag SOC’er og deres rolle i effektiv energikonvertering, -lagring og Power-to-X teknologier?

Dette kursus vil give en introduktion til fastoxidbrændselsceller (SOFC'er) og fastoxidelektrolyseceller (SOEC'er). I løbet af kurset vil destuderende lære om teknologisk relevante fremstillingsmetoder, materialevalg og karakteriseringsteknikker, beskrivelse og fortolkning af elektrokemiske egenskaber af solide oxidceller (SOC'er), kritisk læsning af videnskabelig litteratur om SOFC og SOEC og brug af denopnåede indsigt i forbindelse med analyse for celletestdata (leveret af underviseren). De studerende vil herefter kunne beskrive den teoretiske baggrund bag SOFC og SOEC's virkemåde ogdrift.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This online course consists of seven modules, each focusing on a different aspect of solid oxide cell (SOC) technology—from the basic operating principles to cell components, and finally to the characterization, testing, and data analysis of SOCs. Each module combines theoretical background with practical insights: core principles are introduced through lectures and literature reading, while assignments between sessions reinforce learning and encourage hands-on engagement. After the module on data analysis, participants will be required to submit a short report. The course concludes with an oral exam based on the report, including questions about the work carried out and the general principles of SOC technologies. This course offers an excellent entry point for those interested in pursuing further work within the field of solid oxide cells.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Reading of literature, exercises, data processing as well as report writing.
Formative evaluation of the course will take place during the 13-week period.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Literature will be available on-line for free.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Solid oxide cells (SOCs) are electrochemical devices that can operate both as fuel cells and electrolysers—key components in future energy systems. Curious about the materials behind SOCs and their role in efficient energy conversion, storage, and Power-to-X technologies?

This course will provide an introduction to solid oxide fuel cells (SOFCs) and solid oxide electrolysis cells (SOECs). During the course, the students will deal with the theoretical understanding of relevant methods of fabrication, materials selection and characterization techniques, description and interpretation of electrochemical properties of solid oxide cells (SOCs), comprehension of scientific literature on SOFC and SOEC and use of the gained insights in the analysis of test data (provided by the teacher). The students will then be able to describe the theoretical background behind SOFC and SOEC operation.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Do you want to learn about the material research in solid oxide cells for efficient conversion and storage of energy and for use in Power-to-X plants? If the answer is yes, then this is the course for you.
This course is open exclusively to students enrolled in the BSc General Engineering - taught in Danish. Students from other study lines who are interested in participating must contact the course responsible before registering.</DTU_Remark>
