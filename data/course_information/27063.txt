        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The chromatographic principles and basic theoretical background of ion-exchange, hydrophobic interaction and affinity chromatography, as well as gel filtration. Chromatographic systems design, including pumps, columns, and detector systems. Purification strategies and their optimization. Selection and combination of purification methods. Column materials chemical and physical properties. Handling of proteins, as well as an introduction to other methods used in protein purification on a laboratory scale, for example, SDS-PAGE, ultrafiltration and freeze drying. The above is illustrated through experimental exercises / projects.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Self studies with consultations, experimental exercises and a few lectures. It is mandatory to participate in the exercises. If the student misses more than two exercises, the course will not be approved.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
