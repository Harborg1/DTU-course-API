          <Contents>Dette kursus er bygget op omkring fire hovedmoduler. Det første modul fokuserer på en introduktion til PSS, hvor følgende emner dækkes: PSS-definition; virksomheders motivation for adoption af PSS; PSS-typologier (f.eks. produktorienteret, brugsorienteret og resultatorienteret); PSS-karakteristikker i forhold til forskellige kontekster (B2B, B2C og B2G).
Kursets andet modul fokuserer på udviklingen af PSS-løsninger, hvor følgende emner dækkes: PSS-udviklingsprocesser; PSS-værktøjer; PSS-simulering; PSS-udvikling og -drift.
Kursets tredje modul fokuserer på overvejelser knyttet til livscyklusomkostninger ved PSS-driften. Følgende emner dækkes: Totallivsomkostning som fænomen; modeller til life cycle costing (LCC); omkostningsbaseret vurdering af PSS-løsninger.
Kursets fjerde og sidste modul fokuserer på bæredygtighed (kombination af miljømæssige-, sociale- og forretningsmæssige overvejelser) ved udvikling og drift af PSS, hvor følgende emner dækkes: Livscyklusvurdering af PSS; PSS og den cirkulære økonomi; den miljømæssige rebound-effekt.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Produkt/service-systemer (PSS) er en tilgang til udviklingen og driften af kombinationer af produkter og serviceydelser, som tilbyder ny værdi til slutbrugeren end ved en traditionel produktudviklingsparadigme. Et vigtigt grundlæggende koncept bag PSS er, at øget værdi til slutbrugeren kan opnås gennem en mere effektiv udnyttelse af de involverede produkter og systemer. Dette kursus fokuserer på de forskellige typer af PSS-løsninger, PSS-udviklingsmetoder og værktøjer, samt de økonomiske overvejelser og bæredygtighedspotentiale fra PSS. En række industrielle casestudier bruges igennem kurset, understøttet af videnskabelige artikler, kursusmateriale og øvelser.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Four main modules describe this course. The first module of the course focuses on an introduction to PSS, where the following subjects will be covered: PSS definition; the motivation for companies to adopt PSS; the different PSS typologies (e.g. product-oriented, use-oriented and result-oriented); and the differences and similarities of PSS in B2B, B2C and B2G contexts, respectively.
The second module of the course focuses on the actual design and development of PSS solutions, where the following subjects will be covered: PSS development processes; PSS design methods; PSS simulation during design; PSS implementation and operation.
The third module of the course will focus on the economical and life cycle costing (LCC) considerations connected to PSS design and operation. In this module the following subjects will be covered: total cost of ownership (TCO); life cycle costing (LCC) models; cost-based assessment of PSS solutions.
The fourth and final module of the course will focus on the sustainability (combined environmental, social and business) considerations of PSS, where the following subjects will be covered: evaluation of life cycle environmental performance of PSS; PSS as an opportunity for a circular economy; environmental rebound effects.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Product/Service-Systems (PSS) is an approach to the design, development and operation of combinations of products and services, which offer new value to the end-user than in a traditional product development transactional paradigm. An important underlying concept behind PSS is that increased end-user value can be achieved, through more efficient utilisation of the engineered products and systems involved in value delivery. This course focuses on the different types of PSS solutions and approaches, the methods required to develop PSS solutions, the actions to be taken to ensure efficient operation of PSS solutions, the economical considerations and the sustainability opportunities related to PSS development and provision. A number of industry case studies will act as the case material for the course, which will be supported by academic articles, course material and worked exercises.</Course_Objectives>
