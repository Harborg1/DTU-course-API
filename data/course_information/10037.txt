        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette er et kursus i klassisk feltteori for kontinuerte fysiske felter. Det omfatter diffussionsteori for felter der beskriver masse, impuls eller energi i væsker, gasser og faste stoffer; elastodynamik af deformerbare statiske eller vibrerende elastiske materialer, og fluiddynamik af strømmende væsker eller gasser. Modeller af kontinuumsfelterne opstilles ved brug af bevarelseslove og Gauss’ divergensteorem i form af styrende partielle differentialligninger med randbetingelser. De mest simple eksempler løses analytisk, mens mere realistiske anvendelser simuleres numerisk i Python.</Contents>

Kurset giver en god baggrund for brugen af feltteori i senere kurser i elektromagnetisme og kvantemekanik.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This is a course in classical field theory of continuous physical fields. It includes diffusion theory for fields describing mass, momentum or energy in liquids, gases and solids; elastodynamics of deformable static or vibrating elastic materials, and fluid dynamics of flowing liquids or gases. Models of the continuum fields are set up using conservation laws and Gauss' divergence theorem in the form of governing partial differential equations with boundary conditions. The simplest examples are solved analytically, while more realistic applications are simulated numerically in Python.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, tutorials, and numerical simulation exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Lecture note ”Introduction to continuum physics and classical field theory” by Henrik Bruus, (DTU 2026).
Lecture note ”Continuums physics: Modeling and numerical simulation in Python” by Jakob Schiøtz (DTU 2026).</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>With a synthesis of physical conservation laws, mathematical vector analysis, and the programming language Python, the student is given a basic knowledge of continuum physics, its description by classical field theory, and setting up and solving selected continuum models with numerical simulations. The course forms a good basis for the use of field theory in later course on electromagnetism and quantum mechanics.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course is specifically targeted at students on the BSc Engineering Physics and the BSc Earth and Space Physics and Engineering educations. The course is a central elective course for students at these bachelor study programmes, and it is part of all recommended study lines in these programmes.</DTU_Remark>
