          <Contents>Termisk indeklima, træk. Identifikation af forureningskilder og ventilationsrater. Opfattet luftkvalitet. Elementer af indendørs luftkvalitet: Bioeffluenter, emissionskilder, mikroorganismer, ozon, flygtige organiske forbindelser, partikler. Epidemiologi og sundhed i indeklima. Effekt af beboeradfærd. Den studerende vil være i stand til at analysere og evaluere indeklimaet i en række indemiljøer i praksis.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Thermal indoor climate, draught. Identification of pollution sources and ventilation rates. Perceived air quality. Elements of indoor air quality: bioeffluents, emission sources, microorganisms, ozone, volatile organic compounds, particles. Indoor air epidemiology and health. Effect of occupant behavior. Microbiology in indoor environment. Indoor climate and performance, technologies, and building certification. Debate of indoor climate controversies. Measuring methods and instruments. Planning and performing indoor climate investigations.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, discussions, and practical exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

The student will be able to analyze and evaluate the indoor climate in a variety of indoor settings in practice.</Course_Objectives>
