        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>På grundlag af relevante historiske brande forklares udviklingen af brandrisikovurderingen. Der introduceres både detrministiske såvel som probabilistiske brandrisikometoder. De studerende få kendskab til scenarioanalyser , som f.eks. flamme og røgspredning. Disse scenarier bliver kombineret med evakueringsscenarier og  kriterier til acceptance for personsikkerhed.  De studerende vil forstå basal probabilistisk teori og udføre grundlæggende probabilistiske beregninger. Herunder hører en indføring i Bayesian Networks og Monte Carlo metoder . Forskellene mellem kvalitative og kvantitative risikovurderinger bliver forklaret og de studerende vil kunne anvende dem på forskellige cases.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>forelæsninger i kombination med øvelser og opgaver</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus introducerer de grundlæggende begreber i brandrisikoanalyse og -styring. Gennem kurset vil deltagerne tilegne sig: viden om den generelle fremgangsmåde for brandrisikostyring; forståelse af grundlæggende teori for sandsynlighedsberegning og risiko- og beslutningstagningsanalyse; og blive i stand til at udføre praktisk brandrisikovurdering. Formålet med kurset er at give deltagerne metoder til at foretage rationel brandrisikovurdering og -styring i det byggede miljø.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Based on historical fires of relevance the development of fire risk assessment is explained. Both deterministic and probabilistic fire risk methods are introduced. The students will learn about scenario development analysis for e.g. fire and smoke spread. These scenarios are combined with evacuation scenarios as well as acceptance criteria for occupant safety.
The students will understand basic probabilistic theory and perform basic probabilistic calculations. Hereunder, an introduction to Bayesian Networks and Monte Carlo methods is given. The differences between qualitative and quantitative risk assessment are introduced and applied by the students based on case studies.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>lectures in combination with exercises and assignments</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course introduces the basic concepts and the basic terminology in fire risk analysis and management. Throughout the course, the students will acquire:
knowledge on the general approach to fire risk management; understanding of fundamental probability theory, risk analysis and decision theory; and be able to perform practical fire risk assessment. The course will introduce the students to methods for undertaking a rational fire risk assessment and an ensuing fire risk management process for the built environment.</Course_Objectives>
