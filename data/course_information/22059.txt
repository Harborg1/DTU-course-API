        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Grundlæggende om sensorteknologier og indledende prototyping: introduktion til sensorprincipper; fokus på resistive og kapacitive sensorer, udforskning af materialer til sensorudvikling; begyndende design og prototyping af resistive og kapacitive sensorer.

Avanceret sensordesign og prototyping: dybdegående kendskab til sensorer; materialer og designovervejelser, samt prototyping af temperatur- og piezoelektriske sensorer.

Elektrokemiske sensorer og omfattende prototypudvikling: Oversigt over elektrokemiske sensorer; integrationsstrategier for komplekse sensingbehov, design og prototyping af elektrokemiske og avancerede fleksible temperatursensorer, integration af sensorteknologier i en multifunktionel prototype.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>På dette 3-ugers laboratoriekursus bliver de studerende undervist i design og prototyping af avancerede bærbare sensorer, herunder resistive, kapacitive, induktive, piezoelektriske, elektrokemiske og temperaturtyper. Gennem praktiske projekter, der fører til en multifunktionel prototypedemo, bliver teori kombineret med praktiske fremstillings-, analyse- og integrationsfærdigheder, som forbereder eleverne til teknologisk innovation i sundhedssektoren.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Dette 3-ugers intensive kursus dykker dybt ned i biomedicinsk sensorteknologi, med fokus på design, udvikling og prototyping af fleksible og bærbare sensorer. Studerende vil få praktisk erfaring med en række sensorteknologier, herunder resistive, kapacitive, piezoelektriske, elektrokemiske og temperatursensorer. Kurset er struktureret til at lade studerende udvikle sig fra at forstå grundlæggende sensorprincipper til at designe og analysere sofistikerede sensorprototyper, hvilket kulminerer i et afsluttende projekt, hvor disse prototyper demonstreres.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Studerende fra Medicin og Teknologi har første prioritet til deltagelse i kurset.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Basics of sensor technologies and initial prototyping: Introduction to sensor principles; focusing on resistive and capacitive sensors, exploring materials for sensor development; beginning design and prototyping of resistive and capacitive sensors.

Advanced sensor design and prototyping: delving into sensors; materials and design considerations, and prototyping of temperature and piezoelectric sensors.

Electrochemical sensors and comprehensive prototype development: overview of electrochemical sensors; integration strategies for complex sensing needs, design, and prototyping of electrochemical and advanced flexible temperature sensors, integrating sensor technologies into a multifunctional prototype.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This 3-week lab course teaches designing and prototyping advanced wearable sensors, including resistive, capacitive, inductive, piezoelectric, electrochemical, and temperature types. Through hands-on projects leading to a multifunctional prototype demo, the course blends theory with practical fabrication, analysis, and integration skills, preparing students for healthcare tech innovation.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This three-week intensive course dives deep into biomedical sensor technology, focusing on the design, development, and prototyping of flexible and wearable sensors. Students will gain hands-on experience with a range of sensor technologies, including resistive, capacitive,  piezoelectric, electrochemical, and temperature sensors. The course is structured to allow students to progress from understanding basic sensor principles to designing and analyzing sophisticated sensor prototypes, culminating in a final project where these prototypes are demonstrated.</Course_Objectives>
