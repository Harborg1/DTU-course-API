        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset kombinerer teoretisk viden i forelæsninger med mindre regneøvelser samt praktisk viden opnået gennem laboratorieøvelser. Der indgår et signifikant industri-element i kurset i form af tekniske virksomhedspræsentationer samt besøg hos relevante ’brugere’ af udvalgte fysisk-kemiske vandbehandlingssteknologier.  
Specifikt vil kurset dække følgende emner:
• Membranbaseret separationsteknologi
• Klorering, oxidering og disinfektionsteknologi
• Elektrokemiske teknologier
• Udfældning &amp; koagulationsteknologi
• Sorptions- (aktivt kul, ionbytning) teknologi
• Partikelfjernelse &amp; bundfældningsteknologi
• Beluftning &amp; gas-udvekslingsteknologi</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, gruppearbejde, øvelser / beregninger i klassen, laboratorieøvelser, mindre skriftlige øvelsesrapporter, ekskursioner</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give en målrettet teknologisk viden om de basale processer og metoder, der er vigtige i fysisk-kemisk behandling (f.eks. membran-separation, elektrokemi og absorption) af vand herunder vand til industriformål, drikkevand, samt spildevand. Baseret på en solid teoretisk forståelse af principperne bag metoderne skal de studerende sættes i stand til at vurdere metodernes teknologiske anvendelighed i vandbehandling, både ved hjælp af simple håndberegninger og øvelser. Endvidere at se en eller flere af metoderne i anvendelse gennem ekskursioner til relevante fuldskala anlæg/installationer i hovedstadsområdet. Der lægges vægt på, at teknologierne ses i en bred sammenhæng til behandling af mange vandtyper – relevante både nationalt og internationalt.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course combines theoretical knowledge in lectures with less computational exercises and practical knowledge obtained through laboratory exercises. A significant industrial element is included in the course in the form of technical company presentations and visits to relevant 'users' of selected physicochemical water treatment technologies.
Specifically, the course will cover the following subjects:
• Membrane-based separation technology
• Chlorination, oxidation and disinfection technologies
• Electrochemical technologies
• Precipitation &amp; Coagulation Technology
• Sorption (activated carbon, ion exchange) technology
• Particle Removal &amp; Settlement Technology
• Aeration &amp; Gas Exchanging Technology</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, group work, exercises / calculations in class, laboratory exercises, less written exercises, excursions</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide targeted technological knowledge of the basic processes and methods that are important in the physical-chemical treatment (e.g. membrane separation, electrochemistry and absorption) of water including industrial water, drinking water, and wastewater. Based on a solid theoretical understanding of the principles of the methods, students should be able to assess the technological applicability of the methods in water treatment, using simple hand calculations and exercises. Furthermore, to see one or more of the methods in use through excursions to relevant full scale facilities / installations in the metropolitan area. Emphasis is placed on the technologies seen in a broad context for the treatment of many types of water - relevant both nationally and internationally.</Course_Objectives>
