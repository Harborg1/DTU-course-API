        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Beskrivelse af de grundlæggende fusionsprocesser og hvordan de vil blive udnyttet. Betingelser for ”antændelse” af et fusionsplasma, dvs. betingelsen for at producere mere energi ved fusionsprocesser end den tilførte energi. Indeslutning af varme fusionsplasmaer. Magnetisk indeslutning i toroidale maskiner: Tokamak og stellarator. Plasma ligevægtstilstande i tokamakker. Grundlæggende instabiliteter i magnetisk indesluttede plasmaer. Bølgeudbredelse i fusionsplasmaer. Grundlæggende plasma-opvarmningsmetoder. Diagnostik af fusionsplasmaer.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, regneøvelser og eksperimenter</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Noter</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Målet er at give de studerende en forståelse af de grundlæggende principper bag fusionsenergi og eksperimentel plasmafysik. Kurset vil give et indblik i eksperimental plasmafysik og målemetoder, der kræves for udvikling af fusionsenergi.  Erhvervet viden fra dette kursus giver et fundament for avancerede studier af fusionsplasmaer på den lokale NORTH tokamak samt på internationale plasmafaciliteter.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Description of the fundamental processes of fusion energy and how they will be exploited. Conditions for a "burning" fusion plasma, i.e., the condition for producing more energy by the fusion processes than the input energy. Confinement of hot fusion-grade plasma. Magnetic confinement in toroidal devices: Tokamaks and stellarators. Plasma equilibrium states in tokamaks. Basic instabilities in magnetically confined plasma.  Wave propagation in fusion plasmas. Basic plasma heating schemes. Diagnostics of fusion plasma.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, problem exercises, and experiments</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Notes</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The objective is to give the students an understanding of the basic principles behind fusion energy and experimental plasma physics. The course will provide insight into plasma experiments and diagnostics required for the development of fusion energy. Acquired knowledge from this course provides a foundation for advanced studies of plasma physics at the local NORTH tokamak and at international plasma facilities.</Course_Objectives>
