          <Course_Objectives>To give the students an understanding of marketing's importance to a company and how the environment (national and international) affects the company.
An important benefit of the course is that students gain a clear understanding of the essential theories, concepts, and methods.
Another important benefit of the course is that students gain a clear understanding of how models are applied to solve different types of business-related issues and challenges.
The course emphasizes understanding a holistic, or systemic, way of thinking and the ability to assess both the market approach (the customers and their needs) and the resource approach (the company's competencies), as well as the integration of ESG (people, profit, planet).</Course_Objectives>
