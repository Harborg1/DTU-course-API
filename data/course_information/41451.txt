        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I kurset gennemgås parametre, som definerer det termiske og det atmosfæriske indeklima og dets betydning for menneskers komfort, sundhed og produktivitet. Metoder til design af indeklima på baggrund af standarder og simuleringer vil blive gennemgået. Med udgangspunkt i lovmæssige krav og krav til det termiske og atmosfæriske indeklima projekteres et energirigtigt mekanisk ventilationsanlæg. Fastlægge de fysiske og funktionsmæssige krav til et mekanisk ventilationsanlæg og vælge armaturer for indblæsning og udsugning. Vurdering af brandmæssige krav. Beregning af interne og eksterne termiske belastninger. Luftbehandling (Tilstandsændring). Ventilationsprincipper, CAV og VAV systemer, luftstrømme i rum. Kanalsystem: Tryktabsberegning, lyddæmpere, spjæld, taghætter. Aggregat: Varmeveksler, filtre, ventilator, lydberegning. Introduktion til brug af enkelt værktøj til beregning af ventilationsluftmængder og termisk indeklima.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course reviews parameters that define the thermal and atmospheric indoor climate and its importance to human comfort, health and productivity. Methods for design of indoor climate based on standards and simulations will be reviewed. Based on legal requirements and requirements for the thermal and atmospheric indoor climate, an energy-efficient mechanical ventilation system is being planned. Determine the physical and functional requirements of a mechanical ventilation system and choose fixtures for inlet and extraction. Assessment of fire requirements. Calculation of internal and external thermal loads. Air treatment (Condition change). Ventilation, CAV and VAV systems, airflows in rooms. Duct system: Pressure loss calculation, silencers, dampers, roof hoods. Air-handling units: Heat exchanger, Filters, Fan, Acoustic Calculation. Introduction to the use of simple tools for calculating ventilation airflow and thermal indoor climate.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is that the student acquires knowledge and skills in projecting low energy buildings with a focus on the design of indoor climate and ventilation systems.</Course_Objectives>
