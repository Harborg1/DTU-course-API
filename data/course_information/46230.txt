          <Contents>Kerneelementer på kurset:

          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende en forståelse af de grundlæggende principper for matematisk modellering og balancering af elsystemer. Med en ikke-elektrisk perspektiv, kurset introducerer de studerende til modellering og kontrol af balancen mellem produktion og forbrug i et elsystem med mange vindmøller.  De studerende vil lære og blive teknisk udfordret til at forstå, hvordan vindmøller kan påvirke og bidrage til effekt balancen og frekvens regulering i elsystemet. For at adressere disse udfordringer er en betydelig bestanddel af kurset afsat til teori og anvendelse af modellering og computersimuleringer. Matlab &amp; Simulink er brugt som simuleringsværktøj i kurset for at opnå de læringsmål.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Fundamentals for power balance and frequency control; Power balance equation; Generating units components; Load sharing between multiple units; Per unit calculations; Power system frequency control loop with simple models for generators, loads and governors; Wind turbine concepts and characteristics; Aerodynamic power: Power curve and optimal efficiency of a wind turbine; Wind turbine inertia in per-unit; Modeling fixed speed wind turbines; Modelling and control of variable speed wind turbines; Integration of wind power model into the power system model

The students will work in groups of max. 4 students in the mandatory assignments. The group work will be documented in the form of assignment reports, which must be passed as a condition to participate in the written exam.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, computer exercises, Multiple Choice quizzes, mandatory assignment reports.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Course notes and slides</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the students an understanding of the basic principles of mathematical modeling and balancing  of an electrical system. With a non-electrical perspective, the course introduces the students to the modeling and control of the balance between generation and consumption of a power system with many wind turbines.
The students will learn and be technically challenged understanding how wind turbines can influence and contribute to the power balance and frequency control of the power system. To address these challenges, an important part of the course is devoted to theory and practice of modeling and computer simulations. Matlab &amp; Simulink is used as simulation tool to achieve the learning objectives.</Course_Objectives>
