        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Designopgavens sammensathed, kort og langt sigt, tværfunktionel koordinering og tværproduktkoordinering, disponeringer i forhold til marked og produktion.

Designarbejdets forberedende dele, platformdefinition, arkitekturskabelse, genbrug og variation, standardisering, produktmodellering, organisering og aktører, balancering af fornyende og konsoliderende aspekter.

Platformes og arkitekturers iboende virkemåde, anvendelse og effekter, funktionel alignment, designarbejdets natur i relation hertil, platformes justifikation.

Strukturelle modellers anvendelse i platforme og arkitekturer, modularisering, DFX-aspekter, visualisering af platforme.

Platformes driftsituation, konfigurering, platformorienteret fremtagning, vidensmanagement i relation til platforme, IT-support.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser og opgaver.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>De studerende skal kende  midler (platforme, arkitekturer, konfiguratorer, viden-, data- og product life- management systemer) og deres anvendelse i virksomheder til at strukturere, økonomisere og markedsfokusere produktsortiment og ressourcer i udviklingsprojekter, produktion og i produktets livscyklus, når virksomheder markedsfører sammensatte produkter og serviceydelser.

De studerende skal forstå de tværfunktionelle, produktkoordinerende og markedsfokuserende mekanismer, der betinger platformes og arkitekturtænknings positive effekter.

De studerende skal kunne identificere mål, elementerne og anvendelsesprocesserne ved etablering af platform og arkitekturtænkning i relation til en given virksomhed og angive konceptuelle elementer i realiseringen heraf.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The design task’s composedness, short and long range development, cross-functional coordination and product coordination, dispositions in relation to market and production.

Preparation of design, platform definition, creating architecture, re-use and variation, standardising, product modelling, organising and actors, balancing innovation and consolidation.

The mode of action of platforms and architectures, utilisation and effects, Company cross functional alignment, designing products in a platform, justification of platforms.

The use of structural models for platforms and architectures, modularisation, DFX-aspects, visualisation of platforms.

The utilisation of platforms, configuration, platform oriented development, knowledge management in architectural models, IT-support.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The students shall know about the means (platforms, architecture, configurators, knowledge-, data-, and product life management systems) and their application in companies for structuring, economise, and market-focused the product assortment and resources in product development projects, production, and in the product’s life cycle, when these companies launch composed products and services.

The students shall understand the cross functional, product coordinating, and market focusing mechanisms, which are determining the positive effects of platforms and architectural thinking.
The students shall be able to identify goals, elements, and use processes by establishing of platforms and architectural thinking in relation to a given company, and to point out conceptual elements hereof.</Course_Objectives>
