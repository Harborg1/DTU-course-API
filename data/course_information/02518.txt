          <Course_Objectives>På dette kursus vil du arbejde med 3D volumetriske billeder (dvs. lavet af volumetriske pixels). 3D-billeddannelse er især nyttig, hvis du ønsker at undersøge den indre struktur af et materiale på et mikroskopisk niveau uden at ødelægge den prøve, du afbilder. Dette er især nyttigt inden for medicinsk billeddannelse, materialevidenskab og biologi blandt de andre. På dette kursus får du praktiske færdigheder inden for billedopsamling, bearbejdning og analyse samt relevant matematisk indsigt.

De metoder, vi vil diskutere, vil være generelle og anvendelige til alle 3D volumetriske billeder som dem, for eksempel fra magnetisk resonansbilleddannelse. Der vil dog blive lagt særlig vægt på 3D røntgen µCT-billeddannelse (mikrocomputertomografi) på grund af vores tætte samarbejde med 3D-billedbehandlingscentret på DTU og med MAX IV synkrotronen i Lund.

Det mest udfordrende – men også det mest givende – aspekt ved 3D-billeddannelse er billedanalyse. På dette kursus vil du udvikle praktiske færdigheder til avanceret visualisering og analyse, selvom du ikke har nogen tidligere erfaring med billedanalyse, da vi tager udgangspunkt i det grundlæggende. Derudover vil du bruge high-performance computing (DTU Gbar) til effektivt at analysere store 3D-billeder gennem hele kurset.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Dette kursus vil give dig et solidt metodisk grundlag for volumetrisk billedanalyse ved at introducere de nødvendige analyseværktøjer, samt træne dig i hvordan du implementerer metoder på High Performance Computing clusteret.

Sideløbende med dette kursus er der et andet kursus ’02510’, som vil give dig færdigheder i at analysere store 3D-billeder ved hjælp af deep learning. Det er muligt, og fordelagtigt, at følge begge kurser.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>You will develop core skills essential for 3D imaging, including image acquisition principles, coding, software tools, and data experimentation.

You will explore key methods for image reconstruction, segmentation, registration, filtering, morphology, feature extraction, and quantification. This learning will be hands-on, focusing on implementing these techniques in Python while maintaining a rigorous mathematical perspective.

You will become proficient with software tools for visualization and data exploration, as well as learn how to compute statistics related to the shape and size of imaged structures.

Additionally, you will learn how to optimize performance when processing large datasets, including measuring execution time, managing memory resources, and utilizing parallel computing.

Since 3D images can be several gigabytes in size, handling and processing them on a standard PC can be challenging. Therefore, you will gain experience using high-performance computing systems, such as Gbar, to efficiently process large-scale data.

By the end of the course, you will have the confidence to write your own code for developing image processing pipelines suited for 3D imaging. You will also acquire a strong foundation for further work in 3D imaging, image analysis, and high-performance computing.</Contents>

          <Course_Literature>Course notes.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>In this course, you will work with 3D volumetric images (i.e. made of volumetric pixels). 3D imaging is particularly useful if you want to investigate the internal structure of a material on a microscopic level without destroying the sample you are imaging. This is particularly useful in medical imaging, material science, and biology among the others. In this course, you will get hands-on skills in image acquisition, processing, and analysis as well as relevant mathematical insights.

The methods we will discuss will be general and applicable to any 3D volumetric images like those, for instance, from magnetic resonance imaging. However, a special emphasis will be put on 3D x-ray µCT imaging (micro-computed-tomography) because of our close collaboration with the 3D imaging center at DTU and with the MAX IV synchrotron in Lund.

The most challenging—yet also the most rewarding—aspect of 3D imaging is image analysis. In this course, you will develop practical skills for advanced visualization and analysis, even if you have no prior experience with image analysis, as we will start from the fundamentals. Additionally, you will utilize high-performance computing (DTU Gbar) to efficiently analyze large 3D images throughout the course.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course will give you a solid methodological basis for volumetric image analysis by introducing the necessary analysis tools, as well as training you in how to implement methods on the High-Performance Computing cluster.

Alongside this course, there is another course '02510' which will give you skills in analyzing large 3D images using deep learning. It is possible, and advantageous, to follow both courses.</DTU_Remark>
