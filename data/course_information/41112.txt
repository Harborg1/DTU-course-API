        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Der udføres forsøg i Vandbygningslaboratoriet, bygn. 114.

Der arbejdes med en række forskellige forsøg, f.eks.:
   ·  Impulsstråle
   ·  Strømkræfter på omstrømmede konstruktioner
   ·  Strømning over/under og krafter på overløb/stigbord
   ·  Rørstrømning (Strømning gennem kølevandsindtag)

Der kræves en stor del af selvstændighed i udførelsen af forsøgene for herved at opnå en fornemmelse af strømmende væskers dynamik.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Experiments are carried out in the Hydraulic Laboratory, bldg. 114.

A number of different exercises are considered, e.g.:
   ·  Impact of a jet
   ·  Flow around and forces on vertical piles
   ·  Flow over/under and forces on a weir/sluice gate
   ·  Pipe flow (Flow through cooling water intake)

A great deal of autonomy is required in carrying out experiments in order to obtain a feel for the dynamics of the water flows.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Preparation of a program and assignment/report under supervision</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To introduce students to the more practical hydraulic engineering and hydrodynamics, partly by carrying out experiments in a current flume, partly by calculating the flow theoretically and comparing with own measurements.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is primarily aimed at students who have followed the academic prerequisites in the previous semester</DTU_Remark>
