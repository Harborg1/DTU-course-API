        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course covers a range of topics relevant to both clinical settings and early drug development. 
In clinical settings, the course explores therapeutic regimens, dose-time-effect relationships, therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), dose adjustments, managing missed doses, and dosing for special patient groups such as children and elderly patients. 
For early drug development, the course delves into PharmacoKinetics-PharmacoDynamics (PK-PD) relationships, effects at receptor and ion channel levels, and effect measurements like changes in biomarkers, endogenous hormones, or enzymes. 
Additionally, the course addresses many aspects of pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic variability, including population PK-PD modeling methods, simulation of PK-PD relationships, and analysis methods for PK/PD. 
Regulatory affairs and approval processes for PK/PD are also considered. 
Practical sessions include computer sessions for PK/PD modeling using commercial programs.</Contents>
