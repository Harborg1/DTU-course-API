        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>- Anvendelse af telemedicinske teknologier til monitorering og behandling af kroniske sygdomme. 
- Nationale og internationale erfaringer med telemedicin, fx ”The Whole Demonstrator Project” i Storbritannien.
- Diskussioner om hvordan man måler fysiologiske parametre for klinisk monitorering og behandling af patienter med kronisk sygdom, fx KOL-, diabets- og hjertesvigts patienter: respiration, lungefunktion, iltmætning, temperatur mm.
- Introduktion til kliniske beslutningsstøttesystemer. 
- Trådløse kommunikationsnetværk, antenne, modulation, kommunikationskanal, detektion, bitfejlsandsynlighed, linkbudget. 
- Ad hoc kortrækkende kommunikationsnetværk, minimering af energiforbrug for kommunikationsnetværk, metoder til høstning af energi, effektniveauer ved et udvalg af metoder til energihøstning. 
- Anvendelse af trådløse WAN (Wide Area Networks), LAN (Local Area Networks), PAN (Personal Area Networks) og BAN (Body Area networks) indenfor telemedicin og sundhedsteknologi.
Kurset bidrager til den grønne omstilling ved at gøre de studerende i stand til at udvikle løsninger der reducerer transport og ressourceforbrug</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Undervisning i klasse med aktiv deltagelse af de studerende. Sessionerne opdeles i  præsentation af nyt materiale og løsning af opgaver.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Telemedicin omhandler sundhedspleje, der gives på afstand, således at patienten ikke behøver at tage til klinikken. Telemedicinske systemer anvender teknologi til fjernovervågning af patienter (dvs. overførsel af målinger), algoritmer til analyse af patientdata samt kommunikation mellem sundhedspersonale og patienter.

Kurset har til formål at introducere de studerende til telemedicinske begreber, herunder eksempler fra klinisk praksis. Dette omfatter trådløse teknologier, der anvendes til at etablere kommunikation. De studerende skal  planlægge et telemedicinsk system, der forbinder en patient, f.eks. i hjemmet, med en fjernliggende klinik.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>- Telemedicine applications for monitoring and treatment of chronic diseases. 
- National and international experience with telemedicine, e.g. "The Whole Demonstrator Project" in Britain. 
- Discuss how to measure the physiological parameters for clinical monitoring and treatment of patients having chronic diseases, e.g. COPD patients, diabetic patients and patients with heart failure: respiration, lung function, oxygen saturation, temperature etc. 
- Introduction to clinical decision support systems.
-  Wireless communication networks, antennas, modulation, communication channel, detection, bit-error rate, link budget. 
- Ad hoc short-range communication networks, minimizing energy consumption of communication networks, methods for harvesting energy, power levels in a variety of methods for energy harvesting. 
- Use of wireless WAN (Wide Area Networks), LAN (Local Area Networks), PAN (Personal area Networks) and BAN (Body Area Networks) for telemedicine and health technology.
The course contributes to the green transition by enabling the students to develop solutions that reduce transportation and resource use.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Classroom teaching with the active participation of the students. The sessions are divided into the presentation of new material and solving exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Telemedicine concerns healthcare which is provided remotely in such a way so that the patient does not need to travel to the clinic. Telemedicine systems use technology for remote monitoring of patients (i.e. transmitting measurements), algorithms for analysis of patient data, and communication between health care professionals and patients.

The course aims to introduce students to telemedicine concepts including examples used in clinical practice. This includes wireless technologies that are used to establish communication. Students will  plan a telemedicine system that connects a patient, for example at home, with a remote medical facility.</Course_Objectives>
