          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og projektopgave i grupper. Kurset inkluderer selvstændigt projektarbejde med egen indsamling af litteratur og selvstænding opgaveløsning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Course_Objectives>At give den studerende et overblik over de vigtigste problemstillinger, aktører og øvrige elementer inden for godstransport og -logistik, samt at give indsigt i, hvad industriens spillere og beslutningstagere arbejder med. Dette inkluderer problemer og udfordringer for individuelle interessenter, såsom transportkøbere og transportoperatører. Godstransportsektorens problemstillinger behandles på regionalt, nationalt såvel som internationalt niveau. Alle større transportformer vil blive dækket.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is about transport within the freight supply chain. Problem settings experienced in strategic, tactical and operational contexts in freight transport. The course covers the major transportation modes, freight types, and terminals. Furthermore, the course covers major global trade and freight routes and corridors.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and group project. For the group project, students are expected to work highly independently (e.g. conduct own collection of relevant literature).</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>'The Geography of Transport Systems' by Jean-Paul Rodrigue (latest version), a portion of Port Designers Handbook, and selected papers and other material.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the student a overview of the main issues associated with freight and transport logistics, as well as to provide insights on what the industry players and policy makers are working with. This include problems and challenges for individual stakeholders such as shippers and transport operators. Issues for the freight transport sector as a whole are addressed at the regional, national and international levels. All major modes of transport will be covered.</Course_Objectives>

B.Eng. in Mobility, transport and logistics: 4. semester</Remark>
