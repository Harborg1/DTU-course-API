          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og projekt</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>I dette 2. semester-kursus påbegynder studerende et CDIO-projekt (Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate), hvor de anvender og integrerer viden erhvervet i kurserne 02322 Maskinnær Programmering og 02320 Digital Elektronik. Projektets hovedfokus er skabelsen af et kompakt computersystem implementeret på en FPGA, som interagerer med simple I/O-enheder. Studerende vil navigere i softwareudfordringer ved brug af C og/eller assembly sprog, med vægt på praktisk, hands-on erfaring i systemdesign og udvikling. Gennem samarbejdsbaseret projektarbejde vil studerende forbedre deres evner inden for teamwork, problemløsning og teknisk kommunikation, hvilket forbereder dem til ingeniørmæssige udfordringer i den virkelige verden. Dette kursus tilbyder en unik mulighed for at omsætte teoretisk viden til praksis gennem udviklingen af et funktionelt indlejret system.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and project</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>In this 2nd-semester course, students embark on a CDIO project (Conceive-Design-Implement-Operate), applying and integrating the knowledge acquired in 02322 Machine Oriented Programming and 02320 Digital Electronics. The project's centerpiece is the creation of a compact computer system, implemented on an FPGA, which interfaces with simple IO devices. Students will tackle the software challenges using C and/or assembly language, emphasizing practical, hands-on experience in system design and development. Through collaborative project work, students will enhance their skills in teamwork, problem-solving, and technical communication, preparing them for real-world engineering challenges. This course offers a unique opportunity to bring theoretical knowledge to life through the development of a functional embedded system.</Course_Objectives>
