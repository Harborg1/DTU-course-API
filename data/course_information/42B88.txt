        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Starting a new business is one of the most rewarding experiences one can imagine. The journey from an idea to a successful company is, however, paved with challenges and puts high demands on timing, technology, business and social skills. The upside, though, is tremendous in personal learning and potentially also in an economic sense. One of the defining moments of startups is often when the person with an ambition to start a company realizes that he/she needs additional skills and experiences on the team – and that this includes both the daily operational team and the board of directors.
This elective will include a mix of business theory and start-up experience based on a number of successful high-tech companies familiar to the DTU startup scene. In the elective, will help you determine if you should become a board member. And if so, how can you maximize the value you add for startups?</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. Lectures are paralleled by casework, group work and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This elective is aiming to help people who are considering a startup board position, are looking for startup opportunities or are thinking about starting their own company.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Participation in the program requires separate admission to the MMT program. The price of the MMT program is DKK 420.000. Please contact DTU Executive MBA.</DTU_Remark>
