        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Numerisk modellering af magnetiserede plasmaer. Plasmamodelhierarki: væske-, driftreducerede, kinetiske og gyrokinetiske beskrivelser. Numerisk diskretisering af partielle differentialligninger. Enkeltvæske- og resistiv magnetohydrodynamik. Driftreducerede og tovæskemodeller. Plasmaturbulens og transportdiagnostik. Tokamak-geometri, magnetiske koordinater og geometribevidste numeriske skemaer. Kinetiske modeller, herunder Vlasov- og Particle-in-Cell-metoder. Konceptuel introduktion til gyrokinetisk modellering. Ekstern opvarmning og partikelkilder med vægt på neutralstråleinjektion. Verifikation, validering og usikkerhedsvurdering i plasmasimuleringer.</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med kurset er at give de studerende en anvendelsesorienteret forståelse af, hvordan magnetiserede plasmaer modelleres og simuleres numerisk, med særlig vægt på fusionsrelevante systemer. Kurset introducerer modelhierarkiet fra væske- til kinetiske beskrivelser og demonstrerer, hvordan geometri, turbulens, opvarmning og kilder repræsenteres i numeriske simuleringer.

Gennem forenklede og reducerede modeller opnår de studerende evnen til at implementere, analysere og kritisk vurdere numeriske simuleringer. Kurset danner grundlag for videregående arbejde inden for fusionsplasmafysik, plasmaturbulens og numerisk modellering af komplekse plasmasystemer.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Numerical modelling of magnetised plasmas. Plasma model hierarchy: fluid, drift-reduced, kinetic and gyrokinetic descriptions. Numerical discretisation of partial differential equations. Single-fluid and resistive magnetohydrodynamics. Drift-reduced and two-fluid models. Plasma turbulence and transport diagnostics. Tokamak geometry, magnetic coordinates, and geometry-aware numerical schemes. Kinetic models including Vlasov and Particle-in-Cell methods. Conceptual introduction to gyrokinetic modelling. External heating and particle sources with emphasis on Neutral Beam Injection. Verification, validation, and uncertainty assessment in plasma simulations</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>The objective of the course is to provide students with an applied understanding of how magnetised plasmas are modelled and simulated numerically, with particular emphasis on fusion-relevant systems. The course introduces the hierarchy of models from fluid to kinetic descriptions and demonstrates how geometry, turbulence, heating, and sources are represented in numerical simulations. Through simplified and reduced models, the students acquire the ability to implement, analyse, and critically assess numerical simulations. The course provides a foundation for advanced work in fusion plasma physics, plasma turbulence, and numerical modelling of complex plasma systems.</Course_Objectives>
