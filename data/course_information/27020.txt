        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus introducerer grundlæggende principper inden for biologi og bioteknologi, der relaterer sig til en bred vifte af discipliner. Kurset introducerer det kemiske grundlag for liv og dets relation til biokemi og enzymer. DNA introduceres som livets universelle informationsbærende molekyle, og de studerende introduceres til, hvordan biologi omdanner sekvensinformation gennem processerne replikation, transkription og translation til kemisk funktionalitet.

Kursusindholdet vil bestå af forelæsninger, diskussioner, øvelser og gruppearbejde. Indholdet vil være struktureret i tre blokke:
- Blok I introducerer de grundlæggende principper og anvendelser inden for molekylærbiologi, evolutionsfænomenet, nogle grundlæggende begreber om økologi og den vigtige rolle, som store datasæt spiller i moderne biologi (uge 1-6).
- I blok II (uge 7-10) vil deltagerne kunne vælge mellem fire spor, der har forskelligt fokus og er organiseret af forskellige afdelinger. De brede fokusområder for de fire spor er biosensing (spor BED), biomimetik og biomaterialer (spor MIM), miljømæssig bioengineering (spor ENV) og simuleringer og kontrol af biologiske systemer (spor SIM).

- Blok III vil bestå af et gruppeprojekt, der inkorporerer temaer fra Blok 1 og 2. Projektet vil blive udført i tværfaglige grupper på 6 studerende og evalueret ved en posterpræsentation på kursets to afsluttende moduler (kl. 12-18).</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course will introduce fundamental principles of biology and biotechnology that relate to a wide variety of disciplines. The course will introduce the chemical basis for life and its relation to biochemistry and enzymes. DNA is introduced as the universal information-carrying molecule of life, and the students' are introduced to how biology converts sequence information through the processes of replication, transcription and translation into chemical functionality. 
Course content will consist of lectures, discussions, exercises and group work. Content will be structured in three blocks: 
- Block I will introduce the fundamental principles and applications of molecular biology, the phenomenon of evolution, some basic notions of ecology and the important role that large data sets play in modern biology (weeks 1-6). 
- In block II (weeks 7-10), participants will be able to choose between four tracks, that have different focus and that are organised by different departments. The broad focus areas of the four tracks are biosensing (track BED), biomimetics and biomaterials (track MIM), environmental bioengineering (track ENV), and simulations and control of biological systems (track SIM).
- Block III will consist of a group project incorporating themes from Blocks 1 and 2. The project will be carried out in interdisciplinary groups of 6 students and evaluated at a poster presentation session on the two final modules of the course (12-18h).</Contents>

This course is intended to provide all BSc students with an introduction to biological principles and an understanding of the key fundamental technologies underlying cross-disciplinary applications of biology across engineering areas.</Course_Objectives>
