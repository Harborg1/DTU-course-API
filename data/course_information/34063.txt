        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus giver en dybdegående introduktion til krystaldyrkning, en grundlæggende søjle i moderne halvlederteknologi. Studerende vil udforske centrale epitaksiteknikker, herunder molekylestråle-epitaksi (MBE) og metal-organisk dampfase-epitaksi (MOVPE), samtidig med at de opnår indsigt i termodynamik, dyrkningskinetik, defektdannelse samt kontrol af båndgab og tøjning. Emnerne omfatter også dyrkning af lavdimensionelle nanostrukturer, såsom kvantepunkter, kvantebrønde og nanotråde, kombineret med selektiv epitaksi og monolitisk integration af III-V halvledermaterialer på silicium.
Gennem forelæsninger, diskussioner af forskningsartikler og case-studier vil de studerende udvikle kompetencer til at forstå og analysere epitaksiprocesser, vurdere deres indflydelse på enheders ydeevne og kommunikere effektivt med epitaksiforskningsgrupper og produktionsfaciliteter.
Kurset er designet både for studerende, der ønsker en karriere inden for epitaksi, og for dem, der vil samarbejde med epitaksigrupper og kommercielle leverandører i forskning eller industri. Ved gennemførelse af kurset vil de studerende være rustet til kritisk at vurdere epitaksiforskning, forstå produktionsmæssige begrænsninger og designe dyrkningsstrategier til forskellige anvendelser inden for elektronik og fotonik.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course provides an in-depth introduction to crystal growth, a fundamental pillar of modern semiconductor technology. Students will explore key epitaxial techniques, including molecular beam epitaxy (MBE) and metal-organic vapor phase epitaxy (MOVPE), while gaining insight into thermodynamics, growth kinetics, defect formation, and engineering of bandgap and strain. Topics also include the growth of low-dimensional nanostructures—such as quantum dots, quantum wells, and nanowires—along with selective epitaxy and the monolithic integration of III-V compound semiconductors on silicon.

Through lectures, discussions of research papers, and case studies, the students will develop the skills to understand and analyze epitaxial processes, assess their impact on device performance, and communicate effectively with epitaxial research groups and fabrication facilities.

The course is designed for both students pursuing careers in epitaxy and those collaborating with epitaxial groups and foundries in research or industry. By completing the course, the students will be equipped to critically assess epitaxial research, understand fabrication constraints, and design growth strategies for various applications in electronics and photonics.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, student presentations and discussion of the scientific articles. Ends by a small project and a poster presentation.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The lectures on the theory of epitaxial growth are mainly based on “Epitaxy of Semiconductors. Introduction to Physical Principles” by Udo W. Pohl, Springer Berlin Heidelberg, 2013 (available electronically from the DTU library). A collection of scientific papers will be used to illustrate specific cases and for discussions.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>This course provides a foundational understanding of epitaxial growth processes and techniques and their role in semiconductor technology. The course is designed for both those looking to work directly in the field of epitaxial growth and those who will potentially collaborate with epitaxial foundries, equipping them with the knowledge to assess growth processes, understand growth limitations, interpret characterization data, and communicate effectively with fabrication facilities.</Course_Objectives>
