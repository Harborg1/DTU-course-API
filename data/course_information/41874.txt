          <Contents>Centrale elementer:

I kurset arbejdes der med regionale strategiske udvikling med fokus på infrastruktur, bosætning og erhvervsudvikling samt med byniveauet og inddragelsen af relevante aktører i en strategisk udvikling. Endvidere arbejdes der med at beskrive værktøjer til brug for plan og udviklingsarbejde i Grønland.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Projektopgaver med tilhørende forelæsninger, undersøgelser, øvelser, informationssøgning, gruppearbejde og præsentationer, hvor der gives løbende feedback.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give Arktiske Ingeniører kompetence til at bidrage til 4 dominerende udfordringer i Arktis/Grønland:
•Behovet for planlægning i de grønlandske kommuner, der knytter kommuneplanlægning, lokalplanlægning, infrastrukturplanlægning, bæredygtig by-, bygde- og erhvervsudvikling sammen.
•Behovet for at udvikle, optimere og servicere infrastruktur i de grønlandske ø-driftssamfund. 
•Behovet for strategisk planlægning i Grønland – at kunne tænke strategisk og sammenhængende om ressourcer, aktører og mål. 
•Behovet for vidensledelse i det grønlandske samfund - at kunne forankre viden om strategier, processer og projekter i det grønlandske samfund, dels pga. den store udskiftning i forvaltninger og dels pga. de geografiske afstande og de relativt små faglige miljøer. 
 
Kurset relateres til den aktuelle udvikling i Grønland, og knytter sig til såvel fysisk som strategisk planlægning. Der lægges vægt på, at de studerende arbejder med projektarbejdsformen. Det er et mål, at de studerende udvikler kompetencer til at bidrage til en kommunal forvaltning med tilhørende politisk system, og at de kan bidrage til at øge inddragelsen af relevante aktører i planlægningen og udviklingen af Grønland.</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>Key elements: 
• Sustainable development of small and large urban communities 
• Planning infrastructure in island operation societies
• Municipal planning 
• Settlement and Infrastructure 
• Newer forms of planning and strategic planning 
• The link between industrial base, infrastructure and settlement 
• Planning processes and involvement of stakeholders 
• Quality of planning and the good city 
• Creative work 
• The political system and the municipal workplace 
• Project 
The course work with regional strategic development with a focus on infrastructure, residential and commercial development as well as with byniveauet and the involvement of relevant stakeholders in a strategic development. Moreover, work is to describe tools for plan and development in Greenland.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project work and lectures, exercises, information search, group work and presentations where continuous feedback is given.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To develop the students' competences to contribute to 4 dominating challenges in Arctic/Greenland: - The need for planning in the municipalities (municipal planning, local planning, infrastructure planning, sustainable urban and settlement and trade planning) - The need to develop, optimise and service infrastructure in the Greenlandic island operating communities. - The need for strategic planning in Greenland (to think strategically and holistically about resources, actors and goals. - The need for knowledge management in Greenland (to anchor knowledge about strategies, processes and projects in Greenland. This is important due to high frequency of temporary jobs in the public sector in Greenland and due to the geographical distances and the small professional communities.
The course is related to the current developments in Greenland and is connected to both physical and strategic planning. Emphasis is placed on having the students work with project-based methods. The goal is for the students to develop competencies to contribute to municipal administration with its associated political system, and to be able to help increase the involvement of relevant stakeholders in the planning and development of Greenland.</Course_Objectives>
