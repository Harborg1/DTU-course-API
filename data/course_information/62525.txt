          <Contents>Principperne for opbygning af et digitalt analysesystem for signaler. Arkitekturen af et signalbehandlingssystem ud fra grundlæggende komponenter.           <Contents>Principles for the design of a digital analysis system for signals. The architecture of a digital signal processing system based on fundamental components.
