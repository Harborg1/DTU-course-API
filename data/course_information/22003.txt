        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Psykofysik og det auditive systems fysiologi. Modeller for auditiv signalbehandling og perception. Neurofysiologiske målemetoder og deres relation til psykoakustiske (adfærdsmæssige) fund. Neurale billeddannelsesteknikker. Tekniske og kliniske anvendelser, herunder kompensationsstrategier i moderne høreapparater og cochlear-implantater. Kurset introducerer desuden beregningsmæssige og maskinlæringsbaserede modelleringsmetoder til analyse af auditive data og prædiktion af perceptuelle udfald. Undervisningen suppleres af klassebaserede regneopgaver samt syv MATLAB-baserede øvelser, der knytter sig til udvalgte forelæsninger.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelser.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med kurset er at opnå en forståelse for hørelsens signalbehandlingsmekanismer og deres perceptuelle konsekvenser. De studerende vil arbejde med de funktionelle sammenhænge mellem lydens fysiske egenskaber, deres neurale repræsentation langs den auditive bane og de tilhørende perceptuelle oplevelser med udgangspunkt i en systemorienteret tilgang. Kurset omfatter desuden studiet af sensorisk og central auditiv signalbehandling samt deres anatomiske forankring ved anvendelse af objektive metoder såsom otoakustiske emissioner og auditorisk fremkaldte potentialer. Endvidere opnår de studerende indsigt i potentielle kliniske og teknologiske anvendelser gennem brug af auditiv modelbaseret signalbehandling samt data-drevne maskinlæringsmetoder.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Psychophysics and physiology of the auditory system. Models of auditory signal processing and perception. Neurophysiological measurement methods and their relation to psychoacoustical (behavioral) findings. Neural imaging techniques. Technical and clinical applications, including compensation strategies in modern hearing aids and cochlear implants. The course also introduces computational and machine-learning–based modeling approaches for analyzing auditory data and predicting perceptual outcomes. Classroom-based problem-solving sessions and seven MATLAB-based exercises complement selected lectures throughout the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of this course is to develop an understanding of processing mechanisms in the auditory system and their perceptual consequences. Students will explore the functional relationships between the physical properties of sound, their neural representation along the auditory pathway, and the resulting percepts, adopting a systems-oriented approach. The course further examines sensory and brain processing and their anatomical correlates using objective methods such as otoacoustic emissions and auditory evoked potentials. In addition, students will gain insight into potential clinical and technological applications by applying auditory model–based processing techniques as well as data-driven machine-learning approaches.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is a natural introduction to master thesis work within hearing science and technology.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>E-learning is incorporated through online interactive quizzes, recorded video lectures, a course wiki open to student contributions, discussion boards or blogs, web-based tools, and a digital whiteboard. Students who plan to take 22006 Acoustic Communication are advised to complete that course before enrolling in this course.</Remark>
