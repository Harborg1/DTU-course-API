          <Contents>De studerende vil blive bedt om at udvikle en marketingplanlægning med hensyn til et specifikt produkt/service efter en kundeudviklingsproces.           <Contents>The students will be asked to develop a marketing planning with respect to a specific product/service, following a customer development process. The course will enable the students to think strategically about developing customers through various approaches in entrepreneurial marketing. The course is based on a combination of empirical examples, theories and concepts about market, business design, strategy and marketing.</Contents>
