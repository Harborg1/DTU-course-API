        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>The course consists of 13 modules covering the following content/topics:
- General introduction of wind turbine mechanical, electrical and structural components
- Conceptual design and sizing, introducing design standards IEC 61400
- Drivetrain topology, analytic loads calculations
- Materials usage and recycling (including rare-earth material)
- Tower design and manufacturing technologies
- Condition monitoring and sensing 
- Life cycle analysis of selected components

Each module will be structured with specific learning objectives and tasks. Usually, a typical module consists of one or two video lectures, relevant research problems or publications, and some calculation exercises.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>E-læring</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Wind turbines consist of various components that work together to harness the energy of the wind and convert it into electrical power. Some of the key components include blades, tower, nacelle and drivetrain, sub-support structures. This course on wind turbine components aims to provide students with a comprehensive and basic understanding of the aforementioned components on their functions, design, materials, manufacturing, measurement, digital tools, and end of life. Please note that blades and sub-support structures are not covered in this course because they are well-explained in other classes (for instance, 46W11 and 46W34)</Course_Objectives>
