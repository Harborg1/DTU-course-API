          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Video lectures and digital group work with and without guidance of a teaching assistant.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course is a direct continuation of course 01811, Mathematical Analysis and Modelling. The goal is to provide the students with the ability to apply fundamental mathematical concepts and modern computer tools solving classical problems of engineering on a slightly higher taxonomical level than in 01811. Relevant topics from linear algebra are introduced as a methodical basis for treating multi-dimensional problems arising from engineering applications. The scope and form of the course supports application of the modelling circle aiming at the treatment of relevant problems at increasing taxonomical levels. The digital formation and "computational thinking" of the students is further strengthened compared to course 01811 by working with modern computational tools (currently Maple).</Course_Objectives>
