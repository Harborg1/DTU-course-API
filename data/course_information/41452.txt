          <Contents>I kurset gennemgås den grundlæggende opbygning og funktion af klimaskærmskonstruktioner herunder vindues- og solafskærmningssystemer samt installationer for opvarmning og ventilation.  

Metoder til at beregne bygningers varme- og fugtbalance gennemgås. Dette omfatter:
- Stationær varmetransport i én dimension
- Beregning af varmetab for klimaskærmskonstruktioner i henhold til gældende standard
- Beregning af solindfald gennem vindues- og solafskærmningssystemer
- Beregning af ventilationstab
- Udeklimaets betydning
- Termisk indeklima
- Metoder til bestemmelse af bygningers energiforbrug
- Egenskaber for fugtig luft og bygningers fugtbalance
- Glasers metode for stationær fugttransport

Desuden introduceres til funktion og typisk opbygning af varme og ventilationssystemer.

I kurset gennemgås desuden bygningsreglementets krav til varmetab og energiforbrug herunder især kravene til bygningens energiramme. Eftervisningen af, at bygningsreglementets krav overholdes, foretages ved hjælp af et computerbaseret værktøj.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course presents the basic construction and function of building envelope components, including window and solar shading systems, as well as services for heating and ventilation.

Methods to calculate the heat and moisture balance of building are presented. This includes:
- Stationary one-dimensional heat transfer
- Calculation of heat loss according to current standards
- Calculation of solar energy transmitted through window and solar shading systems
- Calculation of heat loss from ventilation
- Influences from the outdoor climate
- Thermal indoor environment
- Methods to determine energy consumption in buildings
- Properties for moist air and moisture balance in buildings
- Glasers method for calculation of stationary moisture transport

The function and typical layout of services for heating and ventilation are introduced.

In the course, the requirements in the building regulations regarding heat loss and energy consumption are presented—especially the requirements for the energy frame. A computer tool is used to show compliance with the demands in the building regulations.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to make you able to design building envelopes and technical services with regard to indoor climate, energy consumption and durability of the building.

After the course, you can determine heat and moisture properties for building envelope components, including window and solar shading systems. You can describe the function of heating and ventilation systems. You can calculate heat loss and energy consumption for a building and show that requirements in the building regulations are fulfilled. You can calculate moisture transport in building envelope components and evaluate risk of moisture damage.</Course_Objectives>
