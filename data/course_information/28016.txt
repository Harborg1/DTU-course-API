        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Opstilling af stationære og ikke-stationære differentielle masse- og energibalancer. Matematiske modeller for systemer med kemisk og biokemisk reaktion, samt konvektion, diffusion og varmeledning. Formulering af randbetingelser. Sædvanlige differentialligninger og systemer af disse. Anvendelse på kemitekniske systemer og processer, såsom kontinuerligt omrørte tankreaktorer, katalysatorpartikler og andre systemer.
Eksperimentel øvelse med modelopstilling.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Klasseundervisning og projektarbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>For en ingeniør er det vigtigt at kunne analysere en praktisk problemstilling gennem opstilling af simplificerede modeller, der hver tager højde for forskellige aspekter af problemstillingen. I kurset skal de studerende lære at opstille modeller for simple kemiske og biokemiske problemstillinger. Endvidere skal de studerende opnå viden om og færdighed i en række centrale matematiske begreber og metoder for at kunne løse de opstillede modeller analytisk eller med brug af passende software.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset gives på dansk.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The development of steady-state and non-steady-state differential mass- and energy balances. Mathematical models for systems with chemical and biochemical reaction, together with convective and diffusive mass- and heat transport. The formulation of initial and boundary conditions. Ordinary differential equations and systems of such equations. Laboratory exercise with model formulation. Application to chemical engineering systems and processes, like continuous stirred tank reactor, catalyst particles, and other.
Experimental exercise with modeling.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and project work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>For an engineer it is important to be able to analyse practical problems by simplified models, each of which simulates different aspects of the problem. In the course, the students learn to formulate models for simple chemical and biochemical systems. Furthermore, the students must obtain knowledge and capabilities within a number of central mathematical concepts and methods in order to solve the models analytically or with the help of an appropriate software.</Course_Objectives>
