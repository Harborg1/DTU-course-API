        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to the fundamental theoretical basis for the formulation of geometrical non-linear and dynamic behaviour, as well as optimization of collaps load, and description of the corresponding FEM formulation. The course is structured around lectures and exercises covering both analytical approach, further development of supplied Matlab-code, independent programming.</Contents>
