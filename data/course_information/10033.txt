          <Contents>Newtons love og impulsmomentsætningen anvendt dels på partikler, dels på stive legemer. Arbejdssætningen, konservative og ikke-konservative kræfter. Stød. Introduktion af fiktive kræfter. Svingninger, koblede svingninger og introduktion til simple kontinuerte medier (svingende streng). Planetbevægelse. Elementer af  Lagrange og Hamilton formalismerne. Anvendelse af dimensionsanalyse og ikke-dimensionalisering.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>At gøre den studerende fortrolig med mekanikkens grundbegreber og sætte den studerende i stand til med analytiske metoder at løse simple mekaniske og tekniske problemer. Ved at kombinere anvendelse af analytiske metoder med computersimulering tilstræbes det at give den studerende en mere intuitiv forståelse af bevægelseslovene. Samtidig skal det med computersimulering være muligt at løse mere komplicerede og teknisk mere relevante problemstillinger.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Newton’s laws, and the angular momentum relation – applied partly to particles and partly to rigid bodies. The law of conservation of energy, conservative and non-conservative forces. Collisions. Introduction to fictitious forces. Oscillations, coupled oscillations, and introduction to simple continuous media (vibrating string). Planetary motion. Elements of Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalisms. Practical considerations concerning computer simulation using the programming language Python. Applications of error analysis. Applications of dimensional analysis and non-dimensionalization.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, experiments, and problem sessions.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The course has two textbooks.
The first is Engineering Mechanics 3 by Dietmar Gross et al, Spring Verlag. 

The other is Volume 1 of University Physics from OpenStax, that is available free of charge at  https:/​/​openstax.org/​details/​books/​university-physics-volume-1.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Our aim is to make the student familiar with the fundamental concepts of mechanics, and enable the student to apply analytical methods, when solving simple mechanical and technical problems.

To achieve our objective of giving our students a more intuitive understanding of the laws of motion, we use a combination of analytical methods and computer simulations. The use of computer simulations will enable us to perform more complex as well as technically relevant tasks.</Course_Objectives>
