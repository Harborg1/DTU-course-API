          <Contents>Kurset indeholder to dele: Forelæsningsdelen hvor teori, processer og metoder indenfor system engineering bliver gennemgået, samt gruppearbejdsdelen hvor de studerende skal løse opgaver hvor system engineerings metoderne skal udføres på et rumfart/satellit-projekt, med det formål at føre projektet videre mod at bestå et pre-phase A design review.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Kurset omhandler de fundamentale metoder og helhedsperspektiver inden for system engineering, der relaterer til komplekse og højteknologiske industrier, såsom rumfartsindustrien. Emnet system engineering er i høj grad multidisciplinært og kræver bred viden og erfaring at trække på. For at komplekse og bredt favnende projekter, såsom rumfartøjer og satellitter, skal lykkes, er det essentielt med en systemingeniør og dennes metoder for at få enderne til at mødes og opnå en missions succeskriterier.
Kurset baseres i høj grad på gruppearbejde, der kræver samarbejde og fælles indsats fra alle deltagere. Der arbejdes mod et fælles mål, hvor alle studerende bidrager til et missionstudie, der kulminerer i et pre-Phase A review.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Kurset er åbent for alle. Dog ved overtegning af tilmeldte deltagere, vil studerende på kandidatuddannelsen i Geofysik og Rumteknologi blive priorieteret.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course consists of two parts: The lecture hours, where the theory, processes and methods for systems engineering are taught, and the group work part, where the students will solve assignments with a focus on applying the methods of system engineering to a given spacecraft project, to bring a project successfully towards a pre-phase A design review.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and group work during the 13-week spring period.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Main Litterature: 
-Hirshorn: NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, Rev 2, 2007
Supplemental litterature: 
-Fortescue, Swinerd, and Stark: Spacecraft Systems Engineering. 4th edition. Wiley, 2011.
-Pisacane: Fundamentals of Space Systems. 2. Edition. Oxford, 2005.
-Other relevant research litterature</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to teach a fundamental and holistic approach to the field of systems engineering that applies specifically to complex high-tech industries, such as the aerospace industry. The field of systems engineering is highly multidisciplinary, requiring a broad knowledge base and experience to draw from. For complex and wide-ranging projects, like a spacecraft mission, to succeed, a systems engineer's key role and methods are essential to make ends meet and fulfil a mission's requirements.
The course is heavily based on group projects, which require cooperation and joint effort from all participants. We aim for a common goal where all contribute to a larger feasibility study that culminates in a pre-Phase A review.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Course is open for all. However, if the course is oversubscribed, the students in the master's programme in Earth and Space Physics and Engineering will have precedence.</DTU_Remark>
