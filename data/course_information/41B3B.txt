          <Contents>Virksomhedstyper, markedstyper, ansvar. Ledelse af virksomheder kontra projekter. Ejerskab på tværs af organisationen. 
Modeller for strategi og forretningsudvikling; værdikædeanalyse; virksomhedsstrategisk analyse;  cirkulære forretningsmodeller. Dokumentation og rapportering; ESG, CSR. B-corp certificering. Grønne regnskaber, total cost of ownership. EU's taksonomi og FNs Program for Responsible Investments.

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forlæsninger med diskussion

          <Course_Objectives>Den bæredygtige omstilling af byggeriet forudsætter adoption af løsninger, processer og forretningsmodeller på tværs af organisationens værdikæde. Dette gælder såvel for  virksomheders interne adfærd og strategi, samt udadtil i forhold til produkt og marked. 
Målet med kurset er at give de studerende viden og kompetencer til at indgå i denne proces. 
Kurset omhandler modeller for virksomhedsstrategi og forrretningsudvikling, samt metoder og værktøjer til at fremme processen omkring bæredygtigt byggeri, herunder hvordan en virksomhed kan positionere sig for at kunne bidrage til en bæredygtig omstilling.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Del af efteruddannelsen Master i Bæredygtigt Byggeri.</DTU_Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Business types, market types, responsibilities. Management of companies versus projects. Ownership across the organization.
Models for strategy and business development; value chain analysis; business strategic analysis; circular business models. Documentation and reporting; ESG, CSR. B-corp certification. Green accounts, total cost of ownership. The EU's taxonomy and the UN's Program for Responsible Investments.
The students will meet companies where sustainability is a significant part of the company's identity and services.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures with discussion
Project work in smaller groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The sustainable restructuring of construction requires the adoption of solutions, processes and business models across the organisation's value chain. This applies both to companies' internal behavior and strategy, as well as externally in relation to product and market.
The aim of the course is to give the students the knowledge and skills to participate in this process.
The course deals with models for company strategy and business development, as well as methods and tools to promote the process around sustainable construction, including how a company can position itself to be able to contribute to a sustainable transition.</Course_Objectives>
