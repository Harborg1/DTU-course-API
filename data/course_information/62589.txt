          <Course_Objectives>The increasing demand for information to create competitive advantages and optimize business processes has stimulated an increased use of Business Intelligence (BI), Analytics and Big Data. The goal of the course is to give students professional competences to identify information deliverables in a strategic context to innovate business processes, a basic understanding of the technology in use, and skills to develop a BI solution by using a spreadsheet (e.g. MS Excel) or other generally available BI-tool, e.g. Power BI or Tableau.</Course_Objectives>
