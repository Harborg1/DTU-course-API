          <Contents>I kurset introduceres, hvorledes de fysiske omgivelser som termik, luftkvalitet, lyd (støj), lys influerer på menneskets sundhed, komfort og ydeevne.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course provides information on how the physical environment, including thermal conditions, light, air quality, and noise, influences human health, comfort, and performance. Knowledge of these parameters is fundamental for the design of buildings, vehicles, protective clothing, and related systems. The student will learn which parameters are important for a good environment. Existing requirements, standards, and guidelines will be presented. Through this course, students gain important basic knowledge that they can apply in further studies of the influence of the environment and buildings on humans. In addition to lectures and exercises, the students will perform measurement tasks.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, task in groups, presentation of group work, experimental work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Notes, papers, and the book (Grundlæggende Bygningsvidenskab. Indeklima written by Steffen Petersen, published by Akademisk Forlag in 2026)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To obtain basic knowledge on the influence of the physical environment (thermal, air quality, noise, light) on human health, comfort and performance.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is very broad and recommended as an introduction to indoor environment studies at DTU. Teachers will come from different DTU departments and from outside DTU. Both exams and exercises are included in the grading.</Remark>
