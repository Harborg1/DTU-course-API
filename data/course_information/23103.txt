        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will provide in depth coverage of current and relevant food safety issues regarding microbiological hazards. The course presents relevant microbiological hazards in the entire food production chain from farm to fork. The course will enable you to evaluate relevant microbiological hazards in specific food production chains, to identify critical steps in the production line, and to construct a hazard control plan to control or mitigate these hazards. The course will introduce predictive microbiology approaches to assess the safe shelf life and predict effects of product redesigns. You will work with the topics through lectures, assignments, and group/project work.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, seminars, group work, group project assignment, group presentations and one group report.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course will enable you to evaluate relevant microbiological hazards in food production chains. You will relate important microorganisms to their possible roles in spoilage and diseases that can be transmitted through the food chain to humans. Furthermore, you will use predictive microbiology tools to determine shelf life and develop safe products. As part of a group project assignment, you will conduct a hazard analysis and formulate a hazard control plan, which covers the raw materials, production methodologies and unit operations for a given food production chain.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
