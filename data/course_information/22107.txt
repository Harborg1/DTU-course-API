The student will gain forefront knowledge of an area within Life Science, while gaining insight into how the scientific method is put into practice to solve pressing issues facing society. The student will also gain general competencies, e.g. the ability to participate in a constructive mentor-mentee relationships and the ability to communicate science – in writing as well as orally.</Course_Objectives>
