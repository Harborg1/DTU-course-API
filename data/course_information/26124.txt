        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Der gives en indføring i koordinationskemiens koncepter, herunder nomenklatur, hård-blød-syre-base-teorien, isomeri, krystalfeltteorien, ligandfelt- og molekylorbitalteorien. På basis heraf forklares overgangsmetalkompleksers strukturelle, magnetiske, spektrale, termodynamiske og reaktionskinetiske egenskaber. Kemien af udvalgte overgangsmetaller samt koordinationskemiens organometalliske, katalytiske og spektroskopiske egenskaber  behandles.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelsesopgaver.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Housecroft, Sharpe, Inorganic Chemistry, seneste udgave</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give indsigt i moderne koordinationskemi med fokus på at kunne forstå udvalgte eksempler fra: Molekylær koordinationskemi, organometallisk katalyse samt faste materialer med optiske eller magnetiske egenskaber. Kurset vil udvikle en overordnet kemisk forståelse baseret på elektronstruktur og det periodiske system. Der er fokus på samspillet mellem teori og eksperiment.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Af betydning for fagområder, hvor metalioners kemi spiller en rolle, f.eks. biokemi, katalyse, analytisk kemi og miljølære.</Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The concepts of coordination chemistry are introduced, including nomenclature, hard-soft acid-bases, isomeri, crystal field theory, ligand field and molecular orbital theory. On this basis, the structural, thermodynamic, kinetic, spectral, and magnetic properties of transition metal compounds are explained. The chemistry of selected transition metals and the organometallic, catalytic and spectroscopic aspects of coordination chemistry are treated.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Housecroft, Sharpe, Inorganic Chemistry, latest edition</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give insight into modern coordination chemistry, with focus on understanding molecular coordination chemistry, organometallic catalysis and solid state materials with optical or magnetic properties. The course will develop a general chemical understanding based on electronic structure and the periodic table of elements. The interplay between theory and experiment is in focus.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Of importance in areas where metal ions play a role, for example biochemistry, catalysis, analytical chemistry and environmental chemistry.</Remark>
