          <Contents>Bygninger anvender op mod 40 % af Danmarks samlede energiforbrug. For at opfylde regeringens og EU's mål om at reducere den eksisterende bygningsmasses energiforbrug med minimum 50 % inden år 2050, kræver det massive investeringer i renoveringsprojekter.

• Designforslag til nødvendige konstruktive ændringer
• Bæredygtighedsvurdering af designforslagene
• Økonomisk vurdering af omkostninger ved de foreslåede løsninger.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Buildings use up to 40% of Denmark's total energy consumption. In order to meet the government's and the EU's goal of reducing the energy consumption of the existing building stock by at least 50% by 2050, massive investments in renovation projects are required.
At the same time, the requirements for the design and use of our buildings are changing.
The students prepare a registrant with associated design proposals for building renovation, with the following means:
• Analysis and assessment of building condition
• Registration and assessment of mechanical installations and building structures.
• Design proposals for changes to the interior, including a new bathroom
• Design proposals for changes to the climate shield, with an assessment of minimizing energy consumption.
• Design proposals for water, drainage, heating and ventilation conditions for a contemporary comfort level and minimizing future resource consumption
• Design proposals for necessary structural changes
• Sustainability assessment of the design proposals
• Economic assessment of the costs of the proposed solutions.</Contents>
