          <Contents>I efterårssemestret udføres et antal projektarbejder, hver af én eller nogle ugers varighed.

Indholdet af de enkelte projekter vil variere fra år til år.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>A number of independent projects, of one or a few weeks duration, are carried out in the autumn semester. Programming will be incorporated in the majority of the projects, and at least one of the projects will introduce the concept of rigorous mathematical proof. Further, in January, a larger project is carried out.

The aim is that these projects taken together with the recommended courses at the first and second semester will provide an introduction to the most important mathematical subjects of the education and to a number of important applications. In addition, these projects and courses should point toward advanced subjects and courses.

The content of the projects will vary from one year to another.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is given as a series of individual projects each with a duration of one or a few weeks. The form is mostly lectures and group work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide
1)	a general introduction to studies at DTU;
2)	an introduction to mathematics, mathematical modeling and related applications;
3)	an introduction to programming tools for solving mathematical problems;
4)	an engineering perspective on the education through development of core values such as: independence, practical relevance, professional identity, problem solving processes, professionalism and depth, creativity;
5)	training in group work and report preparation.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course is reserved for Applied Mathematics bachelor students.</DTU_Remark>
