        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>This course offers perspectives on the art of strategy and explores how strategy at the business level is conceptualized and practiced. Its design is guided by the premise that the firm is constrained by its environment and the resources it can access but, at the same time, it is also enabled by Management’s ability to leverage and to ultimately change these constraints in ways that create opportunities that may otherwise not exist. Hence, we will be exploring concepts and tools that can help you understand the environment that a company finds itself in, the resources it needs to obtain or create and develop, and the organization it needs to build. 

The course is built on three pillars: strategic positioning, capabilities, and change. Starting from the analysis of the firm’s external competitive environment, we assess the “structural” attractiveness of the relevant industry and consider how a firm’s position in that industry affects its performance. Then shifting focus internally to an analysis of the firm’s resources and capabilities, we explore how competitive advantage is built and sustained. Finally, we turn to organizational change to explore its drivers, challenges, and implications – particularly in a world that is becoming increasingly volatile, uncertain, complex and ambiguous. We also consider how the organization’s behavioral context both constrains and facilitates our ability to put strategy into action – not only to adapt to a changing world but also to drive some of that change.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. Lectures  are paralleled by casework, group work and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of this module is to provide the participants with action-learning based knowledge  about relevant topics within Strategy that can be applied in the participants’ companies.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Participation in the program requires separate admission to the MMT program. The price of the MMT program is DKK 420.000. Please contact DTU Executive MBA.</DTU_Remark>
