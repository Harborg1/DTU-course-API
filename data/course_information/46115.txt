        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer grundlæggende principper inden for computational fluid dynamics (CFD) og turbulens, herunder energikaskaden, bevægelsesskalaer og Navier–Stokes-ligningerne, samt en oversigt over klassificeringer af turbulensmodeller. Det dækker Reynolds-gennemsnitlige Navier–Stokes (RANS) modeller i dybden, herunder almindelige eddy-viskositet-modeller, samt adfærden af vægbegrænsede strømninger. Kurset introducerer også Large Eddy Simulation (LES) med fokus på filtrering, sub-grid scale (SGS) modellering og syntetisk turbulensgenerering, samt udvalgte best practices og avancerede emner såsom anvendelsen af maskinlæring i turbulensmodellering. Studerende får praktisk erfaring med CFD ved brug af OpenFOAM®, herunder implementering af turbulensmodeller.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the fundamentals of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and turbulence, including the energy cascade, scales of motion, and the Navier–Stokes equations, along with an overview of turbulence model classifications. It covers Reynolds-Averaged Navier–Stokes (RANS) modeling in depth including common eddy-viscosity models, as well as the behavior of wall-bounded flows. The course also introduces Large Eddy Simulation (LES), focusing on filtering, sub-grid scale (SGS) modeling, and synthetic turbulence generation, along with selected best practices and advanced topics such as the use of machine learning in turbulence modeling. Students gain practical experience with CFD using OpenFOAM®, including the implementation of turbulence models.</Contents>
