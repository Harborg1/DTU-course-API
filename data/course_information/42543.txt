          <Contents>Kurset fokuserer på ledelse af organisatoriske forandrings- og transitionsprocesser. Det introducerer kerneteorier og modeller for organisatoriske forandringsprocesser - fra at forstå forandringen til at levere og opretholde forandringen. Gennem kurset arbejdes der med forskellige cases om organisatoriske forandringer, hvor man anvender teorier og modeller til at analysere og udvikle anbefalinger, der har fokus på både medarbejdere og performance.           <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus omfatter processen med at håndtere bæredygtige organisatoriske forandringer og overgange. Det sætter studerende i stand til at lære at lede forandringsprocesser, og forstå hvordan man navigerer i individuelle, gruppe og organisatoriske perspektiver for at sikre både organisatorisk præstation og bæredygtighed. Dette opnås ved at introducere teorier og modeller for forandringsledelse, anvende disse på praksiscases og planlægge en organisatorisk forandringsintervention i forhold til en konkret case.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course focuses on leading organizational change and transition processes. It introduces core theories and models for organizational change processes, from understanding the change to implementing the change and sustaining it. Throughout the course, you work with different cases of organizational change where you use theories and models to analyze and make recommendations with concern for both people and performance. In addition, concrete examples are presented through guest lectures.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, peer review of individual assignments, case analysis and project work in teams of 6-7 students.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Main book: Palmer, Dunford &amp; Buchanan (2022) Managing Organizational Change - A Multiple Perspective Approach (4.ed.)
Additional material and lecture slides will be provided during the course.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>This course encompasses the process of managing sustainable organisational change and transitions. It enables students to learn how to manage change processes and how to navigate individual, group, and organisational perspectives to safeguard both organizational performance and sustainability. This is achieved by introducing theories and models of change management, applying these to practice cases and planning an organizational change intervention in relation to a specific case.</Course_Objectives>
