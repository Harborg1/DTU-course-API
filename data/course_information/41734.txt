        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset undervises over 13 dage og dækker forskellige emner. Hver kursusdag er afsat til et enkelt emne, for at give en egnet dækning af 1) Det internationale system af enheder (SI-systemet), 2) Måleusikkerhed og sporbarhed, 3) Geometriske Produkt Specifikationer, 4) Koordinatmetrologi (målinger på koordinatmålemaskiner), 5) Digitalisering og Reverse Engineering), 6) Overflademetrologi, 7) Teknologier til optisk måling, 8) Toleranceverifikation, 9) Karakterisering af værktøjsmaskiner; 10) Måling af procesvariabler undervejs.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, demonstrationer, øvelser og praktiske eksperimenter med flere måleinstrumenter. Formativ feedback gives direkte på forskellige aktiviteter samt gennem peer review og studenteroplæg.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset fokuserer på at bibringe en systematisk opfattelse af geometrisk metrologi som beslutningsgrundlag for at styre konstruktion, fremstilling og funktion, og som stærkt værktøj til løsning af såvel industrielle og videnskabelige problemstillinger. Målingers kvalitetssikring understreges med udgangspunkt i den metrologiske infrastruktur på virksomheds-, nationalt og internationalt niveau. Derudover håndteres de metrologiske udfordringer forbundet med karakterisering af værktøjsmaskiners præstationer (test af værktøjsmaskiner) og fremstillingsprocessers signatur (procesmetrologi) for at understøtte pålidelig kompensation af værktøjsmaskiner og overvågning af procesvariabler.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is taught over 13 days, dealing with different topics. Each course day is structured around a specific theme to give suitable coverage of 1) The international system of units (SI); 2) Measurement uncertainty and traceability; 3) Geometrical Product Specifications; 4) Coordinate metrology (measurements on CMMs); 5) Digitalization and Reverse Engineering; 6) Surface metrology; 7) Technologies for optical metrology; 8) Tolerance verification; 9) Machine tool performance characterization; 10) Measurement of key process variables.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, demonstrations, exercises, and hands-on experiments using several measurement instruments. Formative feedback is given directly on various activities, as well as through peer review and peer role-play.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course is focused on providing a structured vision of geometrical metrology as the decisional basis for controlling design, manufacture, and function, as well as a basic discipline that can serve as a powerful tool to solve industrial and scientific problems. The quality assurance of measurements is emphasized in the context of the metrological infrastructure at the company, national, and international levels. In addition the metrological challenges associated with the characterization of machine tool performance (machine tool metrology) and manufacturing process signature (process metrology) are addressed to support reliable compensation of machine tools and monitoring of process variables.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
