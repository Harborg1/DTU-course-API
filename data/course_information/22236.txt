        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Forelæsningerne vil fokusere på principperne for immunterapi. Studerende vil få en basal forståelse for immunterapi og deres anvendelse indenfor forskellig sygdomskategorier, såsom kræft, autoimmune sygdomme og inflammatoriske sygdomme.
De studerende vil få kendskab til forskellige former for immunterapi inklusiv checkpoint inhibitorer, celleterapi, cytokiner og antistoffer. Eksempelvis vil de opnå viden om de nyeste tiltag indenfor immunterapi-baseret kræftbehandling med brug af checkpoint inhibitorer i solid kræft og CAR T celler i hæmatologiske maligniteter. 
Under forelæsninger vil vi gennemgå de værktøjer, som bruges til at evaluere effekten af immunterapi og undersøge behandlingernes bivirkninger. Kurset vil give detaljeret viden om patientudvælgelse og præcisionsmedicin med state-of-the-art værktøjer og teknologier. Derudover, vil vi drøfte de personaliserede terapistrategier indenfor kræftbehandling. De studerende vil få kendskab til prækliniske modeller og deres anvendelse til vurdering af klinisk aktivitet.  
Vi vil invitere eksterne forelæsere med specialer indenfor forskellige former for immunterapi.
Endelige bedømmelse: skriftlig eksamen, inklusiv case-diskussioner og multiple choice spørgsmål.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>1-2 artikler (mest review artikler) vil blive udvalgt og udsendt forud for hver lektion. Deltagelse i forelæsningerne er nødvendigt for at tillære fagets pensum. Forelæsninger vil kombineres med multiple choice quiz, og gruppebaserede case-opgaver. I den sidste lektion vil vi lave en opfølgning på alle lektioner, med en Q&amp;A session. Deltagelse i begge opgavesessioner er obligatorisk.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med kurset er at give deltagerne en basal forståelse for immunterapi og dens anvendelsesområder, samt baggrunden for rammerne for de nyeste fremskridt indenfor immunterapi. Specielt fokus vil ligge på teknologisk udvikling og udfordringer, som er afgørende for, at effektive immunterapeutiske strategier kan blive overført til klinisk anvendelse.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The lectures will focus on the principles of immunotherapy. We provide students with the basic knowledge of immunotherapies and their possible applications in cancer, autoimmune diseases and inflammation. Students will be familiarized with classes of immunotherapies including checkpoint inhibitors, cellular immunotherapies, cytokines, and antibodies. For example, we will provide knowledge about the recent advancement in immunotherapy based cancer management using checkpoint inhibitors in solid cancers and CAR T cells in haematological malignancies. During lectures we will discuss the tools for evaluating efficacy of immunotherapies and investigating the side effects of the treatment. The course will provide details on patient selection and precision medicine with state-of-art tools and technologies. Moreover, we will discuss the personalized therapy strategies in cancer. Student will familiarize with pre-clinical models and their use in assessment of clinical activity. 
External speakers specialized in different immunotherapy approaches will be invited.
Final assessment: written exam, including case discussions and multiple-choice.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>1-2 articles (preferable reviews) will be selected for each topic and form the basis of the course. Attendance during lectures is critical to understand the curriculum.  Lectures will be combined with multiple-choice, quizzes and group-based case studies. In the final session we will summarize the lectures and having ‘questions and answers’. Attendance at the two sessions in which the assignments take place is mandatory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to provide the participants with a basic understanding of immunotherapies and their applications in various disease settings and provide the background on the framework of recent advancements in immunotherapy.  Specific focus is given to the technological inventions and challenges, critical for translating efficient immunotherapeutics into standard clinical care.</Course_Objectives>
