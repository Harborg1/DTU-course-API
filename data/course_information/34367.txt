          <Contents>Introduction to intelligent systems problems, task analysis, systems development approaches &amp; architecture elements; applications of intelligent systems in automation, Internet of Things (IoT) and Smart Grid / Energy problems.

Structured knowledge representation (e.g. ontologies) and applications to declarative and logic-based programming methods (e.g. rule-based logic, graph-search,  ... ).
