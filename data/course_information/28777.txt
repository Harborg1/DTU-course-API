          <Contents>The participants are introduced to industrial symbiosis and in particular Kalundborg symbiosis who will provide project ideas for this course. Based on these ideas, the participants will analyze the proposed idea and the data foundation in order to select a specific project and scope it based in the relevant information, participants competences and the time constraint by the end of the first week. 

During the first period of the course, lectures in project management, and order relevant technical topics will be planed.

During the week 2-5 the groups will execute and manage their projects with supervision from the course instructors and relevant corporate stakeholders.

In the final week, the groups will disseminate the results in oral presentations and in a technical report with recommendations concerning future exploitation by the stakeholders.</Contents>
