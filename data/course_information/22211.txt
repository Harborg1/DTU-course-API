          <Contents>Kurset er relevant for alle nye ph.d.-studerende indenfor immunologi. De vil lære at betjene grundlæggende udstyr, der anvendes til udførelse af immunologiske eksperimenter. Dette inkluderer flowcytometre (til analyse og sortering), mikroskoper, 10X Chromium og BD Phapsody samt grundlæggende udstyr, der anvendes i de fleste biokemiske anvendelser (f.eks. Derudover vil de studerende få træning i, hvordan man designer relevante eksperimenter (f.eks. konstruere antistofpaneler i flowcytometri). Undervisningstiden vil blive fastlagt mellem den studerende og underviseren i det relevante emne. Relevant undervisningsmateriale vil blive leveret af underviseren i god tid. Det er underviserens ansvar at sikre, at den studerende modtager tilstrækkelig træning til at kunne betjene det nævnte udstyr selvstændigt og uden opsyn.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is relevant to all new PhD students in immunology. They will learn how to operate basic equipment used to conduct immunological experiments. These include flow cytometers (for analysis and sorting), microscopes, 10X Chromium and BD Phapsody as well as basic equipment used in most biochemical applications (e.g. PCR and electrophoresis). The students will also learn how to work under sterile conditions in a cell lab and how to safely work with biological material and various chemicals. Moreover, students will receive training in how to design relevant experiments (e.g. construct antibody panels in flow cytometry). Teaching time will be determined between the student and the teacher of the relevant subject. Relevant teaching material will be supplied by the teacher in advance. The teacher will be responsible for ensuring that the student receives sufficient training to be able to operate the above machinery independently and without supervision.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The teaching will take place at times pre-determined by the student and teacher. The student will have to prepare in advance. Student will be taught how to operate machinery and equipment relevant in the field of Immunology and how to design and execute experiments using this machinery. Assessment will involve the student self-operating the machinery and equipment.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To learn how to operate machinery and equipment relevant in the field of Immunology and how to design and execute experiments using this machinery.</Course_Objectives>
