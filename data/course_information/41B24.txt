          <Contents>Kurset indeholder læring om brandskadernes udvikling og bygningsbrandlovgivningen. Kurset giver introdution til brandteknisk prøvning og klassifikation.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course includes learning about the development of fire damage and building fire legislation. The course provides an introduction to fire testing and classification. The course also introduces knowledge about thermal processes, the fire properties of building materials and an introduction to fire design of building structures. The course also includes learning about active and passive fire protection, fire risk and fire protection of different building categories. Finally, the course includes an introduction to fire engineering analysis and planning of construction projects, including through the presentation of case studies.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Teaching at DTU and distance learning with guidance, as well as exercises/assignments</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide the students with an understanding of building fire protection technology and its importance for securing human, material and economic values ​​that are part of modern construction - and to give participants such insight into the diverse areas of the profession that they will be able to deal with both detailed fire protection problems and cases in a larger context, be they of a practically oriented nature or within research and development.</Course_Objectives>
