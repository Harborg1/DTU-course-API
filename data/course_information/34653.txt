        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Design of magnetic components. EMC aspects. Filter design. Practical considerations w.r.t.  construction of - and measurement on switch-mode converters. Analysis and measurement of small-signal Transfer function - Loop stability. Slope-compensation. Input- and output impedance.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project and laboratory work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course goal is to provide the students with knowledge about the practical considerations when designing switch mode converters.
