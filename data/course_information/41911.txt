          <Contents>Kurset introducerer tegningsmaterialet og modelgrundlaget som forudsætning for kommunikationsform. Undervejs i kurset udføres gruppe- og individuelle øvelser.</Contents>

At deltagerne udvikler deres rumlige opfattelsesevne af bygninger og opnår forståelse for den visuelle dialog mellem nøgleparter i byggeprojekter.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the drawing material and the model basis as a prerequisite for communication. During the course, analog and digital aids are used to present the idea and function of buildings. Problem-solving techniques and the use of various digital tools are practiced through practical examples. The course introduces a systematic naming and classification of objects. The Building Information Modeling (BIM) concept is included as a central element, where topics cover layer structures, objects, types and best practice for the production of model-linked drawing material. Principle solutions and digital tools for drawing production and building modeling are worked on to create a basis for documentation, data extraction/bills of materials, simulation and project presentation. Group and individual exercises are carried out during the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, individual exercises, and group exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The purpose of the course is to build a basic understanding of processing and digital visual communication of information during the building design process. Civil engineering centred introduction to communication through sketching, modeling and using digital tools. That the participants develop their spatial perception of buildings and gain an understanding of the visual dialogue between key parties in building projects. The course introduces essential analogue and digital working methods and builds a basic understanding of Building Information Modeling (BIM).</Course_Objectives>
