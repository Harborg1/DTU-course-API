        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset fokuserer på at udvikle prototyper af næste generations mobile sundhedsteknologiske (mHealth) applikationer, der inkorporerer indsamling, visualisering og analyse af sundhedsdata indsamlet fra mobile og bærbare enheder. Eleverne arbejder i grupper på 2 personer. Projektet vil fokusere på alle aspekter af en sådan applikation, herunder UX-design og datavisualisering, trådløs kommunikation, dataindsamling, databehandling og softwareudvikling og -arkitektur af den slags systemer. Kurset vil også fokusere på, hvordan man dokumenterer sådan et projekt både i form af softwaredokumentation og en endelig rapport.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og projektarbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Ovenpå en kort introduktion til de grundlæggende værktøjer guider kurset de studerende gennem et praktisk og projektorienterede forløb i design af mobile sundhedsteknologier (mHealth), ved at kombinere metoder indenfor user-experience (UX) design, software arkitektur design, software udvikling på mobiltelefoner, samt validering af simple mHealth applikationer som et "minimum viable product" (MVP).</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Dette kursus arbejde med programmering af mobiltelefoner i Flutter/Dart. Det er derfor en forudsætning at du medbringer en laptop og en mobiltelefon du kan programmere. Bemærk at Android telefoner kan programmeres fra både Windows og Mac PCer, hvorimod at hvis du (kun) ejer en iPhone skal du anvende en Mac PC (Xcode) til programmering.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course focuses on developing prototypes of next-generation mobile health technology (mHealth) applications that incorporate the collection, visualization, and analysis of health data from mobile and wearable devices. Students will work in groups of 2 people. The project will focus on all aspects of such an application, including UX design and data visualization, wireless communication, data collection, data processing, and software engineering and architecture of such types of systems. The course will also focus on how to document such a project, both in software documentation and in a final report.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and project work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Building on a briefly introduced foundation, the course guides students through project-driven hands-on applications of methods to the design of mobile health (mHealth) technology, combining user experience (UX) design, software architecture design, mobile phone development, and validation of a simple mHealth application as a minimum viable product (MVP).</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>This course works with Flutter/Dart programming on Smartphones and it is a prerequisite that you have a laptop and a smartphone you can program. Android phones can be programmed on both Mac and Windows PCs, while an iPhone can ONLY be programmed on a MAC PC. Hence, if you (only) have an iPhone, you should have a Mac PC running Xcode.</Remark>
