          <Course_Objectives>Super-lette konstruktioner, perlekædearmering, sandwichbuer og elementkupler er nye betonkonstruktioner opfundet ved DTU Construct. 
De gør det muligt at give bygninger nye former og at reducere pris og ressource forbrug til under det halve af, hvad man kender fra traditionelle konstruktioner af beton og stål. 
Kurset søger at give deltagerne kompetencer til at forstå, designe og beregne super-lette konstruktioner og deltage i udviklingen af deres detaljer, komponenter og arkitektoniske udtryk.
Man lærer at skabe et optimalt og muligt kraftforløb og designe konstruktionen dertil i stedet for at designe en konstruktion først og finde kraftforløbet bagefter. 
Kurset giver desuden kompetence i at opstille beregningsmodeller og identificere kritiske forhold for et design, hvad der er vigtigt for ingeniørers arbejde i de tidlige faser af et bygningsprojekt.</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>At first, the technology and idea is introduced. Then, the participants define different tasks for their project work. 
It could be development of a prefabricated beam, column, a connection detail, a bridge, a hall, an architectural expression of a building or calculation tools, new combinations of materials etc.

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project work, lectures, tutorials etc.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Course book, papers, reports, and student work on the subject plus common technical literature</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Super-light structures and pearl-chain reinforcement, sandwich arches and element cupolas are new concrete structures invented at DTU Construct. 
They open possibilities for new shapes of building structures and for reducing the price and resource consumption to less than the half of that of usual structures of concrete and steel.
The course aims to give the participants competences to understand, design and calculate super-light structures and participate in the development of their details, components and architectural expressions.
You learn how to create an optimal and possible force distribution and to design a structure for it, instead of first designing a structure and then finding the forces in it.
In addition, the course gives competence in establishing calculation models and identifying critical matters for a design, which is important for engineering work at the early phases of a building project.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is a general competence course at the Master in Architectural Engineering.</Remark>
