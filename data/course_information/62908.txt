        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Psykologi - er det videnskabelige studie af menneskers adfærd og psykiske processer, herunder menneskets måde og evne at reagere og handle på. Psykiske processer er bl.a. baseret på vilje, følelser og tænkning (kognitiv adfærd).

Sociologi - er studiet af samfund, deres strukturer, organisation og betydning for de individer, der indgår i samfundet. På dette kursus tages der udgangspunkt i de sociologiske strukturer, der påvirker implementering af teknologi.

Etik - drejer sig om, hvad vi bør gøre som enkeltindivider og som samfund. Etik er styrende for en del af vores handlinger. Kurset præsenterer forskellige former for etiske overvejelser som sundhedsteknologen møder i praksis.

Kommunikation - Kommunikation kan defineres som den samlede verbale og nonverbale interaktion mellem individer. Kommunikation er bæreren af vores viden og erfaring i mødet med det andet menneske, det kan være patienten, borgeren, sundhedsprofessionelle eller samarbejdspartnere.

Pædagogik - Pædagogik handler i dette kursus om den videnskabelige tilgang i forbindelse med uddannelse og undervisning af mennesker, herunder konsulentroller og redskaber til at målrette undervisning til bestemte målgrupper. Pædagogik handler også om læring, som er patienten eller borgerens egen oplevelse og internalisering af den viden der er formidlet. Pædagogik er også patient- og borgerrettet empowerment. Patientens eller borgerens oplevelse af egen situation og mulighed for at håndtere og agere i egen situation.

Litteratursøgning  - Litteratursøgning handler i dette kursus om færdigheder i at søge-og udvælge videnskabelig litteratur. Endvidere præsenteres metoder, der danner grundlag for litteratursøgninger i forskellige databaser, herunder Pubmed, Cinahl og PsyckINFO. Ligeledes gennemgås kriterier for, hvordan kvaliteten af videnskabelig litteratur vurderes.
Kurset introducerer også til AI i litteratursøgning.</Contents>

For at kunne forstå dette samspil vil kurset tage udgangspunkt i mennesket og de komplicerede forhold, der påvirker den enkelte og grupper af menneskers interaktion med teknologi.           <Contents>Psychology - is the scientific study of human behavior and mental processes, including humans capability to react and act. Mental processes include will, emotions, and thinking (cognitive behavioral).

Sociology - is the study of society, their structures, organization, and importance to the individuals that are part of a society. This course is based on the sociological structures that affect the implementation of technology.

Ethics - is about what we should do as individuals and as a society. Ethics dictate parts of human actions. The course presents different kinds of ethical considerations, which the Healthtechnologists experience in practice.

Communication - can be defined as the total verbal and non-verbal interaction between individuals and between individuals in organizations. Communication is the carrier of our knowledge and experience in the meeting with the other person, it can be the patient, the citizen, health professionals or business partners

Pedagogy - in this course, is a scientific approach to training and education of humans, including consulting roles and tools to target learning to specific target groups. Pedagogy is also about learning, which is the patient's or citizen's own experience and internalization of the knowledge imparted. Pedagogy is also patient- and citizen-oriented empowerment. The patient's or citizen's experience of their own situation and the opportunity to handle and act in their own situation.


Seach of Literature -  this course is about skills in searching and selecting scientific literature. Furthermore, methods are presented that form the basis for seach of  literature in various databases, including Pubmed, Cinahl and PsyckINFO. Criteria of how the quality of scientific literature is reviewed are also assessed.
The course also introduces AI in seach of litterature.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Understanding the interaction between people and technology is fundamental to the BEng engineer in healthcare technology. In order to understand this interaction, the course will be based on the human being and the complicated conditions that affect the individual and the groups of people's interaction with technology. The course is based on these main areas: psychology, sociology, ethics, communication, pedagogy, and seach of literature.

After completing the course, the student will have acquired sufficient knowledge, skills and  competencies to comple projects with dissemination in orginazations or companies.</Course_Objectives>
