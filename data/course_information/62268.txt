          <Contents>Every building- and infrastructure project includes handling soil, and in the course you learn to handle the soil sustainably and in terms of legislation and necessary permits. You also learn to take soil samples and analyze for contamination in water and soil. Besides that, it is important to have knowledge about polluting components and their properties. Furthermore, you will work with design of pollution studies, mapping pollution, doing risk assessment as well as assessing methods for remediation.</Contents>
