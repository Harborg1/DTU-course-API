        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset fokuserer på at give de studerende kompetencer til at designe, udvikle og anvende diskrete, hændelsesbaserede simuleringsmodeller til analyse af problemstillinger inden for operations management. Ud fra en række konkrete eksempler på komplekse planlægningsproblemer diskuteres, hvordan simulering kan bidrage med indsigt i systemets ydeevne under driftsforhold. De studerende lærer at analysere komplekse systemer inden for operations management og opstille relevante nøgletal (Key Performance Indicators). 

De studerende introduceres til det diskrete, hændelsesbaserede simuleringsværktøj AnyLogic gennem en række mindre opgaver og oversigtsforelæsninger i kursets første undervisningsuge. Herefter gennemfører de studerende i de følgende to uger et større case-studie, hvor en konkret opgave løses i grupper af to studerende.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course focuses on teaching the students to design, build and use discrete event-based simulation models to analyse problems within operations management. Based upon a wide range of specific complex planning problems, it will be discussed how simulation models can be used to obtain knowledge on the system's operating performance. The students are taught to analyse complex systems within operations management and to define relevant key performance indicators. 

The students are introduced to a discrete event-based simulation tool, AnyLogic, through small-scale practical assignments and introductory lectures during the first week of the course. Hereafter, during the following two weeks, the students are given a more extensive case study in which a specific simulation problem is solved in groups of two students.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and project work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>- Rossetti (2016): “Simulation modelling and Arena”, selected sections, Downloadable from DTU FindIt. 
- Grigoryev: “AnyLogic in three days”, newest edition
- Course notes (compendium)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Simulation is an important dynamic tool in design and optimization of production and service systems. The purpose of the course is therefore to learn and understand the advantages, possibilities and limitations of simulation. The course will enable the students to design, implement and use discrete event-based simulation models.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The students are encouraged to form groups of two students before the course start. Students not part of a two-person group by the beginning of the course will be assigned to a group to carry out the case study.

Physical presence is required at the written exam.</Remark>
