        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>A strong focus on fluorescence microscopy techniques that may be applied in the life sciences.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises.

Exercises include hands-on exercises (up to 30% of the total in class time), image analysis and journal club-type activity (not part of hands-on).

Hands-on exercises are comprised of:
- 2 rotating exercises about basic methods.
- one image analysis exercise.
- a mini-project in smaller groups on research-grade instruments including sample preparation, imaging, image analysis, interpretation of, e.g., imaging artifacts.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
