        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>To practice working on strategic problem situations within the innovation and business development field, which require a broad perspective as well as the ability to integrate various disciplines, methods, and tools to arrive at a coherent solution proposal. To understand and further develop your role as a consultant, and to practice this role effectively. To gain experience in executing a large, comprehensive, cross-disciplinary project, which is valuable and serves as preparation for the Global Consultancy Project in Semester 3 and the Master Project in Semester 4.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. Lectures  are paralleled by casework, group work and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The first objective of the Consultancy Project is to develop a broad perspective and the ability to integrate multiple disciplines by working on a strategic, cross-functional challenge. For example, this could involve innovating the business model, products, or services, which may include the digitalisation of the business or operating model. 
The second objective of the Consultancy Project is to develop your skills as a consultant. To emphasize this role, the project team is responsible for finalizing and agreeing on the problem statement with the project sponsor and designing the consultancy strategy. 
The third objective of the Consultancy Project is to gain an understanding of group processes and how to manage potential conflicts within the team. This learning is facilitated by a business psychologist.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Participation in the program requires separate admission to the MMT program. The price of the MMT program is DKK 420.000. Please contact DTU Executive MBA.</DTU_Remark>
