        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset er opbygget som et teambaseret og projektorienteret forløb, hvor de studerende arbejder med innovation gennem en iterativ proces fra problemidentifikation til udvikling og test af løsninger. Forløbet indledes med en introduktion til innovation i en ingeniørfaglig kontekst, hvor fokus er på innovation som en lærbar praksis samt udvikling af en vækstorienteret tankegang og etablering af et trygt læringsmiljø.
Herefter introduceres de studerende til designtænkning som metodisk ramme med særligt fokus på empati og problemforståelse. De arbejder med brugerinddragelse, dataindsamling og syntese med henblik på at formulere relevante og meningsfulde problemstillinger. Dette danner grundlag for den efterfølgende fase, hvor der arbejdes systematisk med idéudvikling gennem både divergente og konvergente processer samt anvendelse af strukturerede kreative teknikker.
Kurset adresserer desuden komplekse og åbne problemstillinger (wicked problems), hvor de studerende analyserer samspillet mellem tekniske, menneskelige og samfundsmæssige faktorer ved hjælp af systemtænkning og interessentanalyse. På denne baggrund udvikler de prototyper af deres løsningsforslag, som testes og videreudvikles gennem iterative processer med fokus på brugerfeedback og læring. 
Et gennemgående element i kurset er fokus på teamdynamik og samarbejde, hvor de studerende arbejder med rollefordeling, kommunikation og refleksion over egen og gruppens arbejdsproces. Kurset afsluttes med et samlet projekt, hvor de studerende anvender de opnåede metoder og kompetencer til at udvikle og formidle en løsning på en virkelig problemstilling.</Contents>

Der lægges vægt på at styrke de studerendes evne til kritisk tænkning, kreativ problemløsning og samarbejde, således at de kan indgå kvalificeret i tværfaglige og praksisnære udviklingsprocesser.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is structured as a team-based, project-oriented program in which students work on innovation through an iterative process, from problem identification to the development and testing of solutions. The program begins with an introduction to innovation in an engineering context, focusing on innovation as a learnable practice, the development of a growth mindset, and the establishment of a safe learning environment.
Students are then introduced to design thinking as a methodological framework, with a particular focus on empathy and problem understanding. They work with user involvement, data collection, and synthesis to formulate relevant and meaningful problems. This forms the basis for the subsequent phase, where students systematically work on idea development through both divergent and convergent processes, as well as the application of structured creative techniques.
The course also addresses complex and intractable problems (wicked problems), in which students analyze the interplay between technical, human, and societal factors using systems thinking and stakeholder analysis. Against this backdrop, they develop prototypes of their proposed solutions, which are tested and refined through iterative processes with a focus on user feedback and learning.
A recurring element of the course is the focus on team dynamics and collaboration, where students work on role distribution, communication, and reflection on their own and the group’s work processes. The course concludes with a comprehensive project in which students apply the methods and competencies they have acquired to develop and communicate a solution to a real-world problem.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course consists of a combination of lectures, group projects, workshops, and presentations.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to develop students’ fundamental innovation competencies, with a focus on identifying and addressing complex engineering problems. The course places innovation within an academic and societal context and provides students with a foundation for working systematically and reflectively on solution development throughout the remainder of their program.
Emphasis is placed on strengthening students’ ability to think critically, solve problems creatively, and collaborate, so that they can participate effectively in interdisciplinary and practice-oriented development processes. The course also helps build an awareness of their own role as engineers in relation to users, stakeholders, and society.</Course_Objectives>
