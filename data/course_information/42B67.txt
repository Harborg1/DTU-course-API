        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Change Leadership

This section focuses on the leadership required to navigate and implement strategic change. It goes beyond linear models to address the non-linear and complex realities of organisational change. Key areas of focus include:

•	Strategic change theories integrated with real-world examples.
•	Recognising the role of contextual factors in shaping the process.
•	Internal change factors: Power dynamics, culture, interests, and communication.
•	External change factors: Political, economic, and social influences.

Participants will develop practical insights to:

•	Design and position effective change interventions.
•	Work collaboratively in groups to refine strategies.
•	Present proposals and receive feedback to enhance their approaches.


Organisational Design

This section introduces practical tools and frameworks to:

•	Diagnose organisational challenges and identify areas for improvement.
•	Lead organisational design projects, from understanding problems to developing solutions.
•	Refine design solutions to align with strategic objectives and improve performance.

The organisational design process is structured around the following steps:

1.	Strategy: Define what the organisation aims to achieve.
2.	Performance Assessment: Evaluate whether strategic goals are being met.
3.	Diagnosis: Identify what is working and what needs improvement.
4.	Design: Develop solutions to address identified issues.
5.	Change Plan: Formulate a plan to implement the solutions.
6.	Execution: Carry out the changes effectively.
7.	Success: Achieve the desired outcomes.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies.
