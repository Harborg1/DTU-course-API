        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course treats: Gas- and liquid phase reactions in ideal reactors, including reactor chains and recirculation reactors. Catalytic reactions in combination with physical transport inside catalyst pellets. Multiple reactions and choice of reactor type and process conditions for optimal production. Coupling of the energy and mass balance for reactors.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Pre-recorded lectures, online problem solving, home exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Text book: H.S. Fogler, Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering, Prentice Hall, 2022, 6. edition. Global edition</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the student basic knowledge of the coupling between chemical kinetics and the design of chemical reactors. The student will be able to design and optimize chemical reactors. It is further the purpose to give knowledge of the most important reactors for homogeneous and heterogeneous reactors and their use in the industry.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is offered as part of the online BSc education in technology with emphasis on power-to-X. The course overlaps, but is not equivalent with, 28140 and 28342.</Remark>
