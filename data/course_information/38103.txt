        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset dækker alle de emner, man kan forvente at finde i en moderne investorpitch og forretningsplan. X-Tech afdækker en række emner inden for håndtering af teknologiske risici, produkt/service-systemer, pretotyping og MVP'er, immaterielle rettigheder, value proposition, forretningsmodellering, segmentering- og estimering af marked, samt bæredygtighed og brugen af SDG'er som forretningsmodel i start ups.

Teamet vil desuden få en mentor, som vil hjælpe med forretningsdelen af arbejdet.

Projekterne fordeler sig i forskellige tematiske spor, der er velegnede for alle studieretningerne på tværs af DTU.

Kursets afsluttende aflevering er et business pitch deck og en forretningsplan. Kurset ender med X-Tech finalen, hvor de studerende præsenterer dette pitch deck på 4 minutter sammen med en 2 minutter lang teknologidemonstration (samlet 6 minutter). Dette foregår foran eksaminatorer, investorer samt et større publikum.

Der er en prototypefond til rådighed for alle teams i X-Tech. Der er også en præmiefond til vinderne af X-Tech+.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Venture-creation projektopgave løses i teams af ~6 studerende.

Teamet vil sigte mod at udvikle en ny forretningsmulighed baseret på eks: 

- en social/bæredygtighedsudfordring
- kommercialisering af teknologi/patenter
- udvikling af en ny markedsmulighed</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Obligatorisk læsning: 
Aulet, B. (2013) Disciplined entrepreneurship: 24 steps to a successful startup. John Wiley &amp; Sons.
	
Anbefalet læsning:
Read S., Sarasvathy S., Dew N., Wiltbank R., (2017) - Effectual Entrepreneurship 2nd Edition.
Nielsen, N.H. (2017). Startup Funding.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At skabe et læringsmiljø, hvor de studerende samarbejder tværfagligt for at kommercialisere en teknologi, markedsmulighed eller behov for et nyt produkt/service, der kan danne grundlag for nye virksomheder. Modsat almindelige produktudviklings- og iværksætterikurser, har vi fokus på erhvervskunder (B2B), hvilket udfordrer den studerende til at arbejde med ukendte teknologier og lærer dem at agere bag scenen i nye forretningssektorer.

Kurset er iscenesat som en inkubator og fokus er lagt på aktiv læring i forbindelse med gruppearbejdet. For at styrke læringsprocessen inviterer vi nogle af landets mest prominente X-Tech iværksættere ind som gæstetalere (ofte direktører og stiftere). Gæstetalerne bidrager med at understrege vigtigheden af de metoder og teorier, der undervises i, i kurset.

Det overordnede mål med kurset er at lave en prototype, der demonstrerer teknologien og en forretningsplan, der præsenteres for eksaminator og investorer - med målet om at rejse penge til den videre kommercialisering af teknologien/udvikling af startup'en.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course covers all of the topics contained within a modern investor pitch deck and business plan. X-Tech cover topics related to technology risk mitigation, product/service-systems, pretotyping and MVPs, IPR, financing, value propositions, business modelling, segmentation &amp; market estimation as well as sustainability and the use of SDGs in a startup business model.

Every team will be joined by a business mentor who will help progress from technology to business.

The cases/projects fall into different thematic tracks suitable for all different study lines across DTU.

The final deliverable of the course is the business pitch deck and business plan. The course ends with X-Tech Finals where the students make a 4 minute business pitch plus 2 minute tech demo (total time: 6 minutes) in front of your examiners, investors and an audience.

There is prototype fund available for each team. There is also a prize fund for the winning teams of X-Tech.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Venture creation project assignment, carried out in groups of ~6 students.

The team will aim to develop a new business opportunity based on e.g.:

- a societal/environmental challenge
- commercializing a technology/patent
- developing a new market opportunity</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Mandatory: 
Aulet, B. (2013) Disciplined entrepreneurship: 24 steps to a successful startup. John Wiley &amp; Sons.

Recommended:
Read S., Sarasvathy S., Dew N., Wiltbank R., (2017) - Effectual Entrepreneurship 2nd Edition.
Nielsen, N.H. (2017). Startup Funding.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To create a learning environment where students work in mixed disciplinary teams to turn technology or market opportunity/need into a product/service and a new business venture. Unlike usual product development or entrepreneurship courses, we have a focus on business to business (B2B), taking students out of their comfort zones, pushing them behind the scenes of unfamiliar technologies and business sectors.

The course is set in an incubator format and emphasizes a learning by doing philosophy. To speed up the learning process, we bring in some of the country’s top X-Tech Entrepreneurs, most of them founders and CEOs. Each of the keynotes will help to emphasis the importance of the methods and theory being taught in the course.

The overall course objective is to create a technology demonstrator and a business plan that will be pitched to the examiners (and investors) – with the aim to raise investment and to spinout.</Course_Objectives>
