          <Contents>Introduktion til forskellige konstruktionstypologier og deres statiske virkemåder.

Kursets indhold omfatter både beregningsprincipper, formgivning, materialevalg og mere æstetiske overvejelser, når en større konstruktion skal designes.

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to different structural types and their static behaviour.

The course content includes calculation principles, structural shaping, choice of materials and more aestetic considerations when designing a larger construction. 

The students work with the course topics via several exercises where they:
 - Calculate load carrying capacity of components and joined structures of specific materials.
 - Learn how vertical and horizontal loads are transmitted to the foundation via the load-carrying and stabilising system.
 - Design their own building shapes and choose materials from demands to structural size and material consumption.

The course includes time for a mid-way peer assessment, which is not graded, and purely to increase the level of deep learning.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, assignments, etc.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>In the course you need the books:
- The Structural Basis of Architecture by Bjørn Sandaker et al.
- Teknisk Ståbi, latest version</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives you an overview of different structural design principles and the corresponding static calculations.

When you have completed the course, you will have gained an understanding of the force distribution in specific types of structures, such as beams, columns, frames, trusses, cables, arches, and cupolas, when they are subjected to ordinary loads. You can also check the capacity of particular load-carrying and stabilising structural elements. Furthermore, you will be able to design a larger construction with a holistic approach accounting for the material consumption and the function.</Course_Objectives>
