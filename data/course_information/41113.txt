        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus arbejder videre med den grundlæggende strømningsmekanik og bøldgedynamik som er tilegnet i kursus 41111. De studerende vil få en introduktion til de mest anvendte moduler i DHI MIKE software pakken såsom hydrodynamik (HD), spektrale bølger (SW), boussinesq bølgemodel (BW), sandtransport (ST) og mudder transport (MT). Den sidste halvdel af kurset består af gruppearbejde hvori de studerende selv skal etablere en kompleks model til at løse en realistisk rådgivningsopgave inden for det marine eller kystnære område. Sådanne opgaver kan eksempelvis være at: i) beregne bølge- og strømklimaer med henblik på at bestemme designparametre til for eksempel en offshore vindmøllepark, ii) Spredning af forurening i kystmiljøet, iii)  Sedimenttransport og kystlinjeudvikling i nærheden af en havn.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger samt gruppearbejde i små grupper. I slutningen af kurset præsenteres gruppearbejdet mundtligt af de studerende.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At introducere de studerende til numeriske metoder indenfor hydraulisk modellering. I dette kursus løses en række typiske problemstillinger indenfor kyst og offshore engineering. Løsningerne opnås ved anvendelse af et kommercielt software program (MIKE 21 of DHI).</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>In this course the fundamental hydrodynamics and wave dynamics obtained as part of course 41111 will be applied. The students will recieve an introduction to the most relevant modules in the DHI MIKE software package such as the hydrodynamic (HD), spectral wave (SW), boussinesq wave model (BW), sand transport (ST) and mud transport (MT). During the last half of the course the students will work in groups to establish their own complex models to solve a realistic engineering problem. Such problems could be: i) Flow and wave simulations to provide design conditions for a potential offshore wind turbine farm, ii) Dispersion of contaminants in a coastal environment and, iii) Sediment transport and coastline evolution around a harbour.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and group-work in smaller groups. At the end of the course the students will make an oral presentation of their work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To introduce students to the use of numerical methods in hydraulics. In this course the solution to various coastal and offshore engineering problems will be obtained by using a commercial numerical modelling software (MIKE 21 of DHI). The course will focus on typical applications encountered in marine and coastal environments.</Course_Objectives>
