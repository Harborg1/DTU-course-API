        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>The main goal of this course is to provide participants with tools and frameworks to analyze, evaluate, improve, and create business models that address major sustainability challenges. We will revisit and critique modern sustainability thinking in business, analyze business model innovation through a sustainability lens, and examine how “fundamental” frameworks in strategy and operations can be adapted to build innovative sustainability initiatives. We will then dive deeper into emerging sustainability topics such as Regeneration, Industry Self-Regulation, AI and Society, and Decarbonization. We will engage in various case discussions, simulations, and group exercises throughout the course. Importantly, many of the course’s activities allow participants to apply course concepts to design an innovative business model in the context of their choice. At the end of the course, participants will propose a business model innovation that addresses a social or environmental need (either within an existing company or as a start-up idea).</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is lecture-based combined with group exercises, cases and practical theory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Business models are changing with the major transition of our societies, due to decarbonization, sustainability and digitalization. This course provides participants with a modern toolset to analyze, evaluate, improve, and create business models that address major sustainability challenges.</Course_Objectives>
