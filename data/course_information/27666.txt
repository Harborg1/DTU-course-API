        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Gennem forelæsninger, øvelser og gruppearbejde vil dette intensive 3-ugers program dække grundlæggende teori, anvendelse og analytiske tilgange inden for machine learning i proteinvidenskab. Kurset er struktureret til at give en afbalanceret blanding af teoretisk viden og praktiske færdigheder, hvor det engagerer studerende i både grundlæggende koncepter og avancerede anvendelser. Det er tiltænkt at illustrere de seneste fremskridt, aktuelle udfordringer og virkelige anvendelser af ML-styret proteinvidenskab inden for et bredt spektrum af virkelige anvendelser inden for bioteknologi, biologiske lægemidler med mere.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser og gruppearbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus giver en introduktion til machine learning (ML) inden for proteinengineering og dækker grundlæggende ML-modeller, principper og deres anvendelser i proteindesign. Deltagerne vil opnå praktisk erfaring inden for cloud computing/HPC (high performance computing), dykke ned i generative modeller for protein-design og forstå strukturen og funktionaliteten af protein-sprogmodeller. Dette inkluderer design og engineering af proteiner ved hjælp af avancerede ML metoder. Gennem gruppeprojekter og præsentationer vil studerende demonstrere deres praktiske forståelse og anvendelse af kursusmaterialet.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Through lectures, exercises, and group work, this intensive 3-week program will cover basic theory, application, and analytical approaches within the realm of machine learning in protein science. The course is structured to provide a balanced mix of theoretical knowledge and practical skills, engaging students in both foundational concepts and advanced applications. It is meant to illustrate the latest advances, current challenges, and real-life applications of ML-guided protein science across a diverse array of real-life applications within biotechnology, biotherapeutics, and beyond.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, and group work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This course provides an introduction to machine learning (ML) within the realm of protein engineering, covering fundamental ML models, principles, and their applications in protein design. Participants will gain hands-on experience within cloud computing/HPC (high performance computing), delve into generative models for protein design, and understand the structure and functionality of protein language models. This includes designing and engineering proteins using state-of-the-art ML methods. Through group projects and presentations, students will demonstrate their practical understanding and application of the course material.</Course_Objectives>
