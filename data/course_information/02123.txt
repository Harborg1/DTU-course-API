        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Fagprojektet for Computer Engineering inkluderer praktisk læring gennem projektarbejde i små grupper à 3-4 studerende, der relaterer sig til hardware-/softwareudvikling. Kurset begynder med en oversigt over projektarbejde og udviklingsmetoder inden for computer engineering. Derefter vælger de studerende et projekt. Hver gruppe skal forberede en detaljeret projektspecifikation og en skriftlig beskrivelse af projektet, som gennemgås på hvert trin i processen. Projektspecifikationen og beskrivelserne samles derefter i en rapport, der skal godkendes før starten på implementerings- og testperioden. I løbet af denne periode vil de studerende arbejde med at implementere og teste deres projekter. Udover projektarbejde kan kurset også dække relevant teori i forhold til projektets emner, rapportskrivning, litteratursøgning og tekniske fremlæggelser.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Projektarbejde i grupper af 3-4 studerende. Introduktionsforelæsninger og projektvejledning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At skabe ingeniør- og retningsfaglig identitet indenfor computer engineering. At bruge de fagligheder som er lært indtil nu i et samlet hele til selvstændigt at udforske og fordybe sig i et nyt emne eller en ny problemstilling. At gennemføre et projekt- og gruppearbejde inden for computer engineering, herunder litteratur- og informationssøgning, samt at formidle dette arbejde både skriftligt og mundtligt. At forberede til bachelorprojektet.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The Computer Engineering project includes hands-on learning through project work in small groups of 3-4 students related to hardware/software development. The course begins with an overview of project work and development methods within computer engineering. Students then choose a project. Each group must prepare a detailed project specification and a written description of the project, which is reviewed at each stage of the process. The project specification and descriptions are then compiled into a report that must be approved before the start of the implementation and testing period. During this period, students will work on implementing and testing their projects. In addition to project work, the course may also cover relevant theory in relation to the project topics, report writing, literature searches, and technical presentations.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project work in groups of 3-4 students. Introductory lectures and project supervision.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To create an engineering and professional identity within computer engineering. To use the skills learned so far in a holistic way to independently explore and delve into a new topic or problem. To carry out project and group work within computer engineering, including literature and information research, and to communicate this work both in writing and orally. To prepare for the bachelor's project.</Course_Objectives>
