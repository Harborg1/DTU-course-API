        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer miljømæssig bæredygtighed i en ingeniørfaglig kontekst og kobler tekniske løsninger til påvirkninger af klima, miljø og natur. Det er forankret i absolut bæredygtighed, planetære grænser og FNs verdensmål for bæredygtig udvikling.

De studerende opnår en systemforståelse af centrale miljøudfordringer som klimaforandringer, tab af biodiversitet og påvirkninger af økosystemer samt vand- og havmiljø. Et centralt element er livscyklusvurderinger (LCA), hvor de studerende lærer at kvantificere miljøpåvirkninger på tværs af et produkts livscyklus, herunder dataindsamling, modellering og anvendelse af relevante værktøjer, med fokus på fortolkning og kritisk vurdering.

På denne baggrund arbejder kurset med udvikling af mere bæredygtige ingeniørløsninger gennem tilgange som Design for Sustainability, cirkulær økonomi og anvendelse af digitale teknologier til ressourceeffektivisering. De studerende arbejder med løsningsorienterede metoder, der adresserer brugen af energi, materialer og areal.

Afslutningsvis introducerer kurset virksomheders bæredygtighedsrapportering, så de studerende kan forstå og kritisk vurdere, hvordan miljømæssig performance måles og dokumenteres i praksis.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces environmental sustainability in an engineering context, linking technical solutions to impacts on climate, environment, and natural systems. It is grounded in absolute sustainability, planetary boundaries, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Students gain a systems understanding of key environmental challenges such as climate change, biodiversity loss, and impacts on ecosystems and aquatic environments. A core element is life cycle assessment (LCA), where students learn to quantify environmental impacts across product life cycles, including data collection, modelling, and use of relevant tools, with emphasis on interpretation and critical evaluation.

Building on this, the course focuses on developing more sustainable engineering solutions through approaches such as Design for Sustainability, circular economy, and digital technologies for resource efficiency. Students work with solution-oriented methods that address energy, materials, and land use.

Finally, the course introduces corporate sustainability reporting, enabling students to understand and critically assess how environmental performance is measured and documented in practice.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, case work, smaller exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>-</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide students with an understanding of the impacts of engineering solutions on climate, environment, and nature, including solutions within their own field of study (e.g., new products, processes, buildings, infrastructure, energy supply, chemicals, and food). Through life cycle assessments, students will learn to quantify environmental impacts on climate and nature, including ecosystems, biodiversity, and the aquatic and marine environment. The course is based on a fundamental understanding of planetary boundaries and absolute sustainability. Building on life cycle assessments, students will work with solution development addressing the use of resources, including energy, materials, and land.</Course_Objectives>
