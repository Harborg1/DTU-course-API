        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Emnerne som vil blive dækket på kurset inkluderer:
1. Udviklingen og organiseringen af sekundært lymfoidt væv
2. De mekanismer, som opretholder lymfoid og myeloid celleudvikling i primært lymfoidt væv
3. Udviklingsmæssige og stromale cellekrav for primære og sekundære lymfoide væv 
4. Molekylære stier involveret i lymfoidt væv og celleudvikling
5. Musegenetik og eksperimentelle modeller inklusiv etiske hensyn 
6. Grundlæggende immunologiske teknikker: FACS og mikroskop 
7. Flowcytometriske analyseredskaber
8. Kritisk analyse af videnskabelig litteratur 

Derudover er kurset opbygget, så de studerende udvikler præsentations- og skrivefærdigheder, samt eksperimentelle og analytiske kompetencer.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset består af forelæsninger, gruppeøvelser og individuelle øvelser; f. eks journal clubs og rapportskrivning. På kurset indgår også et stort antal laboratorie- og computerøvelser.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Janeway's Immunobiology (9th edition), Garland Science</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende en detaljeret forståelse af, hvordan immunsystemets celler og organer udvikles, og hvordan de er organiseret anatomisk. En stor del af kurset fokuserer derudover på generelle videnskabelige kompetencer så som litteraturkritik/reviews (dette vil foregå ved hjælp af in-class Journal Clubs), eksperimentel udførelse og design (3 hele dage med eksperimentelt arbejde og en øvelse i eksperimentelt design), dataanalyse og fortolkning (klassedemonstration af analytisk software).</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The topics that will be covered in this course include:
1. The development and organization of secondary lymphoid tissues 
2. The mechanisms that sustain lymphoid and myeloid cell development in primary lymphoid tissues
3. Developmental and stromal cell requirements of primary and secondary lymphoid tissues
4. Molecular pathways involved in lymphoid tissue and cell development
5. Mouse genetics and experimental models including ethics involved
6. Basic immunological techniques: FACS and microscopy
7. Flow cytometric analytical tools
8. Critical analysis of scientific literature

In addition, the course is designed so that the students develop presentation, writing, experimental and analytical skills.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course consists of lectures together with group and individual exercises; e.g. journal clubs and report writing. The course also includes a large number of lab and computer exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Janeway's Immunobiology (9th edition), Garland Science</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide students with a detailed understanding of how cells and organs of the immune system develop and how they are organized anatomically. A large part of the course is additionally dedicated to general scientific competences including literature critiquing/reviewing (this will be done in the form of in-class Journal Clubs), experimental performance and design (3 full days of experimental work and an exercise on experimental design), data analysis and interpretation (in-class demonstration of analytical software).</Course_Objectives>
