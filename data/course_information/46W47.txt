          <Course_Objectives>Kursets fokus er på hybridkraftværker i stor skala. De studerende vil lære om koncepterne bag hybridkraftværker, herunder forståelse af deres komponenter - vindmøller, solceller, batterilagring osv. Kurset vil fokusere på design og driftsmæssige aspekter af hybridkraftværker. De studerende vil være i stand til at forstå koncepterne bag elektrisk design og styring af hybridkraftværker. Kurset vil overveje hybridkraftværkers rolle i at opfylde elnetkrav og levere markedstjenester.</Course_Objectives>

          <Course_Objectives>The focus of the course is on utility-scale hybrid power plants. The students will learn about the concepts of hybrid power plants, including understanding their components - wind turbines, solar PV, battery storage, etc. The course will focus on hybrid power plants' design and operational aspects. The students will be able to grasp the concepts of electrical design and control of hybrid power plants. The course will consider the role of hybrid power plants in meeting grid code requirements and market service provision.</Course_Objectives>
