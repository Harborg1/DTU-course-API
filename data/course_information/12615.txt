        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Terms, definitions and units describing social, economic and environmental sustainability, BIM modeling, Ecological Network analysis, Biodiversity, material flow analysis, industrial ecology
The lecturers expect the student to read teaching materials (articles or other) before teaching, and for the students to have a case-study you would like to deepen. You are expected to use 3 models that would give you social, economy and environmental results on your case-study. You are advised to use course 41936 case-studies for the building topic as a data source for your case-study.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is a blend of lectures, group work on case studies, and application of the different models taught throughout the course. This course requires that students have, or are willing to look for, a case study within the built environment, and you should unsubscribe from course 12615 if you do not have a case study. Advise to retrieve building case-studies from course 41936. The student is expected to have knowledge of LCAs, BIM modeling and an idea of social LCAs and LCCs.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Industrial Ecology and Sustainable Engineering</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to bring several existing sustainability tools together for the student to analyze a case study, giving the student (or sustainability  expert) the time and knowledge on how to navigate the data and sometimes conflicting results. How can we use the existing tools to validate our results and/or take decisions with a full view of trade-offs? This course aims to give you the tools to develop your critical sense.</Course_Objectives>
