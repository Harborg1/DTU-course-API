          <Contents>The course contains lectures on 

• Diffraction and interference of light
• Fiber optics
• Fourier optics 
• Light-emitting diodes (LEDs) and laser diodes (LDs)
• Polarization optics
• Ray optics, Gaussian beams and their propagation in optical systems 
• Coherence, interference and the Michelson interferometer
• Laser and lab safety
• Uncertainty and Error Analysis 

The experimental exercises focus on optics instrumentation and its use for characterization of various relevant optical phenomena, and on the use of optics instrumentation for the evaluation of the performance of optical systems within optics engineering.</Contents>
