        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>HF signaler og kredsløb omfattende lineære forstærkere, blandingstrin (mixere), oscillatorer, detektorer, begrænsere og effektforstærkere. Strukturer for radiosendere og -modtagere. Design af integrerede HF kredsløb. Kredsløbsbegreber som stabilitet, støj, forvrængning, intermodulation og dynamikområde. Systemegenskaber som signal-støjforhold, tærskelværdi og bitfejlhyppighed. HF-kommunikationskredsløb som bruges i Bluetooth, WiFi, Internet of Things (IoT), 5G/6G communications, Connected Cars, radarer, medicinske og biomedicinske instrumenter, og sv. 3 laboratorieøvelser.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, grupperegning, laboratorieøvelser, obligatoriske hjemmeopgaver, obligatorisk designprojekt.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

eller WebApp: https://rftoolbox.dtu.dk/</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At introducere HF-kommunikationskredsløb, herunder metoder til design i såvel diskret som integreret form og endvidere samspillet mellem kredsløbsteknologi og kommunikationssystemernes krav. Deltageren bliver i stand til at konstruere HF kommunikationskredsløb og delsystemer på baggrund af data og specifikationer for komponenter, kredsløb og kommunikationssystemer.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>RF signals and circuits, including linear amplifiers, mixers, oscillators, detectors, limiters, and power amplifiers. Transmitter and receiver structures. Design of RF integrated circuits. Circuit concepts like stability, noise, distortion, intermodulation, and dynamic range. System concepts including signal to noise ratio, thresholds, and bit error rates. Circuits, which are used in Bluetooth, WiFi, Internet of Things (IoT), 5G/6G communications, Connected Cars, radars, medical and biomedical instruments and many others. 3 laboratory exercises.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Class lectures, group tutorials, laboratory exercises, mandatory home assignments, and mandatory design project.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Class notes by Prof. Jens Vidkjær are freely available for download through
RF Toolbox App in App Store: https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/rf-circuit-toolbox/id1142515821 or WebApp: https://rftoolbox.dtu.dk/</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To introduce RF communication circuits, their design concepts in discrete or integrated realizations, and the trade-off between circuit technology and communication systems requirements. The student will be able to design RF communication circuits or subsystems based on component, circuit, and system data and specifications.</Course_Objectives>
