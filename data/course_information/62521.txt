        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I design-build projekt skal de studerende designe og udvikle deres eget koncept for et medicinsk udstyr ud fra patientens behov og markedssituationen. Kurset strækker sig over 13- og 3-ugers perioden. I 13-ugers perioden vil de studerende følge forelæsninger og udvikle deres eget koncept for et medicinsk udstyr. I 3-ugers perioden vil de studerende implementere en prototype af konceptet for det medicinske udstyr samt skrive en rapport, der beskriver konceptet, teorien bag det og »sælger« det.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Projektforløb i 13 ugers perioden, samt i den efterfølgende 3 ugers periode.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>I design-build projekt skal de studerende designe og udvikle deres eget koncept for et medicinsk udstyr ud fra patientens behov og markedssituationen. En studerende, der har opfyldt kursets mål, vil være i stand til at analysere, evaluere og specificere krav til et medicinsk udstyr i overensstemmelse med standarder og regler for udvikling af medicinsk udstyr. Derudover vil de lære at fremstille prototyper og "minimal viable products" samt anvende forskellige brainstormingsteknikker. Endelig vil de studerende arbejde i grupper for at gennemføre projektet og vil være i stand til at planlægge, implementere og drive de tjenester, der kræves for at bringe et medicinsk udstyr på markedet.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>In Design-build project students will design and develop their own medical device concept based on the needs of the patient and the market situation. The course span the 13- and 3-week period. In the 13-week period students will follow lectures and develop their own medical device concept. In the 3-week period students will implement a prototype of the medical device concept alongside writing a report which describes the concept, the theory behind it, and “sells” it.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project work in the 13-weeks period and the 3-weeks period, which follows the 13-week period.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>In Design-build project students will design and develop their own medical device concept based on the needs of the patient and the market situation. A student who has met the objectives of the course will be able to analyse, evaluate, and specify requirements for a medical device following the standards and regulations of medical device development. In addition, they will learn how to make mock-ups, minimal viable products, and apply different brainstorming techniques. Finally, students will work in groups to implement the project and will be able to plan, implement, and operate the services required for having a medical device on the market.</Course_Objectives>
