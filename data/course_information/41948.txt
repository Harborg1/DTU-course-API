        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Udøve integreret design og interdisciplinært design baseret på fasemodellen indenfor byggeri jvf. Danske Arks og FRI's ydelsesbeskrivelse. Bygningsregistrering, konceptualisering, forslagsstillelse, alternative løsninger, optimering af funktion, arkitektonisk udtryk, materialeanvendelse, energiforbrug og indeklimakvalitet, projektering, modtage og give feedback, kommunikation af resultater gennem fysiske/BIM-modeller, prototyper, præsentationer, videoformater og rapporter. 

Dokumentation af designbeslutninger indenfor konstruktion, materialevalg, energi, indeklima og LCA. Projektplanlægning, gruppearbejde med individuelle ansvar og samarbejder med eksterne parter.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Kurset giver dig mulighed for at afprøve din opnåede viden og færdigheder på uddannelsen og mere specifik fra kursus 41947 gennem at gennemføre et bæredygtigsorienteret bygnings-transformtionsprojekt. 

Projektet tager udgangspunkt i et virkeligt case - en eksisterende bygning, som har udtjent sin funktion. Gennem projektarbejdets analyser, evaluering og forslagsstillelse dokumenteres at bygningen har potentialer til at kunne anvendes til et nyt formål fremover og på den måde opfylde dette nye formål med et mindre klimaaftryk til følge end en ny bygning ville medføre.

Når du har fulgt kurset, kan du som bygningsdesigner indgå i en integreret bygningsdesignproces indenfor bæredygtig bygningstransformation - og dermed medvirke til at skabe bæredygtige løsninger i forhold til brugbarhed, holdbarhed og skønhed indenfor det byggede miljø.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Practice integrated design and interdisciplinary design based on the phase model within construction cf. Danske Ark's and FRI's service description. Building registration, conceptualisation, proposal, alternative solutions, optimization of function, architectural expression, use of materials, energy consumption and indoor climate quality, planning, receiving and giving feedback, communication of results through physical/BIM models, prototypes, presentations, video formats and reports.

Documentation of design decisions within construction, material selection, energy, indoor climate and LCA. Project planning, group work with individual responsibilities and cooperation with external parties.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Design Lab with group work and guidance.
Meetings with external business partners in their companies.
Presentations.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives you the opportunity to test your acquired knowledge and skills on the education and more specifically from course 41947 by carrying out a sustainability-oriented building transformation project.

The project is based on a real case - an existing building that has served its purpose. Through the analysis, evaluation and proposal of the project work, it is documented that the building has the potential to be used for a new purpose in the future and thus fulfill this new purpose with a smaller climate footprint as a result than a new building would entail.

When you have followed the course, you as an architectural engineer can participate in an integrated building design process within sustainable building transformation - and thus contribute to creating sustainable solutions in relation to usability, durability and beauty within the built environment.</Course_Objectives>
