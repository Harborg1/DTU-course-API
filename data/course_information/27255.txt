          <Course_Objectives>Kurset fokuserer på eksperimentelle teknikker indenfor bioteknologi-relevante områder af bakteriologi og molekylær mikrobiologi. I kurset vil du arbejde med både nye teknologier i molekylær økologi, syntetisk biologi samt metoder til at udnytte eller kontrollere den bakterielle biodiversitet, der findes i naturen. Kurset har specielt fokus på metoder og strategier til at ændre på sammensætningen eller aktiviteten af naturlige mikrobesamfund (microbiome engineering). 
I løbet af kurset vil du:
- Anvende avancerede værktøjer, modeller og metoder indenfor molekylær mikrobiologi og økologi.

Samlet set har kurset til formål at give dig kompetencer, der giver dig mulighed for at designe, udvikle, bruge eller forbedre bakteriesystemer til bioteknologiske anvendelser indenfor biokontrol, bioproduktion og medicinske scenarier.
I øjeblikket er kurset fokuseret på mikrobielle isolater med biokontrol eller biogødningspotentiale i relation til "microbiome engineering" i landbrugsapplikationer</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>The course is based on active participation in laboratory exercises. This includes practical work in the laboratory, preparation before class, data processing and interpretation, presentation of results in plenum, and writing of report. It also includes obtaining the theoretical background underlying the used methods, approaches and tools from the teaching material and from the scientific literature.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, lab exercises, group work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Lab manuals and scientific papers</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course focus on experimental techniques in biotech-relevant areas of bacteriology and molecular microbiology. In the course, you will work with both novel break-through technologies in molecular ecology, synthetic biology, and methods to utilize or control the bacterial biodiversity found in Nature. The course has as special focus on methods and strategies for controlling the composition and activity of natural microbiomes (microbiome engineering). 
In the course, you will:  
-	Employ advanced tools, models and methods in molecular microbiology and ecology. 
-	Critically evaluate experimental results and hypotheses. 
Overall, the course aims to provide you with competences that allow you to design, develop, use or improve bacterial systems for biotechnological applications within agricultural, marine, medical- and biomanufacturing scenarios.
Currently, the course is focused on microbial isolates with biocontrol or biofertilizer potential in relation to "microbiome engineering" in agricultural applications</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>This is a 13-week experimental course requiring active weekly participation. Typically, more students enroll than there are available places. Therefore, only sign up if you can commit to active participation and are highly motivated by the subject.
The course requires active participation in the exercise sessions. Students with significant absence or lack of engagement in the exercise activities will not receive course approval.</Remark>
