        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Der udføres en række synteser af organiske og uorganiske forbindelser. Simple synteseteknikker anvendes til separation og oprensning af de syntetiserede stoffer ved f.eks. destillation, filtrering, omkrystallisation, tørring og ekstraktion.  De fremstillede stoffer analyseres og karakteriseres ved brug af spektroskopiske (IR) og kromatografiske (GC) metoder. Journalføring. Fortrolighed med praktisk laboratoriearbejde og forståelse af sikkerhed i laboratoriet.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Synthesis of organic and inorganic compounds. Simple synthetic techniques are employed for separation and purification of the synthesised compound, e.g. distillation, filtration, recrystallisation, drying and extraction. Analysis and characterisation of the synthesised compounds by spectroscopic (IR) and chromatographic (GC) methods. Notebook writing. Safety in the laboratory, including familiarity with practical work.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Laboratory course</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Manual for courses 26002 / 26003 / 26004 /26474 by Tue B. Petersen. Available from Polyteknisk Boghandel.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To practice laboratory techniques with particular emphasis on syntheses of organic and inorganic compounds, including separation and purification. To use spectroscopic methods for compound identification. To use kemibrug for assessment and evaluation of the risk factors for the chemicals used. To practice a safe behaviour in the laboratory.</Course_Objectives>
