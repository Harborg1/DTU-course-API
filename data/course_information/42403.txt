          <Course_Objectives>Erhvervene i Danmark digitaliseres i stigende grad, hvilket øger behovet for Operations Management til at sikre en bæredygtig økonomisk udvikling. Virksomheds effiktiviteten for både produktions virksomheder og service virksomheder kan forbedres ved brug af analytiske methoder. Optimering og simulation giver i denne forbindelse mange muligheder for forbedret beslutnings støtte og planlægning i en usikker tid. I kurset undervises der i avancerede optimerings- og simulations metoder til Operations Management til anvendelse i produktions og service virksomheder. Den studerende vil lære at tage analytisk understøttede beslutninger og lede kritiske diskussioner indenfor Operations Management området. Kurset udvikler abstrakt tænkning og abstrakte problemløsnings kompetencer.</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>This course will acquaint students with advanced concepts in operations management, emphasizing the impact of digitalization. Modern planning problems from production and service operations management will be analyzed, modeled, and solved using mathematical programming and simulation. The focus is on increasing effectiveness and efficiency in current business operations. The students will work on case studies, applying the theoretical knowledge to real-world decision-making problems. Critical thinking and abstract problem-solving capabilities are taught throughout the course.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>In the era of digitalization, the importance of operations management is tremendous for sustainable economic growth. The effectiveness and efficiency of various production and service systems can be improved by analytics. Optimization methodologies and simulation techniques offer many opportunities for better decision-making and planning in volatile environments. This course aims to teach advanced operations management problem-solving technologies like modeling, optimization, and simulation with application to production and service operations management. Students should be able to make sound decisions and lead critical discussions in the context of operations management. The course promotes abstract thinking and develops abstract problem-solving competencies.</Course_Objectives>
