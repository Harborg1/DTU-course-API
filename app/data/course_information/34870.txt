        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Analogier mellem mekaniske, akustiske og elektriske systemer. Transducere: standard og MEMS højttalere, telefoner og mikrofoner; teori, opbygning, retningsegenskaber, udstrålingsforhold, måle- og kalibreringsmetoder. Akustisk metrologi. Mikrofon- og højttaleropstillinger.
Øvelserne omhandler måling af mikrofoner og højtalere. Endvidere bliver LTspice-modellering brugt til opgaverne. Kurset indeholder også et projekt om design af højttalersystemer, der dækker modellering, akustiske og elektriske målinger samt subjektive audio lyttetests.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaver, simuleringer, obligatoriske laboratorie-øvelser samt et højttaler projekt.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>L.L. Beranek &amp; T.J. Mellow: Acoustics, Sound Fields and Transducers (2012)
M. Leach: An introduction to electroacoustics and audio amplifiers 4. ed
A. Lenk et al.: Electromechanical Systems in Microtechnology and Mechatronics: Electrical, Mechanical and Acoustic Networks, their Interactions and Applications (2011)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give den studerende viden om audio- og målemikrofoner, højttalerenheder og -bokse samt subjektiv evaluering af audiosystemer.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>E-learning anvendes i form a online lab report hand in.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Analogies between mechanical, acoustical and electrical systems. Transducers: standard and MEMS loudspeakers and microphones; theory, construction, directivity, radiation, measurements and calibration. Acoustic metrology. Microphone and loudspeaker configurations.
The exercises comprise the measurement of microphones and loudspeakers. LTspice modeling is used for the practice problems.
The course also contains a project on loudspeaker system design that covers modeling, acoustic and electrical measurements, and subjective audio listening tests.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, problems, simulations, compulsory laboratory exercises and a loudspeaker project.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

A. Lenk et al.: Electromechanical Systems in Microtechnology and Mechatronics: Electrical, Mechanical and Acoustic Networks, their Interactions and Applications (2011)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the student knowledge about audio and measurement microphones, loudspeaker units and boxes, and subjective evaluation of audio systems.</Course_Objectives>
