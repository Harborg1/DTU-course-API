          <Contents>Du vil blive introduceret til metoder, måleprincipper og udstyr gennem forelæsninger og obligatoriske øvelser i første halvdel af kurset, men vil derefter under vejledning bruge det meste af tiden på at designe og bygge en eksperimentel opstilling, hvor elektriske eller optiske målinger skal indgå. Forud for det eksperimentelle arbejde skal du finde sammen med 2-3 medstuderende og danne en gruppe. Herefter skal du og din gruppe bruge din opstilling til at planlægge og udføre et eksperiment.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>You will be introduced to methods, measurement principles and equipment through lectures and compulsory exercises, but will then under supervision spend most of the time on designing and building an experimental setup where electrical or optical measurements must be included. Before the experimental work, you must get together with 2-3 fellow students and form a group. You and your group must then use your setup to plan and carry out an experiment. You must use the last part of the course to analyze your data and document your experiment in a short and concise report, which will form the basis for the assessment.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and lab exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Electronic course materials will be made available.
Neil Storey, "Electronics, A Systems Approach" will be used as reference book.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>In this course you will learn to measure physical quantities as well as to design experiments in physics. The goal of the course is not to demonstrate specific physical laws, but rather to provide you with experimental and engineering skills to build an experimental setup yourself and design an experiment that can illuminate a physical law, where certain physical quantities are varied while others are measured. In the course, you will primarily use electrical and optical experimental methods.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is a central elective course for students in the bachelor study programme "Engineering Physics", and it is part of all recommended study lines in this programme.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>In order to participate in the exam, your group must have completed all the mandatory exercises and have these exercises approved by demonstrating the exercises to one of the teachers. If you become ill during one of the exercises, this must be reported to teammates and teachers by email, no later than 12:00 on the day of the exercise. For the exercises where you are absent due to illness, you must agree with your teammates that they document the exercise with pictures and notes, so that you can explain the exercise to one of the teachers the following course week.</Remark>
