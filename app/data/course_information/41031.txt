        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Ugentlige forelæsninger om relevante kursusemner efterfulgt af ugentlige opgaver, der gennemgås i klassen. En omfattende produktdesignopgave, inklusiv designforskning, designspecifikationer, idéer, mock-ups, konceptvalg, forfining og endelig visualisering afsluttes. Designprocessen omfatter skitser, gengivelser og tredimensionelle modeller. Processen dokumenteres i en portfolio og afleveres i slutningen af ​​semesteret. Portfolioen vurderes af en ekstern censor.

Foredrag i:
- Industrielt design
- Industrielt design historie
- Avancerede visuelle kommunikationsteknikker
- Tekniske tegninger
- Design briefs og mood boards
- Grundlæggende formteori
- Farve og funktion
- Produktgrafik
- Menneskelige faktorer og ergonomi
- Brugerscenarier</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, workshops, gruppearbejde, individuelle opgaver, præsentationer.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At opnå følgende basiskundskaber og færdigheder i den industrielle designproces:
- forstå og anvende industriel design metodik 
- grundlæggende færdigheder i at udarbejde fysiske 3D‑modeller, mock‑ups og prototyper
- forståelse og færdigheder i syntesen af form, farve, materialer og funktionalitet set i et brugerorienteret produktperspektiv.
- udbygge færdigheder i modelteknikker.
- avancerede præsentationsteknikker.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Weekly lectures on relevant course topics followed by weekly assignments that are reviewed in class. A comprehensive product design assignment including design research, design specifications, ideation, mock-ups, concept selection, refinement and final visualisation is completed. The design process includes sketches, renderings and three-dimensional models. The process is documented in a portfolio and handed in at the end of the semester. The portfolio is evaluated by an external censor.

Lectures in:
- Industrial design
- Industrial design history
- Advanced visual communication techniques
- Technical drawings
- Design briefs and mood boards
- Basic form theory
- Colour and Function
- Product graphics
- Human factors &amp; ergonomics
- User scenarios</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, workshops, group work, individual assignments, presentations.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To achieve a basic knowledge of and skills in the industrial design process:
- understand and use industrial design methodology  
- basic skills in physical 3-D modelling, including mock-ups and prototypes
- knowledge of and skills in the synthesis of form, colour, materials, and functionality
- enhanced model building skills
- advanced presentation techniques</Course_Objectives>
