        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kemien af d- og f-blok-grundstofferne behandles indgående med fokus på periodiske trends, reaktivitet og karakterisering. Dette inkluderer organometallisk kemi og homogen katalyse behandlet fra et uorganisk kemisk perspektiv. Kurset indeholder en betragtelig dimension af læsning videnskabelig litteratur med fokus på kritisk diskussion af publicerede resultater.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelser (i grupper)</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give dybdegående kendskab til: deskriptiv uorganisk kemi af d- og f-blokgrundstoffer og deres vigtigste koordinationsforbindelser, organometallisk kemi, overgangsmetalkemi i relation til fx. homogen katalyse, supramolekylær kemi og metal-organiske netværk. Derudover bliver deltagerne introduceret til standard fysiske og spektroskopiske teknikker til karakterisering af uorganiske og organometalliske forbindelser. Kritisk læsning og analyse af moderne videnskabelig litteratur er en integreret del af kurset.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The chemistry of the d- and f-elements is treated comprehensively with particular focus on periodic trends, reactivity, and characterization. This includes organometallic chemistry that lays the foundation for the homogeneous catalysis seen from the transition metal ion perspective. The course is largely based on discussion of scientific articles with a focus on critical assessment of published results.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises (in groups)</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To give in-depth knowledge of the descriptive inorganic chemistry of the d- and f-block elements and their most important coordination compounds, organometallic chemistry, and transition metal chemistry in relation to e.g. homogeneous catalysis, supramolecular chemistry, and metal-organic frameworks. Furthermore, the participants are introduced to the standard physical and spectroscopic characterization techniques for inorganic and organometallic compounds. Critical reading and analysis of contemporary scientific literature is an integral part of the course.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
