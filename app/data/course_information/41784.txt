        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Teoretisk grundlag for beregning af termiske materialeprocesser: Varmetransportmekanismer. Varmebalancer. Varmeledningsligningen i 1D. Klumpanalyse. Anvendelse på varmebehandling. Beregning af størkningstider i støbning herunder Chvorinovs modullov. Simpel analyse af afkøling af smelte i indløbssystem. Simpel varmetransportanalyse i svejsning. Løsninger af varmeledningsligningen for bevægelige varmekilder.

Kort præsentation af grundlaget for beregning af de mekaniske materialeprocesser: Flydekriterier, flydeloven og deformationshærdning. Kort præsentation af analysemetoder i plastisk formgivning. Anvendelse af beregningsmetoderne på processer som smedning, valsning, flydepresning, dybtrækning og strækformgivning.

Eksempler på moderne produktionsmetoder af tons tunge emner til mikroemner vil blive vist.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Theoretical basis for calculating thermal material processes: Heat transfer mechanisms, Heat balances. The heat conduction equation in 1D. Lumped analysis. Application to heat treatment. Calculation of solidification times in casting, Chvorinov's rule, simple analysis of cooling of melt in a gating system. 
Simple heat transfer analysis in welding. Solutions to the heat conduction equation for moving heat sources.

Short presentation of basis for calculating mechanical material processes: yield criteria, flow rule and strain hardening. Short presentation of methods of metal forming analysis. Application of the calculation methods on processes like forging, rolling, extrusion, deep drawing and stamping.

Examples on modern production methods will be shown of components ranging from tons to micro grams.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to provide the students an understanding of thermodynamics, fluid mechanics and continuum mechanics of thermal and mechanical materials processing. The students are acquainted with fundamental theoretical methods of analysis in order to carry out simple estimates as well as a bit more detailed calculations.</Course_Objectives>
