        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kvantemekanisk beskrivelse af rene overfladers fysiske og kemiske egenskaber, herunder elektroniske og strukturelle/geometriske forhold. Beskrivelse af adsorbater på overflader under hensyn til elektronstruktur, epitaksi, adsorption/​desorptionsmekanismer, kemisorption og dynamik af kemiske og fysiske processer. Beskrivelse af forudsætningen for eksperimentelt at kunne studere disse overflader under veldefinerede forhold, hvilket er Ultra-Højt vakuum (UHV) med basis tryk under 10E-13 bar. Gennemgang af de mest anvendte metoder, som for eksempel x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), Auger electron spectroscopy (AES), ion scattering spectroscopy (ISS), low energy electron diffraction (LEED), electron microscopy (EM) og scanning probe microscopy (AFM og STM). Endvidere gennemgås fundamentelle aspekter af fænomenet heterogen katalyse - specielt i forhold til fremtidens energyforsyning. Ovennævnte emner søges belyst ved en kombination af oversigtsforelæsninger, spændende nye forsknings- og anvendelses-artikler. Rådgivning og  samtaler om  at skrive ansøgninger, formulerer et forskningsprojekt og tilhørende litteratur søgning.  Endelig presentation og forsvar af ansøgning.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, mundtlige fremlæggelser, evaluering af rapport og formulering af ansøgning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Lærebog: "Concepts of Modern Catalysis and Kinetics" af Ib Chorkendorff og Hans Niematverdriet (ISBN: 978352733268-7 OBS 3rd edition). Supplerende noter udleveres som pdf filer.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give den studerende indblik i de fysiske fænomener der udspiller sig på atomart niveau på grænsefladen mellem faste stoffer og gasser/væsker. Dette er et emneområde af fundamental betydning for at forstå teknologisk vigtige processer så som katalyse, adhæsion, tribologi, samt mekaniske og kemiske egenskaber af for eksempel nye nanomaterialer. Kurset giver de studerende et overblik over de mest anvendte metoder og en introduktion til hvorledes disse kan anvendes til at løse problemstillinger inden for overfladefysikken. De studerende vil også få lektioner, i hvorledes man skriver ansøgninger samt få indsigt i innovative aspekter med specielt henblik på at lave opstart-firmaer. Et vigtigt aspekt i dette kursus er at kunne kombinere den opnåede viden inden for energiomsætning med en kritisk  tilgang til litteraturen og kunne formulere de rigtige videnskabelige spørgsmål og dermed skrive en forskningsbevillingsansøgning samt at presenterer og forsvare denne mundligt indenfor et relevant område.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Quantum mechanical description of the physics and chemistry of clean surfaces, including the electronic and structural properties. Description of electron structure of adsorbates of the surface, epitaxy, adsorption/desorption mechanisms, chemisorption and the dynamics of chemical and physical reactions. Description of the prerequisite for experimental studies of these surfaces under well-defined conditions - this is ultra-high vacuum (UHV) with base pressures below 10E-13 bar. The most popular methods, e.g. x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), Auger electron spectroscopy (AES), ion scattering spectroscopy (ISS), low energy electron diffraction (LEED), electron microscopy (EM) and scanning probe microscopy (AFM and STM) will also be described. Furthermore, fundamental aspects of heterogeneous catalysis especially in connection with the future energy production will be analysed. All the above-mentioned methods will be discussed by use of a combination of lectures, exciting new research and application papers. Advice and discussion on how to formulate and write a research application. Further more presentation and defense of proposal.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, oral presentations, evaluation of reports and proposal preparation.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Book: "Concepts of Modern Catalysis and Kinetics" by Ib Chorkendorff and Hans Niematverdriet (ISBN: 978352733268-7 OBS 3rd edition). Additional notes will be submitted as pdf files.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the students knowledge of the atomic-scale physics prevailing at the interface between solid and gas/liquid phases. This is of fundamental importance if you want to understand technologically important processes, e.g. catalysis, adhesion, tribology, and mechanical and chemical properties of new nanomaterials. The course aims at giving the students an overview of the most applied methods and an introduction to how these can be used to study realistic problems within the surface physics area. The students will also be given classes in how to write proposals and gaining insight into innovative aspects especially with the goal of initiating spin-off companies. An important part of this course is to combine the acquired knowledge with phenomena in energy conversion, formulating scientific questions based on critical review of relevant literature and writing a proposal for funding such projects. The proposal shall be evaluated after the student have presented and defended it orally.</Course_Objectives>
