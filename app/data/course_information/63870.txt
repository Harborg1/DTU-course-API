        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I kurset arbejder du i et team, der er tilknyttet en specifik hospitalsafdeling, hvor der på forhånd er indgået aftaler. En central del af kurset består af feltarbejde på afdelingen.
Undervisningen omfatter opsamlinger med introduktion til teori og metode samt oplæg fra praktikere. Kurset afsluttes med et åbent seminar, hvor resultaterne præsenteres for paertnere på hospitalerne. Platformen innocloud.dk anvendes til videndeling og interaktion med afdelingerne

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>'Flipped Classroom' og udstrakt feltarbejde</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset etablerer grundlaget for innovation i hospitalsøkosystemet ved at introducere metodisk kontekstanalyse og afdækning af 'anliggender', 'problemer', 'behov' eller 'udfordringer' (herefter: 'behov'). Gennem feltarbejde på hospitalsafdelinger samt relevant teori bliver du i stand til at analysere konteksten for innovation samt din egen rolle i innovationsprocessen. Du introduceres til metodisk identifikation af behov samt udvikling af det vidensgrundlag, der er nødvendigt for at validere og udvikle behovene og dermed skabe grundlag for innovation og problemløsning i fremtidige projekter, herunder eventuelt speciale.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>In this course, you will work in a team assigned to a specific hospital department, where agreements have been made in advance. Fieldwork within the department is a central part of the course.
The teaching includes sessions introducing theory and methodology, as well as presentations from practitioners. The course concludes with an open seminar where the results are presented to partners form the hospitals. The platform innocloud.dk is applied for knowledgesharing and interaction with the wards. 
Teams have access to supervision, either online or through visits to the department. 
Process reflection and learning reflection—both individually and as a team—are an integral part of the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>'Flipped Classroom' and extensive fieldwork</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course establishes the foundation for innovation in the hospital ecosystem by introducing methodological context analysis and the identification of 'matters,' 'problems,' 'needs,' or 'challenges' (hereafter: 'needs'). Through fieldwork at hospital departments, as well as relevant theory, you will be able to analyze the context of innovation and your own role in the innovation process. Also, you will be introduced to methodologically identification of needs and the development of the knowledge base necessary to validate and refine these needs, thereby laying the groundwork for innovation and problem-solving in future projects, including a master's thesis if applicable.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The stated maximum number of participants depends on the number of pre-recruited cases and cannot be exceeded.</DTU_Remark>
