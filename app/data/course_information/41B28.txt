        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer de grundlæggende beredskabs- og indsatsmæssige design og udfordringer indenfor moderne teknologi, udvalgte byggematerialer, komplekse bygninger mv.  Der er i kurset fokus på særlige beredskabsmæssige udfordringer. Kurset indeholder bl.a. risikoanalyse ved bygge- og beredskabslovmæssige forhold, herunder også svigtscenarier. Endvidere er indeholdt læring om slukningskapacitet for særlige (komplekse) beredskabsindsatser. Også miljøkemi samt metodik til analyse af brandfarlige industrivirksomheder er indeholdt i kurset. Særlige udfordringer ved træbyggei, herunder HRR, "cavity" og spredning er også en del af kurset. Indeholder også læring om indsatsveje ved dybe, komplekse bygninger som fx. parkeringskældre og indsatsveje i forbindelse med udvendige trapper (herunder sikkerhed). Kurset indeholder indsatstaktik omkring særlige komplekse bygninger og installationer, herunder højhuse, BESS-anlæg, solceller og Power-to-x. Endeligt, indeholder det læring om alternative byggematerialer og konstruktionsmetoder, robotisering (i p-huse) samt større brandfarligt oplag (træpiller, gasoplag, genbrugsoplag).</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the basic emergency and response design and challenges within modern technology, selected building materials, complex buildings, etc. The course focuses on special emergency challenges. The course includes, among other things, risk analysis in building and emergency legislation, including failure scenarios. It also includes learning about extinguishing capacity for special (complex) emergency responses. Environmental chemistry and methodology for analyzing flammable industrial companies are also included in the course. Special challenges in wooden construction, including HRR, cavity and spread are also part of the course. The course includes learning about response routes in deep, complex buildings such as underground parking lots and response routes in connection with external stairs (including safety). The course also includes response tactics for special complex buildings and installations, including high-rise buildings, BESS systems, solar cells and Power-to-x. Finally, it includes learning about alternative building materials and construction methods, robotization (in parking garages) and larger flammable storage (wood pellets, gas storage, recycling storage).</Contents>
