        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset indledes med en kort introduktion til teleskoper generelt, efterfulgt af en introduktion til astronomiske observationer ved forskellige bølgelængder med yderligere fokus på højenergiteleskoper. En teoretisk introduktion til røntgenteleskoper, der dækker refleksion og spredning fra spejlede overflader og laboratorieøvelser om produktion og karakterisering af røntgenspejle. Kurset vil også omfatte anvendelse af analytiske og numeriske metoder, der anvendes til dataanalyse og simulering af realistiske astronomiske instrumenters videnskabelige evner.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will start with a short introduction to telescopes in general, followed by an introduction to astronomical observations at different wavelengths, further focusing on high-energy telescopes. A theoretical introduction to X-ray telescopes covering reflection and scattering from mirrored surfaces and laboratory exercises on the production and characterization of X-ray mirrors. The course will also include the application of analytical and numerical methods used for data analysis and simulation of the scientific capabilities of realistic astronomical instruments.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and Exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

Lecture notes and selected articles.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give an in-depth understanding of modern scientific instrumentation for X- and Gamma-Ray Astronomy. This will include both the telescopes and the detector side.</Course_Objectives>
