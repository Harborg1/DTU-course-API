          <Contents>Transitionsteori og Strategic Niche Management som tilgang til systemisk forandring af byggesektoren.

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Transition theory and Strategic Niche Management as an approach to systemic change in the construction sector.
Product development, including stage/gate model and Open Innovation.
Organizational development, including Kotter's 8 step change model.
Pilot and demonstration projects – what can they do and what can't they?
Certification schemes and circular economy as driving forces.
Methods and tools, e.g. transformative projects and MCDA methods.
The students will meet companies with a clear strategy for innovation management as part of a green transition.
The students must relate the course content and learning objectives to a specific project, which must be analyzed in relation to this. The students collaborate on the project in small groups.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures with discussion
Project work in smaller groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The student acquire knowledge and competence to participate in the innovative change processes that are a necessity for the green transition, including: selected theories and models for innovation and change management at both company and sector/societal level; selected models for product development and innovation; methods and tools to promote sustainable construction.</Course_Objectives>
