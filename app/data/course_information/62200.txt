          <Course_Objectives>Emphasis is placed on students' work in exploring AI and relating it to job tasks, work functions, business economics, business potential, and macroeconomics.

A series of videos and online tools will be provided for students to use and familiarize themselves with. The course is not designed as a technical AI course but rather as one that connects the technical understanding of AI’s opportunities and risks with economics and business operations.

Where possible, guest lecturers will be involved to provide up-to-date insights.

The course concludes with a presentation in which student groups present the tasks they have worked on and the expected consequences for business economics, business potential, and macroeconomics.</Course_Objectives>
