          <Contents>Uge 1: Introduktion til operationel kontrol af koordinerede energisystemer og distribuerede kontrolsystemer; konceptudvikling.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsning og opgaver, projekt, eksperiment i laboratoriet</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Energisystemet stoler i stigende grad på mindre, vedvarende energikilder, elektrificerede flexible forbruger og energilagring. Men hvordan arbejder disse ressourcer sammen til at balancere energisystemet? Enhederne koordineres til at virke som én samlet enhed, som f.eks. et microgrid, et energifællesskab eller et hybridkraftværk.

Dette kursus introducerer de påkrævede metoder til at implementere et sådant distribueret system, ved at de studerende viser, hvordan fjernmåling og -kontrol kan kombineres med fejltolerant kommunikation i et koordineret energisystem.

Det primære fokus er på hands-on erfaring med at udvikle et distribueret system ved brug af funktionel repræsentation af enheder, anvendelse af mønstre for distribueret system- og kommunikationsdesign, overvågelse af drift, og test af systemets faktiske performance.
Kursets værktøjskasse inkluderer Linux command line (bash) med ssh tunnel, kommunikationsmønstre og Python programmering. Eksempler brugt i kurset relaterer direkte til aktuelle udfordringer indefor både forskning og industriel anvendelse.

Brug af AI er tilladt til at støtte softwareudvikling - dog ikke til at erstatte forståelsen af design konceptet og forklaring af resultater.

Kurset afsluttes med et projekt der gennemføres på det distribuerede energisystem "SYSLAB", beliggende på Risø Campus.

Nøgleord: Distribuerede systemer, sensornetværk, internet of things, kommunikation, kontrol, fault tolerance</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>Week 1: Introduction to smart energy systems control problems, and distributed control  systems; solution concept design; differences between simulated and actual distributed control system. Introduction to physical laboratory facilities, lab safety and remote control of SYSLAB units; implementing communication in a distributed system; implementing controllers. 
Week 2: Coordination and fault tolerance strategies. Implementation of a control system on a simulated testbed, testing for reproducible outcomes. Deployment and evaluation of distributed controllers.  
Week 3: Control solution development, implementation and experiment execution in SYSLAB (Risø campus). Reporting and presentation of results.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, project with lab-experiment</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Variable renewable energy resources, electrified consumers and energy storage together make up the building blocks of a fully renewabe energy system. But how do these technologies work together to balance the energy system? Effective cooperation requires control algorithms and communication to coordinate and balance power and energy levels continuously, forming a cyber-physical distributed energy system. Distributed energy systems can be formed for different goals, such as e.g. microgrids, which increase, energy community, or hybrid power plant.  

This course introduces the methods required to implement such a distributed system, enabling students to develop and test their own solutions, which include remote sensing and control, combined with fault tolerant communication to build a coordinated energy system.

The main focus of this course is on a hands-on experience in designing and implementing a distributed energy system solution, applying control structures, distributed system fault tolerance and communication patterns, monitoring its operation, and testing its actual performance. 
The course main tools include Python programming, use of communication patterns, as well as Linux command line operations, remote operation via ssh and code collaboration tools. Use of AI tools is permitted and encouraged during the course for programming purposes, but not to replace a the student' overview of the concept design, testing strategies and test result interpretation. 
The final project is implemented on the distributed energy system "SYSLAB", located at Risø Campus.

Keywords: distributed systems, sensor networks, IoT, communication, control, fault tolerance.</Course_Objectives>
