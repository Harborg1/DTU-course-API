          <Contents>Uge 1: Introduktion til operationel kontrol af koordinerede energisystemer og distribuerede kontrolsystemer; konceptudvikling. Forskelle mellem simulerede og faktiske distribuerede kontrolsystemer. Design af kontrolsystemers struktur med henblik på overordnede mål. Laboratoriet. Implementering af styringsalgoritmer og kommunikation.
Uge 2: Strategier for koordinering og fejltolerance. Implementering af et kontrolsystem på en simuleret tvilling af SYSLAB, test for reproducerbare udfald. Udrulning og evaluering af distribuerede kontrolsystemer.
Uge 3: Projektarbejde med design og implementering af et koordineret distribueret kontrolsystem i SYSLAB, samt afvikling af eksperimenter. Gruppen udarbejder og afleverer et struktureret designdokument, der beskriver den udviklede løsning og danner grundlag for den individuelle mundtlige eksamen og de individuelle testrapporter.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og opgaver, projektarbejde, implementering og eksperimenter i laboratoriet.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Energisystemet stoler i stigende grad på mindre, vedvarende energikilder, elektrificerede flexible forbruger og energilagring. Men hvordan arbejder disse ressourcer sammen til at balancere energisystemet? Enhederne koordineres til at virke som én samlet enhed, som f.eks. et microgrid, et energifællesskab eller et hybridkraftværk.

Dette kursus introducerer de påkrævede metoder til at implementere et sådant distribueret system, ved at de studerende viser, hvordan fjernmåling og -kontrol kan kombineres med fejltolerant kommunikation i et koordineret energisystem.

Det primære fokus er på hands-on erfaring med at udvikle et distribueret system ved brug af funktionel repræsentation af enheder, anvendelse af mønstre for distribueret system- og kommunikationsdesign, overvågelse af drift, og test af systemets faktiske performance.
Kursets værktøjskasse inkluderer Linux command line (bash) med ssh tunnel, kommunikationsmønstre og Python programmering. Eksempler brugt i kurset relaterer direkte til aktuelle udfordringer indefor både forskning og industriel anvendelse.

Brug af kunstig intelligens er tilladt til at understøtte softwareudvikling og udarbejdelse af projektmateriale, men må ikke erstatte den studerendes forståelse af designkonceptet, de foretagne designvalg, det implementerede system eller fortolkningen af testresultater.

Kurset afsluttes med et projekt i SYSLAB på Risø Campus, hvor de studerende designer, implementerer og tester et koordineret distribueret energisystem. Projektets designdokument danner grundlag for en individuel mundtlig eksamen og en individuel testrapport.
Nøgleord: Distribuerede systemer, sensornetværk, internet of things, kommunikation, kontrol, fault tolerance</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Week 1: Introduction to smart energy systems control problems, and distributed control  systems; solution concept design; differences between simulated and actual distributed control system. Introduction to physical laboratory facilities, lab safety and remote control of SYSLAB units; implementing communication in a distributed system; implementing controllers. 
Week 2: Coordination and fault tolerance strategies. Implementation of a control system on a simulated twin of SYSLAB, testing for reproducible outcomes. Deployment and evaluation of distributed controllers.   
Week 3: Project work involving the design and implementation of a coordinated distributed control system, and its testing in SYSLAB. The group prepares and submits a structured design document describing the developed solution and providing the basis for the individual oral examination and the individual test reports.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises, project work, implementation and laboratory experiments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Variable renewable energy resources, electrified consumers and energy storage together make up the building blocks of a fully renewabe energy system. But how do these technologies work together to balance the energy system? Effective cooperation requires control algorithms and communication to coordinate and balance power and energy levels continuously, forming a cyber-physical distributed energy system. Distributed energy systems can be formed for different goals, such as e.g. microgrids, which increase, energy community, or hybrid power plant.  

This course introduces the methods required to implement such a distributed system, enabling students to develop and test their own solutions, which include remote sensing and control, combined with fault tolerant communication to build a coordinated energy system.

The main focus of this course is on a hands-on experience in designing and implementing a distributed energy system solution, applying control structures, distributed system fault tolerance and communication patterns, monitoring its operation, and testing its actual performance. 
The course main tools include Python programming, use of communication patterns, as well as Linux command line operations, remote operation via ssh and code collaboration tools. 

The use of artificial intelligence is permitted to support software development and the preparation of project material, but it must not replace the student’s understanding of the design concept, the design choices made, the implemented system or the interpretation of test results.

The course concludes with a project in SYSLAB at Risø Campus, in which the students design, implement and test a coordinated distributed energy system. The project design document provides the basis for an individual oral examination and an individual test report.

Keywords: distributed systems, sensor networks, IoT, communication, control, fault tolerance.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The use of artificial intelligence is permitted during the course in accordance with the rules communicated at the start of the course.

After the first eight course days, a mandatory individual multiple-choice test is conducted. The test is assessed as pass/fail and is a prerequisite for admission to the final examination.</Remark>
