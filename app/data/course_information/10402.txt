        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Forskning i fusionsenergi har til formål at realisere drømmen om en bæredygtig, storskala energikilde, der kontinuerligt kan bidrage til verdens energiforsyning – og som dermed bidrager til FNs Sustainable Development Goal #7, Affordable and clean energy.
Fusionsenergiforskningen forudsætter en stor bredde af videnskabelige og tekniskvidenskabelige discipliner, og det er kursets formål at de studerende får et overblik over hvordan de forskellige discipliner spiller sammen mod det overordnede mål. De studerende vil også få mulighed for at afdække hvordan deres specifikke interesser potentielt kan spille ind i forhold til udviklingen af fusionsenergi.
Kurset vil indledningsvis sætte fusionsforskningen i perspektiv og vil derefter behandle en række emner inden for plasmafysik, kernefysik, energisystemer, fusionsdiagnostikker, materialer til fusionsmaskiner, nuværende fusionseksperimenter osv. Anden del af kurset vil være et gruppebaseret selvstudie af et selvvalgt emne med relation til fusionsforskningen. Denne del afsluttes med en postersession, hvor emnet formidles til de øvrige kursusdeltagere.
På den sidste del af kurset præsenteres redskabet interessentanalyse, som derefter anvendes på en række stakeholders (også fra en virksomhed) i relation til fusionsenergi.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, gæsteforelæsninger, regneøvelser, gruppearbejde, litteraturstudie, postersession</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Det overordnede kursusmål er at kunne sætte fusionsenergiforskningen i kontekst – både videnskabeligt, teknisk videnskabeligt og samfundsmæssigt. Du vil opnå et overblik over hvordan en række discipliner skal spille sammen for at udvikle et fusionskraftværk.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Fusion energy research aims at realising the dream of a sustainable, large scale source of energy that can continually contribute to the energy supply of the world. This would contribute to the UN Sustainable Development Goal #7, Affordable and clean energy.
Fusion energy research encompasses a wide range of scientific, technical, and engineering disciplines. The course aims at providing the students with an overview of the interplay between these disciplines and the importance for reaching the overall goal. The course will also allow the students to discover how their specific scientific/engineering interests potentially could contribute to the development of fusion energy.
Initially, the course will put fusion energy research in perspective, followed by an introduction of a series of topics as plasma physics, nuclear physics, energy systems, fusion diagnostics, materials for fusion reactors, status of current fusion experiments etc. The second element of the course is a group-based literature self-study of an elective topic with relation to fusion research. The group work is concluded by a poster session, where the specific topics are presented to the other course participants.
At the last part of the course, the tool stakeholder management is presented and used on stakeholders and cases in relation to fusion energy.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, guest lectures, problem sessions, group work, literature study, poster session</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to be able to see the context of fusion energy research – both scientifically and societal. You will be able to see how a range of scientific and engineering disciplines must contribute in correspondence to develop a fusion power plant.</Course_Objectives>
