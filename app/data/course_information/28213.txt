          <Contents>Klassifikation af polymerer, struktur og molvægtsfordelinger.  Statistisk beskrivelse af polymere molekylers konfigurationer.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Classification, structure and molar mass distribution of polymers. Statistical mechanics of polymer conformations. Viscoelasticity, time-temperature superposition. Rubbers and gels. Polymer degradation, additives blends, nanocomposites.</Contents>
