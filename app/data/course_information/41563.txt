        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Matematisk modellering, analyse, syntese og konstruktion af fluid power systemer med hovedvægt på oliehydrauliske systemer.  Integration i mekatroniske systemer, digital data processering, systemintegration og bevægelsesstyring. Numerisk simulering.

Statiske og dynamiske egenskaber vedr. hydrauliske pumper, motorer, ventiler, flowstyring, trykstyring, retningsstyring og servostyring.
Principielle hydrauliske systemer. Hydrauliske drev og aktuatorer. Loadsensing systemer. Hydrauliske servomekanismer. Stationære og mobile hydrostatiske transmissioner. Hydrauliske pumpestationer og akkumulatorer.

Praktiske eksempler på hydraulisk styrede maskinsystemer.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, projektarbejde, computersimuleringer og eksperimenter</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At sætte den studerende i stand til at analysere og bruge matematisk modellering til at vurdere og konstruere hydrauliske komponenter og systemer, integrere dem med strukturer og maskiner, designe regulatorer og give et helhedsindtryk af det mekatroniske system.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Mathematical modelling, analysis, synthesis and design of fluid power systems, in particular oil hydraulic systems. Integration in mechatronic systems, digital data processing, system integration and motion control. Numerical simulation. 

Static and dynamic properties of hydraulic pumps, motors, valves, flow control, pressure control, direction control and servo control. 
Basic hydraulic systems. Hydraulic drives and actuators. Load sensing systems. Hydraulic servo mechanisms. Stationary and mobile hydrostatic transmissions. Hydraulic power supply and accumulators. 

Practical examples of hydraulically controlled systems.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, project work, computer simulation and experiments</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To enable the student to analyse and use mathematical modelling to evaluate and design hydraulic components and systems, integrate them with structures and machines, design feedback controllers, and build a holistic view of the mechatronic system.</Course_Objectives>
