        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Værdiskabelse: Innovation værdiskabelse for stakeholdere, aktionærer og samfund, forskellen mellem værdiskabelse og værdifangst.

Konkurrencestrategi: analyse af branchestruktur, Porters Five Forces, teknologiens S-kurve samt fordele ved at være first mover frem for late mover på farma- og bioteknologimarkeder.

Immaterielle rettigheder: typer af IP-beskyttelse, patenterbarhedskrav i farma og biotek, patentets livscyklus, freedom-to-operate og strategiske valg mellem patent og forretningshemmelighed.

Strategisk spilteori: strategisk interaktion, Nash-ligevægt, sekventielle spil, patentkapløb, pay-for-delay-aftaler og koordination i IP-intensive industrier.

Prisstrategi: værdibaseret prissætning, betalingsvillighed, markedssegmentering og farma-specifikke prissætningstilgange, herunder sekventiel skimming og adgangsprissætning.

Markedsstørrelse: TAM/SAM/SOM-rammen, epidemiologibaseret estimering og omsætningsprognoser for farmaceutiske produkter.

Finansiel værdiansættelse: nutidsværdi, discounted cash flow-analyse, værdiansættelse af lægemiddelprojekter og en introduktion til kommercialiseringsaftalestrukturer, herunder licensering og spin-out.

Gruppeprojekt: De studerende arbejder i grupper om at udvikle en implementeringsplan for en udvalgt farma- eller bioteknologisk innovation eller opdagelse.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser, gruppearbejde og multiple-choice quizzer (MCQ) baseret på kursets overordnede læringsmål.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset giver de studerende kompetencer til at omsætte videnskabelig innovation til strategisk og kommerciel værdi i industrien.

Selvom kurset er forankret i farma- og bioteknologisektoren, er det åbent for alle studerende, der ønsker at forstå, hvordan forretningsmæssig værdi skabes på baggrund af videnskabelige eller opfindelsesmæssige opdagelser.

De studerende lærer at vurdere de strategiske, markedsmæssige, immaterielle rettighedsmæssige og finansielle dimensioner af en ny opdagelse eller idé samt at udvikle en struktureret plan for dens implementering og kommercialisering.

Kurset er særligt relevant for studerende, der overvejer en karriere i krydsfeltet mellem videnskab og erhvervsliv, hvad enten det drejer sig om en kommercielt orienteret rolle, etablering af en start-up eller anvendelse af forretningsforståelse i forskning.</Course_Objectives>

Studerende uden en case vil blive tilknyttet et eksisterende gruppeprojekt eller tage udgangspunkt i et case-studie fra industrien.</Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Value Creation: stakeholder, shareholder and societal value impact of innovation, value creation versus value capture.

Competitive strategy: industry structure analysis, Porter’s Five Forces, the technology S-curve, and first-mover versus late-mover advantages in pharma and biotech markets.

Intellectual property rights: types of IP protection, patentability requirements in pharma and biotech, patent lifecycle, freedom to operate, and strategic choices between patenting and trade secrecy.

Game theory: strategic interaction, Nash equilibrium, sequential games, patent races, pay-for-delay agreements, and coordination in IP-intensive industries.

Pricing strategy: value-based pricing, willingness to pay, market segmentation, and pharma-specific pricing approaches, including sequential skimming and access pricing.

Market sizing: TAM/SAM/SOM framework, epidemiology-based sizing, and revenue forecasting for pharmaceutical products.

Financial valuation: net present value, discounted cash flow analysis, drug asset valuation, and an introduction to deal structures for commercialization, including licensing and spin-out.

Group project: students work in groups to develop an implementation plan for a selected pharma or biotechnology innovation or discovery.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, group work, and multiple-choice quizzes (MCQ) based on the general course learning objectives.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course equips students with the competencies needed to translate scientific innovation into strategic and commercial value in industry. 

While the course is grounded in the pharma and biotechnology sector, it is open to all students who want to understand how business value can be created from scientific discoveries and inventions. 

Students learn to assess the strategic, market, intellectual property, and financial dimensions of a new discovery or idea, and to develop a structured plan for its implementation and commercialization. 

The course is particularly relevant for students considering a career at the intersection of science and business, whether being in a commercially-oriented role, as a start-up founder, or applying business thinking in research.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Students are encouraged (but not required) to bring a case with commercial potential to use as the basis for the group project.
This can be a master thesis or focus project, a new invention, a start-up idea, or any project with commercial potential from any bio-related field.
Students without a case will be assigned to an existing group project or will use a real-life case study as a starting point.</Remark>
