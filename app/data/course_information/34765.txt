        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus dækker nøgleaspekter af digital styring af lineære systemer samt grundlæggende teoretiske begreber som differensligninger og Z-transformen. Det udforsker forskellige diskretiseringsmetoder, deres forskelle, og hvordan de bør anvendes. Kurset omhandler også valg af samplingsperiode og dens indvirkning på systemets ydeevne. Derudover fokuserer det på design af diskret-tids regulatorer, herunder konvertering af kontinuerlige regulatorer til digital form samt praktisk implementering af digitale regulatorer på computere. Kurset består af 10 forelæsninger og 3 sessioner til det afsluttende projekt. Det praktiske projekt vil inkludere programmering i C++ (Arduino stil)</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, gruppearbejde (Matlab/Simulink) og øvelses-/projektarbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Selected Chapters from "Digital Control of Dynamic Systems (Third Edition, by Franklin, Powell, and Workman, available online)".</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>De studerende vil opnå en forståelse af sammenhæng mellem systemer i kontinuert tid og diskrete systemer, samt grundlæggende design af digitale regulatorer for dynamiske systemer.
Kurset dækker Z-transformation, konsekvenser af diskrediteringsmetoder, valg af samplingsfrekvens og effekt af kvantificering. Desuden indgår implementering, test, fejlfinding og performance evaluering af digitale regulatorer.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course covers key aspects of digital control of linear systems and fundamental theoretical concepts like difference equations and the Z-transform. It explores different discretization methods, their distinctions, and how they should be applied. The course also addresses sampling period selection and its impact on system performance. Additionally, it delves into the design of discrete-time controllers, including the conversion of continuous-time controllers into digital form and the practical implementation of digital controllers on computers. The course includes 10 lectures and 3 sessions for final project. The practical project will include programming in C++ (Arduino style).</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, group work (Matlab/Simulink) and exercise / project work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Selected Chapters from "Digital Control of Dynamic Systems (Third Edition, by Franklin, Powell, and Workman, available online)".</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Students will develop an understanding of the relationship between continuous-time and discrete-time systems, learning fundamentals of digital control design for dynamic systems. The course covers Z-transform, various discretization methods and their effects. Students will also learn to select appropriate sampling periods, evaluate quantization effects, and implement, test, and debug digital controllers to assess their performance.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course is based on a previous course 34747 and had been tested in Fall 2024 as a special course.</DTU_Remark>
