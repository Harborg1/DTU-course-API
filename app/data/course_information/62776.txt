          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus giver en omfattende introduktion til vekselstrøms- (AC) elsystemer. Det dækker de grundlæggende principper for AC-kredsløb, herunder spænding, strøm, impedans og effektforhold.           <Course_Objectives>This course provides a comprehensive introduction to alternating current (AC) power systems. It covers the fundamental principles of AC circuits, including voltage, current, impedance, and power relationships. Students will learn power analysis techniques and how to model key electrical components such as transformers, generators, transmission lines, and the fundamentals of three-phase motors. Emphasis is placed on developing both a strong theoretical foundation and practical analytical skills relevant to modern power systems.</Course_Objectives>
