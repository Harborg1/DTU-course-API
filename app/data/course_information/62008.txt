          <Course_Objectives>Ordet produktudvikling dækker i denne fagbeskrivelse fysiske, virtuelle, interaktions- eller servicebaserede frembringelser med en fokus om fysiske tekniske produkter.

At den studerende opnår kendskab til en produktudviklingsproces med de delprocesser en produktudviklingsproces indeholder. 
At den studerende opnår forståelse for vigtighed og relevans af de enkelte delprocessers placering og indflydelse på den samlede produktudviklingsprocess. 

At den studerende forstår koblingen mellem de forskellige fagområder, der indgår i produktudviklingsprocessen, således at den studerende senere vil kunne påtage sig en projektlederrolle i et givent produktudviklingsprojekt.</Course_Objectives>

          <Course_Objectives>In this course, product development describes physical, virtual, interaction or service-based creations with the focus on physical technical/engineered products.

It’s the objective with the course that the student obtains knowledge of the product development process and its sub-processes. 
The student should also gain an understanding of the importance and relevance of each sub process, their location and impact on the overall product development process.

The student should also understand the link between the different disciplines involved in the product development process, so that the student later on can work as a project manager within the field of product development.</Course_Objectives>
