        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Massebalancer, kemiske ligevægte, karbonatsystemet, redoxprocesser (vandsystemer, jordsystemer), ionbytning, sorption, reaktor hydraulik, strømning i porøse medier, proceskinetik, proceshydraulik faseovergange- og ligevægte i et to fasesystem (fx vand, luft) eller tre fasesystem (jord, vand,luft). Jords egenskaber omtales i relation til miljømæssige problemstillinger. Der lægges vægt på tilegnelse af teorien gennem de praktisk orienterede aktiviteter: laboratorieøvelserne og projektarbejdet. Desuden lægges der vægt på formidling af resultaterne fra øvelserne og projektet gennem veldisponerede rapporter.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaveregning, 2 laboratorieøvelser og projektopgave (temaopgave) i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Som basis tekstbog anvender kurset "Environmental Engineering Science" af WW Nazaroff and A Alvarez-Cohen, som kan købes i Polyteknisk boghandel. Andre materialer vil blive uploaded på DTU Learn.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kursusmålet er at:
Opnå en kvantitativ forståelse for vigtige fysiske, kemiske og mikrobiologiske processer, der bestemmer:
- Stofkoncentrationer i omgivelserne/naturen
- Stofkoncentrationer og masser i tekniske anlæg/reaktorer
Lægge grundlaget for videregående specialicerede fag, bl.a. jord- og grundvandsforurening, vandforurening,og spildevandsrensning.
Illustrere hvordan ingeniøren arbejder med praktiske problemstillinger.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset er et vigtigt forudsætningsfag for mange af kurserne i uddannelsen i Miljøteknologi, og bør som konsekvens heraf tages tidligt i studiet.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Mass balances, chemical equilibria, acids and bases,  carbonate chemistry, chemical precipitation, complex formation, phase distributions in two- and three-phase systems (water, solid gas),redox systems, ion exchange, sorption. Reactor hydraulics, process kinetics,   flow in porous systems. Emphasis is put on learning the theoretical principles through practice-oriented activities: project work and lab. exercises. In addition, emphasis is put on communication of results in the form of well-structured reports.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, calculation, exercises, 2 laboratory exercises and project work in groups.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The basic textbook for the course is "Environmental Engineering Science" of WW Nazaroff and A Alvarez-Cohen, which can be purchased at the DTU book store. Additional teaching materials (lecture materials, assignments, practice presentations, additional compendia etc.) will be available at DTU Learn.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to:
Get a quantitative understanding of important physical, chemical, and microbiological processes that control:
- Concentrations of compounds in the environment
- Concentrations and loads of compounds in environmental engineering reactors/technical installations
Create the foundation for more advanced courses within water technology and water pollution
Illustrate how the engineer works with practical issues.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is a prerequisite for the majority of the courses in the BSc and MSc Environmental Engineering. As a consequence, it should be taken early during the studies.</Remark>
