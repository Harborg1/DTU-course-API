          <Contents>Introduktion til store røntgen- og neutronfaciliteter som synkrotroner og spallationskilder. Principperne for røntgen- og neutronspredning. Spredning fra bløde og hårde materialer. Småvinkelspredning. Enkrystal- og pulverdiffraktion. Uelastisk spredning. Imaging inkl. strukturel MRI skanning og dataanalyse.           <Course_Literature>Undervisningsmaterialet bestående af forelæsningsnoter, artikler og udvalgte lærebogskapitler vil være tilgængeligt online.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To af verdens kraftigste mikroskoper er placeret i Lund; det ene er røntgenfaciliteten MAX IV, som netop er ved at blive taget i brug, det andet er neutronfaciliteten European Spallation Source (ESS), som indvies i 2026. Det overordnede mål med kurset er at blive bekendt med denne type af avancerede mikroskopteknikker, og med hvordan man benytter og får adgang til denne type af stor-skala internationale faciliteter. Disse bruges bredt af både universiteter og industri til avancerede analyser af materialer og komponenter. Vi vil beskæftige os med røntgen- og neutronspredning og imaging på både bløde og hårde materialer, der anvendes i biologi, kemi, fysik og relaterede materiale-, energi-, biomedicin- og life science-teknologier. Vi vil arbejde med de grundlæggende  metoder som småvinkelspredning, enkrystal- og pulverdiffraktion, uelastisk spredning og imaging.  Vi vil studere multi-modal og multi-skala 3D mikroskopimetoder ved at kombinere Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) og røntgenmikroskopi. Kurset er tværdisciplinært og har deltagere fra adskillige danske universiteter, så der er rig mulighed for netværksdannelse.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Du vil lære at anvende stor-skala røntgen- og neutronmikroskoper. Tværfagligt kursus rettet mod den ikke-erfarne. Inkluderer røntgeneksperimenter i DTU's 3D Imaging Center og hos DTU Energi og MRI forsøg på Hvidovre Hospital.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to large-scale facilities as synchrotrons and spallation sources for X-ray and neutron production. The principles of X-ray and neutron scattering. Soft and hard materials in relation to scattering. Small-angle scattering. Crystal and powder diffraction. Inelastic scattering. Imaging incl. structural MRI scanning and data analysis.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Full days (8 hours incl. lunch) with alternating lectures, and problem solving sessions, experiments and data analysis.
Safety in the lab is part of the course.
Some introductory material will be posted online one week before the course starts to allow the students to recap some basic concepts that are needed during the course. These  fundamental concepts will also be discussed during the first days of the course.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The teaching material comprising lecture notes, provided articles and selected textbook chapters will be available online.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Two of the world's most powerful microscopes are placed in Lund; one is the X-ray facility MAX IV, which is already in use, and the other is the neutron facility European Spallation Source (ESS) which will start operation in 2026. The overall goal of this course is to get acquainted with this type of advanced microscopy techniques, how you apply these and how you get access to these types of large-scale international facilities. These facilities are widely used by universities and industry for advanced analysis of materials and devices. We will work with X-ray and neutron scattering and imaging on both soft and hard materials for applications in biology, chemistry, physics and related materials, energy, biomedical, and life science technologies. We will work with the basic experimental techniques: small-angle scattering, crystal and powder diffraction, inelastic scattering, and imaging. We will get insights into multi-modal and multi-scale 3D imaging approaches by combining Magnetic Resonance imaging (MRI) and X-ray imaging. The course is highly cross-disciplinary and involves participants from several of the Danish universities, which will give rich opportunities for networking.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>You will learn how to apply large-scale X-ray and neutron microscopes. Cross-disciplinary course for the non-expert. Includes X-ray experiments in DTU's 3D Imaging Center and at DTU Energy, and MRI experiments at Hvidovre Hospital.</DTU_Remark>
