        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Problem solving is a fundamental capability for leaders responsible for navigating complex decisions in uncertain and dynamic business environments. This course provides a structured approach to identifying, analyzing, and addressing problems where the appropriate solution is not immediately clear. It emphasizes both the formulation of the right problem and the careful selection of methods and tools for solving it.

Throughout the DTU Executive MBA program, participants are introduced to a wide range of frameworks and tools drawn from multiple disciplines. This course helps consolidate that knowledge and trains participants to apply, adapt, and combine these tools effectively when faced with real-world business challenges.

The course is organized around four key phases (Figure 1): Statement, Structure, Solve, and Present. Participants will work in groups on their own consultancy projects and use them as live cases for applying each approach. Through lectures, exercises, case examples, and presentations, they will learn how to define and structure problems, collect and analyze data, and develop well-supported, feasible recommendations. Attention is also given to communicating findings effectively, adapting both content and style to different audiences.

Three complementary approaches are introduced and practiced:
•	Constructivist, which emphasizes creativity and contextual adaptation
•	Hypothesis-driven, which relies on structured logic and testable assumptions; and
•	Issue-driven, which breaks problems into logically distinct areas of inquiry.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The purpose of the module is to equip participants with the knowledge and skills to define, analyze, and solve complex business problems in a structured and evidence-based manner. Through the application of problem-formulation tools, management frameworks, and data analysis techniques, participants will learn to develop actionable solutions, craft clear and persuasive communications, and design realistic implementation plans tailored to diverse organizational contexts.</Course_Objectives>
