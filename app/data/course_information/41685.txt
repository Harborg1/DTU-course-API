        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Forelæsninger i konceptet mikrostruktur og mekanismerne bag interaktionen mellem bølger og partikler med strukturen i faststof. Derudover effekten af eksterne og interne mekaniske spændinger på materialer i relation til testning af egenskaber og ydeevne. Principperne af forskellige karakteriseringsteknikker (mikroskopi, diffraktion og spektroskopi), heriblandt storskala faciliteter, behandles i relation til materialevidenskab og -teknologi. Kurset består af forelæsninger, øvelser, demonstrationer og laboratorieøvelser med adskillige karakteriserings- og testningsmetoder.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser, demonstrationer, laboratorieøvelser</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Introduktion til karakteriserings- og testningsmetoder der kan give svar på materialerelaterede spørgsmål: Hvordan ser det ud? Hvordan er det bygget op? Hvad er sammensætningen? Hvad kan det holde til?</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>B.Sc. studerende i General Engineering har fortrinsret.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Lectures on the concept of microstructure and the mechanisms of the interaction of waves and  particles with the structure of solids as well as the response of materials to external and internal mechanical stress in relation to testing properties and performance. The principles of different characterization techniques (microscopy, diffraction and spectroscopy), including large-scale facilities, and mechanical testing methods will be treated in relation to their application in materials science and engineering. The course consists of lecture classes, exercises, demonstrations and hands-on experience of several of the characterization and testing methods.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, demonstrations, laboratory exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>An introduction to characterization and testing methods that can provide answers to the materials-related questions: What does it look like? How is it build up? What is the composition? What can it stand?</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>B.Sc. students in General Engineering are given priority.</Remark>
