        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>• Mikrobiel fysiologi og biokemi i fermenteringsprocesser
• Bioreaktordesign og procestilstandsovervågning
• Toksicitets- og fermenteringsinhibitorer
• Abiotiske stressfaktorer
• Produktspecifik stress (for eksempel enzymer, proteinlægemidler, biobrændstoffer, bulkkemikalier, og sekundære metabolitter)
• Regulering af metabolisme
• Engineering af metaboliske netværk
• Udfordringer i processkalering (op/ned) og overvågning
• De studerende arbejder i grupper med teoretiske øvelser (baseret på eksperimentelle data fra gæringer) og giver kvalificeret feedback til bidrag fra andre grupper.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaveregning, gruppearbejde</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Reviews, originale videnskabelige artikler og/eller udvalgte lærebogskapitler.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende viden og en grundlæggende forståelse af de effekter, som forskellige typer bioprocesopsætninger har på mikroorganismers ydeevne under biobaseret industriel produktion, med fokus på flere niveauer – fra molekylærbiologi over metaboliske pathways til bioproces- og reaktorniveau.
Efter dette kursus vil de studerende være i stand til at udføre en detaljeret analyse af de forventede effekter af en bioprocesopsætning på produktionsorganismen, forudsige metaboliske og fysiologiske belastninger samt foreslå strategier til optimering af bioreaktionsprocesser ud fra et mikrobiologisk perspektiv.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>• Microbial physiology and biochemistry in fermentation processes
• Bioreactor designs and process monitoring
• Toxicity and fermentation inhibitors
• Abiotic stresses
• Product specific stress (e.g. enzymes, protein therapeutics, biofuels, bulk chemicals, and secondary metabolites) 
• Regulation of metabolism
• Engineering of metabolic networks
• Challenges in process scaling (up/down) and monitoring 
• The students will work in groups with theoretical exercises (based on experimental data from fermentations) and will provide qualified feedback to the contributions of other groups.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, problem solving, group work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Reviews, original scientific literature, and/or selected textbook chapters.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide the students with knowledge and fundamental understanding of the effects that the different types of bioprocess setups have on the microbial performance during bio-based industrial production, thereby targeting different fields, ranging from the molecular biology perspective, over metabolic pathways, to bioprocess and reactor-level considerations. 
After this course, the students will be able to perform a detailed analysis of the expected effects of a bioprocess setup on the production organism, predict metabolic and physiological burdens, and propose strategies to optimize bioreaction processes from a microbial perspective.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is mainly offered for MSc Biotechnology and MSc Chemical and Biochemical Engineering students.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>• This course is mainly offered for MSc Biotechnology and MSc Chemical and Biochemical Engineering students.

• Students taking this course may also be interested in course 28455 Process adaptation in Fermentation Based Biomanufacturing (http://kurser.dtu.dk/course/28455) (autumn)</Remark>
