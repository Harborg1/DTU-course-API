        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus tager udgangspunkt i mikroøkonomi for at analysere energisektoren, energimarkedernes adfærd og den nødvendige regulering. Kurset giver en generel introduktion til energisektoren på både nationalt og internationalt niveau. Hovedfokus ligger på energiforsyning og -efterspørgsel, prisdannelse med detaljerede analyser af forskellige typer energimarkeder (f.eks. el-spotmarkeder, CO₂-certifikater) samt energipolitik.

Derudover omfatter kurset markedsdesign, markedsinteraktioner og potentielle markedsimperfektioner, som analyseres og diskuteres under hensyntagen til miljøafgifter og regulering. Politiske foranstaltninger til at håndtere disse aspekter under forskellige forhold vil også blive behandlet.

Lærebogskapitler samt relevante videnskabelige artikler og afhandlinger vil blive introduceret og diskuteret.

Kurset anvender en ligestillingspolitik uden diskrimination på baggrund af køn, alder, religiøs overbevisning eller handicap.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med kurset er at give de studerende en grundlæggende forståelse af energisektorens struktur og de dynamiske udfordringer, den står over for nationalt og internationalt. Fokus er på energiøkonomi, energimarkeder, energipolitik, regulering og ressourceforvaltning, med økonomisk teori som det teoretiske fundament. 
Ydermere forventes de studerende at kunne relatere disse emner til bæredygtig udvikling og analysere miljøøkonomiske instrumenter i forhold til forskellige politiske mål og institutionelle rammer.           <Contents>This course takes the approach of microeconomics to analyze the energy sector, how energy markets behave and the necessary regulation of markets.
The course provides a general introduction to the energy sector both at the national and the international level. The main focus will be on energy supply and demand, price formation with deep dives into different types of energy markets (electricity spot markets, CO2 certificate emissions, etc.) and energy policy. It also encompasses market design, market interactions, and potential market imperfections. These subjects will be analyzed and discussed under consideration of environmental taxes and regulation. We discuss policy measures to address these aspects under different conditions.
Textbook chapters, as well as scientific papers and articles dealing with the respective subject, will be introduced and discussed.

The course applies an equality policy with no discrimination of gender, age, religious beliefs or disabilities.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The energy economics course consists of a 4-hour weekly schedule, with 2 hours allocated to lectures in the auditorium and another 2 hours dedicated to exercises and group work. Additionally, students should anticipate further engagement beyond the designated course hours.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Course_Objectives>With the point of departure in economic theory, the purpose is to provide the students with a fundamental understanding of the current situation and the dynamic challenges that lie ahead for the energy sector, both nationally and internationally. This includes understanding energy economics, markets, politics, regulation and resources. The theoretical basis of the course is the economic theories relevant to those topics. 
Furthermore, the students are expected to relate these elements to the term sustainability. The energy policy instruments will be compared in relation to different policy objectives and institutional settings in the energy sector. The students are expected to develop competences in interpreting, explaining, and discussing the challenges of the energy sector, as well as policy and regulation from an economic perspective.</Course_Objectives>
