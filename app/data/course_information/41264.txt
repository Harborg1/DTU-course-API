          <Course_Objectives>Forståelse af fysikken bag skibssejlads i stille vand og en præcis estimering af den modstand, et skib oplever, er afgørende for et pålideligt og optimalt design af både skibets fremdrivningssystem og dets motor. Derudover spiller bestemmelsen af skibsbevægelse i søgang en central rolle for skibets sødygtighed og dets konstruktionsstyrke. Begge områder er fundamentale for sikker og bæredygtig skibsdrift, som i disse år bliver stadig vigtigere i takt med den grønne omstilling i skibsfarten. Kursets overordnede formål er derfor at give de studerende en grundlæggende forståelse af skibsmodstand, fremdrivningssystemer og sødygtighed samt at lære dem at udføre ingeniørmæssige beregninger inden for disse områder, som har direkte anvendelse i både skibsdesign og operation.</Course_Objectives>

          <Course_Objectives>Understanding ship behavior in calm water and accurate estimation of the resistance which is experience by ship, is essential for reliable and optimum design of its propulsion plant including the ship engine. Moreover, determining unsteady ship motions due to ocean waves plays a key role in ship seakeeping properties and its structural response. Both of these subjects are vital for safe and sustainable ship operation, which are becoming increasingly important due to the green transition in maritime transport.  The overall objective of this course is that students learn the fundamental concepts of ship resistance, propulsion and seakeeping, and they learn to conduct engineering calculations for these topics with direct application for ship design and operation.</Course_Objectives>
