        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus introducerer digitale sundhedsteknologier i en bred forstand og dækker emner både indenfor medical informatics – såsom forskellige typer af teknologier samt standarder – såvel som emner indenfor softwarekonstruktion, herunder analyse og design af sundhedsteknologi og programmering i Dart, som er et moderne objekt-orienteret programmeringssprog. Kurset vil fokusere på bruger-fokuseret applikationsudvikling af slutbruger applikationer på mobiltelefoner ("mobile health") og den studerende lærer at bygge en mobil-telefon app i Flutter.

Kurset giver de studerende et systematisk grundlag og praktisk erfaring med at designe og programmere simple prototyper, som senere kan udvides til at implementere mere "solide" digitale sundhedsteknologier.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Kurset introducerer den studerende til digitale sundhedsteknologier med det mål at kunne konstruere prototyper af simple digitale sundhedsteknologier. Medical Informatics teori benyttes til at give den studerende et overblik over, og forståelse for, forskellige begreber og typer af digitale sundhedsteknologier. Software Engineering teori anvendes til at give en forståelse for analyse og design af digitale systemer indenfor sundhed. Datalogisk programmeringsteori anvendes til at lære, hvordan digitale sundhedsteknologier kan programmeres til indsamling, transformation, opbevaring, og visualisering af sundhedsdata.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces digital health technology in a broad sense and covers topics in medical informatics, types of medical information systems, and technical standards, as well as topics in software engineering, including analysis and design of medical informatics systems and programming in Dart, which is a modern object-oriented language. The course will focus on user-facing design and development of a mobile health app in Flutter.

The course provides students with a systematic foundation and practical experience in designing and programming digital health prototype designs, which can also later be expanded to implement more "solid" digital health technologies.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>The course introduces the student to digital health technologies with the aim of constructing prototypes of simple health systems. Medical Informatics theory is used for analyzing and understanding different concepts and types of digital health technology. Software engineering theory is used for analyzing, designing, and implementing software systems for the healthcare domain. Computer science programming theory is used to implement digital health software systems for the collection, transformation, storage, and visualization of health data.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>This course works with Flutter/Dart programming on Smartphones and it is a prerequisite that you have a laptop and a smartphone you can program. Android phones can be programmed on both Mac and Windows PCs, while an iPhone can ONLY be programmed on a MAC PC. Hence, if you (only) have an iPhone, you should have a Mac PC running Xcode.</Remark>
