        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset er delt i 2 dele således at alle grupper indledningsvist arbejder med syntese af lineære polymerer ved suspension samt emulsionspolymerisation. Derefter vælges et kort specialiseringsspor, hvor der kan stiftes bekendtskab med gængse teknikker til syntese af polymerer/blokkopolymerer (fx en specifik teknik såsom fri radikal, kontrolleret radikal, ringåbningspolymerisation, enzymatisk polymerisation eller mere polymerteknologiske såsom processering eller formulering af elastomerer). Her kan der også arbejdes med bio-baserede monomerer, netværkspolymerer,  fremstilling og testning af kompositmaterialer og overflademodifikation. 
I alle forløb arbejdes der med oprensning og karakterisering af polymerer. Analyse af de fremstillede polymerer ved SEC, FT-IR, NMR, DSC, TGA og reologi. Strukturbestemmelse baseret på ovenstående teknikker. 
Slutteligt skal de udførte eksperimenter dokumenteres og rapporteres i et videnskabeligt format og præsenteres igennem en postersession.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is divided into two parts. In the first part, all groups work with the synthesis of linear polymers via suspension and emulsion polymerization. In the second part, a short specialization track is chosen, allowing students to gain experience with common techniques for the synthesis of polymers and block copolymers (e.g. free radical polymerization, controlled radical polymerization, ring-opening polymerization, enzymatic polymerization) or more polymer-technology-oriented activities such as processing or formulation of elastomers. The specialization tracks may also include work with bio-based monomers, network polymers, fabrication and testing of composite materials, and surface modification.
In all tracks, the course includes purification and characterization of polymers. The prepared polymers are analysed using SEC, FT‑IR, NMR, DSC, TGA, and rheology. Structural characterization is performed based on the combined results from these techniques.
Finally, the experimental work is documented and reported in a scientific format and presented in a poster session.</Contents>
