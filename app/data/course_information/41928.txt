          <Contents>Kurset introducerer grundlæggende viden om aktuel byggeteknik og færdigheder i at gennemføre ingeniør-designprojekter i løbet af kursets 13-ugers periode. Der afleveres delopgaver både individuelt og i grupper i løbet af semester. Alle kursus elementer anvendes til at foreslå og udforme en mindre bygning i kursets tre-ugers periode. 

Kurset træner bl.a. individuelt arbejde og gruppearbejde, problemformulering, idéudvikling, evaluering, optimering, fremlæggelse, at give og modtage feedback samt rapportskrivning efter et anerkendt format.

En forelæsning i kurset sætter fokus på studerendes læring og samarbejde i grupper. De studerende reflekterer over egen læring og indsats i gruppearbejdet ved brug af kort struktureret spørgeskema i den anden uge af 3-uges periode.</Contents>

Kurset giver forudsætninger for at gennemføre en grundlæggende bygningsdesignproces inden for centrale ingeniørfaglige områder som klimaskærm, tekniske installationer og bærende konstruktioner samt for at indgå i projektarbejde, samarbejde i designteams og formidle designforslag.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces basic knowledge of current construction techniques and skills in carrying out engineering design projects during the course's 13-week period. Assignments are submitted both individually and in groups during the semester. All course elements are used to propose and design a smaller building during the course's three-week period.

The course trains, among other things, individual work and group work, problem formulation, idea development, evaluation, optimization, presentation, giving and receiving feedback, as well as report writing according to a recognized format.

A lecture in the course focuses on student learning and collaboration in groups. Students reflect on their own learning and efforts in group work using a short structured questionnaire in the 2nd week of the 3-week period.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, excursions, literature studies, technical visits, exercises and project work, solved in groups.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The following literature is used on the course:

- Knud Peter Harboe: Funktion, form og konstruktion i arkitekturhistorien
- Bjørn Sandaker: The Structural Basics of Architecture
- Juhani Pallasmaa: The Architecture and the Senses

The literature should first be purchased by agreement in the course with the teacher.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course introduces building design as a field of study, including the stakeholders in the construction industry, professional terminology, and basic methods. It provides insight into contemporary building technology, the functions, systems, and architectural expression of buildings, as well as sustainability and environmental impacts, including life cycle assessment (LCA). The course equips students with the prerequisites to carry out a basic building design process within core engineering domains such as the building envelope, technical installations, and load-bearing structures, and to participate in project work, collaborate in design teams, and communicate design proposals.</Course_Objectives>
