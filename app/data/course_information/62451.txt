        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Objektorienteret udvikling:
- Grundlæggende objektorienterede begreber, herunder indkapsling, arv og polymorfi.
- Klassedesign, ansvarsfordeling og modulær strukturering af software.
- UML-klassediagrammer og deres relation til programimplementering.
- Principper for adskillelse af ansvar (separation of concerns) og grundlæggende arkitektonisk strukturering.
- Refaktoreringsteknikker og forbedring af kodekvalitet og vedligeholdelighed.

Softwaresystemer og samarbejde:
- Iterativ udvikling i samarbejdsbaserede projektforløb.
- Professionelle udviklingspraksisser, herunder versionsstyring og strukturerede arbejdsgange.
- Teststrategier for struktureret og objektorienteret software.
- Dokumentation og begrundelse af designbeslutninger.

Brugercentreret design og evaluering:
- Principper for brugercentreret design.
- Grundlæggende principper for brugbarhed og evalueringsmetoder.
- Integration af evalueringsresultater i iterative udviklingsprocesser.

Softwareøkosystemer:
- Perspektiver på forskellige programmeringssprogs og teknologiers rolle i softwaresystemer.
- Grundlæggende begreber inden for datahåndtering og integration mellem systemkomponenter.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset består af forelæsninger, klasseundervisning, praktiske øvelser og projektbaseret gruppearbejde. Gruppeprojektet forløber parallelt med undervisningen og afsluttes med en struktureret præsentations- og evalueringsproces.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at give de studerende kompetencer inden for struktureret og objektorienteret softwareudvikling i en samarbejdsbaseret projektkontekst. Kurset lægger vægt på design og implementering af modulære softwareløsninger, principper for strukturering og kvalitetsforbedring af software, brugercentreret design og evaluering samt anvendelse af professionelle udviklingspraksisser i teambaserede projekter.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Object-oriented development:
- Fundamental object-oriented concepts, including encapsulation, inheritance, and polymorphism.
- Class design, responsibility assignment, and modular structuring of software.
- UML class diagrams and their relation to program implementation.
- Principles of separation of concerns and basic architectural structuring.
- Refactoring techniques and improvement of code quality and maintainability.

Software systems and collaboration:
- Iterative development in collaborative project settings.
- Professional development practices, including version control and structured workflows.
- Testing strategies for structured and object-oriented software.
- Documentation and justification of design decisions.

User-centered design and evaluation:
- Principles of user-centered design.
- Basic usability principles and evaluation methods.
- Integration of evaluation results into iterative development.

Software ecosystems:
- Perspectives on the role of different programming languages and technologies within software systems.
- Basic concepts of data handling and integration across system components.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course consists of lectures, classroom discussions, practical exercises, and project-based group work. Teaching activities include guided design tasks, collaborative development sessions, and supervised project work with formative feedback. The group project runs in parallel with the course and culminates in a structured presentation and evaluation process.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide students with competencies in structured and object-oriented software development within collaborative project settings. The course emphasizes the design and implementation of modular software solutions, principles of software structuring and quality improvement, user-centered design and evaluation, and the use of professional development practices in team-based projects.</Course_Objectives>
