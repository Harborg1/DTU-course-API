        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Termodynamik anvendt i separationsprocesmodeller: Fugacitet, entalpi og entropi fra en tilstandsligning. Termodynamiske procesbegrænsninger. Termodynamiske modeller for væske- og dampblandinger med henblik på beregning af faseligevægte og tilstandsfunktioner. VLE- og LLE-beregninger for flerkomponentblandinger ved brug af aktivitetskoefficientmodeller, samt tilstandsligninger. Separationsprocesanalyse: Grafiske teknikker, matematisk modellering. Processimulering. Enhedsoperationer forbundet med membranteknologi. Transport af opløsningsmidler og opløste stoffer i trykdrevne membranprocesser. Koncentrationspolarisering. Fælles membranmoduler og hvordan de bruges i forskellige membranprocesser. Gennemsnitlig flux, selektivitet og membranareal for forskellige membranprocesser.</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>Anvende termodynamiske egenskabsmodeller til at analysere og modellere oprensningsprocesser. Anvende systematiske metoder (grafiske, beregningsbaserede) til at modellere og analysere teknisk vigtige separationsprocesser for flerkomponentblandinger. Forstå mekanismer for separation (transport af opløst stof i forskellige semipermeable membrantyper, fasefordeling, overfladeinteraktion).</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Kurset er del af en 4-årige master-uddannelse i Kalundborg.
Kurset gives kun i efterår ulige år</DTU_Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Thermodynamics applied to separation process models: Fugacity, enthalpy and entropy from an Equation of State. Thermodynamic process limitations. Thermodynamic models for liquid- and vapor mixtures with the aim of calculating phase equilibrium and state functions. VLE and LLE calculations for multicomponent mixtures using activity coefficient models, as well as equations of state. Separation process analysis: Graphical techniques, mathematical modelling. Process simulation. Unit operations associated with membrane technology. Solvent and solute transport in pressure driven membrane processes. Concentration polarization. Common membrane modules and how they are used in different membrane processes. Mean flux, selectivity and membrane area for different membrane processes.</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>Apply thermodynamic property models to analyze and model downstream processing systems. Use systematic methods (graphical, short-cut, systems of equations) to model and analyze technically important multicomponent separation processes. Understand mechanisms for separation (solute transport in different semipermeable membrane types, phase distribution, surface interaction).</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is part of a 4-year master's program in Kalundborg.
The course in only given in autumn odd years</DTU_Remark>
