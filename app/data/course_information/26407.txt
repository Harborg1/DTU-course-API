          <Contents>Mere avancerede organiske synteser i forhold til Indledende Kemisk syntese (26002/3/4) og Organisk og uorganisk syntese (26474).

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>More advanced organic syntheses compared to Introductory Chemical Synthesis (26002/3/4) and Organic and Inorganic Synthesis (26474).
Multi-step syntheses including e.g. Grignard reactions, electrophilic aromatic substitution, oxidations, reactions with carbanions, reactions with carbonyl compounds, resolution, carbohydrate synthesis, diazo compounds.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>1 day a week. Laboratory exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To practise basic laboratory techniques, aiming at the synthesis of organic compounds, their purification and analysis using chromatography and spectroscopy. To illustrate and extend the theory in 26400 Organic Chemistry 1 and to some extent 26411 Organic Chemistry 2.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is a prerequisite for bachelor projects in synthetic organic chemistry at DTU Chemistry.
