        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Bygningssimuleringsværktøjer repræsenterer en integreret del af designprocesser for moderne bygninger. De bruges til at estimere ydeevnen af klimaskærm, varme-, køle- og ventilationssystemer og andre relevante komponenter. Desuden bruges de til at vurdere indflydelsen af brugeradfærd og kontrolsystemer. Dette kursus fokuserer på at anvende dynamiske bygningssimuleringer og giver et overblik over muligheder og begrænsninger relateret til deres anvendelse. På kurset anvendes et dynamisk bygningssimuleringsværktøj IDA Indoor Climate and Energy (IDA ICE). Værktøjet muliggør simulering af varmetransport i bygningen samt luftstrømsmodellering. Desuden omfatter IDA ICE et dagslyssimuleringsmodul. Ved hjælp af IDA ICE lærer deltagerne at opsætte en model for klimaskærm, herunder modeller til vægge, vinduer, solafskærmning og naturlig ventilation. Komponenterne, der repræsenterer interne varmekilder, menneske-belastninger (emission af varme og fugt) samt kunstig belysning mv. bruges til at definere bruger aktiviteter og tilhørende termiske og fugtbelastninger. Deltagerne lærer at bruge modeller for varmeelementer (vandradiatorer, fan-coils), køleenheder (klimaanlæg) samt ventilations aggregater og energikilder (kølemaskiner, kedler, varmepumper, fjernvarmecentraler). Kurset introducerer forskellige styrekomponenter (termostat, PI-regulator). Vedrørende analyse af resultater lærer deltagerne at bruge IDA ICEs resultatvisualiseringsmodul bestående af tabelresultater, graffer (tidsserier), ”heat map” graffer og 3D visualiseringer. Udover selve modelopsætningen og -brugen introduceres deltagerne til grundlæggende designprincipper for bæredygtige og højtydende bygninger, der omhandler klimakonteksten, orientering, form og materialer til klimaskærm samt valg af klimaanlæg.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaver og gruppearbejde</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset lærer deltagerne hvordan man bruger dynamisk bygningssimulering til at designe bæredygtige bygninger. Fokus er på hoveddesignparametre som klimaskærm, orientering (placering) af bygningen og klimaet. Yderligere fokuserer kurset på varme-, køle- og ventilationssystemer. Der undervises ikke i grundlæggende principper af varme-, køl- og ventilation. Der arbejdes med en holistisk tilgang til estimering og evaluering af varme-, køl- og ventilations ydeevne. De studerende lærer, hvordan forskellige komponentmodeller i simuleringen fungerer, og hvilke empiriske modeller de er baseret på. De kommer til at udforske konsekvenserne af forskellige designvalg på indeklima, energieffektivitet og bygningens overordnede ydeevne. De studerende lærer at analysere og fortolke simuleringsresultater for at optimere designet. I løbet af en sådan proces skal de tage højde for foruddefinerede krav vedrørende indeklima og energiforbrug.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Building simulations tools are an integral part of design processes for modern buildings. They are used to estimate the performance of building envelope, heating cooling and ventilation systems, and other relevant components and services. Moreover, they are used to assess influence of human behavior and control systems. The present course focuses on applying dynamic building simulations and gives an overview of possibilities and limitations related to their use. In the course, a dynamic building simulation tool IDA Indoor Climate and Energy is used (IDA ICE). The enables simulation of thermal behavior of buildings as well as airflow modelling. Moreover, IDA ICE comprises a daylight simulation module. Using IDA ICE the course participants learn to set up a model for building envelope, including models for walls, windows, solar shading and natural ventilation. The components representing internal heat sources, occupant loads (emission of heat and moisture) as well as artificial lighting etc. are used to define human activities and associated thermal and moisture loads. The participants learn how to use models for heating elements (water radiators, fan-coils), cooling units (air conditioners) as well as air handing systems and energy sources (chillers, boilers, heat pumps, district heating substations). The course introduces use of different control components (thermostat, PI controller). Regarding analysis of results, the participants learn to use IDA ICE’s result visualization module comprising tabular results, graphical time series, heat map plots and 3D visualizations. Besides the actual model set-up and use, the participants are introduced to basic design principles for sustainable and high performance buildings dealing with the climate context, orientation, shape and materials for building envelope as well as choice of climate conditioning systems.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and group work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course teaches the participants how to use dynamic building performance simulation to design sustainable buildings. The focus is on main design parameters like building envelope, building orientation (placement) and climate zone as well as on heating, cooling and ventilation systems. The course does not focus on basic principles related to functionality of heating, cooling and ventilation, but rather on a holistic approach to estimation and assessment of their performance. The students learn how different component models in the simulation work and what empirical models are they based upon. They come to explore the consequences of different design choices on indoor environment, energy efficiency and overall performance of the building. The students learn to analyze and interpret simulation results to optimize the design. During such process, they need to account for predefined performance requirements regarding indoor environment and energy consumption.</Course_Objectives>
