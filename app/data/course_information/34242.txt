          <Course_Objectives>Maskinlæring er ved at blive en uundværlig værktøj for kommunikationsingeniører. Det der kan være udfordrende at identificere en række relevante maskinlæringstekniker til at løse en specifik problemstilling indenfor kommunikationssystemer, da maskinlæringsfeltet er bredt. Formålet med dette kursus er at introducere grundlæggende maskinlæringsteknikker, der kan anvendes til at designe både fiber-optiske og trådløse kommunikationssystemer. Kurset vil give deltagerne kendskab til, hvordan de kan: 1) opbygge datadrevne modeller ud fra input-/outputdata. Dette vil omfatte modeller af forstærkere, transmissionskanaler og transmitter-modtagere, 2) designe for- og efterforstærknings-lineære equalizere baseret på multilags neurale netværk, 3) udføre optimal filtrering og genskabe nyttige oplysninger fra støjende observationer, samt 4) udføre systempræstationsforudsigelse og -optimering af kommunikationssystemer og netværk.</Course_Objectives>

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>3 weeks of lectures and exercises followed where students apply learned machine learning techniques to solve a relevant problem within communication engineering etc. design of equalizer for supression of noise, opticla amplifiers, pulse-shaper and matched filters</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Machine learning is becoming an indispensable tool for communication engineers. However, as the field of machine learning is broad, identifying a set of relevant tools for solving a specific set of problems may be challenging. The objective of this course is to introduce basic machine learning techniques that can be applied for designing fiber-optic and as well as wireless communication systems. The course will provide the participants' with knowledge of how to:  1) build data driven models of from input/output data. This will include models of amplifiers, transmission channels, transmitter-receivers, 2) design pre- and –post nonlinear equalizers based on multi-layer neural-network. 3) optimal filtering and recover useful information from noisy observations and 4) perform system performance prediction and optimization of communication systems and networks.</Course_Objectives>
