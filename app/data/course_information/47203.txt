        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Studerende vil anvende grundlæggende designprincipper, materialevalg, elektronikteknik, programmering og numeriske simuleringer til at designe en soldrevet bøje. De studerende vil arbejde i grupper på seks personer for at designe, bygge og teste en bøje, der er selvdrevet og udfører en miljøovervågningsaktivitet såsom måling af vandtemperatur, havstrøm eller bølgehøjde. Systemmodellering og design er afgørende for at opnå en bøje, der kan modstå havforhold og forblive strømførende i længere perioder. De studerende vil lære, hvordan man går fra et nyt koncept til et prototypedesign.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>3-ugers intensivt design-byggekursus. Studerende arbejder i grupper på seks personer for at designe en soldrevet bøje og bygge en funktionel prototype. Prototypen vil blive skabt ved hjælp af 3D-print og Arduino-mikrocontrollere, og de studerende vil teste deres prototyper i et realistisk eller simuleret miljø. I løbet af kurset vil der være et par introduktionsforelæsninger, mens det meste af kurset er dedikeret til praktiske aktiviteter relateret til design, konstruktion og test af prototypen.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Kurset bruger kun undervisningsmateriale der er tilgængelig online.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Studerende vil lære de generelle principper for projektledelse og gennemgå processen med at designe, skabe og teste en prototype i henhold til et specifikt mål og inden for specificerede begrænsninger. Dette inkluderer brug af numerisk modellering og energisimuleringer for at sikre funktionel drift af bøjen i vand og langsigtet bæredygtig energiproduktion og -forbrug.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Dette kursus handler om design og konstruktion af funktionelle prototyper og vil give en introduktion til solenergiindsamling og måling af vandforhold. De studerende vil få praktisk erfaring ved at designe, bygge og teste prototypen af en solcelledrevet bøje.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Students will apply basic design principles, material selection, electronics engineering, programming, and numerical simulations to design a solar-powered buoy. Students will work in groups of six to design, build, and test a buoy that is self-powered and performs an environmental monitoring activity such as measuring water temperature, ocean current, or wave height. System modeling and design are crucial to achieving a buoy that can withstand ocean conditions and remain powered for extended periods. Students will learn how to go from a novel concept to a prototype design.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is a 3-week intensive design-build course. Students work in groups of six to design a solar-powered buoy and build a functional prototype. The prototype will be created using 3D printing and Arduino microcontrollers, and students will test their prototypes in a realistic or simulated environment. During the course, there will be a few introductory lectures, while most of the course is devoted to hands-on activities related to designing, building, and testing the prototype.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The course only uses teaching materials that are available online.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Students will learn the general principles of project management and go through the process of designing, creating, and testing a prototype according to a specific objective and within specified constraints. This includes utilizing numerical modeling and energy simulations to ensure functional operation of the buoy in water and long-term sustainable energy generation and consumption.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course is about the design and construction of functional prototypes and will give an introduction to solar energy harvesting and water condition measurements. The students will gain hands-on experience by designing, building and testing of the solar powered buoy prototype.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>This course is part of the mandatory study plan for BSc in General Engineering and will build on knowledge gathered from the previous two design-build courses. Only students from BSc in General Engineering can take this course. It is an intensive full-time course over three weeks, where the students' attendance in mandatory activities is required to pass the course and will be monitored.

Please be aware that due to the risk of harmful effects you can NOT follow this course if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. If you have questions regarding the above, please contact the course responsible.</Remark>
