          <Course_Objectives>At få en forståelse principper og strukturen i kvalitetssikring, herunder kvalitetsstyring i regulerede industrier, Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) og farmaceutiske industri. Kurset gennemgår kvalitetssikring i alle led af en virksomhed og, hvordan en ISO certificering opnås samt de fordele og ulemper dette kan give virksomheden. Der skabes en indsigt i hvordan ISO-systemet fungerer i en virksomheds dagligdag og der skabes løbende forbedringer af virksomhedens kvalitetsniveau. Kurset forbereder den studerende til at kunne indgå ISO certificerede virksomheder, komplekse systemer og kraftigt regulerede virksomheder som f.eks. den danske pharma-industri.</Course_Objectives>

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lessons, discussions, papers, exercises to test theories on the case descriptions.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To get an understanding of the principles and structure in Quality Management, with a focus on Quality Assurance in regulated industries, Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and the pharmaceutical sector. The course is about quality management in every area of a company and how to be ISO certified with the pros and cons for the company. Students will gain insight into how the ISO system is working in a company daily business and how to create continuous improvement in company quality. The course will prepare the student for participating in ISO certified companies, complex systems and heavily regulated industries like the pharma industry.</Course_Objectives>
