        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Introduktion til skitseringsteknik, grundlæggende afbildningsregler samt standarder og normer for udførelse af tegninger af mekaniske komponenter. Yderligere vil de studerende blive introduceret til målsætning, herunder angivelse af simple tolerancer og overfladeruhed.

Generel introduktion til grafisk kommunikation, herunder præsentation af resultater i tekniske journaler. Anvendelse af CAD til produktmodellering, fremstilling af tekniske tegninger samt illustrationer og animationer. 

Introduktion til almindeligt anvendte fremstillingsprocesser i industrien. I kurset gennemgås: støbning, formgivning, spåntagningsprocesser, 3D-print, plastprocesser og samleprocesser (herunder svejsning).  Yderligere vil de studerende blive introduceret til at sammensætte enkelte fremstillingsprocesser til en samlet proceskæde.

Som en del af kurset vil de studerende selv udføre produktionen af enkelte emner i værkstedet på DTU Lyngby campus. I værkstedøvelserne vil de studerende forberede en proceskæde til produktionen af et specifikt emne og opstille det nødvendige produktionsudstyr, herunder CAM-systemer. Med udgangspunkt i de fremstillede emner vil de studerende anvende industrielt måleudstyr til kvalitetskontrol.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online undervisning, tegneøvelser, gruppearbejde. Desuden værkstedsøvelser på DTU Lyngby campus</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har som overordnet mål at give de studerende overblik over industrielle fremstillingsprocesser med fokus på samspillet mellem design, materialevalg, præcision og kvalitet. 

De studerende vil få et grundlæggende indblik i mekaniske komponenters funktion, opbygning og fremstilling og bliver i stand til at kommunikere professionelt vha. skitser og tekniske tegninger samt anvende et avanceret CAD-program. De studerende vil desuden lære at overføre 3D CAD-geometrier til CAM-software til at programmere numerisk styrede bearbejdningsmaskiner. 

Yderligere, bliver de studerende i stand til at udpege en eller flere processer, der kan anvendes til at fremstille et emne med givne krav til geometri, funktion og tolerancer. 

De studerende lærer hvordan fremstilling af et konkret produkt forberedes og udføres i praksis, herunder design af emner og værktøjer, samt opstilling og indkøring af produktionsprocesser.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to sketching techniques, basic drawing rules as well as standards and norms for making drawings of mechanical components. Furthermore, the students will be introduced to dimensioning, including indication of simple tolerances and surface roughness.

General introduction to graphic communication, including presentation of results in technical journals. Use of CAD for product modeling, production of technical drawings as well as illustrations and animations.

Introduction to commonly used manufacturing processes in the industry. This course examines: casting, shaping, machining, 3D printing, plastic processes and assembly processes (including welding). Furthermore, the students will be introduced to putting together individual manufacturing processes into an integrated process chain.

As part of the course, the students will carry out the production of individual parts in the workshop on DTU Lyngby campus. In the workshop exercises, the students will learn to prepare the process chain for the production of a specific part and set up the necessary manufacturing equipment, including CAM systems. Based on the manufactured components, the students use industrial measuring equipment for quality control.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online teaching, drawing exercises, group work. Furthermore, workshop exercises on DTU Lyngby campus</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall objective of the course is to give the students an overview of industrial manufacturing processes with a special attention on the interaction between design, material selection, precision and quality.

The students will gain a basic insight into the function, structure and manufacture of mechanical components and will be able to communicate professionally using sketches and technical drawings as well as use an advanced CAD program. The students will also learn to transfer 3D CAD geometries to CAM software to program numerically controlled manufacturing machines.

Furthermore, the students will be able to identify one or more processes that can be used to manufacture a component with given requirements for geometry, function and tolerances. 

The students learn how the production of a specific product is prepared and carried out in practice, including the design of parts and tools, as well as setting up and commissioning of production processes.</Course_Objectives>
