          <Contents>Driftsprincipper for elforsyningsanlæg. Vindenergi og generatortyper.

Frekvens og spænding kontrol og overholdelse netkrav.
Statiske og dynamiske simulering studier med Powerfactory.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>a) Forelæsninger og gruppearbejde

          <Course_Objectives>Kurset introducerer studenten til relevante metoder til kontrol af elforsyningen og giver kendskab til operationel integration af vindkraft.
Dernæst analyseres forskellige elektriske vindmølleteknologier og deres samvirke med elnettet operationelt.
Modellering af vindmøller, load flow samt indvirkning på systemfrekvens og spænding analyseres ved hjælp af simuleringsprogrammet DIgSILENT - Powerfactory.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Power system operation principles. Wind power and wind generator types. 
Modeling of wind turbines and large wind farms.
Impact of wind on power systems stability, particularly on frequency and voltage. 
Frequency and voltage control and compliance with grid codes.  
Static and dynamic simulation studies with Powerfactory.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>a) Lectures and group work
b) Load flow simulations and transient stability problems. Group projects, where integration challenges in term of frequency and voltage stability are investigated by modelling grids with wind power.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>T. Ackermann et al., “Wind Power in Power Systems, Second Edition,” 2012, pp. 1-1049.
available online - https://findit.dtu.dk/en/catalog/54499e30e44c7a86671d2587
and supplementary material</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course provides the students with an overview to relevant control issues of power systems and gives knowledge about operational integration of wind power.
Analysis of the different electrical wind turbines technologies and their interaction with the grid both during transients and steady states.
Modelling of wind turbines, load flows and influence on system frequency and voltages are investigated with the usage of the simulation software DIgSILENT - Powerfactory.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>E-learning is used in the form of on-line quiz (home assignments), interactive quiz and electronic correction system. 
YouTube videos on Mattia's channel support the learning process.
Basic knowledge of Powerfactory is highly recommended - students with no knowledge of the software are strongly advised to take the tutorial before the course begins. 
Pre-course tutorial can be taken by using the PCs in the databars in B325 and B329 (just launch powerfactory, open the help menu and start tutorial).
The course organisation requires an early stage assignment in the first 4 weeks. Non-participation will be considered as drop-out from the course.</Remark>
