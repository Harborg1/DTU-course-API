        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The first part of the course focuses on studying the decomposition of H2O2, which is a central reaction in water treatment. You will combine several experimental techniques and kinetic modelling to understand the reaction mechanism and establish activity descriptors. Based on this, you will devise strategies for the rational design of improved catalysts for this process. 
  The second part will build upon the general concepts learned in the first part. Each team will research a specific catalytic process: the activity descriptors, possible controversies in the literature, and their perspectives on strategies for catalyst design.  
  Interdisciplinary student teams will be formed with special consideration for the required skills/competencies needed for the problem.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, Experiments, Quizzes, Group reports, Poster design and presentation</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
