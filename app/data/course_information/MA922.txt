        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Systems of linear equations, Gaussian elimination, matrix algebra, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, systems of linear differential equations, engineering applications of linear algebra</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, group work with and without guidance of teaching assistants, weekly test in Möbius, and modelling days.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course is a direct continuation of course 01911, Mathematical Analysis and Modelling. The goal is to provide the students with the ability to apply fundamental mathematical concepts and modern computer tools solving classical problems of engineering on a slightly higher taxonomical level than in 01911. Relevant topics from linear algebra are introduced as a methodical basis for treating multi-dimensional problems arising from engineering applications. The scope and form of the course supports application of the modelling circle aiming at the treatment of relevant problems within the different study lines at increasing taxonomical levels. The digital formation and "computational thinking" of the students is further strengthened compared to course 01911 by working with modern computational tools (currently Maple).</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Mandatory course for all students enrolled in the Bachelor of Engineering programme from 2024 and forward
(except students of Fisheries Technology and Mechanical Engineering - digital study programme). The course replaces 01920, Calculus and Algebra 2.</DTU_Remark>
