        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset starter med en generel introduktion til AM, hvilket bliver efterfulgt af en mere dybdegående gennemgang af hver af de tre AM processer.
Teoretisk grundlag for beregninger relateret til ekstruderings-baseret AM: Trigonometri, varmetransportmekanismer, varmebalancer, varmeledningsligningen.
Teoretisk grundlag for beregninger relateret til stereolitografi: Resin arbejdskurver, Lambert-Beers lov, Snell-Descartes lov.
Teoretisk grundlag for beregninger relateret til metal AM: Bethes formel, termiske gradienter og afkølingshastigheder, nukleasionshastigheder, anvendelse på varmebehandling, klumpanalyse, Hall-Petch lignende ligninger, Griffith-Irwins kriterie.
Efterbehandlingsteknologierne der vil blive gennemgået for metal AM inkluderer afspændingsglødning, sintring, laser polering og forskellige overfladebehandlingsoperationer.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og opgaveregning</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har som overordnet mål at bibringe de studerende grundlæggende forståelse for 3D-print (additive manufacturing, AM) teknologier. De studerende gøres fortrolige med grundlæggende teoretiske analysemetoder til beregning af tre forskellige AM processer mhp. at foretage enkle, overslagsmæssige vurderinger såvel som mere detaljerede beregninger. De tre aktuelle AM processer er ekstruderings-baseret AM, stereolitografi og metal AM.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course starts with a general introduction to AM followed by a more in-depth exposition of each of the three AM processes.
Theoretical basis for calculations related to material extrusion AM: trigonometry, heat transfer mechanisms, heat balances, the heat conduction equation.
Theoretical basis for calculations related to stereolithography: resin work curves, Lambert-Beer law, Snell-Descartes law.
Theoretical basis for calculations related to metal AM: Bethe formula, thermal gradients and cooling rates, nucleation rates, applications to heat treatment, lumped analysis, Hall-Petch like equations, Griffith-Irwin criterion.
The post-manufacturing technologies that will be covered for metal AM includes stress relief heat treatment, hot isostatic pressing, laser polishing, and various surface finishing operations.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and problem solving</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to provide the students a basic understanding of 3D printing (additive manufacturing, AM) technologies. The students will learn theoretical methods of analysis in order to carry out simple estimates as well as more detailed calculations relating to three different AM processes: i.e., material extrusion, stereolithography, and metal AM.</Course_Objectives>
