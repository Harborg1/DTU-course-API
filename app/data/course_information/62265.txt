          <Contents>I dette kursus vurderes hvordan man kan udforme vejanlæg i byområder ud fra forskellige trafikanters behov og adfærd samt under hensyntagen til miljøet.

- cyklister
- biler (vejklasser, vejtyper, fremkommelighed, tilgængelighed, trafiksikkerhed, utryghed, barrierer)
Trafik-, sikkerheds-, og miljømæssige konsekvenser af aktiviteter relateret til støjplanlægning, støjforanstaltninger
Trafikteknisk dimensionering: 
- Trafikstrømme, tællinger og analyser, forsinkelse og kølængder, kapacitetsvurdering af vejkryds 
Signalregulering: 
- Funktion og sikkerhed, geometri, bestykning, signalprogrammer, tidsstyring, samordning, trafikstyring.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course assesses how to design road construction in urban areas based on different road users' needs and behavior and taking into account the environment.
Traffic planning for
- cyclists
- cars (road classes, road types, accessibility, availability, road safety, insecurity, barriers)
Traffic, safety and environmental consequences of activities related to noise planning and noise reduction measures
Traffic driven dimensioning:
- Traffic flows, counts and analyses, delays and queues, capacity assessment of crossings
Signal Control:
- Function and security, geometry, equipment, signal programs, time management, coordination, traffic management.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, continuous project assignments. Excursions and exercises in the field.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to give the student a basic understanding of sustainable traffic planning as well as road technology concepts and techniques for the description and design of road traffic facilities in urban areas.</Course_Objectives>
