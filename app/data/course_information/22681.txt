Derudover vil de studerende få træning i præsentationsteknikker, som vil blive opnået ved at læse artikler præsenteret af de andre kursusdeltagere og ved selv at præsentere artikler.  En endelig rapport om et litterært emne skal udarbejdes, bedømmes af en medstuderende på kurset, og afleveres i slutningen af kurset.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course covers a range of subjects in the fields of advanced micro and nanofabrication. Areas are defined in collaboration with the students, and the teacher will ensure broadness in the subjects. The course will include training in literature search as well as citation analysis. The specific content of the course changes from semester to semester depending on the teachers and guests participating in the course and on the interests of the participating students. It is expected that the students prepare themselves for each of the presentations by reading background material.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Journal club sessions, presentation of articles.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The goal of the course is for the students to gain knowledge about topics in advanced micro and nanofabrication and be introduced to critical reading of research literature. Furthermore the students will be trained in presentation techniques, accomplished by reading papers presented by the other participants and by presenting papers. A final report on a literature study is prepared and peer reviewed by another course participant and finally handed in at the end of the course.</Course_Objectives>
