        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Tredimensionelle spændinger og tøjninger. Hovedspændinger/tøjninger og de dertil hørende hovedkoordinatsystemer. Hookes generaliserede lov. Plan spændingstilstand og plan tøjningstilstand. Deformation af tykvæggede rør og cirkulære skiver. Plastiske deformationsmekanismer og flydehypoteser. Dimensionering mod udmattelsesbrud. Der introduceres dimensionering mod varig formændring (plasticitet) samt udmattelsesbrud.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og opgaveregning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At gøre den studerende i stand til at analysere og beregne, hvordan en given struktur dvs. maskinkomponenter, trykbeholdere, krympesamlinger, konstruktioner, rammer, etc. deformerer og hvornår plastisk deformation eller udmattelse indtræffer. Kursets indhold danner en nødvendig forudsætning for benyttelse og forståelse af finite element metoden (FEM), og ikke mindst, evnen til at komme med en kvalificeret bedømmelse og tolkning af resultater opnået gennem FEM beregninger.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is taught in Danish.

Three-dimensional stresses and strains. Principal stresses / strains and the associated principal coordinate systems. Hooke's generalized law. Plane stress and plane strain. Deformation of thick-walled pipes and circular discs. Plastic deformation mechanisms and yield hypotheses. Dimensioning against fatigue fractures. As part of the course, the student is introduced to multidimensional elastic stress and deformation analysis. Dimensioning is introduced against permanent deformation (plasticity) as well as fatigue fractures.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is taught in Danish.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To enable the student to analyze and calculate how a given structure ie. machine components, pressure vessels, shrinkage joints, structures, frames, etc. deform and when plastic deformation or fatigue occurs. The content of the course constitute a necessary prerequisite for the use and understanding of the finite element method (FEM), and not least, the ability to provide qualified assessment and interpretation of results obtained through FEM calculations.</Course_Objectives>
