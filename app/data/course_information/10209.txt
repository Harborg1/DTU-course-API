        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset består af en introduktion til anvendelse af store neutron- og røntgenfaciliteter, såsom European Spallation Source (ESS) og MAX-IV synkrotronen i Lund, samt European X-ray Free Electron Laser (E-XFEL) i Hamborg.
  
Grundlæggende teori om spredning og absorption af røntgen- og neutronstråler introduceres, og der gives en indføring i én-krystaldiffraktion, pulverdiffraktion, småvinkelspredning, røntgen-imaging/tomografi og neutron spektroskopi. Hermed opnår den studerende et overblik over nogle af de mest typiske anvendelser af laboratorie røntgenkilder og store neutron- og røntgen forsøgsanlæg.
 
Øvelser: Den enkelte studerende udfører udvalgte laboratorieøvelser på DTU. I anden uge af kurset gennemføres røntgenforsøg ved en international storskala facilitet (MAX-IV). Der bliver lagt vægt på anvendelser inden for hårde og bløde materialers fysik (hard and soft condensed matter science) samt materialevidenskab.

Kurset afsluttes med data-behandling, færdiggørelse af øvelses-rapporter, fremstilling af postere og eksamination.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelser</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give en praktisk indføring i karakterisering af materialer med røntgen- og neutronspredningsteknikker som de anvendes på storskalafaciliteter (synktrotroner mv.) med fokus på anvendelser indenfor studier af hårde og bløde materialer (hard and soft condensed matter science) samt materialevidenskab. En del af kurset indebærer praktiske øvelser ved et internationalt røntgenforskningsanlæg, formentligt MaxIV-synkrotronen.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Eksperimentelle øvelser vil blive udført på Lyngby Campus. Desuden tilbringer kursusdeltagerne 1-3 dage på en international storskala facilitet. I forbindelse med dette ophold er de studerende ansvarlige for egen rejseforsikring.</Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course consists of an introduction to the use of large scale neutron and X-ray facilities, such as the European Spallation Source (ESS) and the MAX-IV synchrotron, both in Lund, Sweden, and the European X-ray Free Electron Laser (E-XFEL) in Hamburg.

The basic theory of scattering and absorption of x-rays and neutrons is introduced and the course participants gain familiarity with some of the most typical applications of laboratory X-ray and large scale synchrotron X-ray and neutron facilities: single-crystal diffraction, powder diffraction, small-angle scattering, imaging/tomography and neutron spectroscopy.
 
Exercises: Each student carries out selected laboratory exercises. In the second week of the course x-ray experiments are carried out at an international large scale facility (MAX-IV). The main focus of all exercises will be on hard and soft condensed matter science and material science.
 
The course is concluded by data-analysis, completion of exercise reports, preparation of posters and examination.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and practical exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course provides a practical introduction to characterization of materials using X-ray and neutron scattering techniques as they are applied at large-scale facilities like synchrotrons. The main focus will be on applications within hard condensed matter science, soft condened matter science and material science. Part of the course will consist of hands-on exercises performed at an international large scale x-ray facility, most likely the MaxIV synchrotron.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Experimental exercises will be carried out at Lyngby Campus. In addition, the participants will spend 1-3 days at an international large scale facility. The students are responsible for their own travel insurance in connection with this visit.</Remark>
