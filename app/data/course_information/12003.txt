          <Contents>Kurset omfatter mål og metoder i miljøforskning, herunder grundlæggende teori for gennemførelse af felt- og laboratoriearbejde. Som en del af kurset udfører de studerende forskningsorienterede opgaver knyttet til kursusarbejdet og deltager i et kort forskningsforløb, hvor de vælger et emne og i samarbejde med den ansvarlige forsker planlægger og gennemfører felt- og/eller laboratoriearbejde. Derudover udvikler de studerende kompetencer i samarbejde, koordinering og organisering. Kurset afsluttes med en skriftlig rapport og en mundtlig præsentation af resultaterne.</Contents>

De udvikler evnen til at bearbejde, analysere og fortolke indsamlede data, samarbejde og koordinere opgaver i grupper samt anvende relevant faglig litteratur i deres arbejde. Endelig lærer de at formidle deres resultater gennem både skriftlige rapporter og mundtlige præsentationer.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course covers objectives and methods in environmental research, including basic theory for conducting field and laboratory work. Students are introduced to experimental design, observational techniques and systematic field data collection, as well as to the functioning of common measurement instruments. As part of the course, students carry out research‑oriented tasks linked to the course assignments and participate in a short research placement, where they select a topic and plan and conduct field and/or laboratory work together with the responsible researcher. In addition, students develop skills in teamwork, coordination and organisation. The course concludes with a written report and an oral presentation of results.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, group work, field measurements, analysis in the laboratory</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Students learn to plan simple experiments and apply basic principles of experimental design, work safely in both the field and the laboratory, and use field instruments to carry out systematic data collection. They develop the ability to process, analyse and interpret collected data, work collaboratively and coordinate group tasks, and use relevant scientific literature to support their work. Finally, students learn to communicate their results effectively through both written reports and oral presentations.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course is offered in the second semester and is intended for first‑year students in the Bachelor of Environmental Engineering programme. These students are given priority for enrollment.</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>Introductory course offered in the second semester. The course is intended for first‑year students enrolled in the BSc in Environmental Engineering.</Remark>
