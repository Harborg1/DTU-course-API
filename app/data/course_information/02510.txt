        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Efter at have gennemført dette kursus, vil du være i stand til at benytte deep learning modeller til at analysere 2D og 3D billeder samt at udtrække kvantitativ information fra dem. Du vil få viden om relevante eksisterende deep learning modeller, hvordan de kan anvendes og gen-trænes, samt hvordan man kan forberede et datasæt. Strukturering af data er et kerne-element i deep learning, og du vil få erfaring med annoterings-værktøjer til forberedelse og forbedring af billeder til segmentering, klassifikation samt detektion. Du vil undersøge billede indkodning og grupperings-teknikker til at evaluere et datasæts varians og kvalitet, samt benytte Python til at implementere scripts der løser analyse-opgaver baseret på eksisterende biblioteker og funktioner.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelser samt et 3 ugers mini-projekt</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Kursusnoter.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>I dette kursus vil du få praktisk erfaring med at benytte deep learning modeller til billedanalyse med et fokus på betydningen af data håndteringen. Det underliggende datasæt er afgørende for at opnå effektive deep learning modeller, og du vil lære om at forbedre datasæts, evaluere label kvalitet samt vurdere indflydelsen på modellerne brugbarhed. Kurset dækker forskellige metoder herunder billede-segmentering, klassifikation, detektion i 2D og 3D billeder, og er derfor relevant for anvendelser indenfor bl.a. forskning i medicin, biologi samt materialer.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>After completing this course, you will be able to use deep learning models to analyze both 2D and 3D images and to extract quantitative information. You will gain knowledge of relevant deep learning models, how to deploy and retrain these methods, and how to prepare datasets. Data engineering is core to effective learning-based image analysis, and you will gain experience with annotation tools necessary for preparing and refining images for segmentation, classification, and detection. You will explore image embedding and clustering techniques to evaluate dataset consistency and quality, and use Python to implement scripts that solve analysis tasks based on existing deep learning libraries and functions.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises plus a 3 week mini-project</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Course notes.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>In this course, you will gain practical experience in using deep learning models for image analysis, with an emphasis on the importance of data engineering. The dataset is a crucial element in obtaining effective deep learning models, and you will learn how to refine datasets, evaluate label quality and assess the impact on model performance. The course will cover various methods, including image segmentation, classification, and detection in 2D and 3D images making it relevant for applications in medicine, biology, materials research, and more.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>If you want to analyze complex images using deep learning, this course is for you! In parallel to this course, there is the course '02518 Computational 3D Imaging and Analysis’, which introduces other image analysis techniques and imaging techniques. You are also recommended to follow that course.</DTU_Remark>
