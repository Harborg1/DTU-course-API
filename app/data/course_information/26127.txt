          <Contents>Preparation, purification, and characterization of coordination compounds, organometallic compounds, and solid-state materials. Use of databases and primary literature to plan inorganic syntheses. Inorganic synthesis techniques including: handling of volatile substances, electrolysis, inert atmospheres and vacuum techniques, solid state reactions at elevated temperatures, syntheses under autogenous pressure. Employment of common characterization techniques used in inorganic chemistry, including e.g. spectroscopy (NMR, EPR, UV-Vis, IR, luminescence), electrochemistry, mass spectrometry, X-ray and electron diffraction, and thermodynamic measurements (magnetometry, electrical and thermal conductivity, heat capacity).</Contents>
