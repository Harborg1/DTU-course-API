        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>This course examines the organizational forms most suitable for achieving sustainable societal and corporate impact. It covers corporations, social enterprises, nonprofits, and advocacy organizations, exploring their roles and limitations.
The course begins with a macro-level perspective on global governance, international agreements, voluntary codes of conduct, and sector-specific initiatives. Participants analyze organizational culture, leadership styles, and strategic, operational, and tactical layers that shape sustainable transformation. Practical exercises focus on facilitating collaboration across diverse mindsets, value systems, and institutional contexts - a critical skill in forming effective cross-sector partnerships. Case studies and discussions emphasize how global strategies translate to local impact, equipping participants to navigate multi-level governance and organizational challenges while creating tangible sustainability outcomes.
The second part addresses complex strategic sustainability challenges in real organizational contexts. Participants apply research, co-creation, and innovation methods to frame challenges, synthesize solutions, and design actionable strategies that balance environmental, social, and economic considerations. The project develops skills in stakeholder engagement, facilitation, and inclusive collaboration, culminating in a written report and oral presentation to senior management. Solutions aim to create long-term organizational value, align with global sustainability standards, and demonstrate professional consultancy and strategic leadership capabilities.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is lecture-based combined with group exercises, cases and practical theory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Part 1: The objective of this course is to harness organizational forms that are best positioned to cater to positive societal and organizational impact.  This course equips participants to design, lead, and engage sustainable organizations and cross-sector partnerships. It covers leadership, organizational culture, strategy, and governance across global and local contexts. Participants learn to assess effectiveness, engage stakeholders, and foster collaboration to drive measurable sustainability impact. Learning objectives 1-6

Part 2: The second part of the course focuses on addressing complex strategic challenges in sustainable leadership, and how executive consultancy and co-creation approaches can generate actionable, high-impact solutions. The course introduces a consultancy project process covering research, concept development, solution synthesis, evaluation, and presentation, integrating interdisciplinary knowledge and prior learning from the programme. The course runs throughout the full semester. Learning objectives 7-12</Course_Objectives>
