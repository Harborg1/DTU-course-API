          <Contents>Termodynamisk ligevægt og tilstandsvariable. Termodynamiske potentialer. Antal mikrotilstande og entropien. Ensembleteori og middelværdier. Tilstandssummer.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Thermodynamical equilibrium and state variables.  Thermodynamic potentials. Number of microstates and entropy.  Ensemble theory and mean values.  Partition functions. Classical gasses and liquids: Dealing with interacting particles.  Phase transitions.  Critical phenomena.</Contents>
