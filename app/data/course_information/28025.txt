        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset dækker de grundlæggende principper i procesteknik og anvender dem på kemiske og biokemiske processer. Typiske problemstillinger fra industrielle bioprocesser bruges som eksempler og viser, hvordan de kvantitative metoder anvendes i praksis.

Masse- og energibalancer: Systematisk opstilling og løsning af masse- og energibalancer for processer med og uden reaktion.
Strømning og blanding: Den mekaniske energibalance; strømning af gasser og væsker; friktionstab i rørsystemer; pumper; dimensionering af rørsystemer; fermenteringsvæskers reologi; omrøring og effekttilførsel i omrørte tanke.
Filtrering: Grundlæggende principper; filtrering af fermenteringsvæsker.
Varmetransport: Varmeledning, konvektion og kondensation; dimensionering af varmevekslere; temperaturstyring af bioreaktorer.
Stoftransport: Differentielle massebalancer; diffusion; hastighedsbaserede stoftransportoperationer; gas-væske-stoftransport; ilttransport og den volumetriske stofovergangskoefficient (kLa).
Opskalering: Hvordan strømning, blanding, varmetransport og ilttransport ændres med skala.</Contents>

Kurset introducerer de grundlæggende principper i bioprocesteknologi (masse- og energibalancer, strømning og blanding samt varme- og stoftransport) og anvender dem på det udstyr, der bruges i biologisk produktion.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course covers the basic principles of process engineering and applies them to chemical and biochemical processes. Typical problems from industrial bioprocesses serve as examples and show how the quantitative methods are applied in practice.

Mass and energy balances: Systematic formulation and solution of mass and energy balances for processes with and without reaction.
Fluid flow and mixing: The mechanical energy balance; flow of gases and liquids; friction losses in pipe systems; pumps; design of pipe systems; rheology of fermentation broths; mixing and power input in stirred tanks.
Filtration: Fundamental principles; filtration of fermentation broths.
Heat transfer: Conduction, convection and condensation; design of heat exchangers; temperature control of bioreactors.
Mass transfer: Differential mass balances; diffusion; rate-based mass transfer operations; gas–liquid mass transfer; oxygen transfer and the volumetric mass transfer coefficient (kLa).
Scale-up: How flow, mixing, heat transfer and oxygen transfer change with scale.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and class exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Pauline M. Doran, Bioprocess Engineering Principles, 3rd edition</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives students with a background in biology, chemistry or the life sciences a quantitative, engineering-based understanding of bioprocesses. It introduces the core principles of bioprocess engineering (mass and energy balances, fluid flow and mixing, heat transfer and mass transfer) and applies them to the equipment used in biological production. After the course, students can formulate a bioprocess problem in engineering terms, find and assess the data needed to solve it, and carry out the calculations needed to design process equipment and to predict how the performance and capacity of existing equipment change with operating conditions. The course provides the foundation for later courses in fermentation technology, bioprocess design and downstream processing.</Course_Objectives>
