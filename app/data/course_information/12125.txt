          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Gruppearbejde og forelæsninger. Kurset forudsætter aktiv deltagelse i gruppearbejde og diskussioner i undervisningen. Et centralt element i kurset er at de studerende selvstændigt udvikler klimatilpasning for konkrete cases. Det forventes at de studerende hjælper hinanden med at forstå og løse opgaverne.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus har til formål at gøre deltagerne i stand til at designe, planlægge og implementere klimatilpasningsforanstaltninger inden for det byggede miljø. Kurset vil give en introduktion til fremskrivninger af klimarelaterede risici og hvordan disse kan omdannes til designkriterier på lokalt niveau, introducere grundlæggende økonomiske begreber for risikovurdering samt give et overblik over planlægningsrammer, der driver klimatilpasningsprocesser i det byggede miljø. Koncepterne vil blive anvendt ved at designe og planlægge klimatilpasningsforanstaltninger for bygninger og infrastruktur (f.eks. oversvømmelsesbeskyttelse i et bykvarter, tilpasning af vindmølleparker til bølgehøjder og vindhastigheder). Det forudsættes, at studerende har en forståelse af spatiale data og hydrologiske processer og er bekendt med grundlæggende GIS-analyse.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>-Identification of weather extremes (rain, sea level, wave height, wind) from data
-Scenario projections for climate extremes

-Impact of weather extremes on assets
-Economic risk assessment

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>A mixture of lectures and group work. The course requires active participation in group work and discussions in class. A central part of the course is unsupervised group work where climate adaptation measures are developed for concrete cases. During this time the groups are expected to help each other towards understanding and solving the problems at hand.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This course aims to enable participants to design, plan for and implement climate adaptation measures within the built environment. The course will provide an introduction to projections of climate hazards and how they can be converted into design criteria on the local scale, introduce basic economic concepts for risk assessment, and provide an overview of policy and planning practices driving climate adaptation processes in the built environment. The concepts will be applied by designing and planning climate adaptation measures for buildings and infrastructure (e.g. flood protection for an urban neighborhood, adaptation of windparks to wave heights and wind speed). It is assumed that students have an understanding of spatial data and hydrological processes and are familiar with basic GIS analysis.</Course_Objectives>
