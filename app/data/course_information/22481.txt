        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Velkommen til kurset Indledende medicinsk billeddannelse, hvor vi dykker ned i den fascinerende verden med at visualisere strukturen og funktionen af organer i den menneskelige krop. Dette kursus udforsker en række billeddannelsesmetoder, der er afgørende for medicinsk diagnose, herunder ultralyd, røntgen (både skyggebilleder og tomografiske billeder som CT-scanninger), magnetisk resonansbilleddannelse (MRI) samt positronemissionstomografi (PET) og enkeltfoton emissionscomputertomografi (SPECT).

At forstå de fysiske principper bag disse billeddannelsesmodaliteter er altafgørende, da de danner grundlaget for nøjagtig fortolkning og analyse af medicinske billeder. Det primære projekt indeholder en udfordring: at bestemme typen af biologisk materiale i et ukendt fantom ved hjælp af disse billeddannelsesmetoder.

Gennem hele kurset vil du engagere dig i kollaborativ læring i små grupper, hvor du tackler problemer relateret til fortolkning og analyse af medicinske billeder. I jeres grupper vil I undersøge jeres individuelle fantomer. Dette involverer erhvervelse af billeder ved hjælp af forskellige billeddannelsesmodaliteter og anvendelse af de værktøjer og teknikker, I lærer, til at analysere og fortolke disse billeder effektivt.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Interaktive forelæsninger, praktiske øvelser på DTU og Bispebjerg hospital, computerøvelser, projektarbejde. Kurset kræver, at deltagerne er rutinerede i brugen af Python.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

Bogen kan købes (enten som fysisk bog eller online ressource) gennem polyteknisk boghandel.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at forstå og udnytte de grundlæggende fysiske principper bag de vigtigste medicinske billeddiagnostiske modaliteter, der anvendes på hospitalet. Gennem individuelle studier, interaktive e-læringsmaterialer, interaktive forelæsninger og  projektbaseret samarbejde opnår de studerende praktisk ekspertise i at anvende disse principper på scenarier i den virkelige verden.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Welcome to Introduction to Medical Imaging, where we delve into the fascinating world of visualizing the structure and function of organs within the human body. This course explores a variety of imaging methods crucial for medical diagnosis, including ultrasound, x-ray (both shadow images and tomographic images like CT scans), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), as well as positron emission tomography (PET) and single photon emission computer tomography (SPECT).

Understanding the physical principles behind these imaging modalities is paramount, as they form the foundation for accurate interpretation and analysis of medical images. Your primary project revolves around a compelling challenge: determining the type of biological tissue within an unknown phantom using these imaging modalities.

Throughout the course, you'll engage in collaborative learning within small groups, tackling problems related to the interpretation and analysis of medical images. In your groups you will investigate your individual phantoms. This involves acquiring images using different imaging modalities and applying the tools and techniques you've learned to analyze and interpret these images effectively.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Interactive lectures, practical exercises at DTU and Bispebjerg hospital, computer exercises, project work. The course requires the participants to be experienced in Python.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>In course 22481, we use "Medical Imaging - physics, engineering and clinical applications" by Nadine Barrie Smith and Andrew Webb, 2011, published by Cambridge University Press. The book can be purchased (either as a physical copy or an online resource) through Polyteknisk Boghandel.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to grasp and utilize the core physical principles behind main medical imaging modalities used at the hospital. Through individual studies, interactive e-learning materials, interactive lectures, and collaborative, project-based teamwork, students gain practical expertise in applying these principles to real-world scenarios.</Course_Objectives>
