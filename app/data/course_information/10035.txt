        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Det teoretiske stof læres fortrinsvis ved regneøvelser og få forelæsninger. Kurset indeholder desuden 7 eksperimentelle øvelser, som alle skal gennemføres og godkendes. Ydermere skal to multiple choice tests bestås. Resten af kurset består af en såkaldt "konsulentopgave", hvor du som del af en gruppe løser en konkret måleteknisk opgave ude hos en af forskningsgrupperne på et af de deltagende institutter.           <Course_Objectives>Det overordnede mål er at give Fysik og Ingeniørvidenskab studerende en introduktion til laboratoriearbejdet på Fysik og Ingenørvidenskab retningen. Dette opnås ved at gennemføre en række måletekniske eksperimentelle øvelser inden for fysik og basal elektronik. Herudover er det også et mål at lære en systematisk og kvantitativ tilgang til dataanalyse, samt kredsløbsteori. Det er målet at bruge den tillærte måletekniske viden til at gennemføre en konkret måleopgave ('konsulentopgaven') hos en forskningsgruppe ved et af de deltagende institutter, samt vurdere usikkerhederne på de fremkomne måledata.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The theoretical subject matter is primarily learned by computational exercises and a few lectures. The course comprises in addition 7 experimental exercises, which all must be completed and approved. Moreover, two multiple choice tests have to be passed. The rest of the course consists of the so-called "consultancy project", where you as part of a group will solve a concrete measurement task at one of the research groups in one of the participating departments. The consultancy task is documented in a data treatment journal and also presented to your fellow students and the teachers on a poster at a poster session at the end of the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, laboratory exercises, theoretical exercises, experimental group work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Compendium of the book "An introduction to error analysis" by John R. Taylor. Available at the Polyteknisk Bookstore (Lyngby). 
In addition, course notes will be distributed electronically at the course start.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall goal is to introduce the Engineering Physics students to the laboratory work in the Engineering Physics field of study. This is achieved by conducting a series of experimental exercises in physics and basic electronics. Moreover, it is the goal to learn a systematic and quantitative approach to data analysis, and to learn circuit theory. Further, it is the goal to apply the acquired skills to conduct a measurement task (the ‘consultancy project’) within a research group at one of the participating departments, and to estimate the uncertainties of the associated data.</Course_Objectives>
