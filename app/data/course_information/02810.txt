        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset fokuserer på udvikling af funktionelle UX design prototyper der kombinerer flere typer af mobile enheder og interfaces, fra flat UI til dialog- og stemme-baserede design mønstre.

Kurset bruger lean metodik baseret på at identificere behov, hierarkisk user story mapping, multidevice designmønstre og microinteractions system design, såvel som hurtig prototyping der kombinerer både velkendte og nye værktøjer og metoder.

Kurset giver den studerende et systematisk fundament og praktisk erfaring med design af UX prototyper, som også senere kan anvendes til "næste generation" interfaces indenfor andre områder, som det f.eks. adresseres i 02266 "User Experience Engineering".</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger kombineret med praktiske øvelser og projektarbejde</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at gøre de studerende i stand til 1) at anvende lean prototypeudvikling til UX design af personaliserede brugerflader, og 2) at vælge passende værktøjer, metoder og design til opgaverne.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset er en del af DTU Computes Digitale Innovations-Kanon.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is focused on development of functional UX design prototypes that combine multiple mobile devices and interfaces, from flat UI to dialog and voice based design patterns.

It uses lean methods based on pain driven design, hierarchical user story mapping, multidevice design patterns and microinteractions system design, as well as rapid prototyping combining both industry proven and novel tools and methods.

The course provides students with a systematic foundation and practical experience in designing UX prototype designs, which can also later be applied to "next generation" interfaces in other domains, subsequently addressed in 02266 "User Experience Engineering".</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures combined with practical exercises and project work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The objective of the course is to enable students 1) to apply lean prototyping methods for UX design of personalized user interfaces, and 2) to select appropriate tools, methods and designs for the tasks at hand.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is part of DTU Compute's Digital Innovation Canon.</Remark>
