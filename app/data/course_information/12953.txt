          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser og individuel opgave</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Hvis du ikke allerede lever op til de faglige forudsætninger, er det dit ansvar at lære grundlæggende LCA inden kursusstart. Til det formål anbefaler vi:

Life Cycle Assessment - Theory and Practice (Hauschild et al. 2018 - https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-56475-3), særligt kapitel 6-10. Bogen præsentere en skridt-for-skridt guide til LCA.

Du vil modtage yderligere litteratur i løbet af undervisningsugen.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Der er en voksende interesse i at forstå miljøpåvirkninger i et systemperspektiv og at vurdere påvirkningerne fra individuelle lande, organisationer osv. i lyset af globale bæredygtighedsgrænser og mål. Dette understøtter en forståelse af, hvad det kræver af lande, organisationer osv. at kunne betragtes som bæredygtige i absolut forstand og ikke bare "mindre ubæredygtige" end noget andet.
Kurset introducerer absolut bæredygtighed som begreb, giver væsentlige eksempler på dets brug på f.eks. lande, virksomheder og produkter og sætter deltagerne i stand til at udføre  absolutte vurderinger i praksis. Kurset has særligt fokus på deltagernes medbragte cases for at sætte dem i stand til at indarbejde absolut bæredygtighedsvurdering i deres eget arbejde.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>• Lectures on theoretical aspects of absolute sustainability assessment.
• Group work applying absolute sustainability assessment in practice on exercises and the participants’ own case studies.
• One week after end of class: hand in of poster that shows your achievement of the learning objectives with a specific focus on the application of absolute sustainability assessment to your own project.
• Two weeks after end of class: receive the poster from one of your peers in the class for your evaluation.
• Three weeks after end of class: attend online session, where each participant presents the contents of the poster to a group of other participants and a course instructor, receive feedback from a fellow participant, as well as the course instructor, and give feedback to another participant.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and individual assignment</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>If you do not already fulfill the academic prerequisites, it is your responsibility to learn the basics of LCA before the course begins. For this, we recommend:
 
Life Cycle Assessment - Theory and Practice (Hauschild et al. 2018 - https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-56475-3), especially chapters 6-10. The book chapters present a step-by-step guide for how to do an LCA. 

Additional material will be provided during week of class.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>There is an increasing interest in understanding environmental impacts from a system perspective and in assessing impacts of individual countries, organizations, etc., in the context of global sustainability limits and goals. This enables an understanding of what it takes for countries, organizations, etc., to be considered sustainable in an absolute sense and not just “less unsustainable” than something else.
This course introduces the concept of absolute sustainability assessment, gives important examples of its application to, e.g., countries, companies and products, and enables the participants to carry out absolute assessments in practice. The course has a special focus on the participants´ self-defined case studies, thereby enabling them to incorporate absolute sustainability assessment in their own work.</Course_Objectives>
