        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I samarbejde med en virksomhed udvikles en forretningsidé. Det er de studerendes egne idéer, der er i fokus, men der arbejdes med inside-out/outside-in innovation, dvs. at det både kan være virksomheder og de studerende selv, der kommer med det oplæg, som så vil blive udviklet gennem kurset. Der vil være fokus på innovations process modeller, business model canvas og iterativ kundebaseret udvikling af forretningscasen.
Kurset består af forelæsninger, læsestof, gruppeøvelser og opgaver samt fællesbedømmelser. En stor del af læringsaktiviteterne er gruppebaseret og af den grund er det vigtigt at deltage i klasseundervisningen.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger suppleret med projektarbejde. I projektarbejdet arbejder man med et problem stillet af en virksomhed.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Målet er at introducere de studerende for innovation og opnå viden omkring metoder og værktøjer som kan bruges i innovationsprocesser. Desuden gives de studerende mulighed for at udvikle deres egne idéer i samarbejde med eksisterende virksomheder.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset var tidligere udbudt som 26610. Det henvender sig til studerende som er på min. fjerde semester. Kurset supplerer 42500 Engineering and Innovation. I overgangsperioden vil kurset blive tilpasset den enkelte studerende i forhold til om de har bestået 42500 (eller tilsvarende kursus) eller ej.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>In collaboration with a company a business case is developed. Focus is on the students own ideas, but they will work with inside-out/outside-in innovation, which means that the final idea which is pursued in the course may come from the company or from the students. The focus will be on applying a innovation process model, business model canvas and iterative customer based development of the business case.
The course consists of lectures, reading material, group exercises and assignments and peer-reviews. A large part of the learning activities are group based and it thus crucial to participate in class.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and project work. The project work is focused on challenges presented by companies.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The goal is to introduce the students to innovation and knowledge about methods and tools used in innovation processes. In addition the students are given the opportunity to develop own ideas through an innovation process in collaboration with external companies.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course was previously offered as 26610. It is addressed to students at min. fourth semester. The course will supplement 42500 Engineering and Innovation. In the transition period the course will be adapted to each student depending on whether the student has passed 42500 (or similar course) or not.</Remark>
