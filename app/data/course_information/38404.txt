        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kompetencer indenfor innovation er afgørende for fremtidige ingeniører for at skabe nye perspektiver, især for at fremme og drive global forandring i håndteringen af de samfundsmæssige, miljømæssige og sundhedsrelaterede udfordringer, vi står overfor.

Gennem kurset opnår de studerende anvendelige erfaringer og kompetencer med en systematisk tilgang til innovation i en ingeniørmæssig kontekst. De studerende skal udvikle unikke indsigter i, hvordan integreret innovation praktiseres. De vil opleve og stå over for udfordringer med tværfagligt samarbejde, mens de arbejder på en reel industriudfordring. Hver projektgruppe vil også blive tildelt en gruppefacilitator (en studerende, der tager kurset 'Facilitating Innovation in Multidisciplinary Teams'). Disse facilitatorer har allerede innovationskompetencer og vil derfor vejlede gruppen under hele projektarbejdet. Med en facilitator ved hånden vil gruppen have en 'katalysator' til at øge 'Innovationshøjden'.

Kurset er organiseret i to hoveddele. Den første uge - BOOST-modulet - er fokuseret på at engagere de studerende med emnet innovation gennem en række oplæg og introducere de studerende til en systematisk tilgang til innovation, herunder relevant teori. I starten af den første uge vil de studerende blive sat i projektgrupper baseret på deres valgte innovationsudfordring samt deres studielinje m.fl.

I anden og tredje uge - EXPERIENCE-modulet - er gruppens fokus på at udvikle en innovativ løsning i samarbejde med deres facilitator. Dette skal resultere i et koncept, der skal præsenteres for interessenterne i slutningen af forløbet.

Kurset består af forelæsninger, læsestof, gruppeøvelser, opgaver, peer-reviews og multiple-choice-quizzer. Læringsaktiviteterne og afleveringerne er baseret på gruppearbejdet og af den grund er det vigtigt at deltage i undervisningen på DTU Campus.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset er en blanding af gruppearbejde, øvelser, forelæsninger på campus samt videoforelæsninger og opgaver. Gruppearbejdet forventes at foregå på campus i hele varigheden af de 3 uger. Kurset kan ikke tages som et online kursus.
See læringsmål for uddybelse.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset introducerer studerende til at anvende kompetencer og metoder indenfor innovation i ingeniørvidenskab med tværfaglige teams.
Studerende vil:

- Forstå, hvordan man følger og anvender en struktureret innovationsproces på en industri udfordring.
- Opleve det varierede innovationslandskab ved at navigere og anvende deres færdigheder i det
- Bidrage med samarbejdsinitiativer i en teamkontekst for at finde nye perspektiver
- Kombinere deres discipliner gennem forskellige metoder i en faciliteret innovationsproces
- Tilegne sig DTU's Signature Innovations Mindset og anvendelsen af tværfaglige teams
- Være i stand til at kommunikere indsigter til relevante interessenter og justere ud fra deres input
- Tilegne sig en værktøjskasse af strukturerede metoder for at øge innovationssandsynligheden
- Skabe nye løsningsorienterede ideer og modne disse til et endeligt konceptforslag</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Innovation competencies are crucial for future engineers to invigorate new perspectives, particularly to nurture and drive global change in tackling the societal, environmental and health-related challenges we face.

Through the course, students acquire applicable experiences and competencies with a systematic approach to innovation in an engineering context. Students are required to develop unique insights into how integrated innovation is practised. They will experience and face challenges with multidisciplinary collaboration while working on a real industry challenge. Each project team will also be assigned a team facilitator (which are students who are taking the course 'Facilitating Innovation in Multidisciplinary Teams'). These facilitators have attained innovation competencies and will guide the team during all project work. With a facilitator at hand, the team will have a 'catalyst' to increase 'Innovation Height'.

The course is organized into two main parts.
The first week - the BOOST module - is focused on engaging the students with the topic of innovation by an array of speakers, and introducing students to a systematic approach to innovation, including relevant theory. At the start of the first week, the students will be put into project teams based on their chosen innovation challenge as well as their line of study et al.

In the second and third weeks - the EXPERIENCE module - the teams's focus is on developing an innovative solution in collaboration with their facilitator. This should result in a concept that must be presented to the stakeholders at the end of the process.

The course consists of lectures, reading material, team exercises, assignments, peer-reviews and multiple-choice quizzes. The learning activities and submissions are team-based experiences and for that reason, it is important to participate in class at DTU Campus</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is a mix of teamwork, exercises, lectures on campus as well as video lectures and assignments. The teamwork is expected to take place on campus for the entire duration of the 3 weeks. The course cannot be taken as an online course.
See learning objectives for more details.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course introduces students to apply competencies and methods to enable innovation in engineering within multidisciplinary teams.
Students will:

- Understand how to follow and apply a structure innovation process to a real-life engineering challenge
- Experience the varied innovation landscape by navigating and applying their skills in it
- Contribute with collaborative initiatives in a team context to allow new perspectives
- Combine their disciplines through various methods in a facilitated innovation process
- Acquire the DTU's signature Innovation Mindset and application of multidisciplinary teams
- Be able to communicate key findings to relevant stakeholders and adjust from their inputs
- Acquire a toolkit of structured methods to increase the innovative probability
- Create new solution-oriented ideas and mature these into a final concept proposal</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is connected to and runs parallel with 'Facilitating Innovation in Multidisciplinary Teams' 38401(Jan), 38403(Jun), 38405(Aug).
The Facilitation course (38401, 38403, 38405) is intended for students who already have qualifying competencies based on the course prerequisites.</DTU_Remark>
