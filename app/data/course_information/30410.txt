        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kortfattet oversigt over mikrobølgesystemer til anvendelse inden for trådløs kommunikation, radar og radiometre. Repetition af transmissionslinieteori. Analyse og syntese af transmissionslinjer og bølgeledere. Kredsløbsteori for bølgeledende systemer. Impedanstransformation og -tilpasning. Mikrobølgeresonatorer, effektdelere og retningskoblere. Grundlæggende antenneparametre og simple udstrålende systemer. inkl. simple antennegrupper. Tråd- og sløjfeantenner. Bredbåndet antenner (dipoles). Patch antenner.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, projekter og opgaveløsning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Udvalgte kapitler fra D. M. Pozar, Microwave Techniques, Wiley, 4th edition 2012

Udvalgte kapitler fra C. A. Balanis, Antenna Theory - Analysis and Design, Wiley, 4th edition 2016</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Målet med kurset er få de studerende til at forstå teorierne, metoderne, fysisk virkemåde, og teknisk konstruktion af passive mikrobølgekomponenter og antenner til brug i trådløse systemer.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Short review of microwave systems for wireless communication, radar and radiometer applications. Repetition of transmission line theory. Analysis and synthesis of planar transmission lines and waveguides. Microwave network analysis for waveguiding systems. Impedance transformation and matching. Microwave resonators, power dividers and directional couplers. Antenna fundamentals and simple radiation systems incl simple antenna arrays. Wire and loop antennas. Broadband antennas (dipoles). Patch antennas.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, project and problem-solving.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Selected chapters from D. M. Pozar, Microwave Techniques, Wiley, 4th edition 2012

Selected chapters from C. A. Balanis, Antenna Theory - Analysis and Design, Wiley, 4th edition 2016</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to make the student understand the theories, methods, physical mode of operation, and the technical construction of the passive microwave components and antennas used in wireless systems.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
