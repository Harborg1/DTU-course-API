        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dynamiske balanceligninger. Simulering. Linearisering. Afvigelsesvariable. Løsning af lineære differentialligninger ved brug af Laplacetransformation. 1. og 2. ordens systemer, tidskonstanter, forstærkning, dæmpning, oversving, overføringsfunktion. Feedbackregulering: P-, I- og D-regulering. Stabilitet af sløjfer. Andre reguleringsformer: Feedforward-, forholds- og kaskaderegulering. Instrumentering, PI-diagrammer. Eksperimentelt projektarbejde. Rapportskrivning.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, grupperegning, projektarbejde, laboratorieøvelser og rapportskrivning</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kemiingeniøruddannelsen behandler fortrinsvis den stationære driftstilstand for processer - designet med henblik på optimal udnyttelse af ressourcer under hensyntagen til bæredygtighed m.m.. Men procesanlæg skal sættes i drift, skifte operationsområde og operere så forstyrrelser påvirker driften minimalt. Dette kræver procesregulering. Kurset sigter mod forståelse for samspillet mellem procesdynamik og procesregulering, samt opbygningen af automatiske reguleringssystemer og disses virkemåde. Dette omfatter den underliggende teori samt visse anvendte komponenter. Fortrinsvis behandles PID-regulatoren og dens indstillingsmuligheder. Dette med henblik på planlægning og vurdering af instrumentering af relativt simple anlæg.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Dynamic balance equations. Simulation. Linearization. Deviation variables. Solution of linear differential equations using Laplace transforms. 1st and 2nd order systems, time constants, gain, damping, overshot, transfer function. Feedback control: P-, I- og D-control. Stability of loops. Enhanced control strategies: Feedforward-, ratio and cascade control. Instrumentation, PI diagrams. Experimental project work. Reporting.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Classroom Lectures, Tutorials, Project Work, Laboratory, Reporting</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Most of the chemical engineering curriculum deals with processes operating at steady state - designed to optimize economical resources, while taking into account sustainability. But plants are put into operation, are shut down, are subjected to differing operating conditions and disturbances. Process control is necessary in these situations. The course aims at an understanding of how process dynamics and process control is related, and of the construction and implications of control systems. This includes the underlying theory and certain components employed. The course emphasizes the application and settings of a standard PID-controller used in simple feedback control and other enhanced control strategies. All aiming at planning and evaluating instrumentation of simple plants.</Course_Objectives>
