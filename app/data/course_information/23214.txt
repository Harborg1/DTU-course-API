        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Den teoretiske del af kurset giver de studerende en introduktion til basal mikrobiologi med hensyn til mikroorganismers opbygning, vækst og diversitet. Udvalgte strukturelle og funktionelle egenskaber såsom omsætning af næringsstoffer, og antibiotikaresistens relateres til mikrobiologiske metoder. 

De praktiske laboratorieøvelser dækker klassiske mikrobiologiske teknikker som rendyrkning af bakterier, kimtalsbestemmelser, OD-målinger, og brug af selektive agarplader såvel som molekylær biologiske metoder som kvantitativ PCR (qPCR) og MALDI-TOF identifikation af mikroorganismer. Resultater genereret i løbet af kurset behandles med basale statistiske værktøjer og præsenteres i kortfattede rapporter.</Contents>

Praktiske laboratorieøvelser og statistiske beregninger er integreret i kurset.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The theoretical part of the course covers an introduction to basic microbiology in regards to the structure of microorganisms, growth and diversity. Selected structural and functional properties such as nutritional requirements, and antibiotic resistance are related to microbiological methods. 

The experimental work employs classical microbiological techniques for isolation of pure bacterial colonies, determination of CFU in a culture, OD-measurements and selective plating. Students will also use molecular microbiology methods such quantitative real-time PCR (qPCR), and MALDI-TOF. Students will analyze the data generated during the course using basic statistical tools and present the results in concise reports.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, group work including theoretical exercises, oral presentations and laboratory exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to provide the students with skills in microbiological laboratory techniques and use of basic statistical tools to analyze and evaluate microbiological data generated during the course. Practical laboratory work and statistical calculations are integrated in the course.</Course_Objectives>
