        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer den studerende til to væsentlige områder for design, nemlig miljøtænkning og produktlivsdesign. Der introduceres til miljøproblematikken fra en historisk såvel som fremsynsorienteret perspektiv og den studerende bliver udstyret med teknikker til kortlægning og design af produktlivscykler.

Kurset er teoribaseret og har til formål at de studerende både forstår og forholder sig kritisk til teorien, samt gør en egen indsats for at supplere det teoretiske basismateriale med yderlige kilder.

Kursets centrale metoder og teorier er: bæredygtig udvikling, virksomheders bæredygtighed, cirkulær økonomi, produktlivstænkning, referencemodel for eco-design, livscyklusscreening, strategier og retningslinjer for eco-design, og miljøstyringssystemer.

Tre opgaver leder de studerende gennem undervisningspensum og sørger for at den enkelte studerende viser evnerne indenfor både egen forståelse for og videreformidling af materialet.
 
- Første opgave kræver, at de studerende udvikler en rapport, med miljøvurderingen af et givent produkt, med det formål at identificere de centrale krav til produktets redesign. Denne opgave udføres som en gruppeopgave.

- Den anden opgave kræver, at de studerende udvikler og præsenterer en poster, der indeholder tre konceptuelle beskrivelser af produktredesign samt én detaljeret konceptbeskrivelse af et udvalgt produktkoncept med det formål at benchmarke den miljømæssige og demonteringsmæssige ydeevne for det valgte produktkoncept i forhold til det oprindelige design. Opgaven udføres som en gruppeopgave.

- Tredje opgave er en evaluering af en ecodesign-opgave. Her får de studerende en opgavebeskrivelse, som skal vurderes i løbet af 3 timer. De studerende bliver vurderet på deres evner til at sætte sig ind i teorien, vurdere det miljømæssige indhold og give kritisk skriftlig evaluering af ecodesign-opgaven. Opgaven forgår som enkelmandsopgave.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the student to two essential areas for design, namely environmental thinking and product life design. Environmental problems are introduced to the students from both a historical and a future oriented perspective and the student is supplied with techniques for the mapping and design of product life cycles.

The course is theoretically based and has the aim that the student both understands and has a critical opinion of the taught theory, and furthermore makes an effort to supplement the theoretical basis material with additional sources. 

The key methods and theories explored in the course are: sustainable development, corporate sustainability, circular economy, product life thinking, reference model for ecodesign, life cycle screening, ecodesign strategies and guidelines, and Environmental Management Systems.

Three tasks guide the students through the teaching syllabus and ensure that the students demonstrate their abilities to both understand and to further communicate the material.

- The first task requires the students to develop a report, with the environmental evaluation of a given product, with the goal of identifying the key requirements for the product redesign. This task is carried out as a group task.

- The second task requires the students to develop and present a poster, detailing 3 conceptual descriptions for product redesign and 1 detailed concept description for a selected product concept, with the goal of benchmarking the environmental and disassembly performance of the selected product concept against the original design. This task is carried out as a group task.

- The third task is based on an evaluation of an ecodesign task. Each student receives a task description, which shall be evaluated within 3 hours. The students are evaluated according to their ability to submerge themselves in the theory, to evaluate the environmental contents and to give a critical evaluation of the task. This task is carried out as a single-person task.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>This course has the following main goals:

- To give insight into tools for handling environmental problems in connection with the design of products and product life systems

- To introduce environmental issues into the activity of product life design

- To identify and analyse relevant environmental and resource-related opportunities within Ecodesign

- To analyse product life cycles, especially with respect to the involvement of relevant actors and stakeholders

- To conduct a disassembly study and evaluate products for ease-of-disassembly, repairability, and durability 

- To use methodical tools for product life oriented design (DFX) with a particular focus on ecodesign

- To generate scenarios and strategies for the realisation of improved environmental situations, in relation to products and usage situations</Course_Objectives>
