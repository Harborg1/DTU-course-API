        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The students are introduced to sustainability as a theoretical concept and to the different definitions and approaches to sustainability - for example the triple bottom line of environmental, social and economic sustainability and the concept "Doughnut economics". An overview is given of the complexity of sustainability and the complexity of building sustainably. In the same way, interaction between the environment and people is also looked at.  
The students will be introduced to concepts such as the UN's Global Goals and absolute sustainability, as well as how these relate to the built environment. The students will learn about new national and international initiatives to promote more sustainable construction, including DGNB, the Reduction Roadmap, the EU's Taxonomy and Science-Based Targets. The students will apply their acquired knowledge in relation to concrete cases from construction practice.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures with discussion
Project work in smaller groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to give the students an introduction to sustainability as a concept, including the different definitions and approaches to sustainability. The course also deals with the complexity of sustainability and the interaction between different dimensions of sustainability.
The focus is on giving the students a general understanding of this, as well as applying this competence in relation to the construction industry, with the aim that it can contribute to creating a more sustainable society. 
The students will be introduced to national and international sustainability initiatives to promote more sutainable construction.</Course_Objectives>
