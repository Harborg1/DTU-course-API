          <Remark>Section of Mechanical Technology
Global business og engineering: 2nd semester

The workshop exercises are instructed in Danish, but the theory lectures are given in English.

The course ends with an oral presentation (oral exam) of the course outcome to the teacher and an internal examiner. In addition, a report on a course work is handed in.

The assessment is based on the oral exam and assessment of the report.

In addition, there are the following entrance requirements for the exam
• A test in the operation of the CAD program used must be passed.
• There are some instructions and demonstrations throughout the course. It is a requirement that the students both participate in these and pass a subsequent test in these.</Remark>
