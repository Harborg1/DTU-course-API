        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus dækker anvendelser af effektelektronik i både lavspændings- og højspændingssystemer. Omkring 40 % af undervisningstiden vil blive dedikeret til simulationsøvelser og inviterede forelæsninger.

Oversigt over effektelektroniske applikationer i elsystemer.

Distribuerede elforsyningssystemer: effektelektronik og grundlæggende kontrol

UPS-systemer: Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) koncept, UPS-typer og grundlæggende styring.

Active Power Filters: harmoniske og harmoniske resonanser, harmoniske forvrængningsmetoder, passive og aktive filtre, typer af aktive filtre, effektelektroniske konvertere og styring af aktive filtre.

FACTS-enheder: Introduktion til FACTS-teknologi, effektelektronik og grundlæggende teori for Static VAR Compensator (SVC), Thyristor Controlled Series Capacitor (TCSC), Static Synchronous Compensators (STATCOM), Unified Power Flow Controller (UPFC), Static Synchronous Series Compensator (SSSC).

HVDC-systemer: Introduktion til HVDC-applikationer i elsystemer, effektelektroniske konvertere i HVDC-systemer

Microgrids: grundlæggende kontrol af stand-alone AC microgrids

Photovoltaic systemer: modellering, effektelektroniks rolle og grundlæggende kontrol, maximum power point tracking


Vindenergisystemer: effektelektronik og grundlæggende kontrol, grid codes, vindmølleparker, maximum power point tracking</Contents>

          <Contents>This course covers applications of power electronics in both low-voltage and high-voltage power systems. About 40% of the class time will be dedicated to simulation exercises and invited lectures.
 
Overview of power electronic applications in power systems 

Distributed Generation Systems:  power electronics and basic control   

UPS Systems: Uninterruptible power supply (UPS) concept, UPS types and basic control 

Active Power Filters: harmonics and harmonic resonances, harmonic distortion mitigation methods, passive and active filters, types of active filters, power electronic converters and control of active filters.  

FACTS Devices: Introduction to FACTS technology, power electronics and basics of Static VAr Compensator (SVC), Thyristor Controlled Series Capacitor (TCSC), Static Synchronous Compensators (STATCOM), Unified Power Flow Controller (UPFC), Static Synchronous Series Compensator (SSSC)

HVDC Systems: Introduction to HVDC application in power systems, power electronic converters in HVDC systems

Microgrid: basic control of stand-alone AC microgrids

Photovoltaic Systems: modeling, role of power electronics and basic control, maximum power point tracking

Wind Energy Systems: role of power electronics and basic control, grid codes, wind farms, maximum power point tracking</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, guest lectures</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course provides the students an overview and understanding of a range of important power electronic applications in electric power systems, including renewable energy and distributed generation systems, microgrids, UPS systems, HVDC systems, active power filters, FACTS devices, etc. The focus will be on power systems operational challenges and role of power electronics to address them with correlation to fundamental power electronic converters as well as basic control design and simulation of power electronic converters.</Course_Objectives>
