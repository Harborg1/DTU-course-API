        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset foregår over tre uger. Formålet med den første uge er at dække det meste af det materielle materiale. I løbet af den anden uge vil eleverne uddybe deres viden ved at praktisere højere opgaver som diagnostik, evaluering, softwarevalg og projektplanlægning. Den tredje uge er afsat til at gennemføre et overvåget uafhængigt projekt.

Uge 1: Bayesian statistik?

Dag 1
Tema: hvordan og hvorfor skal man tage dette kursus
Emner: Introduktion, kursusressourcer, Bayesian statistik i kontekst
Aktiviteter: 
Bayesian statistik tarot læsning
Rollespil som fermenteringsinteressenter

Dag 2
Tema: Sandsynlighed som model for målingsstøj og information
Emner: Sandsynlighedsrum, begrænsede målinger, kvantitativ vs kvalitativ tro
Aktiviteter: 
Diskuter: Hvad er... Et rum? Et sandsynlighedsrum? Støj? Tro på det?
Motion: hvordan man modellerer forskellige former for måling? Herunder optisk tæthed, isotopologue sammensætninger og kvantitative metabolomics
Computeropsætning
Øvelse: kalibrere en OD meter

Dag 3
Tema: Gradient-baseret Markov kæde Monte Carlo
Emner: Dynamiske systemer, Numerisk løsning
Aktiviteter:
Bryd en symbolsk integrator 
Fix en brudt Bayesian beregning

Dag 4
Temaer: automatisk differentiering og modelevaluering
Emner: automatisk differentiering, push-forward evalueringer, tab funktioner
Aktiviteter:
Automatisk differentiering tarot
Interessentkomitéen kritiserer en model

Dag 5
Tema: Indlejrede dynamiske systemer
Emner: MCMC og automatisk differentiering med indlejret numerisk løsning
Aktiviteter:
Få en simpel model af en fermentering
Idéation af projekt 

Uge 2: Bayesian statistik i praksis

Dag 6
Tema: Software til Bayesian statistik
Emner: typer af software, tommelfingerregler for at vælge mellem alternativer
Aktiviteter:
Undersøg et Bayesiansk softwarebibliotek
Forbedre gæringsmodellen og opdatere kurset repository

Dag 7
Tema: Vanskelige modeller
Emner: vanskelige målinger (manglende data, mætning, heterosekstinfikatisme), vanskelige priorer (sammenhæng, afhængighed, haleoplysninger, ikke-parametriske modeller)
Aktivitet: passer til en vanskelig model og tilføj den til kursuslageret

Dag 8 
Tema: Staten af kunsten
Emner: seneste algoritmer, modeller og applikationer
Aktiviteter
Nogle interessante emner i Bayesian statistik forskning
Find og læs en interessant nylig Bayesian statistik papir
Beslutte i udvalgene, hvordan hovedmodellen kan forbedres på grundlag af resultater

Dag 9
Tema: Gør små forbedringer
Aktiviteter:
Projektplanlægning
Lav en konkret plan for at forbedre Bayesian statistik. For eksempel, find et live problem i en Bayesian software bibliotek og forsøge at løse det, eller udarbejde et forslag til at gøre konferencer mere inkluderende.

Dag 10
Tema: Huller i marken
Aktiviteter:
Diskuter og præsentere: det største problem med beregningsmæssige Bayesian statistik?
Præsentere projektplaner, få og give peer feedback

          <Contents>The course takes place over three weeks. The aim of the first week is to cover most of the substantive material. During the second week the students will deepen their knowledge by practicing higher-level tasks like diagnostics, evaluation, software choice and project planning. The third week is set aside for carrying out a supervised independent project.

Week 1: Bayesian statistics?

Day 1
Theme: how and why to take this course
Topics: Introduction, course resources, Bayesian statistics in context
Activities: 
Bayesian statistics tarot reading
Role play as fermentation stakeholders

Day 2
Theme: Probability as a model for measurement noise and information
Topics: Probability spaces, constrained measurements, quantitative vs qualitative belief
Activities: 
Discuss: What is… A space? A probability space? Noise? Belief?
Exercise: how to model different kinds of measurement? Including optical density, isotopologue compositions and quantitative metabolomics
Computer setup
Exercise: calibrate an OD meter

Day 3
Theme: Gradient-based Markov chain Monte Carlo
Topics: Dynamical systems, Numerical solving
Activities:
Break a symplectic integrator 
Fix a broken Bayesian computation

Day 4
Themes: automatic differentiation and model evaluation
Topics: automatic differentiation, push-forward evaluations, loss functions
Activities:
Automatic differentiation tarot
Stakeholder committee critiques a model

Day 5
Theme: embedded dynamical systems
Topics: MCMC and automatic differentiation with embedded numerical solving
Activities:
Fit a simple model of a fermentation
Project ideation 

Week 2: Bayesian statistics in practice

Day 6
Theme: Software for Bayesian statistics
Topics: types of software, rules of thumb for choosing between alternatives
Activities:
Research a Bayesian software library
Improve the fermentation model and update the course repository

Day 7
Theme: Difficult models
Topics: difficult measurements (missing data, saturation, heteroskedasticity), difficult priors (colinearity, dependence, tail information, non-parametric models)
Activity: fit a difficult model and add it to the course repository

Day 8 
Theme: The state of the art
Topics: recent algorithms, models and applications
Activities
Some interesting topics in Bayesian statistics research
Find and read an interesting recent Bayesian statistics paper
Decide in committees how to improve the main model based on findings

Day 9
Theme: Making small improvements
Activities:
Project planning
Make a concrete plan for improving Bayesian statistics. For example, find a live issue in a Bayesian software library and try to fix it, or draft a proposal for making conferences more inclusive.

Day 10
Theme: Gaps in the field
Activities:
Discuss and present: the biggest problem with computational Bayesian statistics?
Present project plans, get and give peer feedback
