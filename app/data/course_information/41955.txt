          <Contents>Stabilitetsanalyse for plane elastiske rammer ved deformationsmetoden. Plastisk analyse af rammekonstruktioner ved brug af nedre-og øvre-værdimetoder.

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Stability analysis for two-dimensional elastic frames by the deformation method. Plastic analysis of frame structures using lower and upper bound methods.
Basic plate theory including moments, shear forces, corner forces and virtual work.
Stability analysis of plates.
Introduction to the finite element method for plates based on the principle of virtual work.
Calculation of load carrying capacity in plate bending plasticity based on the yield line method. Introductory theory for thinwalled beams.</Contents>
