        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Emnerne vil være: introduktion til grundvandsstrømninger, reaktiv transport i porøse medier, beskrivelse af biogeokemiske processer, herunder begreber som ligevægt, sorption, ionbytning, carbonatsystemet, redoxprocesser, grundvandsforurening via geogene og diffuse kilder (f.eks. nitrat, pesticider, PFAS, arsen). Anvendelse af indsigt i  grundvandsstrømninger og stoftransport til at løse praktiske problemer relateret til  management af grundvandsressourcen, vandkvalitet og vandindvinding.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset anvender en blanding af forelæsninger, opgaver, modelberegninger, afleveringsopgaver, og præsentationer. Afleveringsopgaver foregår i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Appelo &amp; Postma. Geochemistry, groundwater and pollution, 2nd Ed. Balkema, 2005
Extracts from textbooks and Scientific literature</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Det overordnede mål med kurset er at give de studerende de kompetencer, der er nødvendige for en håndtering  af grundvandsressourcen mht. kvalitet. Studerende vil opnå kompetencer indenfor; i) grundvandsstrømninger, transportprocesser og vandindvinding ii), vandkvalitet af grundvandsmagasiner iii) vandkemi og grundvandskvalitet iv) beregning  af geokemiske ligevægte og (bio)geokemiske reaktioner. Eksempler og opgaver vil blive udvalgt med henblik på at illustrere centrale teoretiske problemstillinger, udfordringer for grundvandskvaliteten og give de studerende indsigt i den praktiske forvaltning af grundvandskvalitet.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Topics will include: introduction to groundwater flow, reactive transport in porous media, and water abstraction. Description of biogeochemical processes including concepts of equilibrium, sorption and ion exchange, carbonate system, redox processes, major problems of groundwater contamination by diffuse pollution sources (e.g., nitrate, pesticides, PFAS and arsenic contamination), as well as application of insight into flow, transport and reactive processes to solve practical problems related to  management of quality and quantity of groundwater.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course employs a mix of lectures, tutorials, computer modelling, written assignments and an oral presentation. Assignment work is conducted in groups.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Appelo &amp; Postma. Geochemistry, groundwater and pollution, 2nd Ed. Balkema, 2005
Extracts from textbooks and Scientific literature</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall objective of the course is to provide students with the technical skills required for groundwater resources quality management. Students will gain competences in i) groundwater flow and transport processes, ii) water quality assessment including quantitative evaluation of major environmental problems due to diffuse and geogenic contamination  of aquifer systems, iii) aquatic chemistry and groundwater quality, iv) calculations of geochemical equilibria and (bio)chemical reactions. Examples and problems will be selected to illustrate key theoretical issues, groundwater quality challenges and expose the students to practical groundwater quality management.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
