        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset er projektorienteret og tager udgangspunkt i gennemførslen af et innovationsforløb med fokus på mekatroniske produkter.

I første halvdel af kurset vil de studerende gennemføre en mekatronikøvelse som vil give de studerende praktisk erfaring med design og drift af et mekatronisk produkt. I anden halvdel af kurset vil de studerende bruge denne erfaring til at gennemføre et innovationsforløb. Som centrale dele af dette innovationsforløb skal der i løbet af kurset arbejdes med: Problemidentifikation, idégenerering, indsamling og vurdering af kvantitative og kvalitative informationer, prototyping, samt test og afprøvning af ideer, prototyper og koncepter.

Igennem kurset vil de studerende skulle anvende de faglige kompetencer de har tilegnet sig i 62807, 62694 og 62809. Det anbefales derfor at de studerende tager disse kurser sideløbende med dette.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is project-oriented and starts with the implementation of an innovation process with a focus on mechatronic products.

In the first half of the course, the students will complete a mechatronics exercise which will give the students practical experience with the design and operation of a mechatronic product. In the second half of the course, the students will use this experience to complete an innovation process. As key elements of an innovation process, work will be done in these areas: Problem identification, idea generation, collection and assessment of quantitative and qualitative information, prototyping, and testing and testing of ideas, prototypes, and concepts.

Throughout the course, the students will have to use the competencies they have acquired in 62807, 62694, and 62809. It is therefore recommended that the students take these courses alongside this one.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online lectures, group work, project work and workshop exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The student must acquire his/her own Arduino after instructions during the course.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the students training in the implementation of an innovation process based on the development of a mechatronic product. Mechatronic products are mechanical products that contain electronic components such as sensors, actuators, and motors to control and monitor the products. Mechatronic products are thus central to the development of e.g. automated production facilities or robotics. Throughout the course, students will learn about the typical components of this type of product as well as typical control strategies.

Essential aspects of the course include; Insight and understanding of innovation processes as well as exercises in carrying out a development project in a group where the group participants may have different professional interests. In addition, the course will train cooperation across areas of interest with the creation of common goals in the group, time planning, division of work, etc.</Course_Objectives>
