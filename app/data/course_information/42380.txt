        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Dette kursus vil introducere de studerende til komplekse netværk bestående af leverandører, producenter, distributører, detailhandlere og andre aktører, der samarbejder om at bringe et produkt eller en service frem til kunden. Kurset vil vise, hvordan aktiviteter inden for forsyningsnetværk effektivt kan designes, koordineres og integreres ved hjælp af SCA med henblik på at øge effektiviteten, reducere omkostningerne og forbedre kundeservicen. Der tages derfor et end-to-end perspektiv på styringen og ledelsen af forsyningskæderne. På den operationelle side vil de studerende anvende SCA til efterspørgselsprognoser, produktionsplanlægning, lagerstyring og ordreopfyldelse. På den strategiske og taktiske side vil SCA blive brugt til netværksdesign, logistik- og transportplanlægning, leverandørvalg, udformning af forsyningskontrakter, outsourcing samt vertikalt og horisontalt samarbejde. De SCA-værktøjer, der anvendes, vil blive hentet fra en bred vifte af analytiske områder, herunder sandsynlighedsteori, statistik, maskinlæring, matematisk optimering og spilteori.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course will introduce students to complex networks consisting of suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, retailers, etc., that collaborate in bringing a product or service to the customer. It will be shown how activities within supply networks can be effectively designed, coordinated and integrated using SCA in order to increase efficiency, reduce costs, and improve customer service. An end-to-end view of supply chain management is therefore taken. On the operational side, students will use SCA for demand forecasting, production planning, inventory management, and order fulfillment. On the strategic and tactical side, SCA will be used for network design, logistics and transportation planning, supplier selection, supply contract design, outsourcing and vertical/horizontal collaboration. The SCA tools used will be drawn from a wide range of analytical domains, including probability theory, statistics, machine learning, mathematical programming and game theory.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>Supply Chain Analytics (SCA) utilizes analytical techniques from statistics, machine learning, mathematical optimization and game theory in order to leverage the availability of large amounts of data towards improved decision making and planning across supply chains. The purpose of this course is to teach students the basics of building analytical models to solve supply chain problems, and to build intuition into the logic behind these tools and their potential impact on the competitiveness of supply chains.</Course_Objectives>
