          <Contents>Teaching is built around a number of model examples, which broadly describe the statements of the problems with the chemical- and biochemical contents. The problems are solved numerically, applying programming as a tool. The possible model examples are (the choice may be different in the different years):

Modelling of the enzymatic hydrolyses

Modeling the chromatographic processes

Modeling of the diffusion through the coating

Modeling of adsorption separations

Modeling of the filtration of water containing solid particles</Contents>
