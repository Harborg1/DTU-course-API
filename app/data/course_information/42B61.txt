        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>You will learn how to build a solid financial foundation for your initiatives and how to assess and interpret the strategic and financial performance of your enterprise, its business units, and its projects. As a result, the financial and strategic implications of your decisions will become more transparent, significantly increasing your impact on business management. By the end of the course, you will be able to act as a capable discussion partner for financial colleagues and be better prepared for your next career move, potentially into higher management roles.
In sum, the course introduces you to the following learning outcomes in an international context:
1.	Demonstrate a thorough knowledge of finance, relevant theories, and methodological skills in an international setting. This includes understanding key financial principles and decision-making tools used to allocate resources and determine value.
2.	Adopt strategic perspectives and develop problem-solving abilities in managerial contexts. Students will learn to analyze and provide direction for solving complex issues related to strategy, financing, and investment in international organizations.
3.	Collaborate in diverse, cross-cultural teams to discuss, assess, and present financial and investment challenges in companies or projects. The course enhances personal effectiveness by fostering financial literacy and developing the ability to convincingly present and defend financial plans, supported by constructive feedback.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. Lectures are paralleled by casework, group work and assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Our aim is to enable participants to measure and manage financial information with a focus on long-term value and value creation. This is a “language and tool course” designed to equip participants with the mindset and the skills to develop the financial component of a well-argued business case aligned with strategic objectives, and to deliver a well-founded pitch to management. The course also enhances participants’ ability to communicate effectively—not only with CFOs, treasurers, and controllers, but also with boards, shareholders, and other fund providers.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Participation in the program requires separate admission to the MMT program. The price of the MMT program is DKK 420.000. Please contact DTU Executive MBA.</DTU_Remark>
