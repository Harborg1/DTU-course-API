        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course includes teaching on primary fire dynamic topics such as heat release rate, flame heights &amp; smoke plumes, ventilation flow, pressure profiles and temperature calculations for gas temperatures. The course also includes topics on heat exchange during fire processes, conservation equations for mass and energy, smoke filling, combustion products and an introduction to fire models, especially the so-called 2-zone models. The course also includes the importance of valid input parameters for use in fire dynamic analyses.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Teaching is conducted through lectures with integrated exercises. Voluntary problem sets are provided between lectures for supporting of the students learning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Karlsson, B. &amp; Quintiere, J. G.: Enclosure Fire Dynamics. CRC Press. Supplementary material will be provided during the course.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide students with an understanding of the physical laws and relationships that govern fire development and smoke propagation in building fires, as a basis for fire‑dynamic analyses and calculations.</Course_Objectives>
