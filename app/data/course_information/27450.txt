        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer mikrobiel biogødning og andre cellebaserede biosolutions, samt de underliggende videnskabelige, teknologiske og regulatoriske principper.
Kurset omfatter den end-to-end-baserede udvikling af mikrobielle biosolutions, herunder discovery, karakterisering og selektion, udvikling af bioprocesser og fremstilling, formulering, drivhus- og markforsøg, regulatoriske rammer samt kommercialisering. Undervisningen er baseret på forelæsninger, udvalgt videnskabelig litteratur og case-baserede øvelser, der giver de studerende en systematisk og anvendelsesorienteret forståelse af udviklingspipelinen for biosolutions.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger (online/video), diskussion/seminar og teoretiske øvelser.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kursets formål er at give de studerende en integreret forståelse af de videnskabelige, tekniske, regulatoriske og entreprenørielle aspekter, der indgår i udviklingen af mikrobiel biogødning og cellebaserede biosolutions, fra tidlig opdagelse til markedsparate produkter, samlet betegnet som F&amp;U-pipelinen.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces microbial biofertilizers and other cell-based biosolutions, and the underlying scientific, technological, and regulatory principles. The course covers the end-to-end development of microbial biosolutions, including discovery and characterization, bioprocess and manufacturing development, formulation, greenhouse and field testing, regulatory frameworks, and commercialization. Teaching is based on lectures, selected literature, and case-based exercises that provide students with a systematic understanding of the biosolutions development pipeline.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures (online/video), discussion/seminar, and theoretical exercise.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The objective of the course is to provide students with an integrated understanding of the scientific, technical, regulatory, and entrepreneurial aspects involved in developing microbial biofertilizers and whole-cell biosolutions, from early discovery to market-ready products, called the R&amp;D Pipeline.</Course_Objectives>
