        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer de grundlæggende principper for funktionsbaseret brandteknisk design med særligt fokus på den indledende, konceptuelle fase. Undervisningen tager udgangspunkt i udviklingen af en grovskitse, som danner ramme for diskussion og argumentation omkring centrale designvalg, før der arbejdes med detaljerede analyser.

Der arbejdes med internationale tilgange til brandteknisk design, herunder brugen af konceptuelle modeller, engineering judgement samt strukturering af designprocessen efter anerkendte metoder. Evakuering og tilhørende strategier behandles som en integreret del af konceptudviklingen. Kurset dækker også metoder til at indarbejde beredskab og indsatsforhold i et brandteknisk koncept (FBIM).

Beregningsmetoder introduceres og anvendes som understøttende værktøjer på relevante tidspunkter, men udgør ikke kursets hovedfokus. Der lægges vægt på at identificere, vurdere og begrunde løsningsmuligheder samt at dokumentere designvalg klart og fagligt velbegrundet.

Praktiske øvelser og diskussioner indgår som en væsentlig del af undervisningen, og de studerende arbejder både individuelt og i grupper med at udvikle, analysere og argumentere for konceptuelle brandtekniske løsninger.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the fundamental principles of performance-based fire safety design with a specific emphasis on the initial conceptual design phase. Teaching is centred around the development of an initial sketch that forms the basis for discussion and argumentation of key design choices before detailed analyses are undertaken.

The course presents international approaches to fire safety design, including the use of conceptual models, engineering judgement, and structured methodologies recognised in the field. Evacuation concepts and strategies are addressed as an integral part of the conceptual design process. Methods for incorporating emergency response considerations into the fire safety design concept (FBIM) are also introduced.

Simple analytical methods and calculations are applied where appropriate to support design reasoning, but they are not the primary focus of the course. Emphasis is placed on identifying, evaluating, and justifying possible design solutions and on developing well‑reasoned and clearly documented fire safety design concepts.

Practical exercises and discussions constitute a central part of the course, and students work both individually and in groups to develop, analyse, and justify conceptual fire safety solutions.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The teaching is given during 3 modules. It includes project work with project presentations as well as group-based report writing.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The course literature consists of selected standards, guidelines and technical reports. Materials will be made available through DTU Learn. Additional articles may be introduced during the course.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide students with a fundamental understanding of the key principles of the initial, conceptual phase of performance-based fire engineering design. Emphasis is placed on developing the ability to argue for and justify design choices, including why specific fire engineering approaches are applied, and how these choices form part of an integrated whole encompassing fire safety design, evacuation strategies, and the operational capabilities of the emergency response.</Course_Objectives>
