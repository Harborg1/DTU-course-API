        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I løbet af semestret gennemgås den organiske kemi på baggrund af moderne, mekanistiske begreber. Organisk kemisk nomenklatur indlæres ifølge IUPAC reglerne. Den organiske kemis funktionelle grupper behandles systematisk. Stereokemien behandles grundigt. Desuden introduceres de studerende til strukturen af carbokationer og carbanioner. De studerende indføres i strukturen af kulhydrater, proteiner og fedtstoffer. Metoderne MS, NMR og IR, gennemgås og deres anvendelser inden for organisk-kemisk stof identifikation introduceres.</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>At bibringe de studerende viden inden for den organiske kemi, således at de også kan anvende de organisk kemiske begreber inden for andre fagområder, inklusiv biokemi, biologisk kemi og miljøkemi. At bibringe de studerende den nødvendige baggrund for at forstå og vurdere den organisk-kemiske litteratur på et fagligt forsvarligt grundlag.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The organic chemistry is taught with basis in modern mechanistic concepts. The students are trained in the IUPAC nomenclature. The functional groups in organic chemistry are treated systematically. The stereo chemistry is treated in detail. The students are introduced to the chemistry of carbocations and carbanions. The structure of carbohydrates, proteins and lipids are presented. The methods MS, NMR and IR, are presented and the students are introduced to the use of these methods in identification of organic compounds.</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>The purpose of the course is to familiarize the students with organic chemistry to the extent that enables them to apply the concepts of organic chemistry to problems encountered in biochemistry. biological chemistry and environmental chemistry. To provide the necessary background for understanding the organic chemistry literature on a sound scientific background.</Course_Objectives>
