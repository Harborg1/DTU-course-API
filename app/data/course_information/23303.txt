          <Contents>Gennem forelæsninger, øvelser og gruppearbejde vil dette 13 ugers kursus dække de grundlæggende principper inden for materialer og fødevarevidenskab, teknologier samt miljø- og innovationsvurdering i relation til bæredygtig fødevareemballage.           <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus giver en introduktion til bæredygtig fødevareemballage og dækker de grundlæggende principper inden for materialer og fødevarevidenskab, teknologier samt miljø- og innovationsvurdering. Emballageløsninger påvirker fødevarers kvalitet, holdbarhed og miljøpåvirkning og kræver en balance mellem fødevaresikkerhed, miljøhensyn og teknologiske behov. De studerende lærer at analysere emballageudfordringer på baggrund af ovenstående og undersøge løsninger, der kan anvendes i fremtidssikrede emballagekoncepter med reel samfundsmæssig relevans. Den pædagogiske tilgang prioriterer studentercentreret, problemdrevet læring, hvilket sikrer, at de studerende bidrager aktivt til kursets retning og faglige dybde. Gennem gruppeprojekter og præsentationer demonstrerer de studerende deres praktiske forståelse og anvendelse af begreber inden for bæredygtig fødevareemballage.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Through lectures, exercises, and group work, this 13-week course will cover fundamental principles of materials and food science, technologies, environmental and innovation assessment regarding sustainable food packaging. Students will work in groups, on problem-driven projects, to design future-proof packaging concepts that integrate core concepts learned over the course. The students will present the knowledge gained during the course in a poster and in a written group report.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, and group work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This course provides an introduction to sustainable food packaging, covering fundamental principles of materials and food science, technologies, environmental and innovation assessment. Packaging decisions impact food quality, shelf life, and the environment, requiring a balance of food safety, environmental concerns, and technological needs. Students will learn to analyze packaging challenges, based on the above, and investigate solutions that could be used in future-proof packaging concepts with real-world relevance. The pedagogical approach prioritizes student-centered, problem-driven learning, ensuring that students contribute actively to the course's direction and depth. Through group projects and presentations, students will demonstrate their practical understanding and application of sustainable food packaging concepts.</Course_Objectives>
