        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Introduktion til grundprincipperne bag og metoder til data science og kompression inkl. grundlæggende informationsteori, som entropy, kryds-entropy og gensidig information. Dette omfatter entropi-begrebet som et informationsmål og gensidig information som et mål for delt information. Entropikodning vha. Huffman og aritmetisk kodning. Dekorreleríng af data ved prædiktion eller transformation til frekvensdomænet. Kvantisering af data til kodning med tab eller representation i rum af mindre dimension. En række eksempler på datakompression og -analyse præsenteres, fx. Kodning og analyse af:  datastrenge, billeder i frekvensdomænet (DCT og wavelets - JPEG billedkodnings-specifikationer), lydsignaler, video som billedsekvenser. En Anvendelser er fx kodning af billeder, video eller data fra en drone, samt højkvalitetsbilleder, fx til internet applikationer. 
Midtvejs vælges et projekt inden for de fremlagte områder.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to the basic principles and methods for data science and compression incl. basics of information theory as entropy, cross entropy and mutual information (MI). The entropy is a measure of information and mutual information a measure of shared information. This includes entropy coding by Huffman and arithmetic coding, decorrelation by prediction or transformation to the frequency domain and quantization of data for lossy coding and representation. 
Some examples of data for compression and analysis are presented, eg. coding and analysis of: Data strings, Images in the frequency domain (DCT and wavelets - JPEG image coding specifications), Audio signals, and video as image sequences. Application areas include coding and analysis of images, video and data from drones, as well as high-quality images, e.g. for internet applications. 
Midway, the students choose a project within the areas presented.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and individual project (in groups of two)
Exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the participants solid knowledge of the basic principles of data science and compression and methods for data compression e.g. for image communication. To enable the students to implement and evaluate data analysis and compression algorithms.</Course_Objectives>
