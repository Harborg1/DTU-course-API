        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset dækker forskellige aspekter af operations- og supply chain management (O&amp;SCM) i lyset af bæredygtighedsmål. Kurset består af følgende fem (ikke lige store) dele:

1. Produktdesign for bæredygtighed
2. Bæredygtige operationer, herunder produktion, servitisering og deleøkonomi
3. Bæredygtig logistik
4. Bæredygtige forsyningskæder, herunder digitale, multi-tier og cirkulær økonomi-aspekter
5. Forsyningsnetværk og samfundsmæssige dynamikker, herunder risiko og robusthed samt et sociopolitisk perspektiv

Derudover vil kurset træne og anvende forskningsmetodiske praksisser og værktøjer, særligt litteraturreview, casestudiearbejde og causal loop diagrammer, som er relevante for MSc-ingeniørstuderende.</Contents>

Kurset giver et bredt indblik i de grundlæggende udfordringer ved O&amp;SCM, og udfolder dermed kompleksiteten i at designe og styre komplekse operationer inden for globale forsyningskæder.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course covers different aspects of operations and supply chain management (O&amp;SCM) in light of sustainability goals. The course consists of the following five parts (not equal):

1. Product design for sustainability
2. Sustainable operations, including production, servitization, and the sharing economy
3. Sustainable logistics
4. Sustainable supply chains, including digital, multi-tier, and circular economy aspects
5. Supply networks and societal dynamics, including risk and resilience as well as a socio-political view

Moreover, the course will train and apply research methodology practices
and tools, in particular literature review, case study work, and causal loop
diagrams relevant to MSc engineering students.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, practice cases, exercises, project work and quizzes</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Course_Objectives>The course encompasses the fundamental approaches to operations and supply chain management (O&amp;SCM) based on a sustainability agenda encompassed in the UN sustainability goals. The course gives a broad overview of the fundamental concerns of O&amp;SCM, unfolding the complexity of designing and managing complex operations within global supply chains.</Course_Objectives>
