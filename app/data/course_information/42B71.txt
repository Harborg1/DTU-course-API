        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Globalization of markets and business, cultures, values and ethics, the sustainability imperative, and the expectations of society are the cornerstones of this module. The first half of the module explores the opportunities and challenges associated with globalization, cultures, and values; the second part investigates the tension zones, but also enabling relationships, between business and society. The module then concludes with the very practical challenges of managing global teams.

Practical experience in handling managerial issues in a foreign culture is a unique supplement to in-class sessions and discussions.  Therefore, a consultancy project in a foreign environment is integrated in the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. Lectures  are paralleled by casework, group work and assignments. Integral part of the course is Global Consultancy Project that includes a field trip to a Global location where participants act as consultants for local host companies.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of this module is to provide the participants with action-learning based knowledge  about relevant topics within Internationalization that can be applied in the participants’ companies.</Course_Objectives>
