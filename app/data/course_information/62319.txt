The aim of the course is to provide the students with basic building technical prerequisites to be able to design a small building in a digital environment.
The aim of the course is to combine skills from this course with subject-specific topics from the other courses on the Construction and Infrastructure program in the 1st semester.
 
The aim of the course is also to learn how to present and collaborate on a construction project.</Course_Objectives>
