          <Contents>Centrale elementer:
- Bygningsstatik
- Fundering af bygninger

I statik læres metoder til beregning af kræfter på husets bygningskonstruktioner for at vurdere, om konstruktionerne er stærke nok. Belastninger fra nyttelast, vind, sne og konstruktionens egenvægt omdannes til flade eller linjebelastninger. Disse fordeles på de enkelte bygningsdele som konstruktionsbelastninger. Metoder læres til fordelingen af ​​disse belastninger og beregningen af snitkræfter i strukturelementer i form af stang- og bjælkestrukturer og til styrketest ved tabelopslag.

Omkring fundamenter lærer de studerende det grundlæggende inden for geoteknik og dimensioneringsprincipper for at designe fundamenter.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Core Elements:
- Building statics
- Building foundations

In statics we will explain methods for calculating forces on building structures of the house in order to evaluate whether the constructions are strong enough. Loads from live load, wind, snow and the dead load of the structure are converted into area or line loads. These are distributed to the individual structural elements as construction loads.
Methods are taught for the distribution of these loads and the calculation of internal forces in structural elements in the form of bar and beam structures, and for strength testing by table look-up.

In foundations the students learn basics in geotechnics and dimensioning principles of building foundations.</Contents>

A specific focus is on statics and foundations. The calculations will be performed according to SBI-Instruction 271.</Course_Objectives>
