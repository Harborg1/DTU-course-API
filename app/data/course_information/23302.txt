        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset indeholder en generel beskrivelse af vand, kulhydrater, proteiner, lipider, mineraler, vitaminer, antioxidanter, emulsioner, tilsætningsstoffer, mikro- og nanopartikler samt deres kemiske reaktioner (f.eks. De studerende skal anvende deres viden på en udvalgt fødevare og præsentere deres resultater i form af en poster samt en skriftlig rapport.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, gruppe arbejde, rapport</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset sætter de studerende i stand til at anvende viden om fødevarekemi på fødevarer. Dette inkluderer kemiske reaktioner  og fysiske ændringer som f.eks. fedt, proteiner og kulhydrater deltager i og reaktionernes/ændringernes indflydelse på fødevarernes makroskopiske egenskaber. Herunder vurderes forarbejdning og lagringens indvirkning på udvalgte  fødevaretypers (f.eks. kød, fisk, cerealier, frugt og grønt).</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will consist of a general description of water, carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, minerals, vitamins, antioxidants, emulsions, food improvement agents, micro- and nanoparticles and their chemical reactions (e.g. Maillard reaction, lipid oxidation) and physical changes in relation to food. The students will apply the obtained knowledge to a selected food item and present their findings in the form of a poster and a written report. In addition, the students will practice the presentation of data from scientific manuscripts about food chemistry aspects.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Plenum, group work, report writing</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course will give the student the ability to apply food chemistry knowledge to foods. This includes chemical reactions and physical changes that involve, e.g., lipids, proteins and carbohydrates, and the effect of these reactions/changes on the macroscopic food properties. The course enables the students to evaluate the influence of processing and storage on selected food items (e.g. meat, fish, cereals, vegetables).</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
