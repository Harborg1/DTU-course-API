        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Vectors in plane and space. Dot product, Cross product and scalar triple product. Cartesian, polar and spherical coordinates. Homogeneous coordinates. Coordinate transformation.

Parametric curves, surfaces and solids. Implicitly given curves (in the plane) and surfaces (in space). Arc length parametrization of curves. Frenet-Serret basis, curvature and torsion. Normal of a surface. 

Translation, rotation, scaling, shearing, reflection and projection of a geometric object. 

Center of mass, moment of inertia. Composite motion.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>A course session of four hours duration for 10 of the 13 semester weeks. The sessions include presentation of course material and exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to strengthen the understanding of geometry in the plane and in space. The understanding is improved by defining and calculating geometric quantities, e.g. arc lengths or volumes. Vectors and matrices are used as a common tool, and are also used to emphasize the importance of the chosen coordinate system.</Course_Objectives>
