          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Offered teaching material through videos and quizzes, chat-based communication, artificial intelligens, peer-review, problem solving and lab work, exercises.

          <Course_Literature>Mohan, Ned. Power electronics: a first course. Wiley
Literature is in English language.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall purpose of the course is to provide the student with a basic understanding of power supplies (uninsulated and insulated) in DC and AC converters as well as one-phase and three-phase circuits. In addition, courses include a basic knowledge of the use of switch mode power electronics in simple, regulated systems, eg in motor drives, renewable energy systems and amplifiers.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>A number of exercises are part of the course. Those are distributed throughout the semester. The theoretical part of the course can be taken online.

          <Remark>A number of exercises/problems are part of the course. Those are distributed throughout the semester.

The setup of the course highly reflects working conditions for a practicing engineer.</Remark>
