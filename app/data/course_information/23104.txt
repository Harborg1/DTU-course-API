        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will cover  current and relevant food safety issues with regards to chemical hazards. The course presents relevant chemical hazards in the entire food production chain from farm to fork and includes chemical aspects of the risk assessment. The course will enable the students to evaluate relevant chemical hazards in specific food production chains, to identify critical steps in the production and establish measures that will control the chemical safety of the food product, including management of allergenic foods. The student will work with the topics through lectures, assignments, and group/project work.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, seminars, group work, group project assignment, group presentations and one group report.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course will enable the student to evaluate relevant chemical hazards in food production chains.The student will relate important chemical hazards to their possible health risks in the food chain to humans. This will include calculation and evaluation of the occurrence of chemical hazards in foods using relevant data. Through a project assignment, the student will assemble a chemical risk assessment and write a report, which covers chemical hazards, the raw materials, production methodologies for a given food product.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Due to the mandatory deliverable planned at the beginning of the course, it is recommended that the students register and participate from the course start date.</Remark>
