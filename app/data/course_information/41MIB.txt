        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course consists of independent individual project work within a fire engineering topic. The project includes formulation and delimitation of a problem, selection and application of relevant fire engineering methods and calculation tools, as well as analysis, evaluation, and documentation of results. The project is carried out under supervision and is concluded with a written report and an oral presentation.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course begins with a joint introductory day focusing on project work, including how to identify and define a suitable project topic, the formal requirements of the fire engineering project, literature search, and academic report writing. Subsequently, each student is assigned or selects a supervisor and proceeds with independent work on an individual project under supervision.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The literature is defined individually in collaboration with the supervisor and depends on the project topic.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The fire engineering project concludes the Master’s programme in Fire Safety Engineering and aims to enable the student to independently apply, integrate, and further develop fire engineering knowledge and methods to address a complex, practice‑oriented problem within fire safety. The project shall demonstrate the student’s ability to engage in in‑depth academic work, critical analysis, methodological rigour, and clear, professionally sound documentation.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is the final part of the education</Remark>
