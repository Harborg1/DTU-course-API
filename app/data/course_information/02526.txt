          <Contents>Kursets hovedindhold er løsning af konkrete cases med tilhørende problemstillinger. For hver case skal problemet analyseres, den matematiske model beskrives og implementeres på en computer, og der skal gennemføres beregninger og simuleringer, som belyser modellens relevans og anvendelighed.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser, og rapporter</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Matematisk modellering er en central del af løsningen af de fleste tekniske problemstillinger. Typisk begynder processen med formuleringen af en matematisk model, som derefter implementeres på en computer og anvendes til at understøtte laboratorieforsøg og sidenhen udviklingen af virkelige løsninger. Evnen til at formulere relevante matematiske modeller, implementere dem computerbaseret og anvende dem til at opnå meningsfuld indsigt er derfor en grundlæggende kompetence i moderne ingeniørarbejde. I kurset opnår de studerende værktøjer til at formulere matematiske modeller, udføre simulationer og beregninger samt kritisk analysere og formidle de opnåede resultater.</Course_Objectives>

Kurset er et retningsspecifikt kursus for BSc Anvendt Matematik.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The main content of the course is the solution of specific cases and their associated problems. For each case, the problem must be analyzed, the mathematical model described and implemented on a computer, and calculations and simulations carried out to illustrate the relevance and the applicability of the model. All of this must be documented in a structured manner. The methods applied may include discrete mathematics, image analysis, differential equations, materials science, operations research, and scientific computing. The course also includes peer grading of other students’ reports, and participation in this activity is a prerequisite for taking the exam.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, and reports</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Mathematical modeling is a central component in the solution of most technical problems. Typically, the process begins with the formulation of a mathematical model, which is then implemented computationally and used to support laboratory-scale experiments and, eventually, the development of real-world solutions. The ability to formulate relevant mathematical models, implement them on a computer, and use them to obtain meaningful insight is therefore a fundamental competence in modern engineering. In this course, students will acquire tools for formulating mathematical models, carrying out simulations and calculations, and critically analyzing and reporting the results.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is a prerequisite for the following advanced courses: 02610 Optimization and datafitting, 02614 High performance computing, 02616 Large-scale modeling, 02623 Finite element methods for partial differential equations, 02685 Scientific computing for differential equations. The course is a programme-specific course for BSc Applied Mathematics. The course is reserved for students from Applied Mathematics and General Engineering, and other students are welcome if there are available seats.</Remark>
