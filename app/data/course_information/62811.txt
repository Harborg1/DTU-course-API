          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to give the students a theoretical introduction to, and practical experience with mechatronics and robotics. The course encompasses the technical interaction between the fields of mechanics, electronics, and software development in industrial and robotic systems.

The course equips the student with the competence to design and construct automatic control systems in the control, regulation, and monitoring of industrial and robotic systems.

The course applies control engineering theories and methods used to manage, for instance, mechanical and thermal processes in practical systems, using sensors, actuators, and associated software development on controllers such as microcontrollers (e.g., Arduino and ESP32) or industrial controllers (e.g., PLCs), along with graphical user interfaces.</Course_Objectives>
