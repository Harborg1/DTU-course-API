        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>In Denmark as well as abroad, many kilometers of roads and railways are being built in open land. The course provides important competences for planning and designing roads and railways considering safety level, function, aesthetics and sustainability. The course compares the functions, earthwork volumes and environmental impact for different construction alternatives. The course includes the key fundamental elements, design methodologies and principles, use of digital tools as well as EIA applied to both road and railway aspects. The detailed design guidelines and calculation method are performed on horizontal and vertical alignment as well as cross section for both road and railway.</Contents>

The course aims to enable students to give and justify and communicate choices and solutions of assignments in writing and orally. The course aims to enable students to explain the use of digital tools.</Course_Objectives>
