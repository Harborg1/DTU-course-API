          <Contents>Kurset gennemføres som et innovationsprojekt med en hospitalsafdeling som live case, hvor gruppen udvikler casen i dialog med sundhedsprofessionelle på afdelingen.Der er lavet aftaler med case-stiller før kursets start.           <Course_Literature>Artikler og uddrag af bøger og andre ressourcer på Learn</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kursets mål er at sætte de studerende i stand til at afklare, problematisere og konstruktivt forholde sig til forholdet mellem organisationskultur, vidensdynamik, organisationsforandringer og værdiskabelse, og det underliggende sociotekiske innovationsøkosystem, og på den baggrund i praksis facilitere innovation i et innovationsøkosystem.
Kursusmålet er, at eleverne i samarbejde med partneren
- analysere den organisatoriske kontekst af deres specifikke sag
- afdæk og foreslå behov, der er relevante for partneren, som udgangspunkt for innovation
- facilitere en innovationsproces, baseret på ét prioiteret behov, der leverer et resultat, der i sidste ende skaber oplevet værdi for partneren
Den studerende skal kunne analysere og beskrive virksomhedens kontekst og innovationsøkosystemet ved at anvende relevante teoretiske rammer, udvikle en løsning, forklare hvordan det udviklede løsningen bidrager til værdiskabelsen for organisationen, og hvordan den kan implementeres.
Kurset omhandler både den teoretiske baggrund og de praktiske metoder og værktøjer, der anvendes inden for kursets område.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is carried out as an innovation-project  with a hospital ward as live-case, where the group develops the case in dialogue with the health care professionals. Agreements has been made with case-providers prior to course-start. The project begins with a context analysis. The group is responsible for planning its own time and for executing the innovation process, including coordination with the partner and the allocation of time to the field work. Theoretical concepts, literature and assignments are presented through guides and literature and discussed in class. Knowledge sharing and peer feedback between groups will be facilitate. The scope of theory is relatively extensive at the beginning of the course but decreases thereafter.</Contents>
          <Course_Literature>Articles and book excerpts and other ressources at Learn</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Course objectives are to enable the students to clarify, problematize and constructive relate to the relationship between organizational culture, knowledge dynamics, organizational changes and value creation, and the underlying socio-technological innovation ecosystem, and on that basis in praxis facilitate innovation in an innovation ecosystem.
The course goal is for the students, in collaborate with the partner, to
-analyze the organizational context of their specific case
-scout and suggest needs relevant for the partner, as an origin for innovation
-facilitate an innovation process based on one prioritized need, that delivers a result that ultimately creates value perceived by the partner
The student should be able to analyze and describe the company context and the innovation eco-system, applying relevant theoretical frameworks, develop a result, explain how the developed result contributes to the value creation of the organization, and how it can be implemented.
The course deals with both the theoretical background and the practical methods and tools used in the course field.</Course_Objectives>
