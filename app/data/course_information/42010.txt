        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course provides a foundational understanding of key business disciplines, equipping students with essential tools for business decision-making. It covers four core modules: business statistics, marketing, corporate finance and accounting, and investment. The course introduces fundamental concepts and analytical techniques relevant to various business functions. Through case studies and exercises, students will engage in practical applications.
The course emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical application, with case studies and real-world business scenarios integrated into the learning process. By the end of the course, students will have a broad understanding of how businesses operate and how financial, statistical, and marketing tools contribute to business success.</Contents>
