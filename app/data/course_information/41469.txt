          <Contents>Studerende lærer de grundlæggende principper for solstråling, herunder dens komponenter, geometri og spektralfordeling.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Students learn the fundamental principles of solar radiation, including its components, geometry, variability, and spectral effects. They gain hands-on experience in measuring and quality controlling solar irradiance as well as using reanalysis and satellite-derived datasets. The course also equips students with skills to model and forecast solar irradiance, enabling applications in photovoltaic and solar heating system performance assessment, energy trading, grid integration, and utilization of solar energy in buildings.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, experimental exercises, lecture notes, videos.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Students learn to quantify, analyze, and forecast the solar resource through ground-based measurements, satellite-derived datasets, numerical weather models, and empirical radiation models.</Course_Objectives>
