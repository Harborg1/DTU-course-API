        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Landmark-based registration; intensity-based registration (sum-of-squared differences, Mutual Information, principal axis transform); linear and non-linear transformation models (rigid and affine transformations, thin plate splines, B-splines); surface-based registration and segmentation; voxel-based segmentation using generative and discriminative methods (Gaussian mixture models, Markov random field priors, neural networks); optimization (Gauss-Newton, expectation-maximization, stochastic gradient descent); atlases; validation.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>lectures; computer exercises;</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

The participants will learn to solve typical image analysis problems arising in radiological departments and biomedical research organizations.</Course_Objectives>
