        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Grundlaget for bæredygtighedsvurdering af fødevarer: Hvad bæredygtighed betyder i fødevaresystemet (miljømæssigt, ressourcemæssigt, planetarisk), systemgrænser, funktionelle enheder, allokering i fødevareforsyningskæder.arealanvendelse og biodiversitet, vand- og næringsstofemissioner.
Oversigt over miljøpåvirkningskategorier, der er relevante for fødevarer: Klima (GWP - CO₂ eq), arealanvendelse og biodiversitet, vandforbrug og næringsstofsemisioner. 
Livscyklusvurdering (LCA) i praksis: Indsamling af opgørelsesdata for fødevareproduktionskæder, brug af databaser (Ecoinvent, Agri-fodaftryk), udføre en simpel LCA, identifikation af hotspots og fortolkning af resultater, samt introduktion til usikkerheds- og scenarietestning.
Bæredygtighed af interventioner i fødevaresystemer: Vurdering af interventioner såsom ændringer i opskriftsformulering, i emballage, i ingrediensindkøb eller forarbejdningsmetoder; forståelse af systemeffekter (f.eks. rebound, substitution, skift i fordelingn af byrder); evaluering af "anprisninger" af bæredygtighed i industri- og politiske sammenhænge; kommunikation af bæredygtighedsindikatorer til ikke-tekniske interessenter.
Integration af bæredygtighed med risikovurdering af fødevaresikkerhed: Hvorfor beslutninger om bæredygtighed i fødevarer skal tage hensyn til både miljøpåvirkninger og fødevaresikkerhed; identifikation af typiske afvejninger (f.eks. reduceret forarbejdning -&gt; lavere klimapåvirkning, men højere mikrobiel risiko); kombination af bæredygtighedsindikatorer med fødevaresikkerhedsmål (eksponering, risikokarakterisering, DALY'er); introduktion til Multi Criteria Decision Analysis.</Contents>

Deltagerne introduceres til livscyklusvurdering (LCA), centrale bæredygtighedsindikatorer, der er relevante for industri og regulatorer, samt udbredt software og databaser såsom OpenLCA og Ecoinvent.

DALY'er). Dette sætter deltagerne i stand til at understøtte evidensbaserede beslutninger i produktudvikling, regulatoriske vurderinger og fødevaresystemstrategi.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Foundations of food sustainability assessment: What sustainability means in the food system (environmental, resource, planetary boundaries), system boundaries, functional units, allocation in food supply chains. 
Overview of impact categories relevant for food: Climate impact (GWP - CO₂ eq), land use and biodiversity proxies, water use, and nutrient emissions.
Life Cycle Assessment in practice: Inventory data collection for food chains, using database inventories (Ecoinvent, Agri footprint), performing a simple LCA, identifying hotspots and interpreting results, and intro to uncertainty and scenario testing.
Sustainability of food-system interventions: Assessing interventions such as recipe reformulation, packaging changes, shifts in ingredient sourcing, or processing innovations; understanding system effects (e.g., rebound, substitution, shifting burdens); evaluating sustainability “claims” in industry and policy contexts; communicating sustainability indicators to non-technical stakeholders.
Integrating sustainability with food-safety risk assessment: Why food sustainability decisions must consider both environmental performance and food-safety risk; identifying typical trade offs (e.g., reduced processing -&gt; lower climate impact but higher microbial risk); combining sustainability indicators with food-safety metrics (exposure, risk characterisation, DALYs); introduction to Multi Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA).</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Online e-lectures - self study.
Weekly online sessions of 3-4 hours over 12 weeks. The sessions will be a mixture of lectures, exercises, student presentations, and Q&amp;A. Teaching days will be announced in due time before course start.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This online introductory course provides professionals in the food sector with the fundamental concepts and practical tools needed to assess the environmental sustainability of foods and food systems. Participants are introduced to screening-level life cycle assessment (LCA), key sustainability indicators relevant for industry and regulators, and widely used software and databases such as OpenLCA and Ecoinvent.

The course emphasises hands on understanding of system boundaries, data requirements, interpretation of environmental indicators, and basic uncertainty considerations. Towards the end f the course, participants apply these concepts to examine trade offs between food sustainability and food safety, integrating their prior knowledge of quantitative risk assessment and disease burden metrics (e.g., DALYs). This equips participants to support evidence based decisions in product development, regulatory assessments, and food system strategy.</Course_Objectives>
