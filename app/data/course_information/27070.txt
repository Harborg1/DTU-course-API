        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Den molekylære og cellulære baggrund for immunreaktioner. Det medfødte immunsystem. Mønstergenkendelsesreceptorer. Syntese, struktur og funktion af immunglobuliner. T-celle-receptoren. Vævstypeantiger. Antigen-antistofreaktioner. Celle-medieret immunitet. Cytokiner, leukocytaktivering og migration. Celle-medierede cytotoksiske reaktioner.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset består af læsestof og forelæsningsvideoer, som ses hjemme forud for gruppeopgaver og en fælles opsamling i undervisningslokalet. Aktiv læring understøttes gennem obligatoriske ugentlige selvrefleksioner over centrale indsigter og udfordrende aspekter. Der udarbejdes tre peer-reviewede opgaver i grupper. Disse samles i en afsluttende rapport, som præsenteres i kursets sidste undervisningsuge. Frivillige elektroniske quizzer stilles til rådighed for at understøtte indlæringen.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Kursusbog:
Janeway’s Immunobiology by Kenneth Murphy, Casey Weaver &amp; Lesley Berg, 10th ed., WW Norton (ISBN 9780393884913) (det er ikke muligt at anvende tidligere udgaver af bogen).</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At gøre den studerende bekendt med de grundlæggende principper og celletyper der indgår i vores immunforsvar, herunder sammenspillet mellem medfødt og adaptiv immunitet. At give den studerende den teoretiske baggrund for læsning og forståelse af immunologisk litteratur.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The molecular and cellular background for immune reactions. The innate immune system. Pattern recognition receptors. Synthesis, structure and function of immunoglobulins. The T-cell receptor. The major histocompatibility complex (MHC). Antigen-antibody reactions. Cell-mediated immunity. Cytokines and leucocyte activation and migration. Cell-mediated cytotoxic reactions.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course consists of reading materials and lecture videos watched at home, prior to group assignments and a plenary wrap-up in the class room. Active learning is supported by mandatory weekly self-reflections on key insights and challenging aspects. Three peer-reviewed segment recaps are prepared in groups. These are compiled into a final report, which is presented in the final course week. Optional electronic multiple-choice quizzes are made available to support learning of key elements.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The course book is:
Janeway’s Immunobiology by Kenneth Murphy, Casey Weaver &amp; Lesley Berg, 10th ed., WW Norton (ISBN 9780393884913) (earlier editions of the book cannot be used).</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To make the student familiar with the basics of immunology including the integration between innate and adaptive immunity. To give the student the theoretical knowledge for reading and understanding immunological literature.</Course_Objectives>
