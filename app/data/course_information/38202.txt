          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at forberede eleverne til at håndtere og styre den menneskelige side af at bygge et nyt teknologiprojekt. Kurset vil hjælpe eleverne med at udvikle færdigheder til at klare det dynamiske, komplekse og evigt skiftende miljø i et hurtigt voksende nyt teknologiprojekt.

De studerende vil lære, hvordan man skaber og administrerer en missionsdrevet startup-kultur og identitet, hvordan man forbereder denne kultur til vækst, herunder hvordan man laver en overgang til en ekspanderende politisk kultur. De studerende vil behandle problemstillinger relateret til: indførelsen af ​​organisationsstruktur med formaliserede systemer og processer; understøtte denne overgang med en ansættelsesstrategi og opbygning og opretholdelse af en samarbejdskultur. Desuden giver kurset de studerende mulighed for at udforske forskellige talenterhvervelser og relationsstyringsstrategier for at understøtte de nye venture-missioner.

Endelig introducerer kurset eleverne til de mest almindelige årsager til fejl i tidlige og sene stadier af teknologiprojekter og forbereder dem på at identificere og håndtere den konstante risiko.</Course_Objectives>

          <Contents>Leadership and culture.
Create and manage a mission-driven new venture culture and identity.
How to assemble, motivate and lead a team when you have big ambitions, limited resources and a compass instead of a map.
How to distribute responsibility among the co-founding team.
How to prepare a culture for growth.

Hiring Strategies
Change in hiring profile as venture grows.
Talent Acquisition. Hiring globally. Setting up cross boarder teams.

Relationship Management
Managing investors.
When to establish a board of Directors. Managing the individuals. Profile of board and how it changes with growth.

Organizational Design.
When and how to make the transition from a mission-driven new venture to a more formalized company structure, build on systems and processes.

Communication
Methods of communication and how it changes with growth.

New Venture Failure.
The most common reasons for failure in early and late stage ventures.
How to mitigate and handle the constant risk of failure.
How to both avoid and embrace failure. Failure as a way of learning. Failure as a catalyst for pivoting the business.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to prepare students to deal with and manage the human side of building a new technology venture. The course will help students develop skills to cope with the dynamic, complex and ever-changing environment of a rapidly growing new technology venture. 

The students will learn how to create and manage a mission-driven startup culture and identity how to prepare that culture for growth including how to make a transition to an expanding political culture. The students will address issues related to: the introduction of organizational structure with formalized systems and processes; supporting this transition with a hiring strategy and building and sustaining a collaborative culture. Furthermore, the course provide opportunities for students to explore different talent acquisition and relationship management strategies to support the new venture missions. 

Finally, the course introduces students to the most common reasons for failure in early and late stage technology ventures and prepare them to identify and manage the constant risk.</Course_Objectives>
