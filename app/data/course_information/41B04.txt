        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset Brandkemi introducerer grundlæggende viden om reaktionen på varme af faste, flydende og gasformige materialer, der typisk anvendes i bebyggede omgivelser. Kemiske sammensætninger og kemiske strukturer af en række materialer gennemgås. Det forklares, hvordan de forskellige materialer vil danne gasformige stoffer gennem fordampning og/eller pyrolyse der efterfølgende kan antændes og brande. Vigtige parametre herunder er fordampningsenergien, antændelsesgrænserne i luft og antændelsesprocessen. De kemiske mekanismer og kinetikken bag disse processer introduceres. Vigtige begreber herunder er forbrændingsvarmen og varmedannelsesraten under godt og dårligt ventilerede brandforhold. Brandforholdene karakteriseres ved hjælp af teorien om brændstof-ilt-ækvivalensforhold. Derfor arbejdes der med de kemiske reaktionsligninger, og estimering af forbrændingsvarmen ved hjælp af konceptet om dannelsesvarme for atomer og molekyler præsenteres. Sidst og ikke mindst vises dannelsen af ​​giftige forbrændingsprodukter, og røggassers toksicitet forklares. Kurset er delvist teoretisk og delvist eksperimentelt, da hver studerende vil udføre en række laboratorieforsøg i løbet af kurset.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course Fire Chemistry introduces basic knowledge on the reaction to heat of solid, liquid and gaseous materials typically applied in the built environment. Chemical compositions and chemical structures of a number of materials are reviewed. It is explained how the various materials will generate gaseous species through evaporation and/or pyrolysis. Important parameters herunder are the energy of evaporation, the flammability limits in air and the ignition process.  The chemical mechanisms and kinetics behind these processes are introduced. Important terms herunder are the heat of combustion and the Heat release rate under well and poorly ventilated fire conditions. The fire conditions  are characterized using the theory of the fuel - oxygen equivalence ratios. Therefore  the chemical reaction equations are worked with and  the estimation of the heat of combustion using the concept of the Heat of Formation of atoms and molecules is presented. Last not least the generation of toxic combustion products is shown and the toxicity of smoke gases is explained. The course is partly theoretical and partly experimental, as each student will conduct a number of laboratory experiments during the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>lectures combined with individual and group based exercises/assignments, lab experiments that require physical presence at DTU.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The students vil obtain a basic understanding about  fire chemistry and get skills within the most common methods and theories applied in the field. This enables the students, e.g.  to predict ignition properties, flame spred characteristics, and the type and yields of combustion products an dother impåortant parameters within Fire safety engineering.</Course_Objectives>
