        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>I kurset vil forskellige typiske danske byggetraditioner gennem tiderne blive præsenteret herunder hvilke styrker og svagheder, der kendetegner disse. 

Den studerende vil blive indført i bygningsingeniørens traditionelle bidrag til transformations- og renoveringsprocesser f.eks. gennem kendskab til anvendte materialer og opbygninger. Dette er med henblik på at kunne analysere en eksisterende bygning, både med hensyn til typiske skader og styrker og dermed potentialer for ombygning og transformation. I dette vil der også indgå problemstillinger omkring forskellige typer af farlige stoffer, der er brugt gennem tiden.

På baggrund heraf analyseres forskellige muligheder for renovering, både overordnet og på bygningsdelsniveau.

Ved vurdering af muligheder for renovering og transformation vil der være fokus på optimal ressourceanvendelse, centrale emner vil derfor være:
- Levetid og levetidsforlængelse, ikke blot teknisk men også funktionelt og æstetisk
- Genanvendelse af elementer i byggeriet
- Bidrag til CO2 reduktion samt livscyklusanalyse (LCA) / livscyklusomkostning (LCC) beregninger
- Energibesparelser
- Restbæreevner</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will present various typical Danish building traditions of different eras, including the strengths and weaknesses that characterize these. 

The student will be introduced to the civil engineer's traditional contribution to transformation and renovation processes, e.g. through knowledge of applied materials and structures. This is to be able to analyze an existing building, both in terms of typical damage and strengths and thus potentials for conversion and transformation. This will also include issues regarding different types of hazardous substances that have been used over time.

Based on this, various options for renovation are analysed, both overall and at building component level.

When assessing opportunities for renovation and transformation, the focus will be on the optimal use of resources; key topics will therefore be:
- Service life and lifetime extension, not only technically but also functionally and aesthetically
- Reuse of elements in construction
- Contribution to CO2 reduction and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)/Life Cycle Costing (LCC) calculations
- Energy savings
- Residual carrying capacity</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, report (case-study) in groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives you the opportunity to plan the renovation and transformation of existing buildings in order to extend the life of buildings and thus strengthen sustainable development.

Once you have followed the course, you can assess the condition of existing buildings in order to repair damage and improve usability.</Course_Objectives>
