        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Facilitering af innovationsprocessen kan være afgørende for fremtidige ingeniører for at skabe nye perspektiver, især for at fremme og drive global forandring i håndteringen af de samfundsmæssige, miljømæssige og sundhedsrelaterede udfordringer, vi står overfor.

Gennem kurset opnår de studerende erfaringer og kompetencer i at anvende og vejlede systematiske tilgange til at facilitere innovation i en ingeniørkontekst. Studerende vil udvikle en stærk instinkt og unikke indsigter i, hvordan facilitering af integreret innovation praktiseres. 
De vil opleve og stå over for udfordringer gennem tværfagligt samarbejde, mens de arbejder på en reel industriel udfordring. Hvert projektteam (bestående af studerende, der tager kurset 'Innovation in Engineering') vil blive tildelt en teamfacilitator. Som facilitator vil du bringe dine erfaringsbaserede innovationsfærdigheder i spil og vejlede det tværfaglige team gennem hele projektarbejdet.

Kurset er organiseret i to hoveddele. 
Den første uge - BOOST-modulet - fokuserer på at engagere de studerende i emnet innovation gennem en række oplæg, lære teamet at kende og introducere de studerende til en systematisk tilgang til at facilitere innovation samt andre relevante teorier. I begyndelsen af den første uge vil de studerende facilitere op til to teams baseret på deres valgte innovationsudfordring. 
Den anden og tredje uge - EXPERIENCE-modulet - fokuserer på at facilitere teamet gennem innovationsprocessen. Dette skal resultere i et koncept, som teamet skal præsentere for interessenterne ved slutningen af processen.

Kurset består af forelæsninger, læsemateriale, teambaserede øvelser, opgaver, peer-reviews og multiple-choice quizzes. Læringsaktiviteterne og afleveringerne er teambaserede oplevelser, og derfor er det vigtigt at deltage i undervisningen på DTU Campus.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset er en blanding af gruppearbejde, øvelser, forelæsninger på campus samt videoforelæsninger og opgaver.
Gruppearbejdet forventes at foregå på campus i hele varigheden af de 3 uger. Kurset kan ikke tages som et online kursus.
See læringsmål for uddybelse.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at give den studerende kompetencer til at facilitere innovation i tværfaglige teams i en ingeniørkontekst. 
Studerende vil:

- Forstå, hvad der kræves for at facilitere en innovationsproces i en industry udfordring.
- Opleve det varierede innovationslandskab ved at navigere og anvende deres færdigheder i det, givet deres udvidede innovations- og faciliteringskompetencer.
- Facilitere samarbejdsinitiativer i en gruppesammenhæng for at udvikle nye perspektiver.
- Identificere værdi og begrænsninger af nye løsningsorienterede ideer og motivere til at modne disse til et endeligt konceptforslag.
- Tilegne sig DTU's Signatur Innovations Mindset i anvendelsen af tværfaglige teams og videreudvikle deres egen tilgang til innovation.
- Udvide deres værktøjskasse med strukturerede metoder for at øge innovationssandsynligheden.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Facilitating the innovation process can be crucial for future engineers to invigorate new perspectives, particularly to nurture and drive global change in tackling the societal, environmental and health-related challenges we face.

Through the course, students acquire experiences and competencies in applying and guiding systematic approaches for facilitating innovation in an engineering context. 
Students will develop a strong instinct and unique insights into how the facilitation of integrated innovation is practised. 
They will experience and face challenges through multidisciplinary collaboration while working on a real industry challenge. 
Each project team (consisting of students who are taking the course 'Innovation in Engineering') will be assigned a team facilitator. 
As a facilitator, you will bring your experience-based innovation skills into play and guide the multidisciplinary team through the entire project work.

The course is organized into two main parts. 
The first week - the BOOST module - is focused on engaging the students with the topic of innovation by an array of speakers, getting to know the team and introducing students to a systematic approach to facilitating innovation, as well as other relevant theories. 
At the start of the first week, the students will facilitate up to two teams based on their chosen innovation challenge. 
The second and third week - the EXPERIENCE module - are focused on facilitating the team through the innovation process. This should result in a concept that must be presented by the team to the stakeholders at the end of the process.

The course consists of lectures, reading material, team exercises, assignments, peer-reviews and multiple-choice quizzes. The learning activities and submissions are team-based experiences and for that reason, it is important to participate in class at DTU Campus.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is a mix of group work, exercises, lectures on campus as well as video lectures and assignments.
The group work is expected to take place on campus for the entire duration of the 3 weeks. The course can not not be taken as an online course.  
See learning objectives for more details.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to equip the student with competencies in facilitating innovation in multidisciplinary teams in an engineering context. 
Students will:

- Understand what it takes to facilitate an innovation process in an industry challenge. 
- Experience the varied innovation landscape by navigating and applying their skills in it given their expanded innovation and facilitation competencies.
- Facilitate collaborative initiatives in a group context to develop new perspectives.
- Identify value and limitations of new solution-oriented ideas and encourage maturing these into a final concept proposition.
- Acquire the DTU's signature Innovation Mindset in the application of multidisciplinary teams 
and further develop their own approach to innovation.
- Expand their toolkit of structured methods to increase the innovative probability.</Course_Objectives>

The Innovation course (38400, 38402, 38404) is intended for students with no or introductory innovation competencies, see course prerequisites.</DTU_Remark>
