        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kemisk kinetik, reaktionsmekanismer for homogene og heterogene reaktioner, laboratorieundersøgelser i batch og strømningsreaktorer, opstilling af hastighedsudtryk og beregning af de indgående modelparametre. Dimensionering og design af ideale reaktorer; optimalt temperaturforløb; Katalytiske reaktorer og bioreaktorer. Vigtigste tekniske reaktortyper og processer, beregning af industrielle reaktorer.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Klasseundervisning med opgaveregning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give den studerende et grundlæggende kendskab til kobling mellem kemisk kinetik og design af reaktorer, således at han/hun bliver i stand til at udføre beregningsmæssige løsninger på reaktionstekniske problemer. Herunder planlægning af laboratorieundersøgelser, behandling af forsøgsresultater, samt dimensionering af simple reaktorer til homogene, heterogene og bioteknologiske reaktioner. At give kendskab til de vigtigste reaktortyper og deres tekniske udformning og anvendelse i industrielle processer.

Derudover får de studerende kendskab til Python-programmering og løser enkle reaktionstekniske opgaver ved hjælp af programmering. Dette foregår via en online-baseret kursusdel, hvor de studerende gennemgår forskellige tutorials selvstændigt.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Lærebog: H. Scott Fogler: Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering. 4. udgave, 2006, Prentice Hall.</Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Chemical kinetics. Reaction mechanisms for homogeneous and heterogeneous reactions. Laboratory investigations in batch and flow reactors, determination of the rate equation. Determining size and design of ideal reactors and optimum temperature progression. Catalytic reactors and bioreactors. Important industrial reactor types and processes, calculations on industrial reactors. Students use Python to solve the assignments; material and practical tutorials to learn Python are provided. The content of the course is relevant for sustainable production which will be illustrated in the course. It contributes to SDG 7 and 12 (Affordable and Clean Energy / Responsible Consumption and Production).</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and problem sessions</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to give the students a basic knowledge of the coupling between chemical kinetics and reactor design so that they will be able to conduct calculations to solve chemical reaction engineering problems. These involve planning of laboratory investigations, analysis of experimental results, and dimensioning of simple reactors for homogeneous, heterogeneous and biotechnological reactions. The student should know the most important types of reactors and their technical design and operation in industrial processes.

In addition, the students gain knowledge of Python programming and solve simple reaction engineering tasks using programming. This takes place through an online-based course module where the students independently go through various tutorials.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Textbook: H. Scott Fogler: Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering, 4. ed., 2006, Prentice Hall.</Remark>
