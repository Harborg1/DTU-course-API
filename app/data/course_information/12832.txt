          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Gruppeopgaver, individuelle opgaver og refleksion samt ekskursioner.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Dette kursus giver forståelse af spændevidden af ingeniørarbejde i Grønland, de muligheder uddannelserne åbner og de kompetencer arbejdet kræver. 
Der gives en introduktion til projektarbejde i grupper og dets faser, den interkulturelle interaktion i arbejdet samt til anvendelse af mundtlige præsentationer, interviews, poster-præsentationer og rapportskrivning som værktøjer til kommunikation på studiet og i arbejdet som ingeniør.
Der etableres en forståelse for det moderne grønlandske samfund og dets historiske forudsætninger og de ingeniørmæssige udfordringer knyttet til infrastrukturen i de grønlandske ø-driftssamfund behandles.
Der gives en introduktion til Grønlands økonomi, arbejdsmarked og erhvervsudvikling samt til ledelseskultur i Grønland inkl. formel og uformel ledelse.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Core elements: 
- Engineering work in Greenland
- Communication and collaboration
- Infrastructures in a Greenlandic island operation context
- Social and cultural understandings 
- Culture, management and communication in Greenland 

Topics: 
Engineering competences and jobs and career opportunities in Greenland
Oral and written dissemination
Project work in groups
Greenlandic infrastructural development and the challenges of the infrastructure
Business Conditions / labor relations in the building, construction and fisheries in Greenland 
Historical management in Greenland and modern management theory (with relevance to the Arctic labor markets) 
Cultural analysis and communication mechanisms</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Group assignments, individual assignments and reflection as well as excursions.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>This course provides an understanding of the span of engineering work in Greenland, the possibilities the educations provide, and the required competences of engineering work. 
An introduction is given to project work in groups and its phases including the importance of intercultural interaction, as well as to the use of oral presentations, interviews, poster presentations and report writing as tools for communication during the education and when working as an engineer.
An understanding of the modern Greenlandic society and its historical preconditions and engineering challenges related to infrastructure in the Greenland island operated communities are in focus.
An introduction to Greenlandic economy, labor marked, industrial development and management culture in Greenland including formal and informal management is provided.</Course_Objectives>
