          <Contents>Kurset er opdelt i flere moduler. Hvert modul inkluderer forelæsninger og demonstrationer om de relevante emner, der efterfølges af individuelle øvelser. Under øvelserne øver de studerende sig på det, der blev introduceret i forelæsningen.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser, individuelt projektarbejde, workshops og præsentationer.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Prototyping er en væsentlig del af en designproces og opbygning af fysiske modeller er et virkemiddel for designere til at teste idéer og få input fra interessenter. Dette grundlæggende kursus giver et overblik indenfor prototypingmetoder og -teorier – dvs. hvad, hvornår og hvorfor. Ligeledes opnås praktiske færdigheder, som er forudsætninger for fremstilling, dvs.- hvordan. Kursusdeltagerne bliver introduceret til en række prototypeteknikker, som blandt andet inkluderer teoretisk viden for at kunne vælge den mest formålstjenlige prototype, der bedst besvarer et bestemt spørgsmål. Der gennemgås best practices til test af prototyper og planlægning af prototyper. Studerende opnår ligeledes færdigheder og erfaring med fremstilling af prototyper, som f.eks. hands-on manuelle metoder, digital modellering og 3D printing. Endelig vil kursusdeltagere lære hvordan prototyper anvendes til test idéer og hvordan de bruges til at iterere og videreudvikle koncepter. I dette hands-on kursus vil de studerende lære at prototype idéer gennem forelæsninger, øvelser og individuelle aktiviteter.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course is divided into several modules. Each module includes lectures and demonstrations on the relevant topics that are followed by individual exercises. During exercises students practice what was introduced in lecture. Students rotate through the modules and complete individual and group assignments that are documented in a logbook.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, assignments, individual project work, workshops and presentations.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Prototyping is an essential part of the design process and building physical models enables designer to test ideas and get input from stakeholders. This fundamental course provides an overview of the methods and theories of prototyping - the what, when and why - as well as the practical skills needed for fabrication - the how. Students will be introduced to a variety of prototyping skills, including the theoretical knowledge needed to select the most appropriate type of prototype to answer a particular question, best practices for testing prototypes, and how to plan a prototype. Students will also develop skills and practice fabricating prototypes, including hands-on manual methods, digital modelling and 3D printing. Finally, students will learn how to use prototypes to test ideas and use the results to iterate and refine their concepts. In this hands-on course, students will learn how to prototype ideas through lectures, exercises, and individual activities.</Course_Objectives>
