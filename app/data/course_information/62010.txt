          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Holdundervisning, opgaveregning, praktiske øvelser</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset er obligatorisk for ingeniørstuderende på studieretningen Proces og Innovation.

Målet for kurset er at de studerende, der følger kurset, opnår en grundlæggende viden om udformningen af et fysisk produkt, ud fra de anvendte materialer og deres fremstillingsmetoder, samt træning i at vælge materialer, til specifikke produkter. Med begrebet anvendte materialer, menes de metaller og polymerer, der anvendes i moderne industriproduktion. Den studerende opnår indsigt i at vælge materialer både ud fra anvendelsesmæssige, holdbarhedsmæssige og miljømæssige/bæredygtighedsmæssige synspunkter.

Ydermere arbejdes med CAD, som supplement til de studerendes grundlæggende fremstillingsforståelse.</Course_Objectives>

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Classroom teaching, exercises, practical exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course is a mandatory course for engineering students on the Process and Innovation study line.

The aim of the course is that the students, who follow the course, gain basic knowledge about the design of a physical product by the applied materials and their manufacturing methods, as well as training in choosing materials for specific products. Applied materials, in this context, are the metals and polymers used in modern, industrial production. The student obtains insight in choosing materials both from points of view regarding application, durability, sustainability and environmental considerations.

Furthermore, working with CAD is used as a supplement to the students' fundamental understanding of manufacturing.</Course_Objectives>
