          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset består af syv moduler, hvor hvert modul varer 3 timer. Den første time er typisk en forelæsning og resten består af diskussion og fremlæggelse af opgaver i gruppen. En (lille) opgave kan f.eks. være at finde den bedst mulige titel til artiklen og denne diskuteres og forbedres i gruppen.  
Kurset bygger på at de studerende er aktive og hjælper sine medstuderende med at forbedre deres artikler.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Udtræk fra bøger om videnskabelig kommunikation (f.eks. Eloquent Science by David M. Schultz), artikler, og DTU Code of Conduct for Research Integrity og Writing Science - how to write papers that get cited and proposals that get funded af Joshua Schimel).</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At formulere et klart forståeligt og videnskabeligt underbygget resultat i en artikel til en relevant tidsskrift. Sammenlignet med andre kurser i videnskabelig kommunikation fokuserer vi mere på trinnet mellem et resultat (ofte fra meget avancerede metoder) til hvad resultatet faktisk betyder for andre grupper som forsker i samme område.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Kurset henvender sig til Ph.d.-studerende på Vind og Energisystemer, som er ved at skrive en videnskabelig artikel. Ph.d.-studerende fra andre institutter/universiteter er velkomne hvis deres studie er i nært relaterede fag.</DTU_Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>•	The DTU/Danish guidelines on authorships. How to work with co-authors as a lead author. 
•	Responsible use of AI in writing
•	The scientific method.
•	Choosing the right journal, identifying the audience.
•	DTU library services, search engines, open access, ORCid (guest lecture).
•	Paper structure, first outline, the IMRAD method. 
•	Design of figures.
•	Submission, review, editorial work.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course consists of seven session, where each session lasts 3 hours. The first hour typically consists of a lecture and the rest by student presentations of the assignments and discussions. An assignment can, for example, include to find the best possible title to the paper. The course relies on active student participation and that the students do their best to  give constructive feedback to the other students.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Excerpts from books on scientific writing, such as "Eloquent Science" by David M. Schultz, DTU Code of Conduct for Research Integrity, and "Writing Science - how to write papers that get cited and proposals that get funded" by Joshua Schimel</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To formulate a scientific message based on own research results in a way that is clear and simple, while including sufficient complex detail. Compared to other courses on scientific writing, we will use the benefit of coming from closely related research fields and focus more on the scientific content rather than the language.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is aimed at PhD students at DTU Wind and Energy Systems, who are ready to write a scientific journal paper; other students are welcome in case of closely related PhD subjects.</DTU_Remark>
