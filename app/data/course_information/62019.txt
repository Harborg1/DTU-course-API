          <Contents>Formålet med kurset er at introducere de studerende til interaktionsdesign.
Kurset omhandler bl.a. design patterns og -principper, usability, brugertests.

Kurset bygger videre på det tidligere kursus 62001 'Innovation, design og procesfacilitering', og det forventes, at de studerende anvender teorimetoder, design- og innovationsprocesser fra dette tidligere kursus, hvor det er relevant.

Eksamen består af aflevering af projektrapport (gruppe), en individuel refleksionsrapport, en portfolio, en gruppepræsentation samt individuel mundtlig eksamen.

Vigtigt: Der vil være et antal obligatoriske opgaver, som den studerende skal bestå for at kunne gå til eksamen. Opgaverne kan være skriftlige opgaver, mundtlige prøver, præsentationer og/eller multiple choice tests.
Det er en betingelse for deltagelse i gruppeprojekt og eksamen, at ALLE opgaver gennemføres rettidigt.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The goal of the course is to introduce the students to Interaction Design.

The students must submit a report with a clear argument for the chosen solution and application of the curriculum.

The course builds on the previous course 62001 'Innovation, design and process facilitation', and it is expected that the students use theory methods, design and innovation processes from this previous course where relevant.

The exam is submission of a project report (group), an individual reflection report, a portfolio, a group presentation and an individual oral exam.

Important: A number of mandatory assignments will be posed to the students during the course. The students have to hand in on time and pass ALL of them in order to be allowed to participate in the exam. The assignments can take the form of written text, oral tests, presentations and/or multiple choice tests.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, workshops</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Preece, J., Sharp, H. og Rogers, Y. (2019) Interaction Design: Beyond Human-Computer Interaction, 6th edition, John Wiley &amp; Sons Ltd.

Krug, S. (2009). Rocket Surgery Made Easy: The Do-It-Yourself Guide to Finding and Fixing Usability Problems, New Riders

And selected articles.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course builds on mandatory courses in the first semester of Process &amp; Innovation Bachelor of Engineering program. The course is not advised as an elective for students from other programs.
The overall goal is to introduce the students to Interaction Design:
- The student will get an understanding of interaction design - and UX and usability
- Be able to uncover user needs
- Create an interaction design
- Apply design and usability principles
- Develop a solution through an iterative prototyping and test process gradually taking a mock-up from low-fi to hi-fi
- Clearly present a written argument for the solution
- Reflect upon own learning and contribution in a team</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Section of Innovation Processes and Entrepreneurship
