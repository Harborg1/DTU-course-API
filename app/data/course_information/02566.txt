          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser og projektopgaver</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>I dette kursus vil studerende lære at modellere oplevelser for virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality osv. (samlet under xR). Studerende vil lære essentielle metoder og teknologier til at erhverve geometri, materialers udseende og belysning. Blandt andet dækker kurset xR-teknologier, 3D-indholdsmodelleringsteknikker, rigging, animationsprincipper og gode praksisser for datavisualisering. De visualiserede scener vil typisk være i enten menneskelig skala og fyldt med erhvervede eller 3D-modellerede objekter, eller scener bestående af 3D-billeddata, som er erhvervet ved hjælp af CT eller MR. Fælles for begge er at indlevelse har stor betydning. Studerende vil deltage i et praktisk projekt for at designe og udvikle deres egen xR-oplevelse, hvor de fremviser deres arbejde under en live demo-dag ved semesterets afslutning. En stor del af kurset er dedikeret til projekt-arbejdet i grupper. Som udgangspunkt ligger kurset derfor op til fysisk deltagelse for at sikre et godt samarbejde, and kan kræve en vis portion projekt-styring.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Environment capture using photogrammetry. Simulating material appearance using textures, shaders, and different rendering techniques, interaction methods in xR using controllers, accelerometers, touch, headsets for xR, technologies for AR with handheld devices, Unity3D or similar tools for VR experiences.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises, and project work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>In this course, students will learn how to model experiences for virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality, etc (collectively xR). The students will learn essential methods and technologies for acquiring geometry, material appearance, and illumination. Among other things, the course covers xR technologies, 3D content modeling techniques, rigging, animation principles, and good practices for data visualization. The scenes that we visualize in the course are typically either at the human scale and filled with acquired or 3D modelled objects or the scenes consist of 3D imaging data which has been acquired using CT or MR. In both settings, immersion is of great importance. Students will engage in a hands-on project to design and develop their own xR experience, showcasing their work during a live demo day at the end of the semester. A substantial portion of the course is dedicated to this project-based group work. By default this places a natural emphasis on physical attendance to support effective collaboration, and can require a level of project management throughout the development process.</Course_Objectives>
