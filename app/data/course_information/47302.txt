          <Contents>Kurset kombinerer korte introducerende forelæsninger med vejledt laboratoriearbejde for at forbinde grundlæggende teori med eksperimentel praksis. En grupperapport indsendes for hvert projekt.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course combines short introductory lectures with supervised laboratory work to connect fundamental theory with experimental practice. Through this integrated format, students develop an understanding of both the underlying electrochemical principles and the practical challenges involved in fabricating and evaluating hydrogen energy devices.
The course is organized into two main projects. In the first project, students prepare and characterize platinum-based catalysts, ion-conducting membranes, and electrodes, and then assemble and test a PEM fuel cell. In the second project, students prepare and characterize iridium-based catalysts, membranes, and electrodes, and then assemble and test a PEM water electrolyzer. A group report is submitted for each project.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lab work, group work, introductory lectures
Safety in the lab is part of the course</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>In the course we only use teaching material - textbooks, assignments, articles, etc.- that is available free of charge from the course homepage on DTU Learn.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>This three-week experimental course aims to provide hands-on training in hydrogen energy technologies, with a focus on polymer electrolyte membrane (PEM) fuel cells and water electrolyzers. Students will gain a deep understanding of the working principles and practical experience in preparing core materials and components, including catalysts, membranes, and electrodes, followed by the assembly and performance testing of PEM fuel cells and electrolyzer cells. The theoretical grounds for the techniques are established through a series of short introductory lectures.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Hands-on! Can you make and test your own fuel cell and electrolyzer?</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>The course is designed to provide a strong experimental foundation for further special courses, MSc thesis projects, and PhD projects in energy conversion technologies. Lab coat and safety goggles are mandatory during all exercises. The students are encouraged to bring own lab coats and safety goggles , but it is also possible for some to borrow.</Remark>
