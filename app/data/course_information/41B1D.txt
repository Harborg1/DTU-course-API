          <Contents>Kurset behandler to niveauer af transformation og renovering: dels i forhold til det planlægningsmæssige niveau (regions- og byplanlægning), dels i forhold til eksisterende bygninger.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course deals with two levels of transformation and renovation: partly in relation to the planning level (regional and urban planning), and partly in relation to existing buildings. Better utilization of areas and buildings is a necessity in relation to a number of sustainability parameters (environmental, social and economic sustainability as well as biodiversity, resources and climate). 
The course will partly introduce methods for analysis and assessment, partly provide concrete examples from practice through the involvement of guest lecturers from the business community.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures with discussion
Project work in smaller groups</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
