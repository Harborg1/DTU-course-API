          <Course_Objectives>Kursets overordnede formål er at sætte den studerende i stand til at drive en startup udviklingsproces fra ide til lancering. 

Efter endt kursus er den studerende i stand til at identificere en problemstilling eller forretningsmulighed, og udvikle den til et niveau, hvor den kan indsendes til en relevant konkurrence, optages i en incubator eller få finansiering fra f.eks. business angels. De skal kunne præsentere en forretnings plan til eksamen. De skal endvidere kunne beskrive ideens og løsningens bæredygtighed.
 
Kurset er opbygget efter læringsprincipperne i CDIO, med omdrejningspunkt i de faser i Design-Build metoden der hedder "Build the bridge"  og  "Make it fly" . Det betyder at kursisterne i grupper, kommer igennem alle de processer, der skal til for at en ide, problemstilling eller forretningsmulighed kan kommercialiseres gennem en dokumenteret udviklingsproces. Der bliver benyttet startup processer som Agile udvikling og Lean Startup.

Kurset tager sigte på at deltagerne undervejs også trænes i at anvende de fremtrædende kommunikationsformer som iværksættere, trendsættere og unge mennesker anvender i dag.   Det betyder at kursisterne bl.a. undervises i præsentations teknikker, hvordan man skaffer investeringer til startups, mm. 

Deltagelse i kurset forudsætter indgående kendskab til innovationsprocesser, produktudvikling, 3D modellering, rapid prototyping, samt kendskab til Arduino eller lignende platforme. 

Gennemførelse af kurset kræver aktiv tilstedeværelse.</Course_Objectives>

          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to enable the student to run a startup development process from idea to launch.

Upon completion of the course, the student is able to identify a problem or business opportunity and develop it to a level where it can be submitted to a relevant competition, accepted into an incubator or receive funding from e.g. a business angel. They need to be able to present their business plan at the exam. They also need to be able to describe the sustainability of the idea and solution.
 
The course is structured according to the learning principles of CDIO, the focal point in the phases of the Design-Build method called "Build the bridge" and "Make it fly". This means that the course participants in groups, will go through all the processes needed for an idea, problem or business opportunity to be commercialized through a proven development process. Startup processes like Agile development and Lean Startup will be used.

The course aims at the participants being trained to use the prominent forms of communication that entrepreneurs, trendsetters, and young people use today. This means that the course participants amongst others are taught pitching, how to get funding for startups, etc.

Participation in the course requires a thorough knowledge of innovation processes, product development, 3D modeling, rapid prototyping, and knowledge of Arduino or similar platforms.

Completion of the course requires active engagement.</Course_Objectives>
