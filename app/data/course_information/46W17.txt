        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>This course gives you an introduction to the materials used in wind turbines and the loads they are subjected to. Simplify the wind turbine to 4 connected cantilever beams (the tower and the three blades), and the load and required material properties are addressed. The procedure of extracting the material properties and lifetime from experimental measurements is addressed. Focusing on the composite material used in the wind turbine blades, the ability to tailor, predict, and measure the mechanical properties of composites is addressed. The course will be given as a combination of online lectures, quizzes, demonstration videos, Python Jupyter notebooks, and assignments used as a part of the final grading</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>E-læring</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To give the student a thorough knowledge of materials structures and properties, with special emphasis on its requirements when used in a wind turbine
To make the student capable of tailoring and predicting the mechanical properties of wind turbine materials with an emphasis on composites for wind turbine blades
To make the student capable of designing materials test and critically use measured properties with the aim of designing selected sub-components in a wind turbine
To make the student capable of predicting the material life lifetime given the load history</Course_Objectives>
