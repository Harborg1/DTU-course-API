          <Course_Objectives>Formålet med kurset er at lære de studerende systematiske metoder og værktøjer til at identificere, analysere og styre pålideligheds- og sikkerhedsproblemer i automatiserings- og robotsystemer. Disse er nyttige til at forberede eleverne til at løse nye sikkerhedsproblemer og optagelse af de juridiske, samfundsmæssige og etiske aspekter i industry 4.0.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>This course is to teach students systematic methods and tools for identifying, analyzing and managing reliability and safety issues in automation and robotic systems.

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>This course is designed for master students with engineering education background,

          <Course_Objectives>The objective of the course is to teach students systematic methods and tools for identifying, analyzing and managing reliability and safety issues in automation and robotic systems. These are useful to prepare students to solve new safety problems and uptake of the legal, societal and ethical aspects in the era of industry 4.0.</Course_Objectives>
