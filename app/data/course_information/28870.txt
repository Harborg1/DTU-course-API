        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Fokus i kurset er på bæredygtighed i forhold til energikonvertering. Vi beskæftiger os med forskellige energiteknologier i et systemperspektiv baseret på generelle principper om bæredygtig udvikling. Fokus er på energi- og materialestrømme af relevans for udvalgte vedvarende energiteknologier (især baseret på sol, vind og biomasse) og sammenligning med fossile teknologier. Vi vil diskutere konsekvenserne af samspillet mellem befolkning, energiforbrug, levestandard og klimaændringer for bæredygtig udvikling. De udvalgte teknologier vil blive vurderet i et livscyklusperspektiv. Forskellige værktøjer til vurdering af bæredygtig udvikling vil blive behandlet, herunder livscyklusvurderinger, carbon footprint og nettoenergi-analyse. Desuden vil vi diskutere hvordan innovativ integration af energi og materiale-strømme melem samfundets forskellige sektorer kan styrke den bæredygtige udvikling. Værktøjerne vil blive anvendt til udtænkning af bæredygtige løsninger på samfundsudfordringer samt planlægning af en bæredygtig udvikling for energisektoren i et specifikt geografisk område.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The focus of this course is on sustainability in relation to energy conversion technologies. We deal with various renewable energy technologies both in respect to technical challenges and opportunities of the technologies and we critically present their pros and cons in respect to general principles of sustainability. The focus is on energy and material flows of relevance to selected renewable energy technologies (mainly based on solar, wind, and biomass) and comparison with fossil based technologies. We will discuss the implications of the interactions between population, energy consumption, living standard and climate change for sustainable development. The selected technologies will be evaluated in a life cycle perspective. Different tools for assessment of sustainable development based on energy and material flows will be addressed, including life cycle assessments, carbon footprint and net energy. In addition, it will be discussed how an innovative integration of energy and materials flows between different sectors in society can improve sustainable development. Tools and strategies will be used to conceive new solutions to moderns societal challenges and in particular to plan sustainable development of the energy sector in a specific geographic area.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures. Work in groups of 4-6 on one assignment which will presented in: 1) A project pitch; and 2) short projects.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Reading material consists of papers from journals to be found on DTU Learn.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course gives insight into various renewable energy technologies, and gives basic information on sustainability and life cycle assessment tools, as well as how energy technologies may contribute to a sustainable future development. This insight will be applied to plan a desirable energy future. Further, it can serve as inspiration for how to consider sustainability issues in your later specialization.</Course_Objectives>
