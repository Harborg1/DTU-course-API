        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Introduktion til den kvantemekaniske beskrivelse af molekyler, herunder Schrödingerligningen, sandsynlighedsfortolkning af bølgefunktionen, energiens kvantisering, kemisk binding og molekylorbitaler.
Kemisk termodynamik for rene stoffer. Der behandles emner som: Gassers egenskaber, de termodynamiske hovedsætninger, termodynamiske systemer og tilstandsfunktioner, reversible og irreversible procesforløb, termokemi, faseomdannelser. Introduktion til statistisk termodynamik.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaveregning samt aflevering af hjemmeopgaver.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Atkins, de Paula, Keeler: Atkins' Physical Chemistry, 12th ed., Oxford.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>At introducere den kvantemekaniske beskrivelse af molekyler. At introducere de forskellige termodynamiske energiformer i kemien, som bl.a. kan bruges til at forudsige den spontane retning af processer og til at bestemme udveksling af arbejde og varme.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to the quantum mechanical description of molecules, including the Schrödinger equation, probability interpretation of the wave function, the quantization of energy, chemical bonding and molecular orbitals.
Chemical thermodynamics for pure substances. Specifically, the following issues are discussed: Properties of gases, the fundamental laws of thermodynamics, thermodynamic systems, state functions, reversible and irreversible processes, thermochemistry, phase diagrams of pure substances.  Introduction to statistical thermodynamics.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, problem sessions and homework assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Atkins, de Paula, Keeler: Atkins' Physical Chemistry, 12th ed., Oxford.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To introduce the quantum mechanical description of molecules. To introduce the various thermodynamic forms of energy in chemistry that may be used to predict the spontaneous direction of processes and to determine the exchange of work and heat.</Course_Objectives>
