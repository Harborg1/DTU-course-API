          <Contents>Kurset introducerer prospektiv livscyklusvurdering (pLCA) og miljømæssigt udvidet input-output (EEIO) modellering. Kurset udvikler både tekniske og overførbare færdigheder med fokus på kritisk fortolkning af miljøpåvirkningsresultater og reflektering over begrænsninger og antagelser ved avancerede LCA-metoder.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces prospective life cycle assessment (pLCA) and environmentally extended input-output (EEIO) modelling. Participants are introduced to evolving background and foreground system modelling using shared socioeconomic pathways (SSP), integrated assessment models (IAMs), and the environmental learning and scaling theory. The course provides insights into supply chain impact assessment, hotspot identification, and opportunities to combine EEIO and process-based LCA. Course attendees explore sustainable-by-design approaches and links between future environmental impacts and absolute environmental sustainability. 

Exercises and active learning tasks involve practical problem-solving using the Activity Browser LCA software and guided Python-based modelling. Participants are invited to form their own groups, investigate a self-selected application case, discuss and interpret simulation results, prepare technical summaries, exchange peer feedback, and present their findings. The course develops both technical and transferable skills, with a focus on critically interpreting environmental impact results and reflecting on the limitations and assumptions of advanced LCA methods.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to broaden participants´ knowledge in life cycle assessment (LCA), equipping them to solve complex problems using advanced LCA methods within sustainable engineering and related scientific disciplines. Participants will develop skills to perform prospective life cycle assessment (pLCA), propose sustainable-by-design strategies, and conduct environmentally extended input-output (EEIO) analyses. Through these methods, students will learn to quantify dynamic, evolving environmental impacts, identify supply-chain hotspots, and design future pathways towards absolute sustainability. The course prepares students for real-world applications and a career in life cycle-based sustainability assessment in industry or academia.</Course_Objectives>
