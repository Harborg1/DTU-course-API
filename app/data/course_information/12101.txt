The students learn about terms, definition, and units related to sustainability, the environmental, social and economic dimensions of sustainability and the importance of geographical and temporal scale when considering engineering solutions. Focus will be placed on understanding sustainability and developing sustainable solutions from an industrial perspective.</Course_Objectives>
