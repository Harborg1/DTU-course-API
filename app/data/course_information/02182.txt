          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, øvelser og obligatoriske afleveringer.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Målet er at introducere den studerende til fundamentale begreber og teknikker indenfor symbolsk kunstig intelligens (symbolsk AI) samt anvende disse indenfor søgning, planlægning, spil, multiagent-systemer, social intelligens, humanoide robotter og menneske-robot interaktion.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>1. Symbolic models of the environment: propositional states, PDDL, belief states, epistemic models. 

2. Multiagent systems and social AI: game theory, game theoretical equilibria, models of coordination, Theory of Mind, multiagent planning and plan/goal recognition.

3. Algorithms on symbolic models: informed search algorithms, STRIPS planning, search under partial observability and non-determinism, adversarial search, algorithms for plan/goal recognition. 

4. Symbolic vs subsymbolic AI: Strengths and weaknesses of symbolic vs subsymbolic AI. Introduction to the combination of symbolic and subsymbolic techniques.

5. Applications: Integration of all curriculum elements in applications within socially intelligent humanoid robots and human-robot interaction.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and mandatory assignments.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The goal is to introduce the student to fundamental concepts and techniques within symbolic artificial intelligence (symbolic AI) and to apply these within search, planning, games, multiagent systems, social intelligence, humanoid robots and human-robot interaction. Symbolic AI relies on explicit – often logical – models of the environment. This provides certain advantages in terms of being able to build systems that can form plans and do logical reasoning, as well as giving advantages in terms of explainability and safety. The student will furthermore be introduced to neurosymbolic AI: the combination of symbolic AI with neural networks and other techniques within machine learning.</Course_Objectives>
