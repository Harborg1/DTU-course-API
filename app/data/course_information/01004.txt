        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Funktioner fra flere variable ind i flere variable, differentiabilitet, gradientvektor, kædereglen, Jacobianten, Hesse-matricen, Taylor-approksimationer for funktioner af flere variable, ekstremumsundersøgelse og optimeringsproblemer for funktioner af flere variable, Riemannintegralet, kurveintegraler, planintegraler, stamfunktionsproblemet, vektorfelter, spectralsætningen for Hermitiske matricer, indreprodukt og orthonormal baser for vektorrum af søjlevektorer.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Pr. uge: 2 forelæsninger, 5,5 timers gruppearbejde/​klasseundervisning og 1,5 timers skemalagt selvstudium. Derudover temaøvelser og projektarbejde i nogle uger.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>Kursusindholdet udgør en del af det matematiske grundlag for en bred vifte af tekniske områder og giver også et udgangspunkt for videre studier i matematik og anvendt matematik. Kurset udvikler teorien for funktioner af flere variable, kaldet "multivariate calculus" i engelsk litteratur. Målet er at styrke de studerendes evne til på den ene side at anvende en matematisk tankegang og på den anden side en algoritmisk og beregningsmæssig tilgang til at forstå og arbejde med grundlæggende begreber fra multivariat calculus. Teorien er eksemplificeret ved tematiske øvelser. Yderligere bruges computersoftware til at integrere beregningsmæssige og teoretiske aspekter af pensummet.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Functions from several variables into several variables, differentiability, gradient vector, the chain rule, the Jacobian, the Hesse matrix, Taylor approximations for functions of several variables, extremum investigation and optimization problems for functions of several variables, the Riemann integral, curve integrals, plan integrals, antiderivatives, vector fields, the spectral theorem for Hermitian matrices, inner product and orthonormal bases for vector spaces of column vectors.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Per week: 2 lectures, 5.5h tutorials and 1.5h groupwork. Moreover, thematic exercises and projectwork in some weeks.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course content forms part of the mathematical basis for a wide range of technical areas and also provides a starting point for further studies in mathematics and applied mathematics. The course develops the theory of functions of several variables, called "multivariate calculus" in English literature. The goal is to strengthen the students' ability to, on the one hand, use a mathematical way of thinking and, on the other hand, an algorithmic and computational approach to understanding and working with basic concepts from multivariate calculus. The theory is exemplified by thematic exercises. Additionally, computer software is used to integrate computational and theoretical aspects of the curriculum.</Course_Objectives>
