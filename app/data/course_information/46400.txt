        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Velkommen til målingernes verden! Dette kursus giver en introduktion til almindeligt anvendte måleteknikker inden for vindenergi. Vi dækker in-situ og fjernmålingsteknikker (for eksempel lidars og sodars) til vindmåling, vurdering af vindmøllers effektpræstation, observationer af mekaniske belastninger, måling af støjemission fra vindmøller samt SCADA-data fra vindfarmdrift. Et besøg på Risø er en integreret og populær del af kurset.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Welcome to the world of measurements! This course presents an introduction to commonly used measurement techniques used in wind energy. We cover in-situ and remote-sensing (for example, lidars and sodars) wind measurement techniques, wind turbine power performance assessment, observations of mechanical loads, measurement of wind turbine noise emission and wind farm operational SCADA data. These topics will be presented in relation to the relevant IEC standards that govern all phases of wind turbine/farm operation. The lectures are combined with practical experimental work using instruments and data loggers, as well as exercises and assignments based on wind turbine data from the well-instrumented 850kW turbine at the Risø campus. A visit to Risø is an integrated and popular part of the course.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and lab. work</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To give an overview of measurement techniques used in and around wind turbines.</Course_Objectives>
