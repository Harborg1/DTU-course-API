          <Contents>Litteraturstudie i medikoteknisk signalanalyse. Skrivning af videnskabelige artikler inden for medikoteknisk signalanalyse. Produktion og afholdelse af præsentationer. Litteratursøgning og afrapportering inden for medikoteknisk signalanalyse.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kursusdeltagerne mødes med underviserne hver uge for at drøfte videnskabelige artikler indenfor medikoteknisk signalanalyse og -behandling.           <Course_Objectives>At introducere deltagerne til kritisk læsning af forskningslitteratur indenfor medikoteknisk signalanalyse, skrive videnskabelige artikler og præsentere egne og andres forskningsarbejde. Dette opnås ved at læse andres artikler og efterfølgende præsentere dem over for de andre deltagere og underviseren. Hver studerende skal præsentere mindst tre artikler, hvoraf en af artiklerne er skrevet af dem selv. En endelig rapport baseret på et litteraturstudie afleveres i slutningen af kurset.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Literature reading in biomedical signal analysis. Writing papers within biomedical signal analysis. Producing presentations. Literatur search and reporting within biomedical signal analysis.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course participants (students) will meet with the teachers every week and discuss scientific paper(s) within biomedical signal analysis and processing. Participants should present at least three papers within their research area.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The goal of this course is to introduce the participants/students to critical reading of the research literature within biomedical signal analysis, writing of papers and presentation of own as well as others’  research work. This is accomplished by reading selected papers and subsequently presenting to the other course participants and to the lecturer. Each student is requested to present at least three papers, one of which is written by themselves. A final report on a literature study should be handed in at the end of the course.</Course_Objectives>
