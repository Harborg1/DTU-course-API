        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Introduktion til “Cloud” som et generelt koncept og de teknologier og komponenter som indgår. Teknologier til switching og virtualisering af netværk. Softwarekoncepter i Linux operativsystemet som muliggør virtuelle netværk og systemer. Virtualisering af computere og servere baseret på hypervisorer. Network Function Virtualization (NFV) konceptet som evolution af tjenester i telekommunikationsnetværk, herunder udviklingen mod Service Oriented Architectures og microservices. Containere som implementering af microservices, herunder Container Networking Models. Container orkestrering (Docker Swarm og Kubernetes).</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>At sætte deltagerne i stand til at analysere og designe netværk til cloud-tjenester og cloud-native miljøer med fokus på de underliggende teknologier.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset er i høj grad baseret på hands-on eksempler og øvelser i lektionerne, som afvikles på deltagernes egne computere. Det er derfor forventningen, at deltagerne medbringer en laptop med en Intel-kompatibel x86-64 processor med minimum 8 GB RAM og minimum 40 GB fri plads på harddisken.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Introduction to ”the Cloud” as a generic concept and the technologies and components that are part of it. Technologies for switching and virtualization of networks. Software concepts in the Linux operating system that enable virtual networks and systems. Host virtualization based on hypervisors. The Network Function Virtualization (NFV) concept as an evolution of services in telecommunication networks, including the evolution towards Service Oriented Architectures and microservices. Containers as implementation of microservices, including Container Network Models. Container orchestration (Docker Swarm and Kubernetes).</Contents>
          <Course_Objectives>To qualify the participants to analyze and design networks for cloud services and cloud-native environments with focus in the underlying technologies.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>A significant part of the course are hands-on examples and exercises in the lectures, which are intended to be performed on the participants' own computers. It is therefore expected that the participants bring a laptop with an Intel-compatible x86-64 processor with minimum 8 GB RAM and minimum 40 GB free harddrive space.</Remark>
