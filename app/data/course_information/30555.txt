Strålingsmodeller

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the following topics:  
Microwave remote sensing of the ice, ocean and atmosphere, 
Primary, secondary and tertiary microwave emission and scattering model parameters, 
Emission models, 
Scattering models, 
Ocean, ice and atmospheric radiometry, 
Sea surface temperature and sea ice concentration integrated retrievals and other applications.

This course provides students with competencies relevant to UN SDGs, for example, #13 (Climate Action) and #14 (Life below water).</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The teaching combines lectures, group work, reading of scientific literature and practical programming exercises. Focus is on hands-on implementation of models and model modules and data processing using the models.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Tonboe, R. T. (2023). Modeling Microwave Emission and Scattering from Snow-Covered Sea Ice. In M. Shokr, &amp; N. K. Sinha (Eds.), Sea Ice: Physics and Remote Sensing (2. ed.). Wiley. https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119828242.ch12</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall aim of the course is to give an introduction to microwave emission and scattering models and to implement and use the models with satellite data.</Course_Objectives>
