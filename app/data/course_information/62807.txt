          <Contents>Classification and properties of fluids. Fluid statics. Control volume. Fluid kinematics and vector fields including velocity and acceleration field, divergence and rotation. Conservation of mass, momentum, angular momentum and energy for fluid mechanical systems. Bernoulli's equation. Static and dynamic pressure. Dimensional analysis, modeling and similarity. Introduction to measurement techniques in fluid mechanics. Intern and Extern flows. Head loss calculations in pipes and pipe systems. Archimedes' principle including buoyancy and stability.
