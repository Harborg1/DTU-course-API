          <Course_Objectives>The goal is to provide insight into the physics of the space environment. The focus will be on the interaction between the solar wind on the Earth’s magnetosphere, ionosphere, and upper atmosphere. The student will acquire basic knowledge of the natural phenomena in space, such as geomagnetic storms, substorms, and the aurora. The acquired knowledge also forms the foundation for further studies of the influence of the Sun and the solar wind on the Earth and other planets, as well as magnetospheric physics and the atmosphere.</Course_Objectives>
