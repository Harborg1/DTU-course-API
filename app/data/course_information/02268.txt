        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces the principles for developing process-oriented and event-driven software systems to automate business processes. 

The topics presented are: 
▪ Business Process Lifecycle and Process Architectures.
▪ Formalization of business processes using process modelling notations, e.g., Business Process Model and Notation, DCR graphs, and/or others.
▪ Methods and techniques for analyzing the correctness of a process model
▪ Methods and techniques for redesigning business processes
▪ Technologies for automating business processes (i.e., Process-Aware Information Systems)
▪ Technologies for event processing</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, tutorials and student presentations, along with a project.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
