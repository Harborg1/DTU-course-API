          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Kurset er opdelt i forelæsninger, som hver især dækker en vævsspecifikt immunitetstype  (f.eks. hud- eller slimhinderelaterede) eller en immunologisk sygdom (f.eks. allergi, virusinfektion, autoimmunitet). Forelæsningerne varer 1 time og afholdes emne for emne af forskere med hvert deres speciale. De efterfølges af 2 timers gruppeøvelser, quizzer og en general drøftelse på 30-60 minutter til slut for at facilitere optimal forståelse.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende en forståelse for de immunregulatoriske mekanismer inden for væv. At give de studerende en forståelse for vævsmedieret immunitet, især ved barriereoverflader såsom tarmen, huden og lungerne. At give de studerende en forståelse for immunmedierede sygdomme og immunpatologier, der enten skyldes medfødte fejl eller infektioner. Derudover vil gruppeøvelser baseret på spørgsmål facilitere dyb læring af: 1) udvalgte emner inden for de førnævnte emner, 2) centrale immunologiske begreber (f.eks. tolerance, immun undvigelse).</Course_Objectives>

          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course will be divided in distinct lectures each covering a tissue-specific type of immunity (e.g. cutaneous, mucosal) or an immunological disease (e.g. allergy, viral infection, autoimmunity). The lectures will be delivered by specialized researchers on a topic-by-topic basis for about one hour. They will be followed by 2-hour group exercises and quizzes and a general discussion of 0.5-1hr in the end to facilitate understanding.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide the students with an understanding of immune regulatory mechanisms within tissues. To provide the students with an understanding of tissue-mediated immunity, especially at barrier surfaces including the gut, skin and lung. To provide the students with an understanding of immune-mediated diseases and immune pathologies that are driven either by inborn errors or infections. Moreover, question-based group exercises will facilitate deep learning of: 1) selected topics within the aforementioned subjects, 2) key immunological concepts (e.g. tolerance, immune evasion).</Course_Objectives>
