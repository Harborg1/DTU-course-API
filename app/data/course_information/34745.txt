        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Multivariable (MIMO) kontinuerte og diskrete systemer og deres beskrivende ligninger. Tilstandsbeskrivelse. Linearisering. Laplace- og z-transformation anvendt på MIMO systemer. Sammenhæng mellem tilstands- og overføringsfunktionsbeskrivelser. Tilstandstransformationer og standardformer. Poler, nulpunkter og egenværdier, minimal repræsentation, diskussion af fælles poler og nulpunkter. Styrbarhed, Observerbarhed, Gram-matricer, Stabilitet. Tilstandstilbagekobling og polplacering. Integralregulering. Optimalregulering ved minimering af kvadratisk index i kontinuert og diskret tid (LQR). Kontinuerte og diskrete observere. Introduktion til stokastiske processer for kalmanfiltre og LQG dimensionering.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger (2 gange om ugen), grupperegning øvelser (Matlab/Simulink), hjemmeopgaver.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>E. Hendricks, O. Jannerup and P. H. Sørensen: Linear Systems Control (Springer, ISBN 978-3-540-78485-2)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset sigter mod at skabe analytiske redskaber og metoder til design af lineære styringssystemer til MIMO (Multi-input multi-ouptut) fysiske systemer, baseret på staten rummet rammer. Kurset præsenterer også, hvordan de teoretiske metoder omsætte til computerstøttet teknikker til praktisk design og implementering af kontrolsystemerne.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Multivariable (MIMO) continuous and discrete time dynamic systems. State-space formulation. Linearization, Laplace- and Z-transforms for MIMO systems. State transformations and canonical forms. Poles, zeros, eigenvalues, minimal representation. Controllability, observability, gramians. Stability. State feedback, pole placement. Integral control. Optimal control in continuous and discrete time (LQR). Observers in continuous and discrete time. Introduction to stochastic processes and Kalman filters. LQG design.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures (2 modules per week), group work exercises (Matlab/Simulink), homeworks.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>E. Hendricks, O. Jannerup and P. H. Sørensen: Linear Systems Control (Springer, ISBN 978-3-540-78485-2)</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The course aims at providing analytical tools and methods for the design of linear control systems for MIMO (multi-input multi-ouptut) physical systems, based on the state space framework. The course also presents how the theoretical methods transpose into computer aided techniques for the practical design and implementation of the control systems.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is a relevant optional course for B.Eng.-students.
E-learning is used in the form of on-line voting systems, videos of lectures and digital exam.</Remark>
