        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will provide an overview of the energy situation, energy sources and demand. It will go into detail with solar spectrum, climate, harvesting of solar energy, photosynthesis, CO2 capture, fuel/energy conversion and energy storage, and will provide insights into key challenges toward achieving a carbon-neutral society. For understanding the principles behind these topics we will apply elements of quantum mechanics, thermo dynamics, classical mechanics, material science, semi-conductor physics and surface science.</Contents>
