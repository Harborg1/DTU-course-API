        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Projektets indhold vælges af studerende og vejledere i fællesskab. Forslag til emner offentliggøres foregående semester. De studerende kan desuden selv foreslå projekter og finde vejledere ved DTU Compute eller DTU Management (operationsanalyse projekter).
Projekterne vil typisk omfatte en eller flere af disciplinerne statistik, numerisk analyse/scientific computing, operationsanalyse, signalbehandling, AI, billedanalyse, funktionalanalyse, computer grafik, geometri, kodningsteori, kryptologi, diskret matematik og dynamiske systemer/differentialligninger. Udover selve projektarbejdet vil der indgå seancer om rapportskrivning, gruppearbejde, intensiv undervisning i et specifikt emne, eksempelvis Git og high performance computing, med mere. Kurset afsluttes med en mundtlig fremlæggelse af rapport for samtlige deltagende studerende samt en individuel mundtlig eksamen. Fremlæggelsesdatoen er i slutningen af 3-ugersperioden juni.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Projektarbejde i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give de studerende en fælles forståelsesramme for principper og metoder i matematisk modellering af tekniske, naturvidenskabelige og socio-økonomiske problemstillinger, samt at bringe deres hidtidige faglige kompetencer i anvendelse i et sammenhængende projektforløb. Kurset har til formål at indøve projektarbejdsformen og give dybere indsigt i et eller flere faglige emner inden for matematik og matematisk modellering. Projektarbejdet skal forberede de studerende til bachelorprojektet og omfatter elementer af projektstyring, gruppearbejde, litteratur- og informationssøgning samt skriftlig og mundtlig formidling, herunder rapport formalia. Kurset indeholder desuden plenumaktiviteter om litteratursøgning, referencehåndtering, anvendelse af generativ AI, high performance computing, reproducerbarhed og versionsstyring (git) m.m.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>Kurset er forbeholdt studerende på linjen Bachelor i Anvendt Matematik.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The project content is chosen by the students and the teacher in common. Project subjects are published in previous semesters. The students may suggest projects themselves and find supervisors at DTU Compute or DTU Management. The projects will typically include one or more of the disciplines statistics, numerical analysis/scientific computing, operations analysis, signal processing, AI, image analysis, functional analysis, computer graphics, geometry, coding theory, cryptology, discrete mathematics
and dynamical systems/differential equations. Beside the actual project work, there will be given instructions in report writing, group work, short intensive teaching in specific topics, as Git and high performance computing, and more. The course is finished by oral presentations for all participants and an oral examination. The presentations will take place at the end of the 3-week period in june.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Project work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide students with a common framework for principles and methods in mathematical modeling of technical, natural science, and socio-economic problems, and to enable them to integrate and apply their previously acquired competencies in a coherent project setting. The course aims to develop project work skills and provide deeper insight into one or more academic areas within mathematics and mathematical modeling. The project work prepares students for the bachelor project and includes elements of project management, group work, literature and information retrieval, as well as written and oral communication, including report formalities. The course also includes plenary activities on literature search, reference management, application of generative AI, high-performance computing, reproducibility, and version control (git), among others.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>The course is reserved for students on BSc. in Applied Mathematics.</Remark>
