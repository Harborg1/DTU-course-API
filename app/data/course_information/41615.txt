          <Contents>Parametrisk (Featurebaseret) modellering, solid modellering, overflademodellering, boolske operationer, dimensionsangivelser, assembly modellering, skeletmodellering, modellering af komplekse overflader og definering af overfladeegenskaber (farve, refleksion, lys) og animation. Alle kursusdeltagere vil praktisere disse elementer i 2-persons grupper gennem både introducerende øvelser og projektarbejde med åbne problemstillinger. I forbindelse med gruppearbejdets resultater skal der redegøres for hvert gruppemedlems bidrag.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og øvelser</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At sætte deltagerne i stand til at forstå og anvende et Computer Aided Design (CAD) system i forbindelse med konstruktion og produktudvikling, samt at anvende visualiserings- og animationssoftware.

Efter at have gennemført kurset vil studerende besidde grundlæggende færdigheder, der gør dem i stand til at:

1) kunne konstruere mekaniske komponenter, assemblies og produkter, 
2) Udføre ingeniøranalyser, der kræver CAD repræsentationer, 
3) Udføre grundlæggende simulationer, 
4) forberede mekaniske komponenter til produktion.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Parametric (Feature-based) modeling, solid modeling, surface modeling, Boolean operations, dimensioning, assembly modeling, skeleton modeling, modeling of complex surfaces and defining surface properties (color, reflection, light setting) and animation. Exercises in 3-D modeling and communication thereof. 

All participants will practice these elements in 2-person teams through both introductory exercises and open-ended project work. All group work will contain individualized itemization of each team member's contribution.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To enable participants to comprehend and use a Computer Aided Design (CAD) system in conjunction with construction and product development, as well as to use visualization and animation software. 

Upon completing the course, students will possess fundamental skills enabling them to:

1) Construct mechanical components, assemblies, and products,
2) Perform engineering analyses that necessitate CAD representations,
3) Conduct basic simulations,
4) prepare mechanical components for production.</Course_Objectives>
