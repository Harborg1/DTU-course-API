        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset giver en dybdegående introduktion til mikrobiom engineering og omfatter både design af syntetiske mikrobielle samfund og målrettet modificering af naturlige mikrobiomer. Der lægges vægt på de økologiske og molekylære principper, der styrer mikrobiomers struktur, funktion og stabilitet, herunder mikrobielle interaktioner, kemisk signalering og miljømæssige påvirkninger. Kurset vil dække eksperimentelle og beregningsmæssige metoder til karakterisering af mikrobiomer, herunder sekventeringsbaserede metoder, dyrkningsstrategier, sensorteknologier samt modelsystemer (f.eks. biofilm-modeller). Derudover behandles nyere fremskridt inden for molekylærbiologi, syntetisk biologi og systembiologi, som muliggør manipulation af mikrobiomer. Der lægges vægt på strategier til at kontrollere og optimere mikrobiomer ved at introducere eller fjerne specifikke medlemmer samt ved anvendelse af miljømæssige og genetiske interventioner med henblik på anvendelser inden for sundhed, landbrug og bioteknologi. Samlet set giver kurset de studerende et teoretisk og analytisk grundlag for at forstå, analysere og foretage engineering af mikrobiomer.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course provides an in-depth introduction to microbiome engineering, covering both the design of synthetic microbial communities and the targeted modification of natural microbiomes. It addresses the ecological and molecular principles that govern microbiome structure, function, and stability, including microbial interactions, chemical signaling, and environmental influences. The course will cover experimental and computational methods for microbiome characterization, such as sequencing-based approaches, cultivation strategies, sensor technologies, and model systems (e.g. biofilms). In addition, the course explores recent advances in molecular biology, synthetic biology, and systems biology that enable microbiome manipulation. Emphasis is placed on strategies to control and optimize microbiomes through introduction or removal of community members, as well as environmental and genetic interventions, with applications in health, agriculture, and biotechnology. Overall, the course equips students with the theoretical and analytical foundation to understand, analyze, and engineer microbiomes.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>The course aims to provide students with competences in microbiome engineering, including both the design of microbial communities and the targeted modification of natural microbiomes. These competences will enable students to describe the breadth of biotechnology applications based on microbiomes, to explain recent technological advances in molecular biology, synthetic biology, and systems biology relevant to microbiome engineering, and to design, control, and optimize microbial communities by introducing or removing specific members, as well as by applying environmental or genetic interventions for applications in health, agriculture, and biotechnology</Course_Objectives>
