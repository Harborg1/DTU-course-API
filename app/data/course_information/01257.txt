          <Contents>Vi betragter en matematisk model for en problemstilling med baggrund i for eksempel de tekniske videnskaber, og vi analyserer modellen ved brug af matematisk teori. For studerende, der har taget kursus 01617, vil der tilbydes skræddersyede projekter, der giver de deltagene mulighed for at anvende dynamisk systemteori på konkrete problemstillinger.           <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Heldagsaktivitet i 3 uger med projektarbejde i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

Flere af projekterne tager udgangspunkt i differentialligningsmodeller. Kurset er derfor et naturligt næste skridt for studerende, der har gennemført 01617, hvor flere af metoder herfra bliver anvendt i praksis.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>We consider a mathematical model of a scientific problem originating in for instance engineering, and we analyze the model using mathematical theory. In addition, the model is implemented numerically and through numerical simulations the phenomena are investigated. Students that have taken course 01617 will be offered tailored projects where students will be able to apply dynamical systems theory to real-world problems. Students are expected to work in groups (2-4 persons) with the concrete project.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Full day sessions in 3 weeks with projectwork in groups.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To introduce the students to advanced mathematical modelling through the solution of a project problem from engineering, medicine or physics. Most projects will be about models of differential equations. The course is therefore a natural next step for students of 01617, providing a unique opportunity for putting some theoretical knowledge into practice.</Course_Objectives>
