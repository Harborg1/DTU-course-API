        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Deltagerne lærer at anvende state-of-the-art syntetisk biologi værktøjer til at designe effektive cellefabrikker til biobaseret produktion af værdiskabende stoffer, hvilket inkluderer alle aspekter af design-build-test-learn cyklussen. Kurset består af teoretisk undervisning om formiddagene og laboratoriearbejde om eftermiddagene. Deltagerne kommer til at arbejde i mindre grupper på de forskellige eksperimentelle forløb, hvor de vil udføre et forskningsprojekt gående på stammebygning af P. putida, Streptomyces, S. cerevisiae eller in vitro proteinmodificering.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Eksperimentelt arbejde, dataanalyse, gruppearbejde, forelæsninger og projektarbejde</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At give studerende en omfattende teoretisk viden og praktisk erfaring med moderne syntetisk biologi, beregningsbiologi og stofskifte design til udvikling af industrielle cellefabrikker.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Kurset er arrangeret af Novo Nordisk Foundation Biotechnology Research Institute for the Green Transition (BRIGHT).  og vil finde sted på Centerets laboratorier i bygning 220. Master studerende kan også tage kurset.</DTU_Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The students will learn how to apply the state-of-the-art synthetic biology tools for designing novel cell factories for production of bio-based chemicals and pharmaceuticals. The course is composed of theory lectures and computer exercises in the morning and laboratory project work in the afternoon. The students will choose one of the four experimental tracks, where they will carry out a smaller research project on engineering of: (1) E. coli, (2) S. cerevisiae, (3) Streptomyces, or (4) Pseudomonas putida.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Laboratory exercises, data analysis, group exercises, lectures and project</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To provide the student with extensive theoretical knowledge and practical experience on state-of-the-art synthetic biology, computational biology and metabolic engineering for development of industrial cell factories for sustainable production of chemicals. Students will learn about all phases of cell factory design and engineering. This includes topics ranging from life cycle assessment and techno-economic analysis to metabolic engineering and the scaling up of bioprocesses.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is organized by the Novo Nordisk Foundation Biotechnology Research Institute for the Green Transition (BRIGHT) and will be held at the Center's laboratories in building 220. Master students are welcome</DTU_Remark>
