        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Typically, the Master Project addresses an important or urgent strategic business problem involving specific functions or processes within the organisation. Consequently, the Master Project tackles a strategic business issue in a thorough, well-argued, cross-disciplinary, and action-oriented manner:

• Thorough and well-argued indicates that the analysis of the problem is based on substantial documentation, possibly including empirical investigations.

• Cross-disciplinary indicates that all relevant functions or activities within the organisation affected by the problem are considered and clarified in the analysis.

• Action-oriented indicates that the clarification, analysis, and evaluation are sufficient for a proper management report. The proposed solutions should be practically applicable within the organisation’s context and must be implementable.

In line with the objectives of the Executive MBA programme, the project considers the strategic purpose of the organisation. Additionally, it considers relevant aspects such as innovation, business development, or change management, particularly in relation to technological, economic, or market forces affecting the organisation.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The program is based on action learning where theories, methods, concepts and tools are employed on real business challenges and issues from the participants companies. The master project is written individually or in groups of two participants.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The Master Project has the following main objectives:

•	To demonstrate the capability to conduct analyses across multiple business disciplines and to integrate these disciplines into a coherent form.
•	To demonstrate the ability to develop and present well-grounded proposals that lead to implementable solutions.

The Master Project should reflect the author’s comprehensive perspective and ability to formulate well-grounded proposals for management actions addressing cross-disciplinary and cross-organisational business challenges (both the “who”, "what" and the "how").</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Participation in the program requires separate admission to the MMT program. The price of the MMT program is DKK 420.000. Please contact DTU Executive MBA.</DTU_Remark>
