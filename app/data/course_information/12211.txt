          <Course_Objectives>This course will provide the student with the necessary background and relevant tools for sketching solutions for water supply, sanitation, and waste management in low-income countries. The course will focus on contextualization of these solutions to specific problems and situations and on identifying external factors – technical, socio-economic, cultural, etc. – that may jeopardize a well-working system. The course is problem based and solution oriented and will provide students with competences for developing new, or improving existing, environmental tech solutions within water, sanitation, or waste management. The course and its transcultural environment will make students able to propose ways for the successful implementation of the solutions in a local context, including suggestions for improvements of existing technologies and potential for entrepreneurial activities.
