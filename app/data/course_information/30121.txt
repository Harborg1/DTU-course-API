        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset vil præsentere himmelrummet og forklare, hvordan detektionen af elektromagnetisk stråling er afgørende i astrofysikken for at forstå vores kosmos.

Kurset vil dække hele spektret af astrofysiske skalaer – fra detektion og dannelse af exoplaneter til fysikken og udviklingen af stjerner, kompakte objekter (hvide dværge, neutronstjerner og sorte huller), og fra klassificeringen og egenskaberne af galakser gennem den kosmiske historie til universets geometri og den standard kosmologiske model for Big Bang.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, quizzes, regneøvelser, og online programmering i python.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Universe, Stars and Galaxies, 6th edition (eller Universe 11th edition) og tidligere udgaver af R.A. Freedman, R.M. Geller, W.J. Kaufmann III. Ed. W.H. Freeman and Company, NY, 2019.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Kurset har til formål at introducere hovedprincipperne inden for moderne astrofysik og kosmologi ved at dække det grundlæggende i stjerners og galaksers fysik og natur, såvel som mørkt stof og Big Bang. Kurset har også til formål at give et overblik over de teknologiske udfordringer i moderne astrofysiske faciliteter og observationer.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Kurset er åbent for alle. Dog ved overtegning af tilmeldte deltagere, vil studerende på uddannelsen i Geofysik og Rumteknologi blive priorieteret.</DTU_Remark>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will present the heavens and explain how the detection of electromagnetic radiation is essential in astrophysics to understand our Cosmos.

The course will cover the full range of astrophysical scales, from the detection and formation of exoplanets to the physics and evolution of stars, compact objects (white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes),  and from the classification and the properties of galaxies across cosmic time to the geometry of the universe and the standard cosmological model of the Big Bang.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, quizzes, exercises, and online python programming.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Universe, Stars and Galaxies, 6th edition (or Universe 11th edition) and earlier editions, by R.A. Freedman, R.M. Geller, W.J. Kaufmann III. Ed. W.H. Freeman and Company, NY, 2019.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>This course aims to introduce the main principles of modern astrophysics and cosmology by covering the fundamentals of the physics and nature of stars and galaxies, as well as of dark matter and the Big Bang. The course also aims to provide an overview of the technological challenges in modern astrophysical facilities and observations.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>The course is open to all. However, students from the ESPE programme will be given precedence.</DTU_Remark>
