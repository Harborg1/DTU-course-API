        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset introducerer grundlæggende viden om byggeteknik og arkitektur gennem forelæsninger, ekskursioner og øvelser. 

Undervisningen omfatter skitserings- og noteteknikker, bygningsregistrering, bygningsanalyse med fokus på arkitektur, konstruktion, klimaskærm, materialer og bæredygtighed. Der opbygges såvel en forståelse for bygningers systemer som for bygningers arkitektoniske koncepter, og træk af byggeteknikkens og arkitekturens historiske forudsætninger gennemgås også.

Kurset praktiserer individuelt arbejde, gruppearbejde, fremlæggelse samt rapportskrivning og fremlæggelse efter anerkendte formater.

Kurset inddrager viden fra de øvrige 1. semesterkurser.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course introduces basic knowledge of construction technology and architecture through lectures, excursions and exercises.

The teaching includes sketching- and note techniques, building registration, building analysis with a focus on architecture, construction, thermal envelope, materials and sustainability. An understanding of the systems of buildings as well as the architectural concepts of buildings is built up, and features of the historical prerequisites of building technology and architecture are also reviewed.

The course practises individual work, group work, presentationas well as report writing and presentation according to recognized formats.

The course includes knowledge obtained in the other 1st-semester courses.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, exercises and assignments</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>The course uses Teknisk Ståbi in the latest edition</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to provide an introductory knowledge of building technology and architecture for architectural engineers.

Once you have followed the course, you will be able to record and analyze simpler buildings, explain their architectural concepts and relationships to the development of the history of architecture and technology. You will be able to explain the constructive design and construction engineering of simpler buildings, including explaining their static and climate engineering modes of operation.</Course_Objectives>
