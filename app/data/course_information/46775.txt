        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kurset udgøres af en kombination af forelæsninger og faciliteret gruppearbejde i laboratoriet ved brug af realtidssimuleringsudstyret i PowerLab (Lyngby, B329A). De studerende arbejder i grupper med et projektemne, de har valgt at arbejde med. Projektemnerne dækker compliance test af vindmøller, beskyttelsesdesign og hardware-in-the-loop (HiL)-test. I løbet af kurset forventes hver gruppe at holde et præsentere deres resultater og udveksle erfaringer med andre grupper. Hver gruppe og individuelle studerende har muligheden for at definere læringsmål, der knytter sig til deres egen, fremtidige specialisering.
 
Forelæsningerne og projekterne dækker følgende områder,
• EMT-modeller og realtidssimuleringer med hardware-in-the-loop-løsninger
• Beskyttelse af elforsyningssystem, især med fokus på afstandsbeskyttelse
• Konverterkontrol og systemstabilitet
• Grid code og grid compliance</Contents>

De studerende får mulighed for at få indsigt i konverterkontrol, parametrering og test- og verifikationsprocedurerne af beskyttelsesrelæer.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course will be instructed through a combination of lectures and facilitated group work in the lab using the real-time simulation equipment in PowerLab (Lyngby, B329A). Students will be divided into a few groups with a define project topic up to selection. The project topics cover the area of grid compliance test of wind turbines, protection design, and HiL testing. During the course, each group is expected to give a presentation on the findings and exchange with other groups. Each group and individual student have the flexibility to define the learning objectives linking to future specialization. 

The lectures and projects cover the following fields,
•	EMT models and real-time simulations with hardware-in-the-loop solutions
•	Power system protection in particular focusing on distance protection
•	Converter control and system stability
•	Grid codes and grid compliance</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>1) Lectures 
2) Group projects, where the power system will be investigated by modelling of minor, realistic grid.
3) Real time simulations in the lab with hardware-in-the-loop</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The aim of the course is to give students more in-depth knowledge and hands-on experience on  protection of power systems, electromagnetic transient simulation and hardware-in-the-loop testing methods for power system applications. The students will have the opportunity to gain insight of the converter control, the parameterization of protective relays, and the testing and verification procedures.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This course will be held first time in June 2026</DTU_Remark>
          <Remark>Active participation in project work and interaction with the instructor(s) is an essential condition for understanding the presented theory and methods.</Remark>
