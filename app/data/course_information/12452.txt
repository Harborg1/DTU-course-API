        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Behovet for klimavenlig og effektiv mobilitet sætter nye krav til vores eksisterende transport infrastruktur, som er en forudsætning for at transporterer mennesker og gods fra by til by og på tværs af lande grænser.   
I denne sammenhæng ses det, at en den eksisterende infrastruktur skal kunne klare mere end hvad den oprindeligt blev bygget til. 

Kurset omhandler praktisk anvendelse af ingeniørprincipperne indenfor jernbanen, og bliver undervist af nogle af de førende eksperter indenfor jernbane sektoren. Kurset fokuserer på elektrificering af jernbanen (en bæredygtig transport løsning) med betoning på design af både den horisontale og vertikale linjeføring samt kørestrøm i et CAD baseret program; under hensyntagen til vedligeholdelse af jernbane designet. 

I forbindelse med kurset arrangeres en ekskursion til forskellige virksomheder og infrastrukturforvaltere, således teori bliver til virkelighed. 

Kurset udbydes kun på engelsk.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The need of efficient and climate friendly mobility places new demands on our existing transport infrastructure, which is a prerequisite for transporting people and goods from city to city and across country borders. 
In this context, it is seen that the existing infrastructure must be able to handle more than what it was originally built for. 

This course teaches the practical application of engineering principles in designing railways, and is taught by some of the leading experts within the railway sector. The course focuses on designing electrification for a railway line (a green sustainable energy) taking into consideration the design of both the horizontal and vertical alignment done in a CAD based program, as well connecting to the traction power supply from the public power grid; taking into account the maintenance of the railway design. 

In connection with the course, an excursion to various companies and infrastructure managers is arranged, so that theory becomes reality. 

The course is taught in English.</Contents>
