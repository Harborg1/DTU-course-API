        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Uge 1: Forståelse og empati
De studerende lærer casen at kende gennem en præsentation fra case-ejerne. De henter inspiration ved at besøge faciliteter og tale med rådgivere og klinikere. De studerende præsenteres for værktøjer til universelt design, som de skal anvende i løbet af processen. De opbygger en dybdegående forståelse gennem intensivt skrivebordsstudie (desk research) og kortlægning af interessenter, og de planlægger de relevante brugerstudier, der er nødvendige til det intensive to-ugers projektarbejde.

Uge 2: Forståelse af problemområdet (første del af "Double Diamond"-modellen)
De studerende fokuserer på at udforske og definere deres problemområde, identificere muligheder og skabe en dybere forståelse gennem brugerstudier og analyser. Dette vil danne grundlag for deres definition af problemet, som de i den kommende uge vil udvikle et løsningskoncept og en prototype til.

Uge 3: Udforskning og levering af løsning (anden del af "Double Diamond"-modellen)
De studerende fokuserer på at udforske og udarbejde løsningen. De validerer koncepter hos brugerne, før de færdiggør detaljerne og afleverer løsningen. Ugen afsluttes med præsentationer og aflevering af den endelige løsning.</Contents>

I løbet af den første uge arbejder de studerende i grupper på at opnå en dybdegående forståelse af casen; de sætter sig ind i casens rammevilkår, udfører aktør-netværkanalyser og planlægger det kommende brugerstudie- og designarbejde.

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Week 1: Understanding and empathy
Students learn about the case through a presentation by case owners. Get inspired by visiting facilities and communicating with advisors and clinicians. Students will be presented with universal design tools to apply during the process. Students build a rich understanding through intense desk research and mapping of stakeholders, and plan the appropriate user research needed during the intensive two-week project work. 

Week 2: Understanding of problem area (first part of double diamond)
Students focus on exploring and defining their problem area, identifying opportunities, and creating a richer understanding through conducting user research and analysis. This will inform their definition of the problem which they will develop a solution concept and mock-up to in the upcoming week.

Week 3: Exploring and delivering solution (second part of double diamond)
Students focus on exploring and delivering in the solution area. They conduct user validation of concepts before detailing and delivering. The week ends with presentations and delivery of the final solution concept and mock-up.</Contents>

          <Course_Objectives>The intensive three-week course aims to introduce students to universal design in health innovation through a hands-on, project-based approach. 

Working in teams, students develop a rich understanding of the case during the first week; understanding the constraints of the case, conducting actor network analysis and planning the upcoming user research and design work. 

The second two weeks will entail hands-on project work where the students will apply user-centered design methods with a focus on inclusion and accessibility of their concepts. The final output will be delivery and presentation of concepts and mock-ups they develop through that process.</Course_Objectives>
