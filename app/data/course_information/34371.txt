        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Kursets indhold dækker grundlæggende aspekter af netværkssikkerhed på forskellige lag: fysiske, mac-, netværks- og transportlag, med vægt på IoT-specifikke udfordringer. Efter en indledende introduktion til netværkskommunikations arkitektoniske modeller og grundlæggende mekanismer, fokuserer kurset på typiske sårbarheder og udfordringer ved kommunikationsnetværks systemer på MAC , netværk og transportlag. Alt dette er baseret på praktiske laboratorier (der vil være eksperimenter og praktiske øvelser i alle forelæsninger). Bagefter fokuserer kurset på specifikke IoT-komponenter og -teknologier for at give en grundlæggende forståelse af de systemer og enheder, der skal beskyttes. Desuden vil kurset præsentere elementer relateret til privatlivsbestemmelser og standarder, der gælder for sikring af IoT-systemer. 
Kurset vil også inkludere et projektarbejde, der kræver, at de studerende designer, bygger og evaluerer et praktisk IoT-system. Studerende vil blive bedt om at demonstrere deres prototyper og præsentere deres projekter mundtligt i slutningen af ​​kurset, dokumentere deres design og resultater i en rapport. Det forventes, at gæstetalere præsenterer relaterede emner.</Contents>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course contents cover fundamental aspects of network security at different layers: physical, mac, network, and transport layers, with emphasis on IoT-specific challenges. After an initial introduction to network communication architectural models and basic mechanisms, the course focus on typical vulnerabilities and challenges of communication network systems at MAC, network, and transport layers. All these, are based on practical hands-on labs (There will be experiments and hands-on exercises in all lectures). Afterward, the course turns focus to specific IoT components and technologies to provide a baseline understanding of the systems and devices to protect. Furthermore.  the course will present elements related to privacy regulations and standards that apply to securing IoT systems.
The course will include a project work requiring students to design, build, and evaluate a practical IoT system. Students will be required to demonstrate their prototypes and present orally their projects at the end of the course, documenting their designs and results in a report. It is expected that guest-speakers present related topics.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and exercises</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The course addresses network security challenges at different levels of the OSI layer model.  The course introduces afterward specific aspects related to IoT technologies with a focus on practical cases and setups. Students will learn to operate traffic capturing and generating tools, analyze network security vulnerabilities of different protocols and build up and analyze concrete IoT-related setups, with the aim to increase awareness and understanding of IoT data communication security and its incorporation in future designs.</Course_Objectives>
