        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Separationer baseret på 'phase creation', 'agent-addition', 'solid agent'. 'Barrier'-baserede separationer behandles i et særskilt kursus om membranteknologi. Alle emner studeres ved kombinationer af diagrammer, 'short-cut' metoder og simulering. Videreudvikling af metoder til separation af 2-3 komponenter til flerkomponent-destillation og væske-væske ekstraktion, herunder ekstraktionskaskader. Specielle separationsprocesser: Udvalgte emner såsom azeotrop separation, ekstraktiv destillation, salt destillation, batch destillation, hastighedsbaserede separationsprocesser. 
Søjleprocesser: Adsorption, ionbytning og kromatografi: Materialer, modellering og driftsformer. Databararbejde med programmering: Simulering af specielle og udvalgte processer: MESH/MERSHQ ligninger. Proces Intensification. Introduktion af forskningsrelaterede problemstillinger. Avanceret process analyse.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, opgaveregning, databararbejde.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>At sætte deltagerne i stand til at anvende systematiske metoder til at modellere, analysere og simulere teknisk vigtige separationsprocesser. Såvel grafiske og approksimative metoder, samt matematiske modeller anvendes. Såvel trinbaserede som hastighedsbaserede metoder er omfattede. Deltagerne vil få kendskab til og en vis erfaring med grundlaget for computerbaserede simuleringsværktøjer til dimensionering og analyse af separationsprocesser. Vægten ligger på separation af blandinger af flere komponenter.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>At kunne anvende de teknikker, færdigheder og værktøjer som dette kursus tilvejebringer, er afgørende for at kunne analysere hvorledes design og udvælgelse af separationsprocesser og procesveje bidrager til bæredygtighed.</Remark>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Separations based on the mechanisms of phase creation, agent-addition, solid agent. Barrier-based separations are covered in a course dedicated to membrane technology. All topics are studied using combinations of diagrams, short-cut approaches and simulations. Extension of methods for separation of 2-3 components to multicomponent distillation and liquid-liquid extraction. Extraction cascades.
Special separation processes: Selected topics, such as azeotropic separation, extractive distillation, salt distillation, batch distillation, non-equilibrium. Sorption separation processes: Adsorption, ion exchange and chromatography: Sorbents, modeling and operational modes. Databar exercises with programming: Simulation of special and selected processes: MESH/MERSHQ equations. Process Intensification. Introduction of research topics. Advanced process analysis.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, problem sessions, databar exercises.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>To enable the participants to use systematic methods in order to model, analyze and simulate technically important separation processes. Graphical and short-cut methods, as well as mathematical methods are employed. Equilibrium and rate-based methods are considered. The participants will aquire knowledge about, and experience with the foundations of computer-aided tools for process simulation, design and analysis. Multicomponent separations are emphasized.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>An ability to use the techniques, skills, and tools provided in this course, are necessary for the practice of chemical engineering in order to analyze how separation process design and selection affects the sustainability of processes and processing routes.</Remark>
        </Txt>
