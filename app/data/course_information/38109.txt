        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>DTU X-Tech+ dækker de centrale discipliner, der er nødvendige for at skalere en teknologibaseret startup, herunder forretningsmodel, finansiering, markeds- og konkurrenceanalyse, rekruttering og teamudvikling, investor relations (IR), impact, immaterielle rettigheder (IPR), fundingstrategi samt strategisk kommunikation.

Kurset er struktureret omkring en 8-discipliners ramme, hvor hver startup løbende vurderer og udvikler sin position inden for centrale strategiske områder, der er afgørende for skalering.

De deltagende startups er baseret på forskellige teknologier, hvilket gør kurset relevant for student-startups fra alle studieretninger på DTU. Den tekniske udvikling er en forudsætning for startupens fremdrift og forbliver et centralt fokuspunkt. På grund af den teknologiske diversitet indgår den tekniske udvikling dog ikke som egentlig undervisning, men der afsættes tid til, at teams kan arbejde videre med deres egen teknologi parallelt med det strategiske arbejde.

De afsluttende leverancer omfatter en endelig Discipline-vurdering, en tech demo samt et pitch. Eksamen gennemføres som et formelt advisory board-møde, hvor teamet præsenterer og forsvarer deres strategiske udvikling og prioriteringer for næste fase over for deres advisory board fra DTU Board Education.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>DTU X-Tech+ er et avanceret acceleratorforløb for teknologibaserede startups.

Forløbet markerer overgangen fra inkubation til acceleration med stærkt fokus på teknologimodning, markedsvalidering, strategisk positionering og eksekvering. Det kombinerer peer-to-peer-læring, undervisningssessioner, gruppesupervision og struktureret strategisk arbejde med et advisory board fra DTU Board Education.

For at bestå kurset er fysisk deltagelse i alle planlagte undervisningsaktiviteter obligatorisk.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Contents>DTU X-Tech+ covers the core disciplines required to scale a technology-based startup, including business model, finance, market and competition analysis, recruitment and team development, investor relations (IR), impact, intellectual property rights (IPR), funding strategy, and strategic communication.

The course is structured around a 8 Discipline framework, where each startup continuously assesses and develops its position across key strategic areas essential for scaling.

The participating startups are based on diverse technologies, making the course relevant for student startups from all study lines at DTU. While technical development is a prerequisite for startup progress and remains a central focus, it is not taught as a technical curriculum component due to the diversity of technologies represented. Instead, dedicated time is allocated for teams to advance their own technology in parallel with strategic work.

The final deliverables include a final Discipline assessment, a tech demo, and a pitch. The examination takes place as a formal advisory board meeting, where the team presents and defends their strategic development and next-stage priorities with their advisory board from DTU Board Education.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>DTU X-Tech+ is an advanced acceleration programme for tech-based startups.

It marks the transition from incubation to acceleration, with a strong focus on technology maturation, market validation, strategic positioning, and execution. It combines peer-to-peer learning, in-class sessions, group supervision, and structured strategic work with an advisory board from DTU’s Board Education.

To pass the course, attendance at all scheduled course sessions in person is mandatory.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

          <Course_Objectives>DTU X-Tech+ is structured in an accelerator format and is built around a strong learning-by-doing philosophy.

To strengthen and accelerate the learning process, the startup teams work closely with experienced professionals participating through DTU’s Board Education. This collaboration helps professionalise the teams and ensures that the methods and theory taught in the course are applied directly to real strategic decisions and company development.

The overall objective of the course is to accelerate and professionalise technology-based startups, both teams continuing from DTU X-Tech (38103) and selected external startups, enabling them to reach investment readiness and build impactful tech spinouts.

Throughout the programme, all participating teams compete for prizes and grants, which are awarded at the final DTU X-Tech Finals event at the end of the semester.</Course_Objectives>
