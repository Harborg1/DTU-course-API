        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course consists of presentations and discussions of scientific articles and emerging models from the participants’ own work. The evaluation will focus on both deep knowledge of the information delivered and the way that the information is delivered.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Informal discussion of selected articles and research presented by the participants. Participants are a mix of master and PhD students, postdocs and researchers. Participants are expected to have read the manuscripts and other relevant literature before each session. One or Two participants are responsible for each session, and all participants contributes to the discussion. Roughly  75 pct. of the sessions consist of analysis of scientific articles, 25 pct. will focus on project presentation.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall objective is to increase the knowledge of mathematical modelling work across DTU Aqua and strengthen the level of mathematical expertise in developing, analyzing, evaluating and implementing mathematical models of living systems. The journal club provides a forum to discuss the mathematical details of models that are used in all branches of work at DTU Aqua. The specific topics are drawn from the interests of the participants, either from recent or classic work that they would like to understand, or from emerging ideas in their own work. A secondary objective is to improve the communication skills of the participants and create a common language for discussing mathematical models.</Course_Objectives>
          <Remark>1.0 ECTS per semester. A maximum of 3 ECTS, if taken 3 semesters.</Remark>
