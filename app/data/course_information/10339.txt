        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Grundlæggende begreber indenfor heterogen katalyse. Systematisk, ab initio tilgang til fænomener indenfor overfladereaktivitet. Forening af termisk heterogen katalyse og elektrokatalyse. Illustreret gennem eksempler primært indenfor energikonvertering. Nøgleelementer: Potentiel energidiagram, frienergidiagram, overfladeligevægte, rateteori, overfladereaktionskinetik, lineære energirelationer, reaktivitet trends, kort over reaktivitet og selektivitet, elektroniske faktorer indenfor katalyse, katalysatorstruktur, aktive sites, katalysatorscreening og elektrokatalyse.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger og grupperegning.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Jens K. Nørskov, Felix Studt, Frank Abild-Pedersen, Thomas Bligaard: Fundamental Concepts in Heterogeneous Catalysis, Wiley (2014)
http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/9781118892114</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Introduktion til heterogen katalyse, inklusive modeller af overfladereaktivitet, ligevægte for adsorbater på overflader, kinetik for overfladereaktioner, elektroniske og geometriske effekter indenfor heterogen katalyse, reaktivitetskorrelationer mellem overflader, katalysatorstruktur og komposition, samt elektrokatalyse. Udvalgte anvendelser og udfordringer indenfor energitransformationer vil blive diskuteret. Kursusdeltageren vil lære hvordan man teoretisk kan bestemme gode katalysatorer.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Basic concepts in heterogeneous catalysis. Systematic, first principles approach to the science of surface reactivity. Unification of heterogeneous thermal catalysis and electro-catalysis. Illustrated by examples, primarily in energy transformations. Key elements: The potential energy diagram, free energy diagrams, surface equilibria, rate theory, surface reaction kinetics, scaling relations, trends in reactivity, activity and selectivity maps, electronic factors in catalysis, catalyst structure, active sites, catalyst screening, and electrocatalysis.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures and group work.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Jens K. Nørskov, Felix Studt, Frank Abild-Pedersen, Thomas Bligaard: Fundamental Concepts in Heterogeneous Catalysis, Wiley (2014)
http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/9781118892114</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>Introduction to heterogeneous catalysis, including models of surface reactivity, surface equilibria, kinetics of surface reactions, electronic and geometrical effects in heterogeneous catalysis, trends in reactivity, catalyst structure and composition, as well as electro-catalysis. Selected applications and challenges in energy transformations will be discussed. The participant will learn to theoretically determine good catalysts.</Course_Objectives>
        </Txt>
