          <Contents>Kurset består af praktiske laboratorieøvelser, der understøtter og udvider det teoretiske indhold fra 28216 Formuleringsvidenskab for belægninger.         <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>The course consists of practical laboratory exercises that support and extend the theoretical content of 28216 Formulation Science of Coatings. Students work with the full practical coating-development workflow, including raw-material selection, formulation preparation, mixing and dispersion, substrate preparation, coating application, film formation, and characterization.
The exercises cover how formulation and processing variables influence coating behaviour, film quality, defects, adhesion, mechanical properties, barrier performance, and overall coating performance. Students will generate and analyse experimental data, compare formulation outcomes, and relate practical observations to the underlying principles of colloid science, surface chemistry, rheology, materials chemistry, and product design. The course emphasizes the use of experimental results to support formulation decisions, troubleshooting, and performance-based coating development.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>The course is taught through short introductory lectures, supervised laboratory exercises, coating formulation, application, characterization, data analysis, and group discussions. Emphasis is placed on hands-on learning, critical interpretation of results, and connecting practical coating behavior to formulation science.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Objectives>The overall objective of the course is to provide students with practical, hands-on training in coating formulation, application, and characterization, directly supporting the theoretical concepts introduced in 28216 Formulation Science of Coatings. Students will learn how raw-material selection, formulation variables, mixing and dispersion, application methods, film formation, and testing influence coating properties and performance. The course aims to strengthen the connection between formulation science and practical coating development by training students to execute experiments, interpret results critically, and make scientifically justified decisions in coating design, troubleshooting, and performance evaluation.</Course_Objectives>
