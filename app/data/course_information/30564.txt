          <Course_Objectives>Jordens tyngdefelt varierer over sted og tid, afhængig af sammensætningen af ​​materialerne i undergrunden, og tidsmæssigt som funktion af klimatiske og geodynamiske processer. Disse variationer påvirker Jordens form og får vandet i havene til ikke at falde sammen med en simpel overflade, men afhænger af tyngdefelts variationerne. Data fra tyngdefelt satellitter som GRACE giver unik overvågning af indlandsisens afsmeltning, havniveaustigning og global havcirkulation.

Formålet med kurset er at give de studerende avanceret viden om matematiske modeller, avanceret metodik til bestemmelse af tyngdefelt variationer og brug af satellitter til tyngde målinger og bestemmelse af hjælpedata såsom digitale terrænmodeller og brug af tyngdefelt data i moderne geodætisk kortlægning, geofysik og klimaovervågning.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Træningskursus/sommer skole, velegnet til både til kandidat og ph.d. studerende. Det er baseret på tidligere sommerskoler (IAG geoid schools), GRACE satellit skoler (GRACE hackaton) og satellit altimetri workshops, inklusive klima ændrings applikationer. 
Kurset vil løbe i juni 2027 perioden og årligt herefter, og forventes at delvis at overlappe med  en international workshop om ”gravity field science”. Kurset vil for DTU students blive indledt med materiale til gennemlæsning.</DTU_Remark>

          <Course_Literature>Hoffmann Wellenhof og H Moritz: Physical Geodesy, 2nd Ed (Springer, 2006) 
Papers related to geodetic satellites (GRACE, GOCE, NGGM, satellite altimetry). Airborne measurements and global gravity field modelling.</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>The Earth's gravity field varies with location depending on the composition of the materials in the subsurface and time as a function of climatic and geodynamic processes. These variations impact the shape of the Earth and cause the water in the oceans not to coincide with a simple surface but depend on the gravity field variations. Data from gravity field satellites lika GRACE provide unique monitoring of the melt of the ice sheets, sea level rise, and global ocean circulation.

The goal of the course is to provide the students with advanced knowledge about mathematical models, advanced methodology for determining gravity field variations, and the use of satellites for gravity field measurements and determination of auxiliary data such as digital terrain models and use of gravity field data in moderne geodetic mapping, geophysics and climate monitoring.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>Training course / summer school, suitable for both master and PhD students. It is based on previous summer schools (IAG geoid schools), GRACE satellite schools (GRACE hackathon) and satellite altimetry lectures, inclusive climate applications. 
The course will run in the June 2027 period and yearly after that, and will partially include elements of an international workshop on "gravity field science". The course will for DTU students begin with material to read through.</DTU_Remark>
