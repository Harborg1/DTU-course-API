        <Txt Lang="da-DK">
          <Contents>Fysik og kemi for fremstillingsprocesser til kvantekomponenter, herunder krystaldyrkning, epitaxi, tyndfilmteknik, lavtryks-CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition), oxidation, diffusion, ætsning og nanolitografi samt fysisk og elektrisk karakterisering af materialer og delprocesser. Analyse og beskrivelse  af konstruktionen af specifikke kvantekomponenter, såsom Pockels effekt elektrooptiske transducere, enkeltfotonkilder og superledende elektroniske komponenter.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Forelæsninger, gæsteforelæsninger, case-baseret undervisning, gruppearbejde, litteraturstudier, rapportskrivning i grupper.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>

På baggrund af dette at kunne udarbejde detaljerede procesfølger til fremstilling af kvantekomponenter og integrerede systemer baseret på deres specifikationer, fysiske modeller, materialer og tiltænkte virkemåder.</Course_Objectives>

        <Txt Lang="en-GB">
          <Contents>Physics and chemistry of nanofabrication processes for quantum devices, including crystal growth, epitaxy, thin film techniques, low-pressure CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition), oxidation, diffusion, etching and nanolithography as well as physical and electrical characterization of materials and subprocesses. Analysis and description of the design of specific quantum components, such as Pockels effect electro-optical transducers, single photon sources and superconducting electronic devices.</Contents>
          <Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>Lectures, guest lectures, case-based teaching, group work, literature studies, report writing in groups.</Teaching_And_Learning_Methods>
          <Course_Literature>Lecture notes and scientific articles</Course_Literature>
          <Course_Objectives>To enable you to utilize and evaluate the various available methods for manufacturing quantum components and quantum systems and become familiar with the equipment used in the work. On this basis, to be able to prepare detailed process flows for manufacturing quantum devices and integrated systems based on their specifications, physical models, materials and intended modes of operation.</Course_Objectives>
          <DTU_Remark>This is an advanced case-based course where you will be introduced to the physics and chemistry behind micro- and nanofabrication methods and tools used for fabrication of quantum devices.</DTU_Remark>
